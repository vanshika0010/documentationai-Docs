  Cash on Demand 


  Overview

  Cash on Demand is a premium, opt-in service available to sellers on the
  standard D+8 COD cycle. It enables sellers to receive their COD remittance
  earlier instead of waiting until the regular D+8 settlement.


  Payout Modes


  Sellers can choose how they want to receive their early COD remittance:


  1. Wallet Credit


  COD remittance is credited instantly to the seller’s Shiprocket wallet.

  A wallet remittance fee is applied.


  2. Direct Bank Transfer


  COD remittance is transferred directly to the seller’s registered bank
  account.

  A bank transfer remittance fee is applied.

  Remittance Fee


  The Cash on Demand fee is dynamic and seller-specific.


  The applicable fee can differ across sellers.

  The fee can also differ based on the selected payout mode.

  Wallet and bank transfer may have different fee rates.

  The final applicable fee is displayed to the seller before they confirm the
  transaction.



  Eligible Seller:- 


  1. All sellers are eligible EXCEPT those on the Early COD D+2 settlement cycle

  2. Seller must have an active bank account linked to their Shiprocket account

  3. Seller must have opted in / activated the Cash on Demand service

  4. Applicable to domestic sellers only (not available for international
  sellers)









  Product Page




  Product Page


  Step 1: Land on Product Page

  The seller lands on the Cash on Demand Product page, where the available COD
  remittance amount is displayed.

  Step 2: Select Amount

  The seller selects the amount they want to access through Cash on Demand.

  Step 3: Select Payment Method

  The seller selects their preferred payout method:

  Wallet Credit

  Direct Bank Transfer

  The applicable dynamic, seller-specific remittance fee is displayed based on
  the selected payment method and the final net transfer amount will be
  displayed which will be credited to the wallet or bank as the seller
  requested. 

  Step 4: Submit Request

  The seller reviews the amount, payment method, and applicable fee, then clicks
  Submit to initiate the Cash on Demand request.

  Step 5: OTP Verification

  An OTP is sent to the seller's registered mobile number. The seller enters the
  OTP to authenticate and confirm the transaction.

  Step 6: Processing & Credit

  Once the OTP is successfully verified, the transaction is processed.

  The requested amount is expected to be credited to the selected payment method
  within 30 minutes.

  Step 7: Transaction Status

  The transaction status is displayed on the Cash on Demand page.

  Success

  The transaction is completed successfully.

  The amount is credited to the selected payment method.

  Failure

  The transaction status is shown as Failed.

  The seller can click Try Again to initiate the request again.

  Cash on Demand – FAQs

  1. How fast will I receive the money?

  Withdrawals are processed instantly. Funds are typically credited to your
  registered bank account or Shiprocket Wallet within minutes, subject to
  banking network availability.

  2. Will Cash on Demand affect my credit score?

  For standard Cash on Demand withdrawals adjusted against your Shiprocket
  settlements, there is no impact on your credit score. However, the highest
  available limits may be powered by our lending partners and require lender
  onboarding. In such cases, repayment behaviour may be reported to credit
  bureaus as per the lender's policies. Missed or overdue repayments may impact
  your credit score.

  3. What happens if my settlements are not enough to cover the withdrawal?

  Recovery is automatically adjusted from your future COD remittances. If the
  amount remains unpaid after the repayment period or there are insufficient
  future settlements, direct repayment may be required.

  4. Why is verification required for higher limits?

  The highest withdrawal limits are powered by our lending partner network. To
  unlock these limits, a one-time business verification and lender onboarding
  process is required. Verification may include GST, PAN, business details, and
  bank account verification. Once completed, you can continue accessing eligible
  limits without repeating the process for every withdrawal.

  5. Why is my available limit lower than expected?

  Your available limit is calculated dynamically based on recent shipment
  activity, COD collections, settlement trends, and overall business performance
  on Shiprocket. Limits refresh regularly and may increase or decrease over
  time.

  6. Can I repay a withdrawal manually?

  No manual repayment is required. Recovery happens automatically through your
  future COD settlements. If future settlements are insufficient to recover the
  outstanding amount, direct repayment options may be made available where
  applicable.

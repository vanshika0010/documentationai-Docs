If a buyer contacts SR Assured support:

1. Require AWB number from buyer to identify the dispute in ICRM.

2. Initiate action for the AWB on ICRM (DNR cases).

3. Create Nugget ticket with buyer's contact details (email and number); assign to respective KAM.

5. Send first response to buyer confirming escalation and 14-day resolution TAT.

**Resolution TAT:** Maximum 14 days after buyer raises issue.

**Re-escalation allowed:** Within 48 hours of escalation closure.

## OSLA Cases

If a seller approaches **outside of SLA (Out of SLA)**, tag the case as **OSLA** on FD (excluding international and buyer desk cases).

**Do NOT deny at any instance.**

**Sample OSLA scenarios:**

- TAT of 72 hours is breached for ACR response and seller approaches CC.

- Manifest was requested but seller approached after 72 hours of manifest request.

- TAT to raise weight or delivery dispute is breached.

### Amazon Shipping (1 kg, 2 kg, 5 kg)

**Eligibility:**

- GSTIN invoicing enabled in Shiprocket settings.

- Prepaid orders only (COD coming soon).

- Order value below ₹50,000.

- Dimensions: max 70×70×45 cm.

- Weight: min 0.5 kg, max 18 kg.

- Non-hazmat items only.

- Surface mode; thermal printing labels required.

- Cannot make changes to Amazon label (including hiding SKU name).

**Pickup Slots:**

| Shipment Created | Pickup Window      |
| ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Before 9:45 AM   | 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM |
| Before 12:45 PM  | 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM  |

Pickups are **auto-scheduled** — seller does not need to generate pickup manually.

## SOP — Price Hike (Delhivery)

If seller asks why Delhivery price is high:

> Sir / Ma'am, this is a sudden development at the courier's end. Bearing in mind current trends in fuel prices and other systemic factors in the logistics market, this price hike is being done by the courier across its entire client base.

**Hindi:**

> Sir / Ma'am, fuel price mein increase ke kaaran logistic market mein changes hue hain aur isi kaaran Courier partner ne shipping prices change kiye hain. Yeh poore customer base par lagu hai.

Advisors can suggest alternate couriers: Bluedart, Ekart, DTDC, Kerry Logistics, Amazon Shipping, Smart R, Loadshare.

*Do not suggest plan upgrades. Do not say you are raising a complaint — say you have captured feedback.*

### Product Info — Shipcoin

| Question                        | Answer                                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Where can I check my Shipcoins? | Top right icon on myShiprocket app > Click on coins for details               |
| How can I earn Shipcoins?       | 1% of order value OR 250 Shipcoins per fulfilled order (whichever is minimum) |
| What is the validity?           | 12 months from date of credit                                                 |
| How can I use Shipcoins?        | To avail gifts and rewards from leading brands via partner payment gateway    |
| When are Shipcoins credited?    | On each order delivery                                                        |
| Can I transfer Shipcoins?       | No — not transferable to any other member                                     |
| Will seller have visibility?    | No — Shipcoins are for buyers only                                            |

**Steps for buyer to use Shipcoins:**

1. Choose **Pay with Reward** during payment.

2. Enter OTP sent to device and submit.

3. Points are applied and discount is reflected on the order.

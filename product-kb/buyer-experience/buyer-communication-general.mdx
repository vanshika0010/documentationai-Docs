## Which shipment status updates does Shiprocket send to buyers

Shiprocket sends messages for: Packed, Picked, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Arriving Early, Delivery Delayed, and Delivered.

---

## Can I use my own messages to be sent to the buyer

Currently, it is not possible to fully personalize the messages sent to buyers beyond the template options provided in the Notify settings.

## Which shipment status updates does Shiprocket send?

Shiprocket sends buyer notifications for these order statuses:
1. Order Placed / Confirmed
2. Packed
3. Picked Up (by courier)
4. In Transit / Shipped
5. Out for Delivery
6. Delivered
7. NDR (Failed Delivery)
8. RTO Initiated

You can configure which statuses trigger notifications from Settings >> Communication Settings.

---

## How to stop buyer notifications for specific statuses on Shiprocket?

To disable notifications for specific statuses:
1. Go to Settings >> Communication Settings
2. Find the status you want to disable
3. Toggle it off
4. Save settings

Buyers will no longer receive notifications for disabled statuses.

---

## Can buyers track their orders without a Shiprocket account?

Yes. Buyers can track their orders using:
1. The tracking link sent in the order notification SMS/Email/WhatsApp
2. The post-ship tracking page customized by the seller
3. Direct tracking on the courier's website using the AWB number

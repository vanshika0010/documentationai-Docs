## What are Buyer Communication Settings

Shiprocket sends order notifications to your buyers at every order stage such as Order Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, etc. You can enable or disable these and choose from three different templates. Customization of Email and SMS is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

---

## How to customize email notifications for buyers

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Notify >> Email & SMS Communication tab. Click the edit button on a template. You can change the subject line, body content, logo, and product/order attributes. Click 'Update Details' to save.

Note: Customization is only available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

---

## How to stop sharing notifications from Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Notify. Disable all notifications or just specific ones by unchecking the boxes. Click 'Save Details' to proceed.

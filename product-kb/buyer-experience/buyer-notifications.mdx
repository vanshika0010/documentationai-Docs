title: "Buyer Notifications"
description: "Buyer communication settings — order notifications, SMS, email, customization"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are Buyer Communication Settings?">

Shiprocket sends order notifications to your buyers at every order stage such as Order Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, etc. You can enable or disable these notifications and choose from three different templates. Customization is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to customize email notifications for buyers?">

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Notify >> Email & SMS Communication tab. Click the edit button on a template to customize. You can change the subject line, body content, logo, and product/order attributes. Click 'Update Details' to save. Note: Customization is only available on Pro and Enterprise plans.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to stop sharing notifications from Shiprocket?">

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Notify. You can disable all notifications or just specific ones by unchecking the boxes. Click 'Save Details' to proceed.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

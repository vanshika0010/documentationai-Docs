<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Buyer Protect?">

Buyer Protect is an optional paid add-on that helps customers if an order is lost, not received, damaged, or incorrect. If your claim is approved, you'll receive a refund — usually processed within 48 hours after all details are submitted.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What issues are covered under Buyer Protect?">

Buyer Protect covers:

1. Lost in transit: No movement for 20 days from order date.
2. Delivered but not received: Marked delivered, but you didn't get it.
3. Damaged on arrival: Product received in damaged condition.
4. Wrong or short item: Received the wrong product or missing items.

(For Buyer Protection Plus) Delivery delay — order delivered after the promised date.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What isn't covered?">

Claims won't be accepted for: incorrect or incomplete delivery address, refused or unsigned deliveries with valid proof, damage after delivery or due to poor handling, natural disasters or extreme weather events, fragile or prohibited items not packed properly, and digital goods, gift cards, or open-box items.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

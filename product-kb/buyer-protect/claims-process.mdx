---
title: "Claims Process"
description: "Buyer Protect — buyer-facing coverage for lost/damaged/wrong orders, claim process"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When should I raise a claim?">

Claims must be raised within these timelines:

- Lost / Not delivered: Earliest 20 days from order date, Latest 30 days from order date
- Delivered but not received: After 'Delivered' status, within 5 days
- Damaged / Wrong / Short item: On delivery, within 5 days
- Delivery delay (Plus only): On delivery, within 5 days

Claims submitted late will not be accepted.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do I need to submit a claim?">

You must provide: Order ID, AWB number, and contact details. Proof (photos + unboxing video) showing outer box (all sides), inner packing, product close-ups, and shipping label. Any extra details if requested to verify your claim.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I file a claim?">

Go to your Tracking messages on WhatsApp and select 'Raise Claim.' Select your issue (Lost, Not Received, Damaged, Wrong Item, Delivery Delay), describe it briefly, upload evidence, and add bank details. You'll get confirmation and updates via app, email, or SMS.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will I receive my refund?">

Once approved, your refund is processed to your bank account. Timelines depend on your bank or card issuer, but Shiprocket aims to resolve claims within 48 working hours after receiving all required details.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

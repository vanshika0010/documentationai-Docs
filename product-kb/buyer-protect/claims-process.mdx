## When should I raise a claim?

Claims must be raised within these timelines:

Scenario	Earliest	Latest
Lost / Not delivered	20 days from order date	30 days from order date
Delivered but not received	After "Delivered" status	Within 5 days
Damaged / Wrong / Short item	On delivery	Within 5 days
Delivery delay (Plus only)	On delivery	Within 5 days

Claims submitted late will not be accepted.

---

## What do I need to submit a claim?

* Order ID, AWB number, and contact details.
* Proof — photos and unboxing video showing outer box (all sides), inner packing, product close-ups, and shipping label.
* Any extra details if requested to verify your claim.

---

## How do I file a claim?

Go to your Tracking messages on WhatsApp and select "Raise Claim." Select your issue (Lost, Not Received, Damaged, Wrong Item, Delivery Delay), describe it briefly, upload evidence, and add bank details. You'll get confirmation and updates via app, email, or SMS.

---

## When will I receive my refund?

Once approved, your refund is processed to your bank account. Timelines depend on your bank or card issuer, but Shiprocket aims to resolve claims within 48 working hours after receiving all required details.

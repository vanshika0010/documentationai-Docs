  <Accordion title="How to reduce shipping costs by optimizing packaging?">

Use right-sized boxes to reduce volumetric weight. Avoid excessive void fill. Measure packages accurately before entering dimensions. Consider poly bags for soft goods instead of boxes to reduce both weight and size.

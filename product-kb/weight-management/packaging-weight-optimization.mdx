## How to reduce shipping costs by optimizing packaging

Tips to reduce shipping costs:

* Use right-sized boxes to reduce volumetric weight.
* Avoid excessive void fill.
* Measure packages accurately before entering dimensions.
* Consider poly bags for soft goods instead of boxes.

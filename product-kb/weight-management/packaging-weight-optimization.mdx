## What are the packaging tips to reduce weight discrepancy issues?

Packaging tips can help you reduce weight errors in shipping. Some of the important tips are:
1. Use accurate weighing scales - ensure your scale is calibrated
2. Measure packed weight - always weigh the product with all packaging materials (box, bubble wrap, tape, etc.)
3. Record dimensions accurately - measure L x W x H of the packed box in cm
4. Calculate volumetric weight - check if volumetric weight is higher than actual weight
5. Use appropriate packaging - avoid oversized boxes that increase volumetric weight unnecessarily

## How to reduce packaging costs while maintaining safety on Shiprocket?

To optimize packaging:
1. Use right-sized boxes - avoid empty space that adds volumetric weight
2. Use lightweight packing materials (bubble wrap instead of heavy foam)
3. Calculate volumetric weight before packing to ensure actual weight is higher
4. For non-fragile items, use poly bags instead of boxes
5. Negotiate packaging material rates with suppliers for bulk orders

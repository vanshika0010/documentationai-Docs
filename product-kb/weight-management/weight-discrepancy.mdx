## Why is there a weight discrepancy pop up on my order?

Weight discrepancy arises when there's a difference between the weight entered by the seller at the time of order creation and the actual weight measured by the courier. Reasons include:
1. Incorrect weight entered when creating the order
2. Courier uses volumetric weight which is higher than actual weight
3. Packaging weight not included in declared weight

---

## How to dispute a weight discrepancy on Shiprocket?

To dispute a weight discrepancy:
1. Go to the order with the weight discrepancy
2. Click on "Raise Dispute" or go to Weight Discrepancy section
3. Upload proof: Photo of package on weighing scale along with the AWB/label visible
4. Submit the dispute
Shiprocket's weight dispute team reviews within 7-10 business days.

---

## What proof is needed to dispute weight discrepancy?

Required proof for weight dispute:
1. Clear photo of the package on a weighing scale showing the weight
2. AWB number visible in the same photo
3. Dimensions of the package (for volumetric weight disputes)
4. Original packing slip/product weight certificate if available

---

## How long does weight dispute resolution take?

Weight dispute resolution typically takes 7-10 business days after submission of proof. Complex cases may take longer.

---

## What is the weight discrepancy charge on Shiprocket?

Weight discrepancy charge = Additional freight for the difference in weight

If you declared 500g but courier charged for 1kg, the discrepancy charge = freight for 500g difference (the additional 500g at the applicable rate).

---

## How to avoid weight discrepancy charges?

To minimize weight discrepancy charges:
1. Weigh products accurately before creating orders
2. Include packaging material weight in your measurement
3. Measure dimensions precisely for volumetric calculation
4. Use a calibrated digital scale
5. If frequently facing discrepancies, review your measurement process

---

## What is the weight freeze on Shiprocket?

Weight freeze or on-hold means Shiprocket has put a hold on your COD remittance due to pending weight discrepancy charges. To unfreeze:
1. Pay the disputed/accepted weight charges
2. Or raise and resolve the disputes
3. Once charges are cleared, the hold is released

---

## Can I accept or reject a weight discrepancy on Shiprocket?

Yes. When a weight discrepancy is raised:
1. Review the discrepancy details
2. Accept: Pay the additional charges if you agree
3. Dispute: Upload proof if you disagree

All pending discrepancies are shown in the Weight Discrepancy section under Finances.

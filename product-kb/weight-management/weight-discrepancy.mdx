<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a weight discrepancy?">

A weight discrepancy occurs when the weight or dimensions declared at the time of order creation differ from what the courier measures at their facility. If the courier's measurement is higher, you will be charged the difference.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to dispute a weight discrepancy charge?">

Go to Weight Reconciliation from the left menu. Find the disputed shipment and click 'Raise Dispute.' Upload evidence (photos of the package with weight scale showing actual weight). Shiprocket will investigate with the courier and resolve within 7-14 days.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

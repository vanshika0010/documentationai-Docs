## How is volumetric weight calculated and how does it affect my shipping charges

**Volumetric weight** = (Length cm × Breadth cm × Height cm) ÷ 5000

If this value is higher than the actual dead weight, the courier charges based on volumetric weight.

**Example:** A 40×30×20 cm box = 24,000 ÷ 5,000 = 4.8 kg volumetric. If actual weight is 2 kg, you are billed for 4.8 kg.

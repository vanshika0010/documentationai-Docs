## How to calculate the volumetric weight of my shipment?

Determine the volumetric weight of a box:

Divide the cubic size* of the box by the DIM factor** to determine the Dimensional Weight.

DIM Factor = 5000 (for most couriers on Shiprocket)

Volumetric Weight = (Length cm x Width cm x Height cm) / 5000

For example: A box that is 30 cm x 20 cm x 15 cm = 9000 / 5000 = 1.8 kg volumetric weight

Chargeable weight = Max (Dead weight, Volumetric weight)

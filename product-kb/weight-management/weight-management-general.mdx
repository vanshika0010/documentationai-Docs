## What is the Weight Reconciliation feature in Shiprocket

Weight Reconciliation is a tool that helps you track and dispute weight discrepancy charges from couriers. It shows all shipments where the courier charged a different weight than what you declared, and lets you raise disputes with supporting evidence.

---

## What is a frozen shipment in Shiprocket

A frozen shipment is one where forward movement has stopped (no tracking updates for a prolonged period). Frozen shipments may be lost, stuck at a courier hub, or awaiting customs clearance. Raise a support ticket to investigate.

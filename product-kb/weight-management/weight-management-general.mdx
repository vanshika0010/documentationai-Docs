## How long does it take to resolve my order weight dispute?

Once you raise a weight dispute, it gets forwarded to our weight experts who try to resolve it as quickly as possible. Generally, it takes about 7-10 business days to resolve.

---

## What is chargeable weight on Shiprocket?

Chargeable weight = Maximum of (Dead weight, Volumetric weight)

Shiprocket always charges based on the higher of the two weights to ensure accurate freight calculation.

---

## How to view weight discrepancy history on Shiprocket?

To view weight discrepancy history:
1. Go to Finances >> Weight Discrepancy section
2. View all discrepancies with their status (Pending, Accepted, Disputed, Resolved)
3. Filter by date range or status
4. Download report for detailed analysis

---

## What happens if my weight dispute is rejected?

If your weight dispute is rejected:
1. The disputed charges are confirmed and will be deducted
2. You can escalate by providing additional evidence
3. Raise a support ticket if you believe the rejection is incorrect
4. If accepted previously, the charge stands

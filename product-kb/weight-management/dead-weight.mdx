<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is dead weight in shipping?">

Dead weight is the actual physical weight of a package as measured on a scale. It is compared against volumetric weight, and the higher of the two is used for billing. Accurate dead weight entry prevents unexpected discrepancy charges.

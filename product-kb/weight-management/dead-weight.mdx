## What is Dead Weight?

Dead weight is the physical weight of the package, and it is one of the two weights (along with volumetric weight) used to determine the chargeable weight.

Dead weight = Actual physical weight of the fully packed shipment measured on a scale (in kg)

Chargeable weight = Max(Dead weight, Volumetric weight)

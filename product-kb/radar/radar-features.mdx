## What features does Shiprocket RADAR offer

RADAR features include:

* Real-time pincode-level performance scores per courier
* Stress indicators showing overloaded couriers
* Historical delivery trends
* Comparison view across couriers
* Integration with Shiprocket's courier recommendation engine

---

## What is a stress indicator in RADAR

A stress indicator in RADAR signals that a courier is experiencing unusually high volume or operational pressure at a specific pincode, which may lead to delays or NDRs. Use this to proactively switch to a less stressed courier for that route.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What features does Shiprocket RADAR offer?">

RADAR features include: real-time pincode-level performance scores per courier, stress indicators showing overloaded couriers, historical delivery trends, comparison view across couriers, and integration with Shiprocket's courier recommendation engine.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a stress indicator in RADAR?">

A stress indicator in RADAR signals that a courier is experiencing unusually high volume or operational pressure at a specific pincode, which may lead to delays or NDRs. Use this to proactively switch to a less stressed courier for that route.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

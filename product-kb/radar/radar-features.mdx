## What is the Pincode Performance feature in Radar?

Pincode Performance in Radar shows delivery success rates for specific pincodes across all courier partners. Use this to identify which courier delivers best to a given pincode.

---

## Can Radar help reduce RTO rates?

Yes. By using Radar's courier intelligence:
1. Select couriers with higher delivery success rates for specific pincodes
2. Avoid couriers with high RTO rates for your common delivery areas
3. React quickly when a courier shows stress indicators before RTOs spike
4. Make data-backed courier assignment decisions

---

## Does Radar integrate with courier assignment rules on Shiprocket?

Radar insights can inform your courier assignment rules in the Shiprocket panel. Based on Radar's recommendations, you can update your courier preference settings to automatically route orders to better-performing couriers.

---

## What is the coverage of Radar's pincode data?

Radar covers pan-India pincodes with real-time performance data from all major courier partners active on the Shiprocket platform.

---

## How frequently is Radar data updated?

Radar data is updated in real-time as delivery events occur across Shiprocket's network, ensuring the most current performance insights are always available.

## How does Radar help in courier selection?

Radar helps in courier selection by:
1. Showing real-time performance scores for each courier per pincode
2. Flagging couriers with poor performance for specific routes
3. Recommending the best courier based on delivery success rate
4. Alerting when a previously good courier starts showing stress indicators
5. Enabling data-driven courier assignment rules

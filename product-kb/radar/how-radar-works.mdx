## How does Shiprocket Radar work?

Radar works by:
1. Continuously collecting real-time delivery data from all courier partners
2. Analyzing delivery success rates, NDR rates, and RTO rates by pincode
3. Using AI/ML algorithms to identify stress indicators and patterns
4. Generating courier recommendations based on historical and real-time performance
5. Presenting insights in an easy-to-use dashboard

---

## What are stress indicators in Radar?

Stress indicators in Radar flag pincodes or courier routes that are experiencing:
1. Higher than normal NDR rates
2. Delivery delays
3. Increased RTO rates
4. Service disruptions

These indicators help sellers proactively switch couriers for problematic routes.

---

## How to access Shiprocket Radar?

To access Radar:
1. Go to your Shiprocket panel
2. Look for 'Radar' in the left navigation menu or under Analytics/Intelligence tools
3. The Radar dashboard shows courier performance insights and recommendations

## What should I do if I see an incorrect charge or interest amount?

If you notice any discrepancy:
1. Take a screenshot or note of the transaction
2. Contact our support team via email at capital@shiprocket.com or call 8750538806.

Our team will coordinate with the lending partner to investigate and resolve the issue quickly.

## What is a CRIF credit score, and why is it important

A CRIF credit score is a three-digit number (300–900) representing an individual's creditworthiness based on their past credit behavior. It is used by banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions to assess the risk of lending money. A CRIF score of 730 or above is considered very good.

## Does checking my credit score affect it

No. Checking your own CRIF credit score is a soft inquiry and does not impact your score. However, when a lender checks your score during a loan application, it is considered a hard inquiry, which may slightly lower your score.

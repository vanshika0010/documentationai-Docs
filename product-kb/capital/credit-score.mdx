<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a CRIF credit score, and why is it important?">

A CRIF credit score is a three-digit number ranging from 300 to 900 that represents an individual's creditworthiness based on their past credit behavior. It is used by banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions to assess the risk of lending money to a borrower. A CRIF score of 730 or above is considered very good.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does checking my credit score affect it?">

No, checking your own CRIF credit score is a soft inquiry and does not impact your score. However, when a lender checks your score during a loan application, it is considered a hard inquiry, which may slightly lower your score.

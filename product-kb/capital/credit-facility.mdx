<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a credit facility?">

A credit facility is a pre-approved loan limit that gives you the flexibility to withdraw money whenever your business needs it. Unlike a regular loan, you pay interest only on the amount you withdraw — not on the entire credit line.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the credit facility multiple times?">

Absolutely. As long as your credit line is active and you have sufficient available balance, you can withdraw funds as needed — multiple times. You may have up to 5 active loans at a time.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my limit reset after repayment?">

Yes. Once you repay any withdrawn amount, that portion of the limit becomes available again for use as a top-up. It works just like a revolving credit facility.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

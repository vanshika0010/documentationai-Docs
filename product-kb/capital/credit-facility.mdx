## What is a credit facility?

A credit facility is a pre-approved loan limit that gives you the flexibility to withdraw money whenever your business needs it. Unlike a regular loan, you pay interest only on the amount you withdraw - not on the entire credit line.

---

## Can I use the credit facility multiple times?

Absolutely. As long as your credit line is active and you have sufficient available balance, you can withdraw funds as needed-multiple times. You may have up to 5 active loans at a time.

---

## Will my limit reset after repayment?

Yes. Once you repay any withdrawn amount, that portion of the limit becomes available again for use as a top-up. It works just like a revolving credit facility, helping you manage cash flow efficiently. This depends on the timely repayment behaviour of all the live withdrawals or loans.

---

## How is this different from a regular business loan?

A regular business loan gives you a lump sum upfront with fixed EMIs starting immediately. A credit facility gives you:
1. Pre-approved limit (use anytime)
2. Withdraw in parts, based on business needs
3. Interest only on what you use
4. Limit resets as you repay (like a revolving facility)
It’s much more flexible and cost-efficient for short-term needs or working capital.

---

## Where can I see my Statement of Account?

You can view your Statement of Account from the Capital dashboard. Simply log in, go to the Instant Cash section, and click to download “View Statement” against the credit facility.

---

## Can I make direct payment against my overdue amount?

Yes, you can make a direct payment towards your overdue amount. Simply log in to the Credit Facility Dashboard to make the payment. Additionally, you’ll receive payment reminders via SMS, which will include a secure payment link for your convenience.

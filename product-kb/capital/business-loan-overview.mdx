## What is Shiprocket Business Loan?

Shiprocket Business Loan is a financing solution created for sellers registered on the Shiprocket platform. In partnership with Flexi, this loan service is designed to provide sellers with access to hassle-free term loans for scaling operations, managing cash flow, stocking inventory, or covering other business-related expenses.

This product is fully integrated into the Shiprocket seller panel, ensuring a seamless and efficient experience. Sellers can complete the entire loan journey—from application to disbursement—online, without the need to visit a bank or fill out excessive paperwork.

## How is a credit facility different from a regular loan?

A regular business loan gives you a fixed lump sum amount upfront, and you start paying interest on the full amount from day one. In contrast, a credit facility provides you with a pre-approved limit from which you can withdraw funds as and when needed. You pay emi + interest only on the amount you actually withdraw, not the entire limit.

Each withdrawal from your credit facility is treated as a separate short-term loan, with its own repayment schedule. You can make unlimited withdrawals, but only up to 5 active loans can exist at a time. As soon as you repay one, the slot becomes available again for the next withdrawal.

| Feature | Flexi Credit Facility | Term Loan |
|---|---|---|
| **Disbursement** | Access funds as needed up to the limit provided | Lump-sum disbursed upfront. No flexibility |
| **Repayment** | Pay against each withdrawal as per the flexible schedule provided (weekly installment) | Fixed repayment schedule (monthly) over a set period |
| **Interest** | Charged only on the amount withdrawn or utilised | Charged on the entire loan amount from day one |
| **Purpose** | Short term, recurring expenses (working capital, inventory purchase, cash flow gaps, emergency, etc) | Long term, one-time expenses |
| **Flexibility** | High, funds can be used, repaid and reused | Low, funds are used once, cannot re-draw |

## How is my CRIF credit score calculated?

Your CRIF Credit Score is determined by analyzing your financial and credit behavior using the following factors:

1. Repayment History — 35% — Late or missed payments lower the score.
2. Credit Utilization — 30% — Using over 30% of your available credit negatively impacts the score.
3. Length of Credit History — 15% — A longer history of responsible credit use improves your score.
4. Credit Mix (Secured vs. Unsecured Loans) — 10% — A mix of secured and unsecured credit benefits your score.
5. New Credit Inquiries — 10% — Too many loan applications in a short period lower your score.

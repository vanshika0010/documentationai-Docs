<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is a credit facility different from a regular loan?">

A regular business loan gives you a fixed lump sum upfront, and you start paying interest on the full amount from day one. A credit facility provides a pre-approved limit from which you can withdraw funds as needed. You pay interest only on the amount you actually withdraw, not the entire limit.

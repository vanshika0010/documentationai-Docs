## What is Shiprocket Business Loan

Shiprocket Business Loan is a financing solution for sellers registered on the Shiprocket platform. In partnership with Flexi, this loan service provides access to hassle-free term loans for scaling operations, managing cash flow, stocking inventory, or covering other business-related expenses. The entire journey — from application to disbursement — is done online.

## How is a credit facility different from a regular loan

A regular business loan gives you a fixed lump sum upfront, and you pay interest on the full amount from day one. A credit facility provides a pre-approved limit from which you can withdraw funds as needed — you pay interest only on the amount you actually withdraw, not the entire limit.

## Do I have to withdraw the full amount at once

No. You have the flexibility to withdraw any amount — large or small — as per your needs, up to your available limit.

---

## Is there a minimum amount I need to withdraw

Yes, the minimum withdrawal amount is ₹10,000. You can withdraw any amount starting from ₹10,000 up to your available credit limit.

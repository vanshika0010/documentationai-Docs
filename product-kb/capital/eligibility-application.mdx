<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who is eligible to avail of funds in Shiprocket Capital?">

All brands associated with Shiprocket can apply for our revenue-based financing.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I apply for Shiprocket Business Loan?">

Step-by-Step Application Process:
1. Open your Shiprocket seller panel.
2. Click 'Apply Now' on the home screen.
3. Validate your mobile number via OTP.
4. Customize your loan offer using the sliders for amount (₹50,000 to ₹20,00,000) and tenure (6–36 months).
5. Provide personal and business details (most fields are pre-filled).
6. Upload any missing documents if required.
7. Submit for review — Flexi processes the application in real time.
8. Review and accept the loan offer displayed on screen.
9. Complete eSign and eMandate steps on Flexi's portal.
10. Receive loan disbursement confirmation on your Shiprocket panel.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Minimum Eligibility Criteria to Apply for a Credit facility">

To be eligible:
1. You must be an active Shiprocket seller.
2. Minimum age should be 21 years.
3. Your business should have been operational for at least 12 months.
Eligibility is further determined by your credit profile and order volume on Shiprocket.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My application was rejected. What can I do next?">

If your application is rejected: you can reapply after 6 months, especially if your business performance or credit score improves. Ensure your KYC and business documents are valid and updated. You can also reach out to our support team to understand the reason.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

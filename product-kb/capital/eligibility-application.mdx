## Who is eligible to avail of funds in shiprocket capital?

All brands associated with Shiprocket can apply for our revenue-based financing.

---

## How can I apply for Shiprocket Business Loan?

Step-by-Step Application Process:

1. Open your Shiprocket seller panel.
2. Click to Apply: On the home screen, locate the "Apply Now" button and click it to begin your loan application process.
3. Validate Mobile Number: Your registered mobile number will be prefilled. If needed, you can edit it and verify it via OTP.
4. Customize Your Loan Offer: Use the sliders to adjust the loan amount (₹50,000 to ₹20,00,000) and repayment tenure (6–36 months). The system will dynamically show your estimated EMI.
5. Provide Personal and Business Details: Fill out the forms for your personal and business details. Most fields will be pre-filled from your seller profile.
6. Upload Missing Documents (If Required): If specific documents are required, you’ll be asked to upload them.
7. Submit for Review: Once all details and documents are submitted, Flexi will process your application in real-time.
8. Loan Offer Generated: After successful verification, a loan offer will be displayed with details such as approved loan amount, interest rate and tenure, EMI and any applicable charges.
9. Complete eSign and Compliance Steps: Click “Next” to proceed to Flexi’s portal for eSign and eMandate for repayment, and final KYC verification.
10. Loan Disbursement Details Screen: After approval, you’ll be redirected to the Shiprocket seller panel where you will see the final loan disbursement confirmation.

## Minimum Eligibility Criteria to Apply for a Credit facility

To be eligible to apply for a credit facility:
1. You must be an active Shiprocket seller
2. Minimum age should be 21 years
3. Your business should have been operational for at least 12 months
Eligibility is further determined by your credit profile and order volume on Shiprocket.

---

## My application was rejected. What can I do next?

If your application is rejected:
1. You can reapply after 6 Months, especially if your business performance or credit score improves
2. Ensure your KYC and business documents are valid and updated
3. You can also reach out to our support team to understand the reason and what can be improved

## When does repayment start

Repayment begins only after you withdraw funds. You'll receive a detailed repayment schedule once your disbursement is complete.

---

## What happens if I miss a repayment

If you miss a repayment, a 7-day grace period is provided. After that, interest on the delayed amount and penal charges will be applicable.

---

## When and how do I repay the amount I've used

1. Repayment begins only after you withdraw funds.
2. You will receive a repayment schedule with EMI details.
3. Repayments are primarily done through your COD remittance — a portion of your Shiprocket COD payouts is automatically adjusted towards your loan repayment on the scheduled date.
4. If repayment is due for more than 10 days, the system switches to auto-debit (eNACH) from your registered bank account.

---

## If any loan has an overdue EWI, will I be allowed to use my remaining credit limit

No. You will not be allowed to draw further loans if any of your existing loans have an overdue EWI, even by just one day. You'll be able to access your remaining credit limit only after the overdue amount is cleared in full.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does repayment start?">

Repayment begins only after you withdraw funds. You'll receive a detailed repayment schedule once your disbursement is complete.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I miss a repayment?">

If you miss a repayment, a 7-day grace period is provided. After that, interest on the delayed amount and penal charges will be applicable.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When and how do I repay the amount I've used?">

Repayments are primarily done through your COD remittance — a portion of your Shiprocket COD payouts is automatically adjusted towards your loan repayment on the scheduled date. If repayment is due for more than 10 days, the system switches to auto-debit (eNACH) from your registered bank account.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When does repayment start?

Repayment begins only after you withdraw funds. You’ll receive a detailed repayment schedule once your disbursement is complete.

---

## What happens if I miss a repayment?

If you miss a repayment, a 7-day grace period is provided. After that, interest on the delayed amount and penal charges will be applicable.

---

## Is there a minimum or maximum withdrawal amount?

Minimum withdrawal: ₹10,000
Maximum withdrawal: Up to your available credit limit, which resets as you repay. You can withdraw in multiple tranches based on your needs.

---

## When and how do I repay the amount I’ve used?

1. Repayment begins only after you withdraw funds
2. You will receive a repayment schedule with EMI details
3. Repayments are primarily done through your COD remittance — a portion of your Shiprocket COD payouts is automatically adjusted towards your loan repayment on the scheduled date.
4. If repayment is due for more than 10 days, the system switches to auto-debit (eNACH) from your registered bank account to ensure timely payments.
5. This dual setup ensures a convenient and secure repayment process while minimizing the risk of missed EMIs.

---

## If any loan is having an overdue EWI, will I be allowed to use my remaining credit limit?

No, you will not be allowed to draw further loans if any of your existing loans have an overdue EWI, even if it’s overdue by just one day. You’ll be able to access your remaining credit limit only after the overdue amount is cleared in full.

---

## Is there an option for auto-debit or scheduled repayments?

Yes. As part of the process, you’ll be required to set up auto-debit (eNACH) to ensure smooth and timely repayments. This minimizes the risk of missing a due date.

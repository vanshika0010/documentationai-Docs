## What is NDR?

NDR (Non-Delivery Report) is a shipment status generated when the courier attempts delivery but cannot complete it. 
It appears in the NDR panel within your Shiprocket account and highlights all orders where delivery was unsuccessful. Click on NDR/Delivery Boost option from the left menu to open NDR page.
With the updated NDR experience, Shiprocket automatically triggers reattempts for all eligible shipments. 
However, if seller input is required—for example, if the address is incomplete, the buyer was unreachable, or the courier needs clarification—you can identify such shipments using the "Select Input Required → Yes" filter at the top of the NDR panel.

Once filtered, click "Act on Shipment" to take corrective action such as updating delivery details, submitting buyer intent proof, or reporting a shipment issue. 
These updates help increase the chances of a successful reattempt. Smart nudges like RTO Score, Address Score, and Tracking History also guide you with recommendations on how to improve delivery outcomes.

## What is NDR Panel?

* NDR stands for Non-Delivery Report, which displays the orders in an undelivered status. If NDR is generated, seller have to select the re-attempt delivery my making a contact with the buyer directly through the panel.
* NDR Panel in Shiprocket is a feature designed to provide the sellers with visibility and control over their shipments that could not be delivered successfully. Click on the NDR/Delivery Boost option from the left menu to open NDR page.
* NDR is a component associated with Delivery Boost feature.
* Shiprocket also offers a dedicated NDR Dashboard to empower sellers with comprehensive insights and tools for managing non-delivery-related shipments effectively. You can see the NDR Funnels, NDR Responses, NDR Raised, Courier-wise, Zone-wise, etc.
   * Go to 'Dashboard' from the left menu on your seller panel and Click on 'NDR' tab located at top menu bar to visit the NDR dashboard
   * Here, you can see the all the NDR related details which includes NDR Raised, NDR Raised Percentage, Action Required, NDR Responses, NDR Funnel, etc.

## Why was my package returned to origin (RTO)?

Since your package was returned to origin (RTO), it's likely that the courier executive made a maximum of 3 attempts to deliver the order before marking it as RTO or if there was a Customer Refused OTP Verified scan received. However, the exact number of attempts may vary depending on the selected courier partner and the reason for non-delivery (NDR). The package can also go to RTO if from the Website or from the app you have clicked RTO for the shipment.

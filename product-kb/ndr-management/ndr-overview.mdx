## What is an NDR (Non-Delivery Report)

An NDR (Non-Delivery Report) is generated when a courier is unable to deliver a shipment. Common NDR reasons include: buyer not available, wrong address, buyer refused delivery, and access issues. NDRs require action (reattempt, address update, or RTO) within a specified timeframe.

---

## How to view my NDR shipments

Go to the NDR Management section from the left menu. You can see all shipments with pending NDRs, their NDR reason, and the days remaining to take action.

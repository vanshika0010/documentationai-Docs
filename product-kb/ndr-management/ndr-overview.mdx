## What is NDR?

NDR stands for Non-Delivery Report. It is generated when a delivery attempt fails. Common NDR reasons include:
1. Buyer not available at the time of delivery
2. Wrong or incomplete delivery address
3. Buyer refused to accept the shipment
4. Phone number unreachable
5. Area unreachable by courier

---

## What happens when an NDR is created on Shiprocket?

When an NDR is created:
1. The order moves to NDR status in your Shiprocket panel
2. You get notified about the failed delivery
3. NDR action is required within the SLA timeline
4. Options: Request reattempt with instructions or initiate RTO

---

## What is the NDR action window on Shiprocket?

NDR action window is the time given to sellers to decide what to do with an NDR order. If no action is taken within the specified window, couriers typically initiate RTO automatically. The window varies by courier (usually 2-5 days).

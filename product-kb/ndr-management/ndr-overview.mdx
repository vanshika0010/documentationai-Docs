<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an NDR (Non-Delivery Report)?">

An NDR (Non-Delivery Report) is generated when a courier is unable to deliver a shipment. Common NDR reasons include: buyer not available, wrong address, buyer refused delivery, and access issue. NDRs require action (reattempt, address update, or RTO) within a specified timeframe.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to view my NDR shipments?">

Go to the NDR Management section from the left menu. You can see all shipments with pending NDRs, their NDR reason, and the days remaining to take action.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

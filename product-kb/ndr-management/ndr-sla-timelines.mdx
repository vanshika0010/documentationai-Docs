## How does Contact Buyer or Customer work for NDR order?

The Contact Buyer flow now functions within Act on Shipment → Provide Proof of Buyer Intent. When you contact the buyer, you are required to provide proof that the buyer still wants the delivery. This can be done by:
1. Uploading a screenshot of the buyer's message confirming delivery
2. Submitting the buyer's updated contact details or address

---

## What is the NDR action window?

The NDR action window is the time available to take action on an NDR before the courier automatically initiates RTO. Window duration varies by courier partner (typically 2-5 days). Act on NDRs promptly to avoid unnecessary returns.

---

## What is the SLA for NDR reattempt after submitting a request?

After submitting a reattempt request:
* Standard SLA: Courier takes action within 24-48 hours
* Actual timing depends on the courier's operational schedule for that pincode
* Check the NDR status in the panel for real-time updates

---

## How long does Shiprocket hold an NDR before auto-RTO?

Shiprocket's NDR auto-RTO timeline depends on the courier partner's policies:
* Most couriers: 3-5 days after NDR generation
* After this period, the courier automatically initiates RTO if no action is taken

Use Delivery Boost / Engage 360 NDR recovery for proactive management within this window.

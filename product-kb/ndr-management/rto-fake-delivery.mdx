## What is RTO (Return to Origin)

RTO (Return to Origin) happens when a shipment cannot be delivered and is sent back to the pickup address. Common reasons: buyer not available after multiple attempts, wrong address, buyer refused delivery, or lost shipment.

---

## What is fake delivery and how to report it

Fake delivery is when a courier marks an order as 'Delivered' without actually delivering it. If this happens, raise a support ticket immediately with the AWB number, order ID, and a statement from the buyer that the order was not received. Shiprocket will investigate with the courier.

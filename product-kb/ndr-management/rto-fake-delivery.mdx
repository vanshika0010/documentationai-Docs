## How many attempts will be made by the courier company to deliver my shipment?

The number of delivery attempts varies by courier partner. Typically:
* Most couriers make 2-3 delivery attempts
* The exact number depends on the NDR reason and courier policies
* After maximum attempts, the shipment is returned (RTO)

Check the specific courier's terms in the Shiprocket panel for exact attempt limits.

---

## What is fake delivery?

Fake delivery is when a courier partner marks a shipment as "Delivered" without actually delivering it to the buyer. This is a fraudulent scan and can be disputed.

---

## How to dispute a fake delivery?

To dispute a fake delivery:
1. Go to the shipment in the Orders section
2. Find the order marked as "Delivered"
3. If buyer confirms non-receipt, click "Raise Dispute" or "Fake Delivery"
4. Provide:
   * Buyer's statement confirming non-receipt
   * Proof that the delivery was not made (if available)
5. Shiprocket will investigate and coordinate with the courier

---

## What is OTP delivery verification?

OTP (One-Time Password) delivery verification requires the buyer to share a delivery OTP with the courier agent before the order is marked as delivered. This prevents fake delivery scans.

To enable OTP delivery:
* Go to Settings in your Shiprocket panel
* Find the OTP Delivery Verification option
* Enable it for eligible couriers

---

## When does a shipment get marked as RTO?

A shipment gets marked as RTO in these situations:
1. Maximum delivery attempts exhausted (2-3 attempts)
2. Buyer refused to accept the delivery
3. Address not found or incomplete
4. Customer was unavailable on all attempts
5. Seller initiated RTO from the NDR panel
6. COD amount not ready

---

## Can I stop an RTO in progress?

If an RTO is in progress but the shipment hasn't been returned yet:
1. Contact Shiprocket support immediately with the AWB number
2. The support team will try to intercept with the courier
3. If the courier has already processed RTO, interception may not be possible

Act quickly as the window for interception is limited.

---

## What are RTO charges on Shiprocket?

RTO (Return to Origin) charges are applied when a shipment is returned. These charges:
* Are based on weight slab and zone (same as forward shipping)
* Are deducted from your Shiprocket wallet
* Are detailed in your freight invoice

Check the Rate Calculator in your panel for specific RTO charges.

---

## Can I avoid RTO charges if the return was due to courier's fault?

If the RTO was due to the courier's error (fake NDR, wrong address scan, etc.):
1. Raise a dispute with evidence
2. Shiprocket will investigate
3. If the courier's fault is established, you may be eligible for a waiver of RTO charges

Raise a support ticket with the AWB number and evidence.

---

## What happens after RTO is delivered to my address?

After RTO is delivered:
1. Update the shipment as RTO Received in the Shiprocket panel
2. Check the item condition
3. If item is damaged due to courier, raise a claim
4. Restock the item for future orders

---

## How to track my RTO shipment?

To track an RTO shipment:
1. Go to Orders section in Shiprocket panel
2. Find the order
3. Check the tracking section
4. RTO tracking shows the return journey from the buyer's location back to your pickup address

---

## What is RTO rate and how to reduce it?

RTO rate is the percentage of orders that are returned to origin. To reduce RTO:
1. Verify address and phone number before shipping
2. Use Engage 360 Order Confirmation flow for COD orders
3. Use Address Verification via Engage 360
4. Choose courier partners with better delivery performance for specific pincodes
5. Use pre-delivery confirmation to ensure buyer availability
6. Enable NDR recovery via Engage 360

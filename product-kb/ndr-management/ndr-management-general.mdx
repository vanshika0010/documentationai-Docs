## How long does it take for an action to be implemented on my NDR?

Generally, courier takes action within 24 to 48 hours on your undelivered shipment.

---

## Can I take action on NDR from the Shiprocket mobile app?

Yes. You can take action on NDR orders from the Shiprocket mobile app. The NDR management features are available in the app similar to the web panel.

---

## What is the difference between NDR and RTO?

NDR (Non-Delivery Report): A status indicating that a delivery attempt was made but was unsuccessful. Action can still be taken to reattempt delivery.

RTO (Return to Origin): The shipment is being returned to the seller. This happens after all delivery attempts are exhausted or when the seller/buyer requests return.

---

## How does the NDR Dashboard help sellers?

The NDR Dashboard in Shiprocket provides:
1. Overview of all NDR orders (action required, auto-triggered)
2. NDR reason breakdown (CNA, wrong address, refused, etc.)
3. Courier-wise and zone-wise NDR analysis
4. NDR funnel tracking from NDR raised to delivery/RTO
5. Performance trends for NDR management

Access from Dashboard >> NDR tab.

---

## What is Delivery Boost in Shiprocket?

Delivery Boost is Shiprocket's AI-powered NDR management feature that:
1. Automatically triggers reattempts for eligible NDR shipments
2. Sends WhatsApp/IVR communication to buyers for failed deliveries
3. Captures buyer responses to enable successful reattempts
4. Provides smart nudges (RTO Score, Address Score) to guide seller actions
5. Reduces RTO rates through proactive intervention

---

## Can Shiprocket automatically handle NDR without seller intervention?

Yes. With Delivery Boost enabled, Shiprocket automatically triggers reattempts for eligible NDR shipments. However, for shipments where seller input is required (wrong address, unreachable buyer), the seller needs to take action via the NDR panel.

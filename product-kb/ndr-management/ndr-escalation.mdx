## How to report or raise a concern with NDR shipment?

To raise a concern about an NDR shipment:
1. Go to the NDR order in NDR Management
2. Click on Raise Concern or Escalate
3. Select the issue type
4. Provide details and supporting information
5. Submit the escalation

Alternatively, raise a support ticket with the AWB number and NDR details.

---

## How to escalate an NDR that hasn't been acted upon by courier?

If courier hasn't taken action on your NDR request:
1. Check the NDR status in Shiprocket panel
2. Verify the action was submitted properly
3. If still no movement after 2 days, raise a support ticket
4. Include AWB number, NDR action date, and expected action
5. Shiprocket will coordinate with the courier for resolution

---

## How to contact buyer for NDR on Shiprocket?

For NDR where buyer needs to be contacted:
1. Use the Contact Buyer option in NDR Management
2. Shiprocket/Engage 360 can send automated WhatsApp/SMS to buyer
3. You can also use the buyer's contact details to call directly
4. After contacting buyer, update the NDR with action

---

## How many reattempts are allowed for NDR on Shiprocket?

Reattempt limits vary by courier partner. Typically:
- Most couriers allow 2-3 delivery attempts
- After maximum attempts, RTO is initiated

Check specific courier terms for reattempt limits. Shiprocket coordinates reattempts within courier limits.

---

## What is the difference between NDR and RTO on Shiprocket?

NDR (Non-Delivery Report): Failed delivery attempt. The shipment is held with the courier for reattempt or return.

RTO (Return to Origin): Decision has been made to return the shipment to the seller. RTO can be initiated by seller (from NDR) or automatically by courier after maximum attempts.

## How to reduce NDR rate on Shiprocket?

To reduce NDR rate:
1. Verify buyer contact before shipping (use Engage 360 Address Verification)
2. Ensure accurate delivery address
3. Provide correct phone number
4. Use Engage 360's pre-delivery confirmation flow
5. Enable NDR recovery via Engage 360
6. Choose reliable courier partners with high delivery rates

---

## How to dispute fake delivery on Shiprocket?

If courier marks order as delivered but buyer didn't receive:
1. Go to the shipment in Orders section
2. Click on Raise Dispute or Fake Delivery
3. Provide buyer's confirmation that they didn't receive
4. Shiprocket investigates with courier
5. OTP delivery verification is recommended to prevent such disputes

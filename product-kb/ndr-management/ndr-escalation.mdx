## How to report or raise a concern with NDR shipment?

To raise a concern about an NDR shipment:
* Go to the specific NDR order in NDR/Delivery Boost section.
* Click on "Act on Shipment".
* Select "Report an Issue" or "Raise Concern".
* Describe the specific issue.
* Submit the concern.

Alternatively, raise a support ticket from Help & Support with the AWB number and NDR details.

---

## How to escalate if courier isn't acting on my NDR reattempt request?

If the courier has not acted on your reattempt request:
1. Check the NDR status in Shiprocket panel
2. If no movement after 2 business days, raise a support ticket
3. Include the AWB number, NDR action date, and reattempt request date
4. Shiprocket will coordinate with the courier on your behalf

## What is the Contact Buyer feature in NDR?

The Contact Buyer feature allows you or Shiprocket to reach out to the buyer directly when an NDR is raised. This can be done via:
* Automated WhatsApp messages (via Engage 360)
* IVR calls
* Manual calls from the Engage panel

The buyer can then confirm delivery availability, update address, or choose RTO.

---

## How to dispute a fake delivery on Shiprocket?

If the courier marks an order as delivered but the buyer hasn't received it:
1. Go to the shipment in the Orders section
2. Click on "Raise Dispute" or "Fake Delivery"
3. Provide buyer's written confirmation that they didn't receive the order
4. Shiprocket will investigate with the courier

Note: OTP-based delivery verification helps prevent fake delivery disputes.

---

## How many delivery attempts does a courier make before RTO?

Most courier partners make 2-3 delivery attempts before initiating RTO. The exact number varies by courier partner. After the maximum attempts, the shipment is marked for RTO unless the seller requests intervention.

---

## What happens if I don't act on an NDR within the deadline?

If no action is taken within the NDR deadline:
1. The courier will automatically initiate RTO for the shipment
2. The shipment will be returned to your pickup address
3. RTO charges will apply
4. You will lose the opportunity to reattempt delivery

Always act on NDRs promptly to avoid unnecessary RTOs.

---

## How to reduce NDR rate on Shiprocket?

To reduce NDR rate:
1. Verify buyer's address and contact number before shipping
2. Use Engage 360 Address Verification feature
3. Use Engage 360's pre-delivery confirmation flow
4. Choose reliable courier partners for specific pincodes
5. Enable NDR recovery via Engage 360
6. Monitor NDR Dashboard for patterns and take corrective action

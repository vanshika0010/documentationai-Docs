---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to process an NDR in Shiprocket?">

Go to NDR Management, select the shipment, and choose an action: Request Reattempt (with updated delivery instructions or address), or Initiate RTO. Provide the reason and confirm. The courier will act on your request within the TAT.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many reattempts are allowed for an NDR shipment?">

Most couriers allow 2-3 reattempts before initiating RTO. The exact number varies by courier. After exhausting reattempts, the shipment will be returned to origin.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

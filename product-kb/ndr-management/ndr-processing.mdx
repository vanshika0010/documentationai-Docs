## How to Process an NDR?

To process an NDR on Shiprocket:
* Go to NDR/Delivery Boost from the left menu.
* You will see all NDR orders listed.
* For each NDR, you need to take an action:
   * Reattempt Delivery: Request the courier to make another delivery attempt with updated instructions.
   * Return to Origin (RTO): Request the courier to return the shipment to your pickup address.
* You can also use the Bulk Action option to process multiple NDRs at once.

---

## How to update delivery address for an NDR?

To update the delivery address for an NDR:
* Go to NDR/Delivery Boost from the left menu.
* Find the specific NDR order.
* Click on "Act on Shipment".
* Select "Update Address" option.
* Enter the correct delivery address.
* Submit the update.
The updated address will be sent to the courier for the next delivery attempt.

---

## Can I take bulk action on NDR orders?

Yes. To bulk process NDR orders:
* Go to NDR/Delivery Boost from the left menu.
* Select multiple NDR orders using the checkboxes.
* Click on "Bulk Action".
* Choose Reattempt or RTO for all selected orders.
* Confirm the action.

## What are the different NDR reasons?

Common NDR reasons include:
1. Customer Not Available (CNA)
2. Wrong/Incomplete Address
3. Customer Refused Delivery
4. Phone Unreachable
5. Out of Delivery Area
6. Office/Shop Closed
7. Customer Asked to Call Later
8. COD Amount Not Ready
9. Premises Locked
10. Delivery Rescheduled by Customer

## How does Engage 360 help with NDR management?

Engage 360's NDR Recovery (Delivery Boost) automatically:
1. Sends WhatsApp messages to buyers when delivery fails
2. Asks buyer to confirm re-delivery or update address
3. Captures buyer preference for time slot
4. Reduces RTO by proactively resolving delivery issues
5. Available as part of Engage 360 RTO Suite

---

## What is the NDR action window on Shiprocket?

The NDR action window is the time available to take action on an NDR before the courier automatically initiates RTO. This varies by courier partner (typically 2-5 days). It is important to act on NDRs promptly to avoid RTOs.

## How to Process an NDR?

To process an NDR on Shiprocket:
1. Go to NDR Management from the left menu
2. Find the NDR order
3. Review the NDR reason
4. Choose action:
   a. Request Reattempt: Provide updated instructions (contact buyer, update address)
   b. Initiate RTO: Start the return process
5. Submit your action
The action is communicated to the courier.

---

## How to update delivery address for NDR on Shiprocket?

To update delivery address for an NDR:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Find the specific NDR
3. Click on Update Address
4. Enter the correct delivery address
5. Submit - the updated address is sent to the courier for reattempt

---

## How to request reattempt for NDR on Shiprocket?

To request reattempt:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Find the NDR order
3. Click Request Reattempt
4. Select reattempt reason/instructions:
   - Buyer is available now
   - Updated contact number
   - Corrected address
   - Preferred time slot
5. Submit the reattempt request

---

## How to bulk process NDR orders on Shiprocket?

For bulk NDR processing:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Select multiple NDR orders using checkboxes
3. Click Bulk Action
4. Choose action (Reattempt/RTO) for selected orders
5. Confirm bulk action

---

## What are the NDR reasons on Shiprocket?

Common NDR reasons:
1. Customer not available
2. Address not found / incomplete address
3. Customer refused delivery
4. Phone unreachable
5. Open delivery not accepted
6. Out of delivery area
7. Office/shop closed
8. Customer asked to call
9. Shipment not meeting COD amount

## How does Engage 360 help with NDR management?

Engage 360's NDR Recovery (Delivery Boost) automatically:
1. Sends WhatsApp messages to buyers when delivery fails
2. Asks buyer to confirm re-delivery or update address
3. Captures buyer preference for time slot
4. Reduces RTO by proactively resolving delivery issues
5. Available in RTO Suite settings in Engage 360

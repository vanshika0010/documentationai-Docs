## How to process an NDR in Shiprocket

Go to NDR Management, select the shipment, and choose an action:

* **Request Reattempt** — with updated delivery instructions or address.
* **Initiate RTO** — to return the shipment to origin.

Provide the reason and confirm. The courier will act on your request within the TAT.

---

## How many reattempts are allowed for an NDR shipment

Most couriers allow 2–3 reattempts before initiating RTO. The exact number varies by courier. After exhausting reattempts, the shipment will be returned to origin.

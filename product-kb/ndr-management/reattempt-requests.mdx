## How to request a reattempt for an NDR shipment

Go to NDR Management, select the shipment with an NDR, choose 'Reattempt Delivery,' provide updated delivery instructions or corrected address if needed, and submit. The courier will be notified to attempt delivery again.

---

## Can I update the delivery address for an NDR shipment

Yes. You can update the delivery address when requesting a reattempt for NDR shipments. However, address updates are subject to courier approval and may not be allowed for significant changes.

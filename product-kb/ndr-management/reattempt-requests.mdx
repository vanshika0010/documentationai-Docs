## How to raise my NDR reattempt request?

To raise NDR reattempt request on Shiprocket:
1. Go to NDR Management from the left menu
2. Find the NDR order
3. Click on Request Reattempt
4. Provide buyer instructions:
   - Updated contact number
   - Corrected address
   - Preferred delivery time/slot
   - Availability confirmation
5. Submit the reattempt request

---

## Can I add specific instructions for NDR reattempt?

Yes. When requesting a reattempt, you can add instructions for the delivery agent:
1. Correct address (if address was wrong)
2. Updated contact number
3. Preferred delivery time
4. Specific instructions (e.g., "Call before delivery", "Deliver to neighbor")

---

## How long does it take for NDR reattempt to happen after request?

After submitting reattempt request:
- Most couriers attempt reattempt within the next 1-2 business days
- Actual time depends on the courier's operational schedule for that pincode
- High-demand periods may have longer wait times

---

## What is the maximum number of reattempts for NDR on Shiprocket?

Reattempt limits vary by courier:
- Most couriers: 2-3 attempts total (including first attempt)
- After maximum attempts, courier initiates RTO

For specific courier reattempt policies, check the courier's terms in Shiprocket panel.

---

## How to check reattempt status for NDR order on Shiprocket?

To check reattempt status:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Find the specific NDR
3. Check the NDR history/timeline
4. Status shows: Reattempt Requested, Reattempt Scheduled, Reattempt Done

---

## What happens if NDR reattempt also fails?

If reattempt also fails:
1. Another NDR is created
2. You can request another reattempt (if within courier limits) or initiate RTO
3. After maximum courier attempts, RTO is automatically initiated
4. Use Engage 360 NDR recovery to proactively resolve before another failed attempt

---

## Can I cancel an NDR reattempt request on Shiprocket?

Cancelling an NDR reattempt request depends on timing:
1. If courier hasn't yet acted on the request, contact Shiprocket support immediately
2. Raise a ticket with AWB and request to cancel the reattempt
3. If courier has already dispatched for reattempt, it may not be possible to cancel

---

## How does Shiprocket communicate NDR reattempt to the courier?

Shiprocket sends the reattempt request and instructions electronically to the courier's system. The courier's delivery team receives updated instructions for the next delivery attempt.

---

## Can I request specific time slot for NDR reattempt?

Yes. When requesting reattempt, you can specify preferred time slots (morning, afternoon, evening) if the courier supports time-slotted deliveries. This information is passed to the courier with the reattempt request.

---

## What is buyer feedback in NDR management on Shiprocket?

Buyer feedback in NDR context refers to the buyer's response when contacted about a failed delivery:
1. Confirm re-delivery availability
2. Update address
3. Provide preferred time slot
4. Choose RTO if no longer interested

Engage 360's NDR recovery flow captures buyer feedback automatically via WhatsApp.

---

## How to bulk reattempt NDR orders on Shiprocket?

For bulk reattempt:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Select multiple NDR orders
3. Click Bulk Actions >> Request Reattempt
4. Provide common instructions (or individual)
5. Submit bulk reattempt request

---

## Why is my NDR reattempt request not visible to the courier?

If courier hasn't received the reattempt request:
1. Check if the request was submitted successfully in Shiprocket panel
2. Verify the NDR status shows "Reattempt Requested"
3. If status shows submitted but no action by courier after 2 days, raise a support ticket
4. Include AWB number and reattempt submission date

---

## Can I initiate RTO instead of reattempt for an NDR?

Yes. When processing an NDR, you can choose to initiate RTO instead of requesting reattempt:
1. Go to NDR Management
2. Find the NDR order
3. Select Initiate RTO
4. Confirm the RTO initiation

RTO is irreversible once confirmed.

## How to raise my NDR reattempt request?

To raise an NDR reattempt request:
* Go to NDR/Delivery Boost from the left menu.
* Find the NDR order.
* Click "Act on Shipment".
* Select "Reattempt Delivery".
* Provide updated delivery instructions:
   * Updated contact number
   * Corrected address (if applicable)
   * Preferred delivery time/slot
   * Any special instructions
* Submit the reattempt request.

---

## Can I provide specific instructions for NDR reattempt?

Yes. When requesting a reattempt, you can provide specific instructions for the delivery agent:
* Correct or updated delivery address
* Updated or alternative contact number
* Preferred delivery time slot
* Special instructions ("Call before delivery", "Deliver to neighbor", etc.)

---

## How long does it take for a reattempt after submitting a request?

After submitting a reattempt request:
* Most couriers attempt reattempt within 1-2 business days
* Timing depends on the courier's operational schedule for that pincode
* Peak periods may have longer wait times

---

## What is the maximum number of reattempts allowed?

Reattempt limits vary by courier:
* Most couriers: 2-3 total delivery attempts (including the first attempt)
* After maximum attempts, courier initiates RTO automatically

For specific courier reattempt policies, check the courier's terms in the Shiprocket panel.

---

## How to check reattempt status for an NDR order?

To check reattempt status:
* Go to NDR/Delivery Boost section
* Find the specific NDR
* Check the shipment timeline/history
* Status will show: Reattempt Requested, Reattempt Scheduled, Reattempt Done

---

## What happens if the reattempt also fails?

If the reattempt delivery also fails:
1. A new NDR is generated
2. You can request another reattempt (if within courier limits) or initiate RTO
3. After maximum courier attempts, RTO is automatically initiated
4. Use Engage 360 NDR recovery to proactively resolve before another failed attempt

---

## Can I cancel an NDR reattempt request?

If you need to cancel a reattempt request:
1. Contact Shiprocket support immediately with the AWB number
2. Raise a ticket requesting cancellation of the reattempt
3. If the courier has already dispatched for reattempt, it may not be possible to cancel

---

## How does Shiprocket communicate reattempt instructions to the courier?

Shiprocket sends reattempt requests and updated instructions electronically to the courier's system. The courier's delivery team receives the updated instructions for the next delivery attempt.

---

## Can I request a specific time slot for NDR reattempt?

Yes. When requesting a reattempt, you can specify preferred delivery time slots if the courier supports time-slotted deliveries. This information is passed to the courier with the reattempt request.

---

## How to bulk reattempt NDR orders?

For bulk reattempt:
1. Go to NDR/Delivery Boost section
2. Select multiple NDR orders using checkboxes
3. Click Bulk Actions >> Reattempt Delivery
4. Provide instructions (applies to all selected orders)
5. Submit

---

## What is buyer feedback in NDR management?

Buyer feedback refers to the buyer's response when contacted about a failed delivery. Options include:
1. Confirm re-delivery availability
2. Update delivery address
3. Provide preferred time slot
4. Choose RTO if no longer interested

Engage 360's NDR recovery flow captures buyer feedback automatically via WhatsApp.

---

## Can I initiate RTO instead of reattempt for an NDR?

Yes. When processing an NDR:
1. Go to NDR/Delivery Boost
2. Find the NDR order
3. Click "Act on Shipment"
4. Select "Return to Origin (RTO)"
5. Confirm

Note: Once RTO is confirmed, it cannot be reversed.

---

## How to track the complete history of an NDR order?

To view NDR order history:
1. Find the order in the Orders section
2. Click on the AWB number or order details
3. View the full tracking timeline including all NDR events, reattempt requests, and courier updates

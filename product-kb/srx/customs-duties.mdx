## Who pays customs duties on international shipments

This depends on your shipping terms:

* **DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid)** — the buyer pays customs duties at delivery.
* **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** — you pay the duties upfront.

Clearly state your terms in your store to avoid confusion at delivery.

---

## What is an HS code and why is it required

HS (Harmonized System) codes are internationally standardized numerical codes that classify products for customs. They determine the applicable duty rate and export/import regulations. An accurate HS code is mandatory for all international shipments.

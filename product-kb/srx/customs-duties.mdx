## What is EORI?

EORI stands for "Economic Operators Registration and Identification". It's a unique identification number used by businesses and individuals for customs processes within the European Union (EU). An EORI number is required for:
1. Businesses importing/exporting to/from EU countries
2. Filing customs declarations in EU
3. Communicating with EU customs authorities

For Indian sellers shipping to EU, the European buyer typically needs an EORI for commercial imports.

## What is IOSS?

IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop) is an EU VAT simplification scheme for e-commerce sellers. For orders under €150 shipped to EU countries:
1. Seller registers for IOSS and charges VAT to buyer at checkout
2. IOSS number is declared on shipment for customs clearance
3. Enables faster customs clearance without additional duty at destination

Shiprocket supports IOSS number entry for EU shipments.

## What is DDP vs DDU for international shipping?

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): Seller is responsible for all duties and taxes. Buyer receives package without paying anything at customs. Smoother buyer experience.

DDU/DAP (Delivered Duty Unpaid/At Place): Buyer pays import duties and taxes at destination customs. Less predictable for buyer.

Most small parcel international shipping is DDU unless specifically configured.

## How are import duties calculated for international shipments?

Import duties are calculated based on:
1. HS code of the product
2. Declared value (CIF - Cost, Insurance, Freight)
3. Destination country's duty rates
4. Any applicable trade agreements

Duty rates vary significantly by country and product category.

---

## What is HS Code and why is it important for international shipping?

HS (Harmonized System) code is a standardized numerical classification for goods used in international trade. It determines:
1. Applicable import duty rates
2. Whether the product requires permits or is restricted
3. Country of origin eligibility for trade agreements

Always enter the correct 6-8 digit HS code for accurate customs processing.

---

## How to find the correct HS code for my product?

To find the HS code:
1. Use India's DGFT HS code lookup tool
2. Check the WCO (World Customs Organization) HS database
3. Consult a customs broker for complex products
4. Search your product on trade databases

Using the wrong HS code can result in customs delays or penalties.

## What happens if I undervalue my international shipment?

Underdeclaring the value of international shipments:
1. Is a customs violation subject to penalties
2. Can result in shipment seizure
3. May invalidate insurance coverage
4. Can lead to account suspension on Shiprocket

Always declare the accurate market value of goods.

## What are customs duties for electronics shipped internationally?

Customs duties for electronics vary by destination:
* USA: Most electronics have 0% import duty under trade agreements
* EU: Varies by product, typically 0-5%
* Middle East: 5% customs duty on most goods
* India (imports): 10-20% on various electronics

Consult a customs broker for specific rates to your destination.

## How to avoid customs delays for international shipments?

To minimize customs delays:
1. Use accurate HS codes
2. Declare correct product value
3. Provide complete commercial invoice
4. Include packing list
5. Avoid prohibited/restricted items
6. Ensure recipient has import clearance capability
7. Include IOSS number for EU orders under €150

## What is the de minimis threshold for different countries?

De minimis is the value below which no import duty is charged:
* USA: $800 USD
* EU: €150 (for IOSS) / €0 (duty-free threshold removed for commercial goods)
* UK: £135
* Australia: AUD $1000
* UAE: AED 300

Shipments below de minimis typically clear customs faster.

## Are there penalties for on-hold international shipments?

Yes. A penalty of ₹1,180 per shipment applies for holds due to:
* Dangerous goods (undeclared lithium batteries, chemicals)
* Incorrect documentation
* Prohibited items

Ensure accurate declarations to avoid penalties.

## What is the difference between customs duty and VAT/GST for international shipments?

Customs Duty: Tax on imported goods based on HS code and value. Goes to destination government.

VAT/GST: Value Added Tax applied in addition to customs duty in many countries (UK, EU, Australia). Often charged on total value including customs duty and shipping.

Both may apply to your international shipments depending on destination country.

## What is a customs hold and how long can it last?

A customs hold occurs when customs authorities stop a shipment for inspection or documentation verification.

Duration varies:
* Random inspection: 2-5 days
* Documentation issue: Until resolved (days to weeks)
* Value dispute: Until additional duty is paid or value verified

Respond quickly to customs queries to minimize hold duration.

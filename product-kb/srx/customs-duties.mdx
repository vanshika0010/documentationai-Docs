<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who pays customs duties on international shipments?">

This depends on your shipping terms. With DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid), the buyer pays customs duties. With DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), you pay the duties upfront. Clearly state your terms in your store to avoid confusion at delivery.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an HS code and why is it required?">

HS (Harmonized System) code is an internationally standardized numerical code that classifies products for customs. It determines the applicable duty rate and export/import regulations. An accurate HS code is mandatory for all international shipments.

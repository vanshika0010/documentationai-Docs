## Are there penalties for on-hold international shipments?

Yes. A penalty of ₹1,180 per shipment applies for holds due to:
* Dangerous goods (undeclared lithium batteries, chemicals, flammable materials)
* Misdeclared/underdeclared items
* Prohibited items found during screening

Always declare all items accurately and check prohibited items list before booking international shipments.

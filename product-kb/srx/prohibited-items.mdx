## What items are prohibited for international shipping

Prohibited items vary by country but commonly include: currency and negotiable instruments, weapons and ammunition, perishables without special packaging, hazardous materials, counterfeit goods, and items banned under the destination country's import laws. Always check destination-specific restrictions before shipping.

## Can I connect my walmart channel to shiprocket?

Yes, you can connect your Walmart store on shiprocket and effortlessly manage your Walmart orders and ship them across India or internationally.

---

## How to connect international sales channels to Shiprocket?

To connect international channels (Shopify, Etsy, eBay, Amazon Global, etc.):
1. Go to Settings >> Channels in your Shiprocket panel
2. Find the international marketplace or platform
3. Click Connect/Add Channel
4. Authenticate with your channel credentials
5. Configure sync settings
6. Orders from international buyers will sync to Shiprocket for fulfillment

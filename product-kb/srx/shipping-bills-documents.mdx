<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What documents are required for international shipping?">

Required documents: Commercial Invoice (with product description, value, HS code, buyer/seller details), Packing List, and KYC documents. Some countries may require additional certificates (Certificate of Origin, MSDS for hazardous items, etc.).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a shipping bill for international exports?">

A Shipping Bill is the primary export document filed with customs for international shipments. It contains details about the exporter, consignee, product, value, and HS code. It is required for customs clearance and export benefits.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

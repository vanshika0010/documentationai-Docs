## What documents are required for international shipping

Required documents:

* Commercial Invoice (product description, value, HS code, buyer/seller details)
* Packing List
* KYC documents

Some countries may require additional certificates (Certificate of Origin, MSDS for hazardous items, etc.).

---

## What is a shipping bill for international exports

A Shipping Bill is the primary export document filed with customs for international shipments. It contains details about the exporter, consignee, product, value, and HS code. It is required for customs clearance and export benefits like RODTEP.

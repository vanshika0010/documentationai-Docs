## What is the process to get the international shipping Bills?

To get shipping bills for international shipments at Shiprocket, follow these steps:

Step 1: Go to the "Finances" > "Freight & VAS Charges" section from left menu.
Step 2: Select Region Type as International from the filter. 
Step 3: Search Your AWB Number and find your shipping bill easily.

Or visit https://app.shiprocket.in/seller/finances/freight-vas-charges to directly access.

## What documents are required for international shipping via Shiprocket?

Documents required for international shipping:
1. Commercial Invoice (describing goods, value, HS code)
2. Packing List
3. Shipper's Letter of Instruction (SLI)
4. Airway Bill / Waybill
5. Certificate of Origin (if required by destination)
6. Export Declaration (required for high-value shipments)
7. Customs declaration form

Specific requirements vary by destination country and courier.

---

## What is a Commercial Invoice for international shipping?

A Commercial Invoice for international shipments includes:
1. Seller and buyer details
2. Detailed description of goods
3. HS (Harmonized System) code for each item
4. Value of each item in USD/INR
5. Country of origin
6. Reason for export (sale, gift, sample, etc.)
7. Seller's signature

This document is used by customs for duty calculation.

## How to generate shipping documents on Shiprocket for international orders?

Shiprocket automatically generates required shipping documents when you create an international order:
1. Create the international shipment in Shiprocket
2. Fill in all product details including HS code and value
3. Documents (AWB, invoice, packing list) are generated automatically
4. Download from the shipment details page

## What is HS code for international shipping?

HS (Harmonized System) code is an international standardized numerical method to classify traded products. It is mandatory for customs clearance.

For Shiprocket international shipments, enter the 6-8 digit HS code for each product. You can find the HS code for your product using the HS code lookup tool or by consulting your customs broker.

---

## What is the Shipper's Export Declaration (SED)?

A Shipper's Export Declaration is a document required by customs for exports above a certain value threshold. It declares the details of the exported goods. Shiprocket helps generate the required export documentation as part of the international shipment process.

## What is KYC for international shipping on Shiprocket?

KYC for international shipping requires:
1. Business/individual PAN
2. IEC (Import Export Code) - mandatory for commercial exports
3. GST certificate
4. Bank account details
5. Business registration documents

IEC is issued by the DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) and is required for all commercial exports.

---

## What is IEC code and why is it needed for international shipping?

IEC (Import Export Code) is a 10-digit code issued by India's DGFT, mandatory for all commercial exports and imports. It is required to:
1. Clear customs for international shipments
2. Receive foreign currency payments
3. Claim export benefits/incentives

Apply for IEC at https://dgft.gov.in. It typically takes 1-2 business days to get the IEC.

## What is an AWB for international shipping?

AWB (Air Waybill) is the tracking document for international air freight. It serves as:
1. Receipt of goods by the carrier
2. Contract of carriage
3. Customs declaration
4. Tracking reference

The AWB number is used to track international shipments through the courier's system.

## What is the packing list for international shipments?

A packing list details the contents of each package in the shipment:
1. Description of each item
2. Quantity per item
3. Weight of each package
4. Dimensions of each package
5. Total packages in the shipment

It accompanies the commercial invoice for customs clearance.

## What is Freight on Value (FOV) for international shipping?

Freight on Value is an additional charge levied as a percentage of the declared value of the shipment. It covers the liability taken on by the carrier. Some international couriers charge FOV on high-value shipments.

---

## What is IOSS for European shipments?

IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop) is an EU VAT scheme for e-commerce sellers. For shipments under €150 to EU countries:
1. Sellers register for IOSS and collect VAT at point of sale
2. The IOSS number is included on the shipment documentation
3. This enables faster customs clearance without additional VAT collection at destination

Shiprocket supports IOSS number inclusion for EU shipments.

---

## What is GST on international shipments from India?

Exports from India are zero-rated under GST (0% GST on export goods and services). However, IGST may be applicable on shipping services. Exporters can claim refund of IGST paid on inputs used for exported goods through the GST refund process.

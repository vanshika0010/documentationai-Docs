## I want to send my shipment outside India. How can I calculate the shipping cost?

Follow the below mentioned steps on Shiprocket website to easily calculate international shipping rates:
1. Go to Tools >> Rate Calculator
2. Select International shipping
3. Enter source pincode (India)
4. Select destination country
5. Enter package weight (kg) and dimensions (L × W × H cm)
6. View rates for all available international couriers

---

## What is the pricing structure for international shipping on Shiprocket?

International shipping rates include:
1. Base freight rate (per kg, based on chargeable weight)
2. Fuel surcharge (varies by carrier, typically % of base)
3. Security surcharge
4. Remote area surcharge (if applicable)
5. Additional handling charges (hazardous material, oversize, etc.)

All-inclusive rates are shown in the Rate Calculator.

---

## What is the minimum chargeable weight for international shipping?

Minimum chargeable weight for international shipments is typically 0.5 kg. If your package is lighter, you'll still be billed for 0.5 kg.

---

## How is volumetric weight calculated for international shipping?

Volumetric weight = (Length cm × Width cm × Height cm) / 5000

For example: 30 × 20 × 10 cm = 6000/5000 = 1.2 kg volumetric weight

Chargeable weight = Max(actual weight, volumetric weight)

---

## Are there any additional surcharges for international shipping?

Additional surcharges that may apply:
1. Fuel surcharge (variable, typically 15-25%)
2. Security surcharge
3. Remote delivery area surcharge
4. Oversize/overweight surcharge
5. Dangerous goods handling fee
6. Address correction surcharge
7. Residential delivery surcharge (USA/EU)

---

## How to get discounted international shipping rates on Shiprocket?

For discounted international rates:
1. Higher volume typically leads to better rates
2. Contact your Shiprocket account manager for volume-based negotiations
3. Shiprocket regularly runs promotional rates for international shipping
4. Enterprise accounts may get customized rate cards

---

## What is the currency for international shipping charges on Shiprocket?

International shipping charges are billed in Indian Rupees (INR) through your Shiprocket wallet. The rates incorporate currency conversion where applicable.

---

## Can I get a fixed monthly rate for international shipping on Shiprocket?

For high-volume international shippers, Shiprocket offers fixed rate cards. Contact your account manager to negotiate a monthly rate card based on your expected volumes and destinations.

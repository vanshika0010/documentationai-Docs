## What is the typical delivery time for international shipments

Delivery times vary by destination:

* **Express services** — 3–7 business days
* **Economy services** — 7–15 business days
* **Economy Plus** — 15–30 business days

Exact TAT depends on destination country, courier, and customs clearance time.

---

## Why is my international shipment delayed

Common reasons for international delays: customs clearance hold, incorrect documentation, prohibited items flagged, customs duties unpaid by recipient, remote area surcharges, or transit country delays. Check tracking for the specific reason.

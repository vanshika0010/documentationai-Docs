## What is the delivery TAT for different international courier partners on Shiprocket?

The delivery TAT (Turnaround Time) refers to the amount of time it takes for a package to be delivered from the date of shipment. Here are the approximate delivery TATs for different international courier partners on Shiprocket:

* Aramex: 5-10 business days
* DHL Express: 3-7 business days
* FedEx: 3-7 business days
* UPS: 5-10 business days
* Delhi Post (EMS): 7-14 business days

Note: These are approximate TATs and can vary based on the destination country, customs clearance, and other factors.

---

## What factors affect international delivery time?

Factors affecting international delivery time:
1. Destination country (developed vs developing)
2. Courier service selected (express vs economy)
3. Customs clearance delays
4. Public holidays in origin and destination countries
5. Weather or operational disruptions
6. Incorrect/incomplete documentation causing customs holds
7. Remote delivery locations

## How to track international shipments on Shiprocket?

To track international shipments:
1. Go to International Shipments section
2. Find the shipment by AWB or order ID
3. View real-time tracking status
4. Use the tracking link shared with buyer
5. Track directly on the courier's international website using the AWB

## What is transit time for international economy shipping?

International economy (non-express) services have longer transit times:
* USA/UK/Europe: 10-15 business days
* Middle East: 7-12 business days
* Southeast Asia: 7-12 business days
* Australia: 10-15 business days

Actual times vary by carrier and destination.

---

## How to calculate expected delivery date for international orders?

Expected delivery date = Pickup date + Transit time + Customs clearance time

For example: If shipped on Monday with a 5-7 day courier, EDD is approximately the following Monday-Wednesday (excluding weekends/holidays in both countries).

## What happens if international delivery is delayed?

For international delivery delays:
1. Check tracking for last update
2. If stuck at customs: Contact the recipient for customs duty payment or documentation
3. If carrier delay: Raise a query with Shiprocket support providing AWB number
4. Shiprocket coordinates with carrier for investigation

---

## What is the SLA for international shipments stuck at customs?

Customs hold duration varies widely by country and reason:
* Routine inspection: 2-5 days
* Documentation issue: Can extend to weeks if not resolved
* Prohibited items: Shipment may be seized

Ensure accurate documentation to minimize customs holds.

Yes. Shiprocket provides real-time tracking for all international shipments with status updates at key milestones (picked up, in transit, customs, out for delivery, delivered). Tracking is available in the panel and via tracking links.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the typical delivery time for international shipments?">

Delivery times vary by destination: Express services (3-7 business days), Economy services (7-15 business days), and Economy Plus (15-30 business days). Exact TAT depends on the destination country, courier, and customs clearance time.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my international shipment delayed?">

Common reasons for international delays: customs clearance hold, incorrect documentation, prohibited items flagged, customs duties unpaid by recipient, remote area surcharges, or transit country delays. Check the tracking for the specific reason.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What are all the courier options for international shipping on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket has its own services that cater to all international shipping needs:
1. Aramex - Good for Middle East, South Asia
2. DHL Express - Premium express for worldwide delivery
3. FedEx International - Express delivery worldwide
4. UPS - Reliable for USA, Europe
5. Delhi Post (EMS) - Economy option for worldwide
6. CargoX - Shiprocket's own international service

---

## How to choose the right international courier on Shiprocket?

Choose international courier based on:
1. Destination country (some carriers are better for specific regions)
2. Delivery speed required (express vs economy)
3. Package weight and dimensions
4. Shipment value (insurance requirements)
5. Budget
6. Tracking capabilities required

Use the Rate Calculator to compare available options.

---

## Which international courier is best for USA shipments?

For USA shipments from India via Shiprocket:
* FedEx International Economy or Priority
* DHL Express
* UPS Worldwide

Best choice depends on weight, speed required, and budget. Use the Rate Calculator for current pricing.

## Which courier is best for Middle East international shipping on Shiprocket?

For Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, etc.):
* Aramex - Strong network in Middle East
* DHL Express
* FedEx

Aramex typically has competitive rates and strong coverage for Gulf countries.

---

## What is the difference between DHL Express and DHL eCommerce for international shipping?

DHL Express: Premium express service, 2-5 day delivery, higher cost, full tracking.

DHL eCommerce: Economy e-commerce service, longer transit, lower cost, suitable for low-value small packets. May not be available on all Shiprocket accounts.

---

## How does Shiprocket select the courier for international orders?

Shiprocket presents all available couriers for the destination with rates, TAT, and features. You select the courier based on your preference. You can also set default courier rules for international orders.

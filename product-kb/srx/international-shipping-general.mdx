## What is the minimum weight for international shipments on Shiprocket?

Minimum weight for international shipments is typically 0.5 kg. However, chargeable weight may be higher based on volumetric calculation.

---

## What is volumetric weight for international shipments?

Volumetric weight formula for international = (L cm × W cm × H cm) / 5000 (kg)

The chargeable weight is the higher of actual weight vs volumetric weight.

## How does customs clearance work for international shipments?

Customs clearance process:
1. Shipment arrives at destination country customs
2. Customs inspects documentation (commercial invoice, packing list, HS codes)
3. Duties and taxes are assessed based on declared value and HS code
4. If duties apply, buyer is notified to pay before release
5. After payment/clearance, shipment proceeds to delivery

---

## Who pays customs duties for international shipments?

Customs duties responsibility:
* DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): Seller pays duties - transparent for buyer
* DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid): Buyer pays duties at destination

Most Shiprocket international shipments use DDU unless specifically configured for DDP.

## What is the EORI number for European shipments?

EORI (Economic Operators Registration and Identification) number is required for businesses importing/exporting to/from the EU. For Indian sellers shipping to EU countries, the European buyer typically needs an EORI number for commercial shipments.

International returns are complex due to customs requirements. Options:

2. Arrange international reverse logistics
3. Dispose at destination

Contact Shiprocket's international team for specific return arrangements.

## What is the GST refund process for exports on Shiprocket?

For exports from India:
1. Exports are zero-rated under GST
2. IGST paid on inputs can be claimed as refund
3. File LUT (Letter of Undertaking) or export under bond
4. Claim refund through GST portal (RFD-01 form)
5. Shiprocket provides the required export documentation for GST refund claims

## What is the process for customs hold release for international shipments?

For customs holds:
1. Check the tracking for hold reason
2. For duty payment holds: Buyer pays applicable duties
3. For documentation issues: Shiprocket coordinates with carrier for document submission
4. For prohibited item holds: The shipment may be seized or returned
5. Contact Shiprocket support for assistance with customs hold resolution

## How to get a refund for failed international delivery on Shiprocket?

For failed international delivery refunds:
1. Raise a claim with Shiprocket support
2. Provide AWB number and delivery failure evidence
3. If shipment is insured, file insurance claim
4. Shiprocket coordinates with carrier for investigation
5. Refund/compensation processed after carrier investigation completes

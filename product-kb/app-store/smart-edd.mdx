## What is the Smart EDD app

Shiprocket Smart EDD displays precise, real-time delivery dates using pin code validation, shipping rules, and multi-warehouse intelligence. Key features include:

* Validate pincodes and show accurate delivery dates in real time
* Seamlessly sync inventory with OMS platforms for precise EDDs
* Personalize the EDD widget for a consistent brand experience
* Highlight discounts and banners directly inside the widget to drive sales
* Set delivery rules by pincode and SKU for a tailored delivery experience

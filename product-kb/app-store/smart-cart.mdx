## What is the Smart Cart app?

Shiprocket Smart Cart helps you personalize your slide cart to match your brand and boost Average Order Value (AOV) with upsells, discounts, and free gifts. Key features include:

* Dynamic progress bars that help customers add more items to the cart
* Milestone-based free gifts and smart discount options
* Upsell best-sellers and frequently bought together items in cart
* Personalized cart drawer design to match your brand theme
* Highlight promotions, shipping thresholds, and seasonal deals in real-time

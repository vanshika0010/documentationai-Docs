## How can I contact an app developer for support?

Contact information for app developers is usually available on the app's listing page in the Shiprocket App Store.

---

## Can I request custom features for an app?

Some app developers offer custom development services to add features or tailor the app to your specific needs. Contact the developer directly to inquire about custom development options.

---

## Can I develop my own app for my Shiprocket store?

Yes, if you have development skills, you can create your own custom app using the Shiprocket API and development tools. Shiprocket provides comprehensive documentation and resources for app development.

---

## How do I submit an app to the Shiprocket App Store?

To submit an app, you need to follow the submission guidelines provided by Shiprocket. This includes developing your app, testing it thoroughly, and submitting it for review through the Shiprocket Developer Dashboard.

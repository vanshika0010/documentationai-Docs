## How do I know if an app is available on my account?

Most apps are designed to be compatible with a wide range of sellers. Check the app's listing — if it mentions "App is not available on your account" then you might not fulfil the requirement. Contact the app developer or your KAM for specific concerns regarding activation.

---

## Is there a limit to the number of apps I can install?

While there is no strict limit, be mindful of the performance and functionality of your Shiprocket account when installing multiple apps. Only install apps that are essential for your business operations.

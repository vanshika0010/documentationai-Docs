## Are the apps in the Shiprocket App Store free?

The Shiprocket App Store offers both free and paid apps. Some apps are free to use, while others offer a free trial period before requiring a subscription or one-time payment.

---

## How are app charges billed?

App charges are billed through your Shiprocket wallet account. Depending on the app, charges can be one-time, monthly, or usage-based. These charges will appear on your Shiprocket invoice and Passbook.

---

## Can I try apps before purchasing?

Many paid apps offer a free trial period, allowing you to test the app's features before committing to a purchase. Check the app's listing in the Shiprocket App Store for trial details.

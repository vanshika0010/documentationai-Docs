## What is Shiprocket AppStore

Shiprocket AppStore is a dynamic platform designed to enhance your e-commerce experience. It offers a variety of apps to streamline your operations and boost business efficiency, including diverse apps, seamless integration, enhanced functionality, and a user-friendly interface.

---

## How do I access the Shiprocket App Store

You can access the Shiprocket App Store directly from your Shiprocket admin panel by clicking on "Apps" in the sidebar and then visiting Shiprocket App Store.

---

## How can I find the right app for my needs

Use the search bar and categories in the Shiprocket App Store to find apps that suit your needs. You can also read user reviews, check ratings, and review app descriptions and features.

---

## Are the apps in the Shiprocket App Store secure

Shiprocket reviews and approves all apps listed in the App Store to ensure they meet security and functionality standards. It's always good practice to read reviews, check the app's privacy policy, and understand the permissions required before installing.

---

## What are the requirements for an app to be approved

Apps must meet Shiprocket's quality, security, and performance standards. This includes providing clear documentation, offering reliable functionality, and adhering to best practices for user experience and security.

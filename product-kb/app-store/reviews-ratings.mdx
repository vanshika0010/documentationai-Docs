<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I leave a review for an app?">

After using an app, you can leave a review by going to the app's listing in the Shiprocket App Store and clicking the "Write a review" button. Share your experience to help other merchants make informed decisions.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I trust the reviews and ratings in the Shiprocket App Store?">

While reviews and ratings provide valuable insights, it's important to read multiple reviews and consider both positive and negative feedback. Look for reviews from merchants with similar business needs to your own.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How can I leave a review for an app

After using an app, go to the app's listing in the Shiprocket App Store and click the "Write a review" button. Share your experience to help other merchants make informed decisions.

---

## Can I trust the reviews and ratings in the Shiprocket App Store

While reviews and ratings provide valuable insights, it's important to read multiple reviews and consider both positive and negative feedback. Look for reviews from merchants with similar business needs to your own.

## How do I install an app from the Shiprocket App Store?

Browse or search for the app you need, click on it to view its details, and then click the "Add app" button. Follow the prompts to install and configure the app for your platform.

---

## How do I update my installed apps?

Most apps update automatically to ensure you have the latest features and security patches. If an app requires manual updates, you'll typically receive a notification from the app developer with instructions.

---

## What should I do if an app update causes issues with my panel?

If an app update causes issues, contact the app developer immediately for support. You can also temporarily disable the app if it's causing significant problems until the issue is resolved.

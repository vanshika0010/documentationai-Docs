## What are the charges for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick charges are based on:
1. Distance (pickup to delivery)
2. Vehicle type (2-wheeler is cheapest, 4-wheeler is most expensive)
3. Weight/size of the consignment
4. Additional services (COD handling, insurance)

Contact the Quick team or use the in-panel rate calculator for current pricing.

---

## Is there a minimum charge for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick has a minimum charge per delivery that covers the base distance. The exact minimum charge depends on your city and the vehicle type. Check the Quick rate calculator for current minimum charges.

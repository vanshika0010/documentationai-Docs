## Does Shiprocket offer insurance for shipments

Yes, Shiprocket provides insurance for shipments. If your item is lost or damaged and costs more than the freight charge, you can opt for our Shiprocket Secure plan. The insurance coverage is up to the declared value of the shipment. Shiprocket Secure provides compensation in case of loss or damage during transit.

---

## What are the charges for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick charges are based on:
1. Distance between pickup and delivery location
2. Vehicle type selected (2-wheeler, 3-wheeler, or 4-wheeler)
3. Weight and dimensions of the consignment
4. Any add-on services (COD, insurance)

Use the Quick rate calculator in your Shiprocket panel for current pricing specific to your city and route.

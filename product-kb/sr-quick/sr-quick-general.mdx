## Does Shiprocket Quick support cash on delivery (COD) orders?

Yes, Shiprocket Quick offers a "Pay on Delivery" option, allowing you to pay for your orders at the time of delivery. This feature provides flexibility for customers who prefer to pay upon receiving their order.

---

## How does Shiprocket Quick work?

Shiprocket Quick enables hyperlocal same-day deliveries:
1. Create a delivery order with pickup and drop address in the Quick panel
2. System assigns a nearby delivery agent
3. Agent picks up the package from your location
4. Real-time GPS tracking throughout delivery
5. Delivery completed with proof (photo/OTP)

---

## What is the difference between Shiprocket Quick and Shiprocket Express?

Shiprocket Quick:
* Hyperlocal — intra-city only
* Same-day or instant delivery
* 2/3/4-wheeler vehicle options
* Shorter distances within city

Shiprocket Express:
* Pan-India shipping
* 1-7 day delivery
* Standard courier network
* All distances and delivery zones

---

## How is pricing calculated for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick pricing is based on:
1. Distance between pickup and delivery point
2. Vehicle type selected (2-wheeler is most economical, 4-wheeler is highest)
3. Weight/size of the consignment
4. Any additional services (COD handling, insurance)

Use the Quick rate calculator in the panel for exact pricing.

---

## How to enable Shiprocket Quick for my account?

To enable Shiprocket Quick:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
2. Or raise a request from the Quick section in your panel
3. The team will verify serviceability for your area and enable the feature

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for pharmacy or medical deliveries?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick can support pharmacy and medical deliveries requiring urgent same-day fulfillment. Contact the Quick team for pharmaceutical delivery-specific requirements and compliance.

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick support proof of delivery?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick provides proof of delivery including:
1. Delivery photo
2. Digital signature (if configured)
3. OTP-based delivery confirmation

---

## Can I integrate Shiprocket Quick with my website or app?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick has API endpoints that allow integration with your website, mobile app, or e-commerce platform for automated hyperlocal delivery booking. Contact Shiprocket's developer team for API documentation.

---

## What is the maximum delivery distance for Shiprocket Quick?

Maximum delivery distance varies by city and partner:
* Standard coverage: 10-25 km from pickup point
* Extended coverage: Up to 30+ km in select cities

Check serviceability in the Quick section for your specific pickup and delivery locations.

---

## How to handle damaged goods with Shiprocket Quick?

For damaged goods delivered via Quick:
1. Document damage with photos immediately at delivery
2. Contact Shiprocket Quick support promptly
3. Raise a claim with AWB/tracking number and damage evidence
4. Shiprocket will investigate with the delivery partner

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick operate on weekends and holidays?

Shiprocket Quick typically operates 7 days a week including weekends. Holiday availability depends on the delivery partner. Check the Quick panel for current operational hours in your city.

---

## How to add a new pickup address for Quick deliveries?

New pickup addresses for Quick can be added in:
1. Settings >> Manage Warehouses (add new warehouse/pickup address)
2. Or directly when creating a Quick delivery order by entering a new pickup location

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Quick?">

Shiprocket Quick is a hyperlocal and same-day delivery solution that enables businesses to deliver orders within hours using 2-wheeler, 3-wheeler, or 4-wheeler vehicles. It's ideal for local businesses, food delivery, grocery, and urgent deliveries.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to create a Quick delivery order?">

Go to Quick from the left menu in your Shiprocket panel. Enter pickup address, delivery address, package details, and select vehicle type. Confirm the booking and a nearby delivery partner will be assigned.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

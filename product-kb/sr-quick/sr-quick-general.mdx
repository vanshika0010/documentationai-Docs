## How does Shiprocket Quick work?

Shiprocket Quick enables hyperlocal same-day deliveries:
1. Create a delivery order with pickup and drop address
2. System assigns a nearby delivery agent
3. Agent picks up the package
4. Real-time tracking during delivery
5. Delivery completed with proof (photo/OTP)

---

## What is the difference between Shiprocket Quick and Express?

Shiprocket Quick:
* Hyperlocal (intra-city)
* Same-day or instant delivery
* 2/3/4-wheeler options
* Shorter distances

Shiprocket Express:
* Pan-India shipping
* 1-7 day delivery
* Standard courier network
* All distances and zones

---

## How is pricing calculated for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick pricing is based on:
1. Distance between pickup and delivery
2. Vehicle type selected (2/3/4-wheeler)
3. Any additional services (COD, insurance)

Use the Quick rate calculator for exact pricing.

---

## How to enable Shiprocket Quick for my account?

To enable Shiprocket Quick:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
2. Or raise a request from the Quick section in your panel
3. The team will evaluate serviceability for your area and enable it

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for pharmacy/medical deliveries?

Shiprocket Quick can support pharmacy and medical deliveries that require urgent same-day fulfillment. Contact the Quick team for pharmaceutical delivery specific requirements.

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick support proof of delivery?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick provides proof of delivery including:
1. Delivery photo
2. Digital signature (if configured)
3. OTP-based delivery confirmation

---

## Can I integrate Shiprocket Quick with my website/app?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick has API endpoints that allow integration with your website, mobile app, or e-commerce platform for automated hyperlocal delivery booking.

---

## What is the maximum delivery distance for Shiprocket Quick?

Maximum delivery distance varies by city and partner:
* Standard coverage: 10-25 km from pickup
* Extended coverage: Up to 30+ km in some cities

Check serviceability in the Quick section for your specific locations.

---

## How to handle damaged goods with Shiprocket Quick?

For damaged goods delivered via Quick:
1. Document damage with photos immediately
2. Contact Shiprocket Quick support
3. Raise a claim with the AWB/tracking number
4. Shiprocket will investigate with the delivery partner

---

## Can I schedule recurring Quick deliveries?

For recurring/scheduled Quick deliveries, contact the Shiprocket Quick team for bulk delivery arrangements and scheduling options.

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick operate on weekends and holidays?

Shiprocket Quick typically operates 7 days a week including weekends. Holiday availability depends on the delivery partner. Check the Quick panel for current operational hours in your area.

---

## How to add a new pickup address for Quick deliveries?

New pickup addresses for Quick can be added in:
1. Settings >> Manage Warehouses (add new warehouse/pickup address)
2. Or directly when creating a Quick delivery order

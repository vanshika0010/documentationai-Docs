## What is Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick is a hyperlocal and same-day delivery service that enables fast deliveries within cities. It supports 2-wheeler, 3-wheeler, and 4-wheeler delivery options for different package sizes and weights.

---

## What vehicle types are available on Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick offers:
1. 2-Wheeler (Bike): For small packages, documents, food orders
2. 3-Wheeler (Auto/Tuk-tuk): For medium-sized packages
3. 4-Wheeler (Car/Van): For larger or heavier packages

Vehicle availability depends on the delivery location and order size.

---

## Which courier partners are available on Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick works with hyperlocal courier partners that specialize in same-day and instant delivery. Available partners depend on your city and serviceability area. Check the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel for current partners.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for food delivery?

Shiprocket Quick can be used for food and restaurant deliveries that require same-day or instant delivery. Contact the Quick team for food delivery specific configurations.

---

## What is the maximum weight for Shiprocket Quick deliveries?

Weight limits vary by vehicle type:
* 2-Wheeler: Up to 20 kg
* 3-Wheeler: Up to 100 kg
* 4-Wheeler: Up to 200+ kg

Contact the Quick team for specific weight limits for your use case.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for B2B deliveries?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick can handle B2B hyperlocal deliveries such as office supplies, retail restocking, and commercial deliveries within cities.

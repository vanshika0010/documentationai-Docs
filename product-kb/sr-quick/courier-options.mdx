<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What couriers are available on Shiprocket Quick?">

Shiprocket Quick partners with hyperlocal delivery providers like Shadowfax, Dunzo, Borzo (formerly WeFast), and others for same-day and instant delivery. Available couriers depend on your city and serviceability.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What vehicle types are available for Quick deliveries?">

Shiprocket Quick supports 2-wheeler (bike), 3-wheeler (auto/tempo), and 4-wheeler (car/van) vehicles depending on package size and weight. Select the appropriate vehicle when creating a quick delivery order.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What courier partners can I choose from for my local deliveries?

You can select from a range of reliable courier partners including Ola, Rapido, Flash, LoadShare Network, and more. The available options depend on your city and the serviceability at the pickup and delivery pincodes.

---

## What vehicle types are available on Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick supports multiple vehicle types for hyperlocal delivery:
1. 2-Wheeler (Bike): For small packages, documents, food
2. 3-Wheeler (Auto/Tempo): For medium-sized packages
3. 4-Wheeler (Car/Van): For larger or heavier items

Vehicle availability depends on delivery location and order size/weight.

---

## Which courier partners are available on Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick works with hyperlocal delivery partners including Ola, Rapido, Flash, and LoadShare Network among others. Available partners vary by city. Check the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel for current partner availability in your area.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for food delivery?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick supports food and restaurant deliveries that require same-day or instant fulfillment. Contact the Quick team for food delivery specific configurations and requirements.

---

## What is the maximum weight for Shiprocket Quick deliveries?

Weight limits vary by vehicle type:
* 2-Wheeler: Typically up to 20 kg
* 3-Wheeler: Up to 100 kg
* 4-Wheeler: Up to 200+ kg

Contact the Shiprocket Quick team for specific weight limits applicable to your use case.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick for B2B deliveries?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick supports B2B hyperlocal deliveries such as office supplies, retail restocking, and commercial deliveries within cities. Use the appropriate vehicle type based on consignment size.

## What couriers are available on Shiprocket Quick

Shiprocket Quick partners with hyperlocal delivery providers like Shadowfax, Dunzo, and Borzo (formerly WeFast) for same-day and instant delivery. Available couriers depend on your city and serviceability.

---

## What vehicle types are available for Quick deliveries

Shiprocket Quick supports:

* **2-wheeler (bike)** — for small packages
* **3-wheeler (auto/tempo)** — for medium packages
* **4-wheeler (car/van)** — for larger packages

Select the appropriate vehicle based on your package size and weight.

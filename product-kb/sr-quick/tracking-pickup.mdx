## How do I ensure that my local order is picked up?

It's very simple. You can easily track your order pickup by using our live real-time shipment tracking feature. Just enter your order number on our platform to get live updates on your order pickup status. Our system ensures you're always in the loop about the progress of your delivery.

---

## How does tracking work for Shiprocket Quick deliveries?

Shiprocket Quick provides real-time tracking:
1. Live GPS tracking of the delivery agent on the map
2. Status updates at each stage (Assigned, Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered)
3. Estimated arrival time (ETA) updates
4. Notifications to both sender and receiver

---

## How to track a Shiprocket Quick order?

To track a Quick order:
1. Go to the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Find the order by order ID or tracking number
3. View real-time tracking status and agent location
4. Share the tracking link with the buyer/receiver

---

## What is the pickup time for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick offers:
* Instant pickup: Delivery agent typically arrives within 30-60 minutes
* Scheduled pickup: Pickup at a specified time slot

Actual pickup time depends on delivery agent availability in your area at that time.

---

## How to schedule a Quick delivery?

To schedule a Quick delivery:
1. Go to Quick section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Create a new delivery request
3. Enter pickup and delivery address
4. Select vehicle type based on package size
5. Choose immediate dispatch or scheduled delivery time
6. Confirm and track in real-time

---

## Can I cancel a Shiprocket Quick order?

Shiprocket Quick orders can be cancelled before the delivery agent is assigned or before pickup. After pickup has commenced, cancellation may incur charges. Cancel from the Quick section in the panel or contact Quick support promptly.

---

## What is the delivery radius for Shiprocket Quick?

Delivery radius for Quick varies by city and courier partner:
* Same city intra-city: Typically up to 25-30 km
* Outstation / inter-city: Not available via Quick — use Shiprocket Express for inter-city deliveries

---

## How to contact Shiprocket Quick support?

For Quick-related queries:
1. Raise a support ticket from Help & Support in your Shiprocket panel (select Quick category)
2. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
3. Use the in-panel chat support

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick offer COD (Cash on Delivery)?

Yes, Shiprocket Quick supports Cash on Delivery for eligible orders. The delivery agent collects cash at delivery. Remittance follows the standard Shiprocket COD remittance process.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick API for integration?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick offers API integration for businesses needing programmatic hyperlocal delivery booking. Contact Shiprocket's developer team or check the API documentation for Quick-specific endpoints.

---

## What happens if the Quick delivery fails?

If delivery fails on the first attempt:
1. You are notified immediately
2. You can schedule a reattempt
3. Contact the buyer to arrange alternative delivery
4. If no resolution, the order is returned to the pickup address

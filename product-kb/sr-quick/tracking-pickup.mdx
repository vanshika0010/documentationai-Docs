## How to track a Shiprocket Quick delivery

Quick deliveries provide real-time tracking with live map updates. After booking, you and the recipient receive a tracking link showing the delivery partner's live location.

---

## How is pickup handled for Quick deliveries

For Quick deliveries, the delivery partner picks up directly from your location (store/warehouse). Pickup is typically within 30–60 minutes of booking. Ensure the package is ready before booking.

## How does tracking work for Shiprocket Quick deliveries?

Shiprocket Quick provides real-time tracking:
1. Live GPS tracking of the delivery agent
2. Status updates at each stage (Picked Up, In Transit, Delivered)
3. Estimated arrival time
4. Notifications to sender and receiver

---

## How to track a Shiprocket Quick order?

To track a Quick order:
1. Go to the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Find the order by ID or tracking number
3. View real-time tracking status and delivery agent location
4. Or use the tracking link shared with the buyer

---

## What is the pickup time for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick offers:
* Instant pickup: Delivery agent arrives within 30-60 minutes
* Scheduled pickup: Pickup at a specified time slot

Pickup time depends on delivery agent availability in your area.

---

## How to schedule a Quick delivery?

To schedule a Quick delivery:
1. Go to Quick section in Shiprocket panel
2. Create a new delivery
3. Enter pickup and delivery address
4. Select vehicle type
5. Choose immediate or scheduled delivery time
6. Confirm and track

---

## Can I cancel a Shiprocket Quick order?

Shiprocket Quick orders can be cancelled before the delivery agent is assigned or before pickup. After pickup, cancellation may incur charges. Cancel from the Quick section in the panel or contact support.

---

## What is the delivery radius for Shiprocket Quick?

Delivery radius for Quick varies by city and partner:
* Same city: Typically up to 25-30 km
* Outstation Quick: Not available, use express shipping for inter-city

---

## How to contact Shiprocket Quick support?

For Quick-related queries:
1. Raise a support ticket from Help & Support (select Quick category)
2. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
3. Use in-app support chat

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick offer COD?

Yes, Shiprocket Quick supports Cash on Delivery for eligible orders. The delivery agent collects cash and remittance follows the standard process.

---

## Can I use Shiprocket Quick API for integration?

Yes. Shiprocket Quick offers API integration for businesses needing programmatic access to hyperlocal delivery. Contact Shiprocket's developer team or check the API documentation for Quick endpoints.

---

## What happens if the Quick delivery fails?

If delivery fails on the first attempt:
1. You are notified immediately
2. You can schedule a reattempt
3. Contact the buyer for alternative delivery arrangements
4. If no resolution, the order can be returned to pickup address

## Which cities does Shiprocket Quick serve?

Shiprocket Quick covers major metropolitan and tier-1/tier-2 cities in India. Coverage is expanding. Check the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel for current serviceable cities and pincodes.

---

## How to check if a pincode is serviceable for Shiprocket Quick?

To check Quick serviceability:
1. Go to Quick section in Shiprocket panel
2. Use the serviceability checker
3. Enter the delivery pincode
4. Available vehicle types and partners are shown if serviceable

---

## What is the coverage area for hyperlocal delivery?

Hyperlocal delivery via Quick typically covers:
* Intra-city: Same city deliveries
* Distance: Usually within 10-30 km radius from pickup
* Coverage varies by city and partner availability

---

## Can Shiprocket Quick deliver to gated communities or apartments?

Yes, Quick delivery agents are equipped to deliver to gated communities, apartment complexes, and office buildings. Ensure the buyer's address includes building name, floor, and contact details for smooth delivery.

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick support inter-city deliveries?

Shiprocket Quick is primarily designed for intra-city (same city) hyperlocal deliveries. For inter-city shipments, use Shiprocket Express (SREX) or Cargo depending on the shipment size.

---

## How to expand Quick delivery coverage to new areas?

If Quick doesn't serve your required area, contact the Shiprocket Quick team. Coverage expansion depends on partner availability in that area.

---

## What is the minimum order value for Shiprocket Quick?

Shiprocket Quick doesn't have a standard minimum order value. Pricing is based on distance and vehicle type. Check the Quick section for pricing details for your area.

## Is there a minimum delivery distance for using Shiprocket Quick?

No, there's no minimum delivery distance for using Shiprocket Quick. Whether you're sending packages across the street or across the city, Shiprocket Quick can accommodate your delivery needs within its serviceable coverage area.

---

## Which cities does Shiprocket Quick serve?

Shiprocket Quick covers major metropolitan and tier-1/tier-2 cities in India. Coverage is continuously expanding. Check the Quick section in your Shiprocket panel for the current list of serviceable cities and pincodes.

---

## How to check if a pincode is serviceable for Shiprocket Quick?

To check Quick serviceability:
1. Go to Quick section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Use the serviceability/rate calculator
3. Enter the delivery pincode
4. Available vehicle types and partners will be shown if serviceable

---

## What is the coverage area for hyperlocal delivery on Shiprocket Quick?

Hyperlocal delivery via Quick typically covers intra-city deliveries within a radius of 10-30 km from the pickup location. Coverage varies by city and partner availability.

---

## Can Shiprocket Quick deliver to gated communities or apartments?

Yes. Quick delivery agents are equipped to deliver to gated communities, apartment complexes, and office buildings. Ensure the buyer's address includes building name, apartment number, floor, and a contact number for smooth delivery.

---

## Does Shiprocket Quick support inter-city deliveries?

No. Shiprocket Quick is designed exclusively for intra-city (same city) hyperlocal deliveries. For inter-city shipments, use Shiprocket Express (standard couriers) or SR Cargo for heavy freight.

---

## How to expand Quick delivery coverage to new areas?

If Quick doesn't currently serve your required area, contact the Shiprocket Quick team. Coverage expansion is ongoing and depends on partner availability in the target area.

## What exactly is 30-days free storage?

Shiprocket Fulfillment offers 30 days of free storage for products when they are first received at the warehouse. This means you won't be charged for storing your products for the first 30 days after they arrive at the warehouse. After this free period, standard storage charges will apply.

---

## How to download fulfillment reports on Shiprocket?

To download fulfillment reports:
1. Go to Fulfillment section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Navigate to Reports section
3. Select report type (inventory, orders, returns, etc.)
4. Choose date range
5. Click Download/Export

---

## What is the SRF service level agreement?

SRF SLA includes:
1. Same-day order processing for orders before cut-off time
2. Inventory accuracy standards
3. Returns processing timeline
4. Damage claim resolution timeline

Specific SLA terms are defined in your SRF agreement.

---

## How does packaging work at SRF?

SRF provides packaging services:
1. Standard packaging materials available at the warehouse
2. Custom/branded packaging available as a value-added service
3. Products are packed per Shiprocket's packing standards
4. Fragile items get additional padding/protection
5. Packaging cost is included in the fulfillment fee or charged separately

---

## What quality checks does SRF perform?

SRF performs quality checks:
1. During GRN: Product count, condition check, barcode verification
2. Before dispatch: Pick and pack verification
3. For returns: Condition assessment before restocking

---

## Can I visit the SRF warehouse?

Yes. You can schedule a warehouse visit by contacting your SRF account manager. Visits are typically pre-scheduled to minimize disruption to warehouse operations.

---

## How does SRF handle peak season/high volume?

SRF prepares for peak seasons by:
1. Pre-planning with sellers on expected volume
2. Increasing staffing and resources
3. Priority processing for high-volume sellers
4. Contact your SRF account manager before peak season to plan inventory

---

## What is pick and pack service at SRF?

Pick and pack is the process of:
1. Picking the ordered items from warehouse shelves
2. Packing them securely in appropriate packaging
3. Attaching the shipping label
4. Handing over to courier for dispatch

SRF handles this entire process as part of the fulfillment fee.

---

## How to handle returns at SRF?

Returns handling at SRF:
1. Create return order in Shiprocket
2. Courier picks up from buyer
3. Return delivered to SRF warehouse
4. Quality check performed
5. Good condition items restocked
6. Damaged items held separately for seller decision

---

## Does SRF offer kitting services?

Yes. SRF offers kitting (combining multiple products into a single unit/bundle) as a Value Added Service. Contact the SRF team for kitting requirements and pricing.

---

## How to transfer inventory between SRF warehouses?

Inventory transfer between SRF warehouses can be requested through the Fulfillment portal or by contacting your SRF account manager. Transfers are handled by SRF team with appropriate documentation.

---

## What is the turnaround time for GRN at SRF?

GRN (Goods Receipt Note) is typically processed within 1-2 business days of receiving the shipment at the SRF warehouse. Processing time may vary during peak periods.

---

## How to handle dead/obsolete stock at SRF?

For dead or obsolete stock at SRF:
1. Contact SRF team to arrange stock retrieval
2. You can request return of inventory to your location
3. Disposal services may be available as a VAS
4. Storage charges continue until stock is removed or disposed

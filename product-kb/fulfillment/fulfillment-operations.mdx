<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is GRN in Shiprocket Fulfillment?">

GRN stands for Goods Received Note. It is a document generated by the SRF team when your inventory arrives at the fulfillment center, confirming the quantity and condition of goods received.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an ASN in Shiprocket Fulfillment?">

ASN stands for Advance Shipping Notice. It is a document you create in the SRF portal before sending inventory to a fulfillment center, listing all SKUs and quantities being sent. This allows the warehouse team to prepare for the incoming inventory.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What exactly is 30-days free storage?

Shiprocket Fulfillment offers 30 days of free storage for products when they are first received at the warehouse. This means you won't be charged for storing your products for the first 30 days after they arrive at the warehouse. After this free period, standard storage charges will apply.

---

## How to create an inbound shipment (ASN) in SRF?

To create an ASN in SRF:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Inbound Shipments
2. Click on Create ASN
3. Select the products and quantities to send
4. Add the estimated arrival date
5. Save and print the ASN document
6. Attach the ASN document to the shipment
7. Send the inventory to the designated SRF warehouse

---

## How long does GRN take after delivery at SRF?

GRN (Goods Receipt Note) is typically processed within 1-2 business days of receiving the shipment at the SRF warehouse. During peak periods, it may take slightly longer.

---

## What quality checks does SRF perform during GRN?

During GRN, SRF performs:
1. Quantity count verification against ASN
2. Physical condition check
3. Barcode/SKU verification
4. Damage assessment
5. Special handling identification (fragile, temperature sensitive, etc.)

---

## What packaging materials does SRF use?

SRF provides standard packaging materials including:
1. Corrugated boxes (various sizes)
2. Bubble wrap and foam padding
3. Poly bags
4. Tape and sealing materials

Custom/branded packaging is available as a Value Added Service (VAS) for an additional charge.

---

## How does SRF handle peak season volumes?

SRF prepares for peak seasons by:
1. Pre-planning with sellers on expected volumes
2. Increasing warehouse staffing
3. Extending operating hours
4. Prioritizing high-volume sellers

Contact your SRF account manager before peak season to plan inventory accordingly.

---

## What is pick and pack at SRF?

Pick and pack is the fulfillment process:
1. Picking the ordered items from warehouse shelves
2. Quality checking before packing
3. Packing in appropriate packaging
4. Attaching shipping label
5. Handing over to courier for dispatch

This is included in the SRF fulfillment fee.

---

## Does SRF offer kitting services?

Yes. SRF offers kitting (combining multiple products into a bundle/kit) as a Value Added Service. Contact the SRF team for kitting requirements and pricing.

---

## How to transfer inventory between SRF warehouses?

Inventory transfer between SRF warehouses can be requested through the Fulfillment portal or by contacting your SRF account manager. All transfers are documented with proper paperwork.

---

## What is the process for returning inventory from SRF to seller?

To return inventory from SRF:
1. Raise a request with the SRF team specifying the products and quantities
2. SRF will pack and ship the inventory to your address
3. Outbound transfer charges will apply
4. Stock will be deducted from your SRF inventory

---

## How to get SRF reports?

SRF reports are available in the Fulfillment section:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Reports
2. Select report type (Inventory, Orders, Returns, etc.)
3. Choose date range
4. Download the report in CSV format

---

## Does SRF operate on Sundays and public holidays?

SRF warehouse operations typically run Monday to Saturday. Operations on Sundays and public holidays may vary by location. Contact your SRF account manager for the specific operating schedule of your assigned warehouse.

---

## What is the SRF helpdesk contact?

For SRF-related queries:
1. Raise a support ticket from Help & Support section in your Shiprocket panel (select Fulfillment category)
2. Contact your dedicated SRF account manager
3. Email the SRF support team

SLA for resolution varies by issue type and severity.

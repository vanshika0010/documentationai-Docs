## How do you add products to your Shiprocket Fulfillment Account?

To add products to your SRF account:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Click on Add Product
3. Enter product details:
   - Product name
   - SKU code
   - Category
   - Weight and dimensions
   - Barcode/EAN
   - MRP
   - HSN code
4. Save the product

Alternatively, use bulk upload via CSV template.

---

## How to bulk upload products to SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Click Bulk Upload
3. Download the CSV template provided
4. Fill in all required product fields
5. Upload the completed CSV
6. Review import summary and confirm

---

## What product information is required for SRF catalog?

Required fields for SRF product catalog:
1. SKU code (unique identifier - cannot be changed once in stock)
2. Product name
3. Category
4. Weight (in kg)
5. Dimensions (L x W x H in cm)
6. Barcode/EAN
7. MRP
8. HSN code

---

## How to update product information in SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Click Edit
4. Update required fields
5. Save changes

Note: SKU code cannot be modified once set.

---

## Can I sync products from my sales channels to SRF catalog?

Yes. Products from connected sales channels (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon) can be mapped to SRF SKUs. Contact the SRF team for channel-to-SRF catalog mapping assistance.

---

## How to deactivate a product in SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Toggle to inactive status

Deactivated products won't be available for new ASNs or order fulfillment, but existing stock remains trackable.

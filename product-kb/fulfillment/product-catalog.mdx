## How do you add products to your Shiprocket Fulfillment Account?

To add products to your SRF account:
1. Go to Fulfillment from the left menu
2. Click on Product Catalog
3. Click on Add Product
4. Fill in the required details:
   - Product name
   - SKU code (unique identifier)
   - Category
   - Weight and dimensions
   - Barcode/EAN
   - MRP
   - HSN code
5. Save the product

Alternatively, use bulk upload via CSV template for multiple products.

---

## How to bulk upload products to SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Click on Bulk Upload
3. Download the CSV template
4. Fill in all required product fields
5. Upload the completed CSV
6. Review and confirm the upload

---

## What information is required for adding a product to SRF catalog?

Required information:
1. SKU code (unique, cannot be changed once products are in stock)
2. Product name
3. Category
4. Weight (in kg)
5. Dimensions (L x W x H in cm)
6. Barcode/EAN
7. MRP
8. HSN code

---

## How to update product details in SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Click Edit
4. Update the required fields
5. Save changes

Note: SKU code cannot be changed once the product has been received at the warehouse.

---

## Can I sync my sales channel products with SRF catalog?

Yes. Products from connected sales channels (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon) can be mapped to SRF SKUs. This enables accurate order fulfillment when orders are placed from these channels.

---

## How to deactivate a product in SRF catalog?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Toggle to inactive status

Deactivated products won't be available for new ASNs or order fulfillment, but existing stock remains trackable.

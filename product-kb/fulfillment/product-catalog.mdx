## How do you add products to your Shiprocket Fulfillment Account?

To add products to SRF:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Click Add Product
3. Enter product details: SKU, name, category, weight, dimensions
4. Add product images
5. Set barcode/EAN if available
6. Save product

Alternatively, bulk upload via CSV template.

---

## How to bulk upload products to SRF catalog?

For bulk product upload:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Click Bulk Upload
3. Download the CSV template
4. Fill in all required product fields
5. Upload the completed CSV
6. Review and confirm the upload

---

## What product information is required for SRF catalog?

Required product information:
1. SKU (unique identifier)
2. Product name
3. Category
4. Weight (in kg)
5. Dimensions (L x W x H in cm)
6. Barcode/EAN (if available)
7. MRP (for invoice)
8. HSN code

---

## How to update product details in SRF catalog?

To update product details:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Click Edit
4. Update required fields
5. Save changes

Note: SKU cannot be changed once products are in stock at warehouse.

---

## Can I integrate my Shopify/WooCommerce catalog with SRF?

Yes. When your sales channel is connected to Shiprocket, the product catalog can be synced to SRF. Channel products are mapped to SRF SKUs for accurate order fulfillment.

---

## How to deactivate a product in SRF catalog?

To deactivate a product:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Product Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Toggle the active/inactive status
Deactivated products won't be available for new ASNs or order fulfillment.

## What happens when the product goes out of stock?

When a product goes out of stock at SRF warehouse:
1. Shiprocket sends a low stock alert
2. Orders for out-of-stock products cannot be fulfilled from SRF
3. You need to create a new ASN and send replenishment stock
4. Orders will be on hold until stock is available

---

## How to check inventory levels at SRF?

To check inventory at SRF:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Inventory in your Shiprocket panel
2. View current stock count per SKU
3. Filter by warehouse location
4. Download inventory report for detailed view

---

## How to replenish SRF inventory?

To replenish SRF inventory:
1. Create new products in SRF catalog (if new SKUs)
2. Create an ASN with the products and quantities to send
3. Ship inventory to the SRF warehouse address
4. SRF warehouse receives and generates GRN
5. Stock is updated in your inventory panel

---

## What is the minimum inventory I need to send to SRF?

There is typically no strict minimum quantity requirement for SRF. However, sending sufficient stock to cover expected demand is recommended to avoid stockouts. Contact the SRF team for specific requirements.

---

## How to handle damaged inventory at SRF?

For damaged inventory at SRF warehouse:
1. SRF team marks damaged items during GRN process
2. A damage report is generated
3. You can file a claim for damaged inventory
4. SRF has insurance coverage for stored inventory

---

## What is SRF's inventory accuracy rate?

Shiprocket Fulfillment maintains high inventory accuracy through:
1. Barcode scanning for all inventory movements
2. Regular cycle counts
3. Automated inventory reconciliation

Contact the SRF team for specific accuracy metrics.

---

## Can I do returns management through SRF?

Yes. SRF handles return orders. Return flow:
1. Return order created in Shiprocket
2. Courier picks up from buyer
3. Returned to SRF warehouse
4. Quality check performed
5. Restocked if in good condition
6. Disposed or sent back to seller if damaged

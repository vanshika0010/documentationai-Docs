## How to send inventory to a Shiprocket Fulfillment center

Create an ASN (Advance Shipping Notice) in the SRF portal listing all products and quantities. Pack your inventory with proper labels and ship it to the designated fulfillment center. The SRF team will process the GRN (Goods Received Note) upon arrival.

---

## How to check my inventory levels in SRF

Log in to the SRF portal and go to Inventory Management. You can see current stock levels for all SKUs across fulfillment centers, including available stock, reserved stock, and damaged inventory.

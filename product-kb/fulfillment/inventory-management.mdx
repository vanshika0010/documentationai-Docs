## What happens when the product goes out of stock?

When inventory goes out of stock at SRF:
1. You will receive a notification (email/SMS) about the stockout
2. Orders for out-of-stock products will not be fulfilled from SRF
3. You must create a new ASN and send replenishment stock to the warehouse
4. Affected orders will be on hold until replenishment stock arrives and is processed

---

## How to set low stock alerts for SRF inventory?

Low stock alerts can be configured in the Fulfillment >> Inventory section. Set minimum stock threshold for each SKU, and Shiprocket will notify you when stock falls below the threshold.

---

## How to replenish SRF inventory?

1. Go to Fulfillment >> ASN section
2. Create a new ASN (Advance Shipping Notice)
3. Add products and quantities to send
4. Print ASN document and send with the shipment
5. Ship inventory to the designated SRF warehouse
6. SRF processes receipt and generates GRN
7. Stock is updated in your inventory panel

---

## How to check inventory levels at SRF?

To check SRF inventory:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Inventory from the left menu
2. View current stock count per SKU
3. Filter by warehouse location
4. Download inventory report for detailed view

---

## How to view inventory movement history at SRF?

Inventory movement history is available in the Fulfillment >> Inventory section. Filter by SKU and date range to view all inbound, outbound, and adjustment transactions.

---

## What happens to damaged inventory at SRF?

For damaged inventory:
1. SRF team marks damaged items during GRN process
2. Damage report is generated and shared with seller
3. Damaged items are held separately
4. Seller can file insurance claim for covered damages
5. Damaged items can be returned to seller or disposed per agreement

---

## Can I do inventory adjustments at SRF?

Inventory adjustments at SRF are done through formal processes:
1. Raise a request with the SRF team for inventory adjustment
2. Physical count/audit is conducted at warehouse
3. Discrepancies are documented and adjusted accordingly
4. Updated inventory reflects in your panel

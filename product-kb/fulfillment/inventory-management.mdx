## What happens when the product goes out of stock?

When inventory goes out of stock at SRF:
1. Shiprocket sends low stock alerts via email/SMS
2. Orders for out-of-stock products cannot be fulfilled from SRF
3. You need to create a new ASN and send replenishment stock to the warehouse
4. Orders will be on hold until replenishment stock is received and processed

---

## How to set up inventory alerts in SRF?

Low stock alerts can be configured in the Fulfillment >> Inventory section. Set minimum stock thresholds for each SKU. When stock falls below this threshold, you will receive an alert notification.

---

## How to view current inventory at SRF?

To view current SRF inventory:
1. Go to Fulfillment from the left menu
2. Click on Inventory
3. View current stock count per SKU
4. Filter by warehouse location if needed
5. Download the inventory report for detailed view

---

## How to send replenishment stock to SRF?

To send replenishment stock:
1. Go to Fulfillment >> Inbound Shipments (ASN)
2. Create a new ASN
3. Add products and quantities
4. Print the ASN document
5. Ship inventory to the designated SRF warehouse address
6. SRF processes receipt and generates GRN
7. Stock is updated in your panel

---

## What happens to damaged inventory at SRF?

For damaged inventory:
1. Damaged items are marked during GRN process
2. Damage report is generated and shared with seller
3. Damaged items are held separately
4. Seller can file a claim for insured damages
5. Damaged items can be returned to seller or disposed per agreement

---

## Can I do cycle counts of my inventory at SRF?

Yes. SRF conducts regular cycle counts for inventory accuracy. You can also request an inventory audit by raising a request with the SRF team.

---

## How to handle returns and restocking at SRF?

Returns handling at SRF:
1. Create return order in Shiprocket
2. Courier picks up return from buyer
3. Return delivered to SRF warehouse
4. Quality check performed at warehouse
5. Items in good condition are restocked
6. Damaged items held separately

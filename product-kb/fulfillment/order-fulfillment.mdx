## Can I also manage my return or exchange orders with fulfillment?

Yes. SRF supports return and exchange order management:
1. Create return/exchange order in Shiprocket
2. Courier picks up return from buyer
3. Return delivered to SRF warehouse
4. Quality check performed
5. Good condition items are restocked
6. Exchange items are re-dispatched if applicable

---

## What is the order processing time at SRF?

SRF processes orders within the following SLAs:
- Orders placed before cut-off time: Same-day dispatch
- Orders placed after cut-off: Next business day dispatch

Cut-off times vary by warehouse location. Contact your SRF account manager for specific timings.

---

## How to cancel an SRF order?

To cancel an SRF order:
1. Go to Orders section in Shiprocket panel
2. Find the order
3. Click Cancel (only possible before the order is picked up by courier)
4. If already dispatched, initiate a return order

---

## How are shipping labels generated for SRF orders?

Shipping labels for SRF orders are automatically generated by Shiprocket when the order is assigned to a courier. The SRF warehouse team prints and attaches the label before handover to the courier.

---

## Does SRF support B2B / bulk order fulfillment?

Yes. SRF supports B2B and bulk order fulfillment. For special handling requirements for large quantity B2B orders, contact the SRF team for customized arrangements.

---

## What is the cut-off time for same-day dispatch at SRF?

Cut-off times vary by SRF location. Contact your SRF account manager for the cut-off times applicable to your assigned warehouse.

## Can I also manage my return or exchange orders with fulfillment?

Yes. SRF supports return management. Return orders can be:
1. Processed through Shiprocket's return management system
2. Picked up from buyers and delivered to SRF warehouse
3. Quality-checked at the warehouse
4. Restocked if in sellable condition

---

## How to cancel an SRF order?

To cancel an SRF order:
1. Cancel the order in your Shiprocket panel before it's picked up by the courier
2. If already dispatched, initiate a return order
3. Contact SRF support for assistance with specific cases

---

## What is the cut-off time for same-day dispatch at SRF?

Cut-off times vary by SRF location. Typically:
- Orders before 12 PM: Same-day dispatch
- Orders after 12 PM: Next-day dispatch

Contact your SRF account manager for location-specific cut-off times.

---

## How to handle partial orders at SRF?

For partial orders (some items out of stock):
1. SRF can fulfill available items and hold the rest
2. Or wait for complete stock availability
3. Configure split shipment preferences in your SRF settings

---

## Can SRF handle bulk/B2B orders?

Yes. SRF can handle bulk orders for B2B fulfillment. Contact the SRF team for special handling arrangements for large quantity orders.

---

## How are shipping labels generated for SRF orders?

For SRF orders, shipping labels are generated automatically by Shiprocket when the order is assigned. SRF warehouse team prints and attaches the label to the packed order.

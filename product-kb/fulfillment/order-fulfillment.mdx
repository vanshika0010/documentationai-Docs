## Can I also manage my return or exchange orders with fulfillment?

Yes. SRF supports return management. The return process:
1. Return order is created in Shiprocket
2. Courier picks up return from buyer
3. Returned shipment is delivered to SRF warehouse
4. Quality check is performed at warehouse
5. Items in good condition are restocked
6. Damaged items are held separately

---

## How to cancel an SRF order before dispatch?

To cancel an order before it's dispatched by SRF:
1. Go to Orders section in Shiprocket panel
2. Find the order and click Cancel
3. If the order has been processed by the warehouse but not dispatched, contact SRF support immediately

---

## What is the dispatch cut-off time for SRF?

SRF dispatch cut-off times vary by location. Typically, orders placed before the cut-off time are dispatched same day. Orders after cut-off are dispatched the next business day. Contact your SRF account manager for location-specific cut-off times.

---

## What is the SLA for SRF order processing?

SRF maintains strict SLAs for order processing. Standard SLA is same-day dispatch for orders before cut-off time. Specific SLAs are defined in your SRF service agreement.

---

## How are shipping labels generated for SRF orders?

Shipping labels for SRF orders are generated automatically by Shiprocket when the order is assigned to a courier. The SRF warehouse team prints and attaches the label to the packed order before handover to the courier.

---

## Can SRF handle fragile or special packaging requirements?

Yes. SRF offers VAS (Value Added Services) including special packaging for fragile items, custom branding, and other specific packing requirements. These are charged as additional services.

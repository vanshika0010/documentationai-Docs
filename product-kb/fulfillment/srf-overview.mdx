## What is Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF) or warehousing service?

Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF) is a 3PL (third-party logistics) service that offers storage, picking, packing, and shipping from Shiprocket's warehouses. Sellers store their inventory at SRF warehouses, and Shiprocket handles the entire fulfillment process from storage to delivery.

---

## What are the benefits of using SRF?

Benefits of Shiprocket Fulfillment:
1. Faster delivery times (same-day/next-day) as inventory is stored closer to buyers
2. No need to manage your own warehouse
3. Reduced shipping costs due to zone optimization
4. Free 30-day storage for new inventory
5. Professional packing and quality control
6. Integration with all major sales channels
7. Real-time inventory tracking

---

## What products can I store at SRF warehouses?

SRF accepts most non-hazardous products. Products should be:
1. Non-perishable
2. Non-hazardous
3. Within size/weight limits accepted by SRF
4. Properly labeled with barcode/SKU

Contact the SRF team for specific product category acceptance.

---

## How to register for Shiprocket Fulfillment?

To register for SRF:
1. Go to the Fulfillment section from your Shiprocket panel
2. Click on Get Started or Register
3. Complete the registration form
4. Add your products to the SRF catalog
5. Create an ASN (Advance Shipment Notice) to send inventory to the warehouse

---

## What is the process to send inventory to SRF warehouse?

Process to send inventory to SRF:
1. Create products in SRF catalog
2. Create an ASN (Advance Shipment Notice) specifying products and quantities
3. SRF generates a GRN (Goods Receipt Note) after receiving inventory
4. Inventory becomes available for fulfillment once GRN is processed

---

## What are the charges for SRF?

SRF charges include:
1. Storage fee: Per unit per day after free 30-day period
2. Fulfillment fee: Per order processed (picking, packing, shipping label)
3. Shipping charges: As per courier partner rates
4. Returns handling fee: For processing return orders

Contact the SRF team for detailed pricing.

---

## How does order fulfillment work with SRF?

SRF order fulfillment flow:
1. Order comes from your sales channel (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, etc.)
2. Order syncs to Shiprocket panel
3. Shiprocket assigns order to SRF warehouse
4. Warehouse team picks, packs, and ships the order
5. Tracking is updated in your Shiprocket panel

---

## What is GRN in SRF context?

GRN (Goods Receipt Note) is a document generated by the SRF warehouse when they receive inventory from you:
1. Confirms quantity and condition of received items
2. Makes inventory available in your SRF stock
3. Resolves any discrepancies between ASN and actual received quantity

---

## What is ASN in SRF context?

ASN (Advance Shipment Notice) is a document you create to notify SRF warehouse about incoming inventory:
1. Specifies products, quantities, and expected delivery date
2. Helps warehouse prepare for receiving
3. Required before sending inventory to SRF

---

## What SLA does SRF offer for order processing?

SRF processing SLA:
- Same-day dispatch for orders placed before cut-off time
- Orders placed after cut-off: Dispatched next business day
- Specific SLAs depend on your SRF agreement

---

## Can I use SRF for multiple warehouses?

Yes. Shiprocket Fulfillment has warehouses in multiple cities. You can distribute inventory across locations for faster delivery and zone optimization. Orders are automatically routed to the nearest warehouse with available stock.

---

## What channels can integrate with SRF?

SRF supports inventory and order sync with:
- Shopify
- WooCommerce
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Custom channels via API
- All channels connected to your Shiprocket account

---

## How to track inventory at SRF warehouse?

To track SRF inventory:
1. Go to the Fulfillment section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Navigate to Inventory tab
3. View current stock levels per SKU per warehouse
4. Set up low-stock alerts to replenish in time

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF)?">

Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF) is a third-party logistics (3PL) service that handles warehousing, inventory storage, order picking, packing, and shipping on your behalf. Store your inventory in Shiprocket's fulfillment centers and orders are automatically processed and shipped.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the benefits of using Shiprocket Fulfillment?">

Key benefits: faster processing and dispatch, access to Shiprocket's courier network, reduced packing errors, inventory management, multi-location fulfillment, and ability to focus on selling rather than logistics.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

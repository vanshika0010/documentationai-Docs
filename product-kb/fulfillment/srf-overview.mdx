## What is Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF) or warehousing service?

Shiprocket Fulfillment is a service that provides storage, packaging, and shipping assistance to sellers. There are various benefits of having a warehousing facility for sellers:
1. Faster shipping and fewer RTO
2. Reduced shipping costs
3. Reduced operational headaches
4. Inventory management
5. Multi-channel fulfillment

---

## How does SRF or Shiprocket warehousing work?

An overview how shiprocket fulfillment works:
A). Start by registering yourself for Shiprocket Fulfillment
    1. First, create a Shiprocket account. 
    2. Then create a Fulfillment account using the Fulfillment tab in your Shiprocket dashboard
    3. Get in touch with the Fulfillment team to activate your account
B). Add your products to the Fulfillment catalog
C). Set up a pickup location or use the Fulfillment warehouse as a pickup location
D). Create an inbound shipment (ASN) to receive products into the warehouse
E). Manage your inventory
F). Start creating orders and have them fulfilled by the warehouse team

---

## What is GRN?

GRN or Goods Received Note is a record that confirms the receiving of goods at the warehouse. It's a process where we allot a unique number to each received product.

---

## What is ASN?

ASN stands for Advanced Shipping Notice. It's a document that is sent before the delivery of goods to the Warehouse. It includes the details of the goods to be delivered, such as the quantity, type, and other relevant information. This helps the warehouse to be prepared for the incoming goods.

---

## What is WMS?

WMS stands for Warehouse Management System. It's a software solution that helps manage and optimize the day-to-day operations in a warehouse or distribution center. WMS provides real-time visibility into inventory levels and locations, assists in planning and managing the put-away process, helps in picking and packing processes, and manages the shipping process.

---

## What is VAS in SRF context?

VAS stands for Value Added Services. In Shiprocket Fulfillment context, it includes services like quality checks, relabeling, kitting, bundle creation, and other special handling services provided at the warehouse for an additional charge.

---

## How to calculate the Shipping Cost for Fulfillment?

Shipping cost for fulfillment orders is calculated based on:
1. The weight of the product
2. The destination pincode
3. The courier partner assigned
4. Any applicable surcharges

You can use the Rate Calculator in the Shiprocket panel to estimate shipping costs.

---

## What are the SRF charges?

SRF charges include:
1. Storage fee: Per unit per day after the 30-day free storage period
2. Fulfillment fee: Per order processed (includes picking, packing, and labeling)
3. Shipping charges: As per the courier partner selected
4. Returns handling fee: For processing return orders

Contact the SRF team for the current rate card.

---

## What happens when SRF receives wrong quantity of products?

When SRF receives a quantity that doesn't match the ASN:
1. The warehouse team documents the discrepancy in the GRN
2. The actual received quantity is updated in your inventory
3. You are notified about the discrepancy
4. You can raise a dispute if needed

---

## What is the process for raising an issue with SRF?

To raise an issue with SRF:
1. Raise a support ticket from the Help & Support section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Select 'Fulfillment' as the category
3. Describe the issue with relevant details (ASN number, order ID, etc.)
4. The SRF support team will respond within the agreed SLA

## What are the key benefits of SRF for D2C sellers?

Key benefits of SRF for D2C sellers:
1. Faster delivery with pan-India warehouse network
2. Reduced RTO through better packaging and quality control
3. Lower shipping costs due to zone skipping
4. No need to manage own warehouse
5. Scale up/down inventory based on demand
6. Integrated with all major sales channels

---

## What channels can be connected to SRF for order fulfillment?

SRF supports order fulfillment from:
- Shopify
- WooCommerce
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Meesho
- Custom channels via Shiprocket API
- All channels connected to your Shiprocket account

---

## What is the minimum inventory I need to send to SRF?

There is no strict minimum inventory requirement for SRF. However, maintaining adequate stock levels to cover expected demand is recommended to avoid stockouts.

## I'm on the Shiprocket Lite plan. Can I still register for SRF?

Yes. SRF availability depends on your Shiprocket account status and SRF eligibility, not just your shipping plan. Contact the SRF team or your Shiprocket account manager to check SRF eligibility and registration process.

---

## What are the steps to get started with SRF?

Steps to start with SRF:
1. Contact Shiprocket SRF team or account manager
2. SRF eligibility review
3. SRF agreement signing
4. Add products to SRF catalog
5. Create first ASN
6. Send inventory to SRF warehouse
7. After GRN, orders can be fulfilled through SRF

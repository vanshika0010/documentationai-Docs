## I'm on the Shiprocket Lite plan. Can I still register for SRF?

Yes. Your Shiprocket shipping plan does not restrict SRF registration. SRF eligibility is evaluated separately. Contact the SRF team or your account manager to check eligibility and begin the onboarding process.

---

## How to get started with SRF?

Steps to onboard with SRF:
1. Contact the Shiprocket SRF team or raise a request from the Fulfillment section in your panel
2. SRF team evaluates your eligibility
3. Complete SRF onboarding documentation and sign the agreement
4. Add your products to the SRF product catalog
5. Create your first ASN
6. Ship inventory to the assigned SRF warehouse
7. After GRN is processed, orders can be fulfilled through SRF

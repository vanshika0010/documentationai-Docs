## I'm on the Shiprocket Lite plan. Can I still register for SRF?

Yes, your Shiprocket plan does not restrict you from registering for SRF. SRF is a separate service with its own eligibility criteria. Contact the SRF team to check eligibility and start the onboarding process.

---

## How to get started with SRF?

Steps to onboard with SRF:
1. Contact Shiprocket SRF team or raise a request from the Fulfillment section
2. SRF team will evaluate eligibility
3. Complete SRF onboarding documentation and agreement
4. Add your products to SRF product catalog
5. Create your first ASN
6. Ship inventory to the assigned SRF warehouse
7. After GRN is processed, you can start fulfilling orders through SRF

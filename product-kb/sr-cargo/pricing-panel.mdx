## How is Shiprocket Cargo pricing calculated?

Shiprocket Cargo pricing is calculated based on:
1. Actual weight vs volumetric weight (whichever is higher)
2. Origin and destination
3. Courier partner selected
4. Type of goods
5. Special handling requirements

Contact the Cargo team or use the Cargo Rate Calculator for specific rates.

---

## How to check Cargo rates on Shiprocket?

To check Cargo rates:
1. Go to the Cargo section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Use the Rate Calculator
3. Enter pickup pincode, delivery pincode, and weight
4. View rates for available Cargo couriers

---

## How to create a Cargo shipment on Shiprocket?

To create a Cargo shipment:
1. Go to Cargo section
2. Click Create Shipment
3. Enter pickup and delivery address
4. Enter consignment details (weight, dimensions, product type)
5. Select courier
6. Confirm and generate labels

---

## What is the Cargo dashboard on Shiprocket?

The Cargo dashboard provides an overview of all Cargo shipments with:
1. Active shipments and their status
2. Pending pickups
3. In-transit consignments
4. Delivered and returned shipments
5. Financial summary (charges, pending invoices)

---

## How to download Cargo invoices on Shiprocket?

Cargo invoices are available in the Finances section under Cargo invoices or in the Cargo dashboard. Download from the Bills/Invoices section by selecting the Cargo invoice type.

---

## What is volumetric weight calculation for Cargo?

For Cargo shipments:
Volumetric weight = (L cm × W cm × H cm) / 5000 (in kg)

The chargeable weight is the higher of actual weight vs volumetric weight, similar to standard express shipping.

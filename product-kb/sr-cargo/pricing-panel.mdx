## Do I have to pay for the Crago panel?

No, our panel is free of cost. You will just have to pay for the shipping services that you use.

---

## How is SR Cargo pricing calculated?

SR Cargo pricing is calculated based on:
1. Actual weight vs volumetric weight (higher is charged)
2. Origin and destination pincodes
3. Courier partner selected
4. Type and nature of goods
5. Any special handling requirements

Use the SR Cargo Rate Calculator in the panel for specific rates.

---

## How to check cargo rates on Shiprocket?

To check SR Cargo rates:
1. Log into your Shiprocket panel
2. Navigate to SR Cargo section
3. Use the Rate Calculator
4. Enter pickup pincode, delivery pincode, and shipment weight/dimensions
5. View rates for all available cargo couriers

---

## What is the volumetric weight calculation for SR Cargo?

Volumetric weight = (Length cm × Width cm × Height cm) / 5000

Chargeable weight = Max(Actual weight, Volumetric weight)

This ensures accurate pricing for large but light shipments.

---

## How to create a shipment on SR Cargo panel?

To create a cargo shipment:
1. Go to SR Cargo in your Shiprocket panel
2. Click Create Shipment
3. Enter pickup and delivery address
4. Enter consignment details (weight, dimensions, goods type)
5. Select courier partner
6. Confirm and generate shipping documents

---

## How to download SR Cargo invoices?

SR Cargo invoices are available in the Finances section. Go to Finances >> Bills/Invoices and filter by Cargo invoice type to download your billing documents.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is pricing calculated for Shiprocket Cargo?">

Cargo pricing is based on total consignment weight, dimensions, origin and destination, and the selected courier/freight service. Unlike parcel shipping, cargo uses freight-based pricing. Contact the Cargo team for a customized rate card.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to access the Shiprocket Cargo dashboard?">

Shiprocket Cargo is accessible from your Shiprocket panel. Go to Products >> Cargo from the left menu, or your dedicated Cargo account manager will provide access to the Cargo dashboard.

  </Accordion>

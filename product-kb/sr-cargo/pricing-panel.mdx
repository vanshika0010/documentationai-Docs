## How is pricing calculated for Shiprocket Cargo

Cargo pricing is based on total consignment weight, dimensions, origin and destination, and the selected courier/freight service. Unlike parcel shipping, Cargo uses freight-based pricing. Contact the Cargo team for a customized rate card.

---

## How to access the Shiprocket Cargo dashboard

Shiprocket Cargo is accessible from your Shiprocket panel under Products >> Cargo. Your dedicated Cargo account manager will also provide access to the Cargo dashboard.

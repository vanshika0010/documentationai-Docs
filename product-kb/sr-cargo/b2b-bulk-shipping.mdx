## What is B2B shipping on Shiprocket Cargo?

B2B (Business-to-Business) shipping via Shiprocket Cargo handles commercial consignments between businesses:
1. Manufacturer to distributor
2. Distributor to retailer
3. Supplier to business customer
4. Inter-company transfers

B2B shipments typically involve larger quantities and heavier consignments compared to B2C.

---

## Does Shiprocket Cargo handle e-way bill requirements?

For B2B Cargo shipments above Rs. 50,000 in value, e-way bills are mandatory under GST. Shiprocket Cargo supports e-way bill entry during shipment creation. Ensure you enter the valid e-way bill number for compliant B2B shipments.

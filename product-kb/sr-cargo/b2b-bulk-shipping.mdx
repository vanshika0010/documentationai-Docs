## What types of B2B shipments does Shiprocket Cargo support

Shiprocket Cargo supports:

* Retail replenishment shipments
* Distributor deliveries
* Inter-warehouse transfers
* E-commerce returns in bulk
* Project cargo

Both FTL (Full Truck Load) and LTL (Less Than Truck Load) options are available.

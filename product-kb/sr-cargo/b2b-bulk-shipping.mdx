## Is Cargo suitable for my business?

Shiprocket Cargo caters to eCommerce sellers, wholesalers, manufacturers, and any business requiring B2B freight or bulk shipping. If you regularly ship heavy or large consignments, SR Cargo is designed for your needs.

---

## Does SR Cargo support e-way bill requirements for B2B shipments?

For B2B cargo shipments above Rs. 50,000 in value, e-way bills are mandatory under GST regulations. SR Cargo supports e-way bill entry during shipment creation. Ensure you enter the valid e-way bill number for compliant B2B commercial shipments.

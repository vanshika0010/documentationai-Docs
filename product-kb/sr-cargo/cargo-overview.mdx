## What is Shiprocket Cargo?

SR Cargo by Shiprocket offers comprehensive B2B cargo shipping solutions, catering to heavy and bulk shipments across Pan India. SR Cargo features specialised courier partnerships for high-volume, time-sensitive, and heavy goods, ensuring reliable and efficient transportation. The services include air freight, surface freight, and last-mile delivery.

---

## Who can avail SR Cargo services?

SR Cargo by Shiprocket is designed for businesses that need to send large and heavy cargo. Anyone requiring bulk or heavy-item shipping, such as:
* eCommerce sellers
* Wholesalers
* Manufacturers
* Retailers
* Any business that needs to ship large quantities or heavy goods

---

## What types of shipments can I send with SR Cargo?

SR Cargo handles various types of B2B shipments including:
1. Heavy shipments (typically above 10-20 kg)
2. Bulk/pallet shipments
3. Commercial/industrial goods
4. Large appliances and furniture
5. Manufacturing components and raw materials
6. Retail restocking shipments

---

## How is SR Cargo different from Shiprocket Express (SREX)?

SR Cargo (B2B): For heavy, bulk shipments above 10 kg. Designed for business-to-business commercial freight. Longer transit times, freight-level solutions.

Shiprocket Express (SREX): For standard e-commerce B2C orders, typically under 10 kg. Faster delivery, suited for individual customer orders.

## How to get started with SR Cargo?

To get started with SR Cargo:
1. Log in to your Shiprocket account
2. Navigate to the SR Cargo section
3. Register/onboard for Cargo services
4. Complete KYC if required
5. Start creating cargo shipments using the Cargo panel

## What is Shiprocket Cargo?

Shiprocket Cargo is a B2B (business-to-business) heavy shipping service for bulk and large shipments. It handles consignments typically above 10 kg up to several hundred kilograms, serving manufacturers, distributors, and businesses needing freight/cargo shipping.

---

## What types of shipments can be sent via Shiprocket Cargo?

Shiprocket Cargo is ideal for:
1. Heavy/bulk shipments (10 kg+)
2. B2B deliveries between businesses
3. Industrial goods
4. Furniture and large appliances
5. Bulk FMCG consignments
6. Manufacturing components

---

## How is Shiprocket Cargo different from Shiprocket Express?

Shiprocket Express (SREX): Standard e-commerce shipping for B2C orders, typically under 10 kg, faster delivery.

Shiprocket Cargo: B2B freight for heavier consignments, bulk shipments, longer transit times, suitable for non-urgent large deliveries.

---

## How to access Shiprocket Cargo?

To use Shiprocket Cargo:
1. Log in to your Shiprocket panel
2. Look for the 'Cargo' section in the left navigation
3. Or contact your Shiprocket account manager to get Cargo enabled for your account

---

## What is the minimum weight for Shiprocket Cargo shipments?

Shiprocket Cargo is typically for shipments above 10 kg. For shipments below 10 kg, use Shiprocket's standard express shipping service. Contact the Cargo team for specific weight thresholds.

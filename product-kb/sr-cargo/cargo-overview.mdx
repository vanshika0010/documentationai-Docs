## What is Shiprocket Cargo

Shiprocket Cargo is a B2B bulk shipping solution for businesses that need to ship large, heavy, or high-volume consignments. It handles bulk shipments to retailers, distributors, and warehouses with specialized logistics support.

---

## How is Shiprocket Cargo different from regular Shiprocket shipping

Regular Shiprocket is optimized for B2C individual parcel shipping. Shiprocket Cargo is designed for B2B bulk shipments with higher weight limits, pallet-level handling, dedicated pickup scheduling, and freight-based pricing.

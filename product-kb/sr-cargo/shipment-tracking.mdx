## Can I track my shipments with Cargo?

Yes, you can track your shipments on our panel. You can also send shipping updates to the receiver.

---

## How to track a Cargo shipment on Shiprocket?

To track your SR Cargo shipment:
1. Log into your Shiprocket panel
2. Go to SR Cargo >> My Shipments
3. Search by docket number or AWB
4. View real-time tracking status and history
5. Alternatively, use the tracking link shared with the consignee

---

## What tracking updates are available for SR Cargo shipments?

SR Cargo shipment tracking milestones:
1. Booked: Shipment created and booked
2. Picked Up: Consignment collected by courier
3. In Transit: Moving through the carrier network
4. At Hub/Facility: At carrier sorting or transit hub
5. Out for Delivery: Last-mile delivery in progress
6. Delivered: Successfully delivered to consignee
7. Delayed/Exception: Any delivery issue flagged

---

## How to share tracking updates with the receiver in SR Cargo?

You can share tracking updates with the receiver by:
1. Sharing the tracking link generated for the shipment
2. Enabling automated SMS/email notifications to the consignee from the Cargo panel settings

---

## How to raise a complaint for an SR Cargo shipment issue?

For SR Cargo shipment issues:
1. Go to SR Cargo panel >> My Shipments
2. Find the specific shipment
3. Click on Raise Issue / Raise Complaint
4. Describe the issue with supporting details
5. Or raise a support ticket from Help & Support section with the docket number

---

## What is the transit time for SR Cargo shipments?

SR Cargo transit times are typically longer than express B2C shipments:
* Within state: 2-4 business days
* Metro to metro: 3-5 business days
* Remote/tier-3 locations: 5-10 business days

Actual transit depends on the courier partner and route.

---

## How to report damage to a Cargo shipment?

If a cargo shipment arrives damaged:
1. Document the damage immediately with clear photos
2. Note the damage on the delivery receipt before signing
3. Raise a claim within 24-48 hours via SR Cargo support
4. Provide: photos, docket number, description of damage
5. Shiprocket will coordinate with the courier for investigation and claim processing

---

## Does SR Cargo offer insurance for shipments?

Yes, SR Cargo provides insurance options for consignments. Insurance covers loss and damage during transit. Contact the SR Cargo team for available insurance plans and coverage details.

---

## Can I send multiple boxes as one SR Cargo consignment?

Yes. Multi-box consignments are fully supported in SR Cargo. Each box receives a separate label linked to the master docket number for the complete consignment, enabling unified tracking.

---

## What is a docket number in SR Cargo?

A docket number is the unique shipment identifier for SR Cargo consignments, similar to an AWB in B2C express shipping. Use the docket number for all tracking, communication, and claim filing related to the shipment.

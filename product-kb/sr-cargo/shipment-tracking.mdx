## How to track a Cargo shipment on Shiprocket?

To track a Cargo shipment:
1. Go to Cargo section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Find the shipment by docket number or AWB
3. View real-time tracking status
4. Alternatively, track directly on the courier's website using the provided tracking number

---

## What tracking updates are available for Cargo shipments?

Cargo shipment tracking milestones:
1. Picked Up: Courier collected from sender
2. In Transit: Consignment moving through carrier network
3. At Hub: Consignment at carrier hub/sorting facility
4. Out for Delivery: Last-mile delivery in progress
5. Delivered: Successfully delivered to receiver
6. Delayed: Shipment experiencing delays

---

## How to raise a complaint for a Cargo shipment issue?

For Cargo shipment issues:
1. Go to Cargo section
2. Find the specific shipment
3. Raise a complaint/dispute from the shipment details
4. Or raise a support ticket from Help & Support section
5. Include docket number, issue description, and supporting documents

---

## What documents are required for Cargo shipments on Shiprocket?

Documents required for Cargo:
1. Shipping label (generated by Shiprocket)
2. Invoice/e-way bill (for commercial goods)
3. Packing list (for multi-box consignments)
4. Any special permits for regulated goods

---

## What is the transit time for Cargo shipments on Shiprocket?

Transit times for Cargo are longer than express:
* Within state: 2-4 business days
* Metro to metro: 3-5 business days
* Remote/tier-3 areas: 5-10 business days

Actual transit time depends on the courier partner and route.

---

## How to report damage to a Cargo shipment?

If a Cargo shipment arrives damaged:
1. Document the damage immediately with photos
2. Note the damage on the delivery receipt
3. Raise a claim with Shiprocket support within 24-48 hours
4. Include photos, docket number, and description of damage
5. Shiprocket will coordinate with the courier for investigation and claim

## How to schedule a Cargo pickup on Shiprocket?

After creating a Cargo shipment:
1. A pickup is automatically scheduled
2. Courier contacts you or arrives on the scheduled date
3. For specific pickup time requests, contact Shiprocket Cargo support

---

## Can I send multiple boxes as one Cargo consignment?

Yes. Multi-box consignments are supported in Cargo. Each box gets a separate label linked to the master docket number for the complete consignment.

---

## What is docket number in Shiprocket Cargo?

A docket number is the unique identifier assigned to a Cargo consignment, similar to AWB in express shipping. Use this number for tracking and all communication about the shipment.

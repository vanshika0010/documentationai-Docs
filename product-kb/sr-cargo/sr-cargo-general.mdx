## Which products are prohibited to ship via Cargo?

Prohibited items for Shiprocket Cargo:
1. Hazardous materials (explosives, flammables, corrosives, radioactive)
2. Narcotics and controlled substances
3. Weapons and ammunition
4. Live animals
5. Perishable items (unless refrigerated cargo is arranged)
6. Currency and negotiable instruments
7. Items prohibited by law or carrier policy

Contact the Cargo team for a complete list of prohibited items.

## What is the KYC requirement for Shiprocket Cargo?

KYC for Cargo may require additional business documentation beyond standard Shiprocket KYC:
1. Business registration certificate
2. GST certificate
3. PAN card of the company
4. Bank account details

Contact the Cargo team for specific KYC requirements.

## What courier partners are available for Shiprocket Cargo?

Shiprocket Cargo works with multiple freight/cargo courier partners for different routes and consignment types. Available partners depend on:
1. Origin and destination pincodes
2. Consignment weight and type
3. Delivery timeline requirements

View available options in the Cargo Rate Calculator.

---

## What is the maximum weight per consignment for Cargo?

Shiprocket Cargo handles consignments up to several hundred kilograms. For very heavy or oversized consignments, contact the Cargo team for customized solutions.

## How to contact Shiprocket Cargo support?

For Cargo-related queries:
1. Raise a support ticket from Help & Support (select Cargo category)
2. Contact your dedicated Cargo account manager
3. Email the Cargo support team
4. Call the Cargo helpline if available

---

## Does Shiprocket Cargo support COD?

Shiprocket Cargo primarily handles B2B shipments where COD is less common. For COD requirements on Cargo shipments, contact the Cargo team for feasibility and configuration.

## Which products are prohibited to ship via Cargo?

Several goods are prohibited from shipping including hazardous materials, perishables goods, precious metals/stones, currency, live animals, human remains, weapons, narcotics, and items restricted by law. Contact the SR Cargo team for a complete prohibited items list.

---

## What is the KYC requirement for SR Cargo?

KYC for SR Cargo may require additional business documentation:
1. Business registration certificate
2. GST certificate
3. Company PAN card
4. Bank account details in company name

Contact the SR Cargo team for specific KYC requirements for your account.

---

## What courier partners are available on SR Cargo?

SR Cargo works with multiple B2B freight and cargo courier partners. Available partners depend on:
1. Origin and destination pincodes
2. Consignment weight and type
3. Delivery timeline requirements

View available options in the SR Cargo Rate Calculator in your panel.

---

## What is the maximum weight per consignment on SR Cargo?

SR Cargo handles heavy consignments typically above 10 kg with no strict upper limit for freight-level shipments. For very heavy or oversized cargo, contact the SR Cargo team for customised freight solutions.

---

## How to contact SR Cargo support?

For SR Cargo queries:
1. Raise a support ticket from Help & Support in your Shiprocket panel (select SR Cargo category)
2. Contact your dedicated SR Cargo account manager
3. Reach out to your Shiprocket KAM for Cargo onboarding

---

## Does SR Cargo support COD (Cash on Delivery)?

SR Cargo primarily handles B2B commercial shipments where payment is typically prepaid or on credit terms. For COD requirements on Cargo shipments, contact the SR Cargo team to check feasibility.

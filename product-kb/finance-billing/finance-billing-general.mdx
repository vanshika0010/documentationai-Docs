## **How to check Credit Receipts?**

To check credit receipts, follow these steps:

* Go to the **Finances** section from the left menu on the seller panel.
* Click on **Passbook** from the Finances dropdown.
* Here, filter by 'Credit' type transactions to see all credit receipts.

## **How to check Credit Receipts?**

To check credit receipts, follow these steps:

* Go to the **Finances** section from the left menu on the seller panel.
* Click on **Passbook**.
* Filter the transactions by type 'Credit' to see all credit entries including COD credits, refunds, and recharges.

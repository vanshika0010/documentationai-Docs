## **How to check Credit Receipts?**

To check credit receipts on Shiprocket:
1. Go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu
2. Filter by transaction type "Credit" to see all credit entries
3. This includes COD credits, refunds, adjustments, and wallet recharges
4. Download the passbook for a complete credit history

## How to contact Shiprocket finance support?

For finance-related queries on Shiprocket:
1. Raise a support ticket from the Help section
2. Email: billing@shiprocket.in or support@shiprocket.com
3. Contact your dedicated account manager (if on higher plans)
4. Use the in-panel chat support for quick queries

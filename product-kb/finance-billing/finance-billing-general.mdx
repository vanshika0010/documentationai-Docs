## **How to check Credit Receipts?**

To check Credit Receipts on Shiprocket, you need to follow these steps:
* Login to your Shiprocket account.
* Navigate to Finance from the left sidebar.
* Click on the Passbook option under the Finance section.
* Here, you can see all the transactions including the Credit Receipts.
* You can use the filter to select "Credit" type transactions to view only the credit entries.
* You can also download the CSV of these transactions for your reference.

## Where can I get help for billing issues on Shiprocket?

For billing-related issues on Shiprocket:
1. Raise a ticket from the Help section in your panel
2. Contact support at support@shiprocket.com
3. Contact your dedicated account manager (available on higher plans)
4. Use the in-app chat support for quick queries

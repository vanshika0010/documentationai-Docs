## What is Shiprocket Early COD (ECOD)

Shiprocket's Early COD helps online sellers receive COD payments faster without waiting for the standard remittance cycle. Get guaranteed remittance in just 2 days from the shipment delivered date.

Note: Once activated you cannot change or deactivate your plan for the next 30 days.

---

## What are the plans or charges of Early COD (ECOD)

Three plans are available:

* **Plan 1: Delivery + 2 Days** — 0.99% of total COD amount
* **Plan 2: Delivery + 3 Days** — 0.69% of total COD amount
* **Plan 3: Delivery + 4 Days** — 0.49% of total COD amount

Note: Once activated you cannot change or deactivate your plan for the next 30 days.

---

## How to activate Early COD (ECOD)

Go to Settings >> COD Payments >> Early COD. Select your preferred plan, accept the Terms & Conditions, and click the 'Activate' button.

---

## When will my COD amount be remitted to my bank account

You can expect to receive your COD amount within 8 to 9 days from the date of order delivery. Alternatively, opt for Early COD to receive payments in as few as 2 days after delivery.

---

## What is Shiprocket Postpaid? How do I activate it

The Postpaid plan gives sellers more transparent reconciliations, uninterrupted shipping, and faster COD remittance. To switch:

1. Go to Settings >> Postpaid under COD Payments.
2. Turn the toggle on.
3. Click Submit.

You can also connect with your Key Account Manager.

---

## What are the necessary steps for hassle-free COD remittance

To get a hassle-free COD remittance, ensure:

* Bank details are updated in your Shiprocket panel.
* Your Shiprocket wallet balance is always positive (for prepaid sellers).
* There is no freight invoice pending for more than 30 days.

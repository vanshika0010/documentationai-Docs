## What is Shiprocket Early COD(ECOD)? Should I opt for it?

Shiprocket Early COD (ECOD) is a feature that allows sellers to receive their COD (Cash on Delivery) remittances earlier than the standard schedule. Instead of waiting for the normal remittance cycle, sellers can get their COD amount credited to their Shiprocket wallet or bank account faster.

You should opt for ECOD if:
1. You need faster access to your COD funds for business operations
2. You want to improve your cash flow
3. You are comfortable with the associated fees for early remittance

---

## What is the difference between COD and ECOD on Shiprocket?

COD (Cash on Delivery): Standard remittance where COD amount is credited after the courier completes their remittance cycle (typically 7-10 business days after delivery).

ECOD (Early COD): Accelerated remittance where COD amount is credited to your Shiprocket wallet within 2 business days of delivery, for a small fee.

---

## How does COD remittance work on Shiprocket?

COD remittance process:
1. Buyer pays cash to the courier agent on delivery
2. Courier collects and consolidates COD amounts
3. Courier remits the amount to Shiprocket after their internal processing
4. Shiprocket credits the amount to your Shiprocket wallet
5. You can then withdraw to your bank account or use for recharge

Standard timeline: 7-10 business days after delivery.

---

## When will I receive my COD remittance?

COD remittance timelines vary by courier partner. Typically:
- Standard: 7-10 business days after delivery
- ECOD: 2 business days after delivery (with ECOD feature enabled)

Check the COD Remittance section in Finances for specific courier-wise timelines.

---

## How to check COD remittance status on Shiprocket?

To check COD remittance status:
1. Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu
2. You can see pending, in-transit, and credited amounts
3. Filter by date range, courier, or status
4. Download the report for detailed transaction history

---

## What is the COD remittance fee on Shiprocket?

COD remittance fees vary by plan and courier. Standard COD handling charges are included in the shipping rate. ECOD (early remittance) has an additional fee which is a percentage of the COD amount. Check your account's current plan for specific COD charge rates.

---

## What is Instant COD on Shiprocket?

Instant COD is Shiprocket's product that enables sellers to receive COD amounts in their wallet immediately after delivery confirmation, before the courier's standard remittance cycle. It provides maximum cash flow flexibility.

---

## How to enable ECOD on Shiprocket?

To enable ECOD (Early COD):
1. Go to your Shiprocket account settings or Finances section
2. Look for the ECOD option
3. Review the terms and fee structure
4. Enable the feature
Contact your Shiprocket account manager for ECOD activation if not available in panel.

---

## Why is my COD remittance delayed on Shiprocket?

COD remittance delays can happen due to:
1. Courier's internal processing delays
2. Discrepancies in shipment status
3. Bank processing delays
4. Disputes or holds on specific orders
5. Public holidays affecting processing

Raise a support ticket with specific order details if remittance is significantly delayed.

---

## How to download COD remittance report on Shiprocket?

To download COD remittance report:
1. Go to Finances >> COD Remittance
2. Select the date range
3. Click Download/Export
The report includes order-wise COD details, status, and payment dates.

---

## What is the COD to wallet transfer process on Shiprocket?

Once courier remits COD to Shiprocket, the amount is credited to your Shiprocket wallet. From the wallet, you can:
1. Keep it for future shipment charges
2. Transfer to your linked bank account (requires KYC verification)

---

## How to withdraw COD amount to bank account from Shiprocket?

To withdraw wallet balance to bank:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet
2. Click on Withdrawal
3. Enter the amount to withdraw
4. Select the linked bank account
5. Confirm the transfer

Note: Bank account must be verified through KYC for withdrawals.

---

## What documents are needed for COD withdrawal on Shiprocket?

For COD withdrawal, you need:
1. Completed KYC (PAN card, Aadhaar)
2. Verified bank account (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
3. GST certificate (if applicable)

Ensure KYC is complete before attempting withdrawals.

---

## What is the minimum withdrawal amount from Shiprocket wallet?

The minimum withdrawal amount from Shiprocket wallet is Rs. 1,000. Amounts below this threshold cannot be withdrawn to bank account.

---

## How long does wallet withdrawal take on Shiprocket?

Wallet withdrawal to bank account typically takes:
- NEFT/IMPS: 1-3 business days
- Processing time may vary based on bank

## Why is my COD amount showing as pending on Shiprocket?

COD amount shows as pending when:
1. The courier has not yet remitted the amount to Shiprocket
2. The shipment is still in the remittance processing cycle
3. There's a dispute or hold on the order

Track the specific order's COD status in the COD Remittance section.

---

## What happens to COD for RTO orders on Shiprocket?

For RTO (Return to Origin) orders:
- If the order was never delivered (RTO before delivery), no COD amount is collected
- If courier collected COD but order was later marked as RTO, courier will adjust in next remittance cycle
- Contact support if COD was collected but not remitted for RTO orders

---

## Can I split COD remittance across multiple bank accounts on Shiprocket?

Currently, COD remittance goes to the primary bank account linked to your Shiprocket account. Multiple bank account support for splitting remittance is not available as a standard feature.

## What is the COD handling charge on Shiprocket?

COD handling charge is a fee charged for processing Cash on Delivery orders. It is typically a percentage of the order value or a flat fee, included in your shipping invoice. The exact rate depends on your Shiprocket plan and the courier partner used.

## Is there a GST on COD handling charges on Shiprocket?

Yes. GST (18%) is applicable on COD handling charges and other service fees charged by Shiprocket. These are reflected in your monthly freight invoice.

3. Raise a support ticket with order details and the expected vs received amounts

## What is Secure COD on Shiprocket?

Secure COD is a feature that provides insurance coverage for COD amounts in case of courier default or loss. It ensures your COD money is protected even if the courier fails to remit on time. This feature may be available as an add-on service.

COD availability refers to when the COD amount becomes available in your Shiprocket wallet after the courier remits it. Shiprocket processes courier remittances and credits your wallet typically within 24 hours of receiving from the courier.

## What is the difference between COD remittance and freight invoice on Shiprocket?

COD Remittance: Amount collected from buyers for COD orders, remitted to you after delivery.

Freight Invoice: Shipping charges billed to you by Shiprocket for using courier services.

These are separate financial transactions on your Shiprocket account.

## What happens to COD if the order is lost in transit on Shiprocket?

If an order is lost in transit:
1. No COD is collected (order wasn't delivered)
2. You can file a claim for the order value (if insured)
3. Shiprocket Secure or Buyer Protect covers lost orders
4. No COD remittance applies for lost orders

## What is COD confirmation on Shiprocket?

COD confirmation refers to the courier agent confirming that they have collected cash from the buyer at the time of delivery. This triggers the COD remittance process.

"COD Available for Withdrawal" refers to the COD amount that has been credited to your Shiprocket wallet and can be transferred to your bank account. This excludes amounts still in transit or pending remittance.

## What is Shiprocket Early COD(ECOD)? Should I opt for it?

* Online sellers in India largely depend on Cash-on-Delivery for purchases made from their website or through a marketplace. While everything about Cash on Delivery seems fine, there's always a concern about collecting money on time. The timelines for receipt of the remittances are long, creating a burden on the cash flow. 
* Shiprocket, in its continuous effort to solve problems for e-commerce, has started offering COD remittance to seller's Bank Account within D+2. 
* Remittance cycle varies from courier to courier and can range from 7-10 days.
* Shiprocket has introduced ECOD (Early COD ) to make it possible to receive funds from COD orders at a much faster pace. The fund is remitted within D+2, D+3 or D+4 based on the plan.

---

## What are the plans or charges of Early COD (ECOD)?

* There are three different plans under Early COD:
   * Plan 1: Delivery + 2 Days
      * Opt for this plan if you wish to receive your COD payment within 2 days of order delivery.
      * A charge of 1.5% + GST will be applied on COD amount
   * Plan 2: Delivery + 3 Days
      * Opt for this plan if you wish to receive your COD payment within 3 days of order delivery.
      * A charge of 1% + GST will be applied on COD amount
   * Plan 3: Delivery + 4 Days
      * Opt for this plan if you wish to receive your COD payment within 4 days of order delivery.
      * A charge of 0.5% + GST will be applied on COD amount

## What is Shiprocket Postpaid? How do I activate it?

* The Postpaid plan is an effective way for the seller to get more transparent reconciliations, uninterrupted shipping experience and hassle-free business operations.
* In this plan, a seller is provided with a credit limit up to which he can make transactions without maintaining any balance.
* The credit limit is backed by the COD receivables, which means the more COD deliveries the seller makes, the higher the credit limit.
* An invoice gets raised when the credit reaches its threshold. The seller then has to settle the invoice within the given time period.
* You can contact your key account manager (KAM) to get the Postpaid plan activated for your account.

## Some of the important terminologies under the COD Remiitance.

COD Received: The COD amount collected from the buyer by the courier partner.

## What is Secure Postpaid?

Secure Postpaid provides sellers with a credit limit based on their daily Cash on Delivery (COD) remittances. This feature requires the seller to maintain a fixed deposit (FD) as a security measure. The credit limit is calculated as follows:
* Credit Limit = 3 x Daily COD (rolling 30-day average)

## Why Did I Receive a Lesser COD Amount Than Expected?

There could be several reasons why you received a lesser amount than expected in your Cash On Delivery (COD) remittance. Here are some potential reasons:
1. Freight Invoice Deduction: If there are any outstanding freight invoices on your account, Shiprocket will deduct the amount from your COD remittance.
2. Weight Dispute Charges: If there are any pending weight dispute charges, they will be deducted from your COD remittance.
3. RTO Reversal Amount: If any of your orders were returned (RTO), the COD amount for those orders would have been reversed.
4. Early COD Plan Charges: If you're on an ECOD plan, the applicable charges would be deducted from your COD remittance.

## What is Partial COD in Shiprocket?

Partial COD (Collectable Cash on Delivery) is a hybrid payment model that allows customers to pay a portion of the order amount online at the time of placing the order, while the remaining balance is collected in cash at the time of delivery by the courier partner.

---

## What are the benefits of using Partial COD?

Partial COD offers multiple advantages: it helps confirm genuine orders by securing a partial payment upfront, reduces the risk of fraudulent orders and returns, improves cash flow for sellers, and enhances customer trust through flexible payment options.

---

## How does Partial COD work?

Sellers can define a fixed amount or percentage of the total order value to be collected online, while the remaining amount is collected in cash at the time of delivery. This can be configured in the Shiprocket panel.

---

## Who can use the Partial COD functionality?

Partial COD is not available to all sellers by default. Interested sellers need to contact their Key Account Manager (KAM) to get the feature enabled on their account.

---

## How can sellers enable Partial COD?

To enable Partial COD, sellers should reach out to their KAM, Sales Onboarding representative, or the Shiprocket support team to request activation of the feature for their account.

---

## Can sellers customize the Partial COD amount?

Yes, sellers can customize the upfront payment by setting a fixed amount or a percentage of the order value that customers must pay online when placing the order.

---

## Is the COD amount visible to the buyer?

Yes, the remaining COD amount is clearly shown in the order details and is also printed on the shipping label, so the delivery agent and the buyer are both aware of the amount to be collected.

---

## How does Partial COD help in reducing order fraud?

Partial COD requires buyers to pay a small portion of the order value online. This upfront commitment filters out fake or casual orders, as buyers who make even a partial payment are more likely to accept the delivery.

---

## What type of orders is Partial COD ideal for?

Partial COD is ideal for high-value orders where customers may be hesitant to pay the full amount upfront, and for sellers who want to reduce RTO rates by ensuring a minimal payment commitment from the buyer.

---

## What label types support Partial COD?

To ensure accurate display of the collectable COD amount on shipping labels, sellers must use Label Type 2 when generating shipment labels for Partial COD orders.

---

## How does RTO work? Do I need balance in my account?

RTO stands for Return to Origin, which indicates that a shipment is being returned to the sender due to failed delivery attempts. For RTO shipments, the freight charges are deducted from your wallet. So yes, you need to have sufficient balance in your wallet to cover RTO charges.

## What is Shiprocket Instant COD, and how does it work?

Shiprocket Instant COD or ICOD is a smart payout option that lets you get up to 70% of your Cash on Delivery (COD) earnings transferred directly to your bank account on the same day your order is delivered. The remaining 30% is paid out after the standard courier remittance, just like the regular process.

---

## How can I get started with Shiprocket Instant COD?

To activate Shiprocket Instant COD on your account, simply navigate to Settings → Seller Remittance → and look for the Instant COD option. If it's visible, you can activate it directly. If not, please reach out to your KAM or contact our support team for assistance.

---

## Is Instant COD different from Early COD - D+2, D+3 and D+4 plans?

Shiprocket Instant COD is a next-level enhancement of the Early COD. 

While Early COD allows for the remittance to be done in D+2, D+3, and D+4 days, Instant COD is designed to provide you with 70% of the COD amount on the same day of delivery. The remaining 30% will be remitted through the regular cycle.

---

## What is the pricing for Instant COD?

A standard fee of up to 5% will be charged on the total Cash on Delivery (COD) amount remitted one day before the delivery date. This fee is deducted directly from the COD payout.

---

## Will I receive Instant COD on a daily basis?

Yes, Instant COD will be processed and credited to your account on a daily basis, but only on working days (Monday to Saturday).

---

## How will my business benefit from Instant COD?

The faster access to funds allows you to reinvest in your business immediately, manage inventory more effectively, and avoid cash flow crunches that are common with delayed COD remittances.

---

## Will Early COD remain active if I disable Instant COD?

Yes, if you disable Instant COD, your Early COD plan will stay active, and you will continue to receive COD remittances as per your selected Early COD plan (D+2, D+3, or D+4).

---

## Once Instant COD is enabled on my Shiprocket account, how will it affect the remittance process for shipments made prior to enabling Instant COD?

Once Instant COD is enabled, it will apply to all shipments that have been shipped but not yet delivered. It will not apply retroactively to shipments that have already been delivered before the activation.

## What is HSN code? Why do we require it?

* HSN code stands for Harmonised System Nomenclature code. It is a code used to classify goods for taxation purposes. The HSN code helps in identifying the tax rate applicable to a particular product.
* It is required for GST compliance and should be mentioned on the invoice.

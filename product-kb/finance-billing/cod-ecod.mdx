<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Early COD (ECOD)?">

Shiprocket's Early COD helps online sellers receive COD payments faster without waiting for the standard remittance cycle. Get guaranteed remittance in just 2 days from the shipment delivered date. Note: Once activated you cannot change or deactivate your plan for the next 30 days.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the plans or charges of Early COD (ECOD)?">

Three plans are available:
- Plan 1: Delivery + 2 Days — 0.99% of total COD amount
- Plan 2: Delivery + 3 Days — 0.69% of total COD amount
- Plan 3: Delivery + 4 Days — 0.49% of total COD amount

Note: Once activated you cannot change or deactivate your plan for the next 30 days.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to activate Early COD (ECOD)?">

Go to Settings >> COD Payments >> Early COD. Select your preferred plan, accept the T&C, and click the 'Activate' button.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will my COD amount be remitted to my bank account?">

You can expect to receive your COD amount within 8 to 9 days from the date of order delivery. Alternatively, opt for Early COD to receive payments as soon as 2 days after delivery.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Postpaid? How do I activate it?">

The Postpaid plan gives sellers more transparent reconciliations, uninterrupted shipping, and faster COD remittance. To switch: Go to Settings >> Postpaid under COD Payments, turn the toggle on, and click Submit. You can also connect with your Key Account Manager.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

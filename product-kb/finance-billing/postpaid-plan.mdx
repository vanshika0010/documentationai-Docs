## What is Unsecure Postpaid?

Unsecure Postpaid is a billing plan where you can ship orders on credit without a security deposit. Credit limit is set based on your account history and creditworthiness. Monthly invoices are raised and must be paid within the credit period.

---

## What is Secure Postpaid on Shiprocket?

Secure Postpaid is a billing plan where a security deposit is maintained with Shiprocket. The credit limit is linked to the security deposit amount. It typically offers a higher credit limit compared to Unsecure Postpaid.

---

## How to apply for Postpaid plan on Shiprocket?

To apply for Postpaid:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
2. Based on shipping volume and account history, eligibility is evaluated
3. For Secure Postpaid: Provide security deposit
4. For Unsecure Postpaid: Based on creditworthiness
5. Once approved, credit limit is set and postpaid billing begins

---

## What is the credit period for Postpaid on Shiprocket?

The credit period for Shiprocket Postpaid is typically 7-15 days from invoice generation. Payments must be made within this period to avoid account restrictions.

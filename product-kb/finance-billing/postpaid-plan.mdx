## What is Unsecure Postpaid?

In Unsecured Postpaid, a seller is provided with a credit limit up to which they can make transactions without maintaining any balance. The credit limit is not backed by any security deposit.

---

## Does wallet amount goes negative in case of postpaid?

Yes, the wallet amount can go negative up to the assigned limit.

---

## How to deactivate the postpaid payment mode?

Reach out to your KAM or our support team if you want to switch from postpaid to prepaid mode.

---

## I have already activated Shiprocket Postpaid, but it's not showing active for my account.

* If you're experiencing this issue, don't worry! Here's what you can do:
   * Email Support: Reach out to our support team at support@shiprocket.in with details about your issue.
   * Contact your KAM: If you have a dedicated Key Account Manager, please reach out to them directly for faster resolution.
   * Wait for Activation: Sometimes, there might be a delay in the system updating your account status. Please wait for up to 24 hours.

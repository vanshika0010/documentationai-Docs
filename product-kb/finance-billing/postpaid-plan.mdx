<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the Postpaid plan in Shiprocket?">

The Postpaid plan allows sellers to ship orders without maintaining a prepaid wallet balance. Sellers get a daily dynamic shipping limit based on their COD delivered shipments. Shipping charges are deducted from COD remittance.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who is eligible for the Postpaid plan?">

Eligibility for Postpaid is based on your shipment history, COD delivery rate, and account standing. Contact your Key Account Manager or Shiprocket support to check eligibility and activate Postpaid.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

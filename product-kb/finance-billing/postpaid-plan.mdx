## What is Unsecure Postpaid?

Unsecure Postpaid is a Shiprocket billing arrangement where sellers can ship orders on credit without maintaining wallet balance, up to a defined credit limit. It is "unsecured" meaning no collateral or security deposit is required. Charges are invoiced monthly and must be paid within the credit period.

---

## What is Secure Postpaid on Shiprocket?

Secure Postpaid is a postpaid arrangement where a security deposit is held with Shiprocket. The credit limit is based on the security deposit amount. It typically offers a higher credit limit and better terms compared to Unsecure Postpaid.

---

## How to apply for Postpaid plan on Shiprocket?

To apply for Postpaid:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
2. Based on your shipping volume and history, Shiprocket will evaluate eligibility
3. For Secure Postpaid, provide security deposit
4. For Unsecure Postpaid, eligibility is based on account history and creditworthiness
5. Once approved, the credit limit is set and postpaid billing begins

---

## What is the credit period on Shiprocket Postpaid?

The credit period for Shiprocket Postpaid is typically 7-15 days from invoice generation. Payments must be made within this period to avoid account suspension. Specific credit period terms are agreed upon during plan setup.

## What is the Postpaid plan in Shiprocket

The Postpaid plan allows sellers to ship orders without maintaining a prepaid wallet balance. Sellers get a daily dynamic shipping limit based on their COD delivered shipments, and shipping charges are deducted from COD remittance.

---

## Who is eligible for the Postpaid plan

Eligibility is based on your shipment history, COD delivery rate, and account standing. Contact your Key Account Manager or Shiprocket support to check eligibility and activate Postpaid.

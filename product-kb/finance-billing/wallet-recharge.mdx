## What are Paid and Unpaid Freight Invoices?

Paid Freight Invoices: These are Shiprocket freight invoices that have been paid or the charges have been deducted from the wallet. You won't owe any money for these.

Unpaid Freight Invoices: These are outstanding Shiprocket freight invoices that have not been settled. You need to make payment for these invoices.

---

## How to recharge the wallet on Shiprocket?

To recharge your Shiprocket wallet:
* Go to Finances > Wallet from the left menu
* Click on the Recharge/Add Money button
* Enter the amount you wish to add to your wallet
* Select your preferred payment method (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking)
* Complete the payment process
* Funds are credited to your wallet instantly after successful payment

---

## How to withdraw money from Shiprocket wallet to bank?

To withdraw wallet balance:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet
2. Click on Withdraw
3. Enter the withdrawal amount (minimum Rs. 1,000)
4. Select the linked, verified bank account
5. Submit the request
6. Processing takes 1-3 business days

---

## What payment methods can I use to recharge Shiprocket wallet?

Accepted payment methods for wallet recharge:
* UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.)
* Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
* Debit cards
* Net banking
* Cheque/NEFT/RTGS (for large amounts - contact support)

---

## What is the minimum wallet recharge amount on Shiprocket?

The minimum wallet recharge amount is Rs. 100.

---

## How to view passbook/wallet transaction history on Shiprocket?

To view wallet transaction history:
1. Go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu
2. All transactions are listed with date, amount, type, and description
3. Filter by date range or transaction type
4. Download the passbook report for accounting

---

## How to set up auto-recharge for Shiprocket wallet?

To configure auto-recharge:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet
2. Find the Auto Recharge settings
3. Set your minimum balance threshold (e.g., Rs. 500)
4. Set the auto-recharge amount (e.g., Rs. 2,000)
5. Link a payment method
6. Enable auto-recharge
Wallet will be automatically recharged when balance drops below the set threshold.

---

## What happens if my wallet balance goes negative on Shiprocket?

If wallet balance becomes negative:
1. New shipments may be blocked until wallet is recharged
2. Weight discrepancy charges can cause negative balance
3. Recharge immediately to restore service
4. On postpaid plans, negative balance within credit limit is allowed

---

## How long does it take for wallet recharge to reflect on Shiprocket?

Wallet recharge reflects:
* UPI/Cards: Instantly
* Net banking: Few minutes to 1 hour
* NEFT/RTGS: Same day to 1 business day

---

## Can I get a refund of my Shiprocket wallet balance?

Yes, unused wallet balance can be refunded:
1. Raise a refund request through support
2. Amount is refunded to the original payment source
3. Processing time: 5-7 business days

---

## What is the wallet credit limit on Shiprocket?

There is no maximum limit for wallet recharge. You can add any amount as needed.

---

## How is the Shiprocket wallet balance used?

Wallet balance is used for:
* Shipping charges for each order
* COD handling charges
* Value added service charges (Engage, VAS)
* Plan subscription fees
* Weight discrepancy charges

---

## What is freight invoice on Shiprocket?

Freight invoice is the monthly billing document showing all shipping-related charges:
* Base freight for each shipment
* Fuel surcharge
* COD charges
* Weight discrepancy charges (if applicable)
* GST (18%)

Available in Finances >> Invoices section.

---

## How to pay freight invoice on Shiprocket?

Freight invoices are paid from your wallet balance automatically. For postpaid customers:
1. Invoice is generated monthly
2. Must be paid within the credit period
3. Go to Finances >> Invoices to view and pay

---

## When is the Shiprocket freight invoice generated?

Freight invoices are generated monthly, typically within the first week of the following month for the previous month's shipments.

---

## How to download freight invoice on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Finances >> Invoices from the left menu
2. Find the required month's invoice
3. Click Download to get the PDF

---

## What if there is a discrepancy in my Shiprocket invoice?

To raise an invoice dispute:
1. Go to Finances >> Invoices
2. Find the disputed invoice
3. Click on specific line items to dispute
4. Provide supporting documentation
5. Shiprocket reviews within 7-10 business days

---

## What is the GST rate on Shiprocket services?

GST at 18% is applicable on all Shiprocket service charges including shipping, COD handling, and other fees.

---

## How to register GST number on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Account Settings >> Company Details
2. Enter your GSTIN
3. Upload GST certificate
4. Save changes

Future invoices will include your GSTIN for Input Tax Credit (ITC).

---

## What is Shiprocket passbook?

The Shiprocket passbook is a complete record of all financial transactions:
* Wallet recharges
* Shipping charge deductions
* COD credits
* Refunds and adjustments
* Service fee deductions

Access from Finances >> Passbook.

---

## How to download passbook on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Finances >> Passbook
2. Select date range
3. Click Download/Export
Available in CSV format.

---

## What is wallet balance alert on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket sends wallet balance alerts when your balance drops below a set threshold. Configure the minimum balance alert in Finances >> Wallet settings.

---

## Can I have multiple bank accounts for wallet withdrawal on Shiprocket?

You can add multiple bank accounts to your Shiprocket account, but must verify each account through KYC. Select the desired bank account when initiating a withdrawal.

---

## How to check wallet balance on Shiprocket dashboard?

Wallet balance is visible on:
1. Main Dashboard (top right)
2. Finances >> Wallet section

## How to recharge my Shiprocket wallet

Go to Finances >> Wallet from the left menu. Click 'Add Money,' enter the amount, and select a payment method (Net Banking, UPI, Credit/Debit Card). Minimum recharge amount is ₹500.

---

## How to check my wallet balance and transactions

Go to Finances >> Passbook/Wallet from the left menu to view your current balance and all transactions including recharges, shipping deductions, and COD credits.

---

## Why was my wallet recharge declined

Wallet recharges can fail due to: insufficient funds in your bank, payment gateway timeout, incorrect card/UPI details, or bank security blocks. Try a different payment method or contact your bank.

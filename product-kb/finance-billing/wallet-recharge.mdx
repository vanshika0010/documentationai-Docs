## What are Paid and Unpaid Freight Invoices?

Paid Freight Invoices: Shipping invoices that have been settled either through wallet deduction or direct payment.

Unpaid Freight Invoices: Shipping invoices that are yet to be settled. These must be paid to continue using Shiprocket services without interruption.

---

## How to recharge my Shiprocket wallet?

To recharge your Shiprocket wallet:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet from the left menu
2. Click on Recharge/Add Money
3. Enter the amount
4. Choose payment method (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking)
5. Complete payment
Funds are credited instantly in most cases.

---

## What payment methods are accepted for wallet recharge on Shiprocket?

Accepted payment methods:
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Debit cards
- Net banking
- Cheque/NEFT (for larger amounts)

---

## Why is my wallet balance negative on Shiprocket?

Wallet can go negative when:
1. Shipping charges exceed available balance (in postpaid setup)
2. Weight discrepancy charges applied retroactively
3. Auto-deduction for pending invoices

Recharge the wallet to bring it to positive and avoid service interruption.

---

## How to view wallet transaction history on Shiprocket?

Go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu. The Passbook shows all wallet transactions with timestamps, amounts, and descriptions.

---

## What is the Shiprocket passbook?

The Shiprocket passbook is a complete record of all financial transactions on your account:
- Wallet recharges
- Shipping charge deductions
- COD credits
- Refunds and adjustments
- Service fee deductions

---

## How to download wallet/passbook report on Shiprocket?

Go to Finances >> Passbook, select date range, and click Download/Export. The report is available in CSV format.

---

## What is the minimum recharge amount on Shiprocket?

The minimum wallet recharge amount on Shiprocket is typically Rs. 100. Check the recharge page for current minimum amount.

---

## Are there any charges for wallet recharge on Shiprocket?

No additional charges are applied for recharging your Shiprocket wallet. The full recharge amount is credited to your wallet. Payment gateway charges (if any) depend on your bank/payment provider.

---

## What happens if my wallet balance is insufficient for an order on Shiprocket?

If wallet balance is insufficient:
1. On prepaid plan: Order cannot be assigned a courier until wallet is recharged
2. On postpaid plan: Order can be processed up to your postpaid credit limit
3. Notification is sent when balance is low

---

## How to set up wallet auto-recharge on Shiprocket?

Auto-recharge can be configured in the wallet settings:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet
2. Look for Auto Recharge settings
3. Set minimum balance threshold
4. Set recharge amount
5. Link a payment method
When balance drops below threshold, wallet is automatically recharged.

---

## Can I get a refund for unused wallet balance on Shiprocket?

Yes. Wallet balance refund requests can be raised with Shiprocket support. The refunded amount goes back to the original payment method. Processing time may vary.

---

## How to transfer money from wallet to bank account on Shiprocket?

To transfer wallet balance to bank:
1. Go to Finances >> Wallet
2. Click Withdrawal
3. Enter amount (minimum Rs. 1,000)
4. Select verified bank account
5. Submit withdrawal request

Processing: 1-3 business days.

---

## What is freight invoice in Shiprocket?

Freight invoice is the monthly billing document from Shiprocket showing all shipping charges incurred during the billing period. It includes:
- Base freight charges
- Fuel surcharges
- COD charges
- Weight discrepancy charges
- GST breakdown

---

## How to pay a freight invoice on Shiprocket?

Freight invoices can be paid:
1. Automatically from wallet balance (if sufficient)
2. Manually via wallet recharge + payment
3. On postpaid plan: Within the credit period

Go to Finances >> Invoices to view and pay pending invoices.

---

## When is the freight invoice generated on Shiprocket?

Freight invoices are generated monthly. The billing cycle is typically from the 1st to the end of each month, with the invoice generated within the first week of the following month.

---

## How to download freight invoice on Shiprocket?

Go to Finances >> Invoices from the left menu. Find the invoice for the required period and click Download to get the PDF invoice.

---

## What if there is an error in my freight invoice on Shiprocket?

If you find an error in your freight invoice:
1. Go to the specific invoice
2. Click on Raise Dispute or contact support
3. Provide details of the error with supporting documentation
4. Shiprocket will review and issue a credit note if the dispute is valid

---

## Is GST included in Shiprocket shipping charges?

Yes. GST (18%) is applicable on Shiprocket's service charges including shipping, COD handling, and other fees. The freight invoice includes a GST breakdown.

---

## How to get GST credit for Shiprocket shipping charges?

To claim GST credit:
1. Ensure your GST number is registered in your Shiprocket account
2. Download the monthly GST invoice from Finances >> Invoices
3. Use this invoice for GST filing and ITC (Input Tax Credit) claims

---

## What is the wallet credit limit on Shiprocket?

There is no maximum limit for wallet recharge. You can add as much as needed. For withdrawal, the minimum is Rs. 1,000 per transaction.

---

## How to view wallet balance on Shiprocket dashboard?

Wallet balance is displayed on:
1. The main Dashboard overview (right panel)
2. The quick access widget at the top
3. Finances >> Wallet section for detailed view

## What are Paid and Unpaid Freight Invoices?

* Freight invoice: Invoice for the payment charged for shipping your orders through ShipRocket. To see your freight invoice:
* Go to Finances  from the left menu and select bills from the drop down menu
* Here you can see details like Invoice date, Due date, Total, Status and Action.
* There are three types of status: Paid, Unpaid and Cancelled
1. Paid: Invoices for which you have already paid either from your Shiprocket wallet or any other mode of payment
2. Unpaid: Invoices which are yet to be paid, or are due for payment
3. Cancelled: Invoices which have been cancelled

## Where can I check my wallet history or recharge history?

*To check your wallet history:
   * Go to 'Finances' from the left menu.
   * Click on the 'Wallet Transactions' option.
   * You will be redirected to the wallet transactions page where you can see the details of all your transactions.

## How to check the Passbook?

To check the passbook, follow these steps:
* Go to the 'Finances' section from the left menu.
* Then click on 'Passbook'.
* Here you will be able to see all the transactions associated with specific Order IDs and AWB numbers.

## Can I use UPI or other payment methods instead of using Shiprocket Wallet?

No, You can only make payments using Shiprocket Wallet.

## What is the difference between Passbook and Wallet History?

In the Passbook, you will find transaction details associated with specific Order IDs and AWB numbers.
In Wallet History, you will find information about wallet recharges, deductions, and refunds.

## What are all payment methods available to recharge shiprocket wallet?

To recharge your shiprocket wallet you can use below payment options:
1. UPI
2. Debit/Credit Card (Visa, MasterCard, Rupay)
3. Net Banking
4. Cheque/DD/NEFT (for larger amounts - contact support)

No, there is no alternative of shiprocket wallet. To make transactions on shiprocket panel, you need to use shiprocket wallet only.

There is no minimum balance requirement for your wallet. However, to proceed with shipping, you need to have sufficient balance to cover the shipping charges.

## What happens to my remaining balance when I close my account?

Currently, there is no option to close a client account on the SR panel. If you wish to stop shipping, you can simply stop using the account. The remaining balance will stay in your wallet.

## How to add money in wallet or recharge my wallet?

To add money to your wallet or recharge your wallet, follow these steps on Shiprocket panel:

* Click on 'Recharge Wallet' from the top navigation bar.
* Enter the amount you wish to add.
* Choose your preferred payment method.
* Complete the payment process.
* The amount will be credited to your wallet instantly upon successful payment.

## I have done my wallet recharge but amount not received?

You can raise a ticket for this issue from Help & Support section. 
* Go to Help & Support from left menu
* Click on 'Raise a Ticket'
* Select 'Wallet & Payments' as category
* Describe your issue and submit
Our team will resolve it within 24-48 hours.

## If the order is cancelled, will I get a refund to my wallet?

If the order is marked cancelled on Shiprocket, the freight charges will get refunded to your wallet within 24-48 hours.

## Why is my wallet balance negative?

For prepaid account wallet balance can be negative due to auto acceptance of weight discrepancy , kindly recharge your wallet to continue shipping.

## How is my wallet balance calculated?

Wallet balance is calculated as follows 
wallet Balance = Wallet credits - Total unbilled amount - Total billed amount (unpaid)

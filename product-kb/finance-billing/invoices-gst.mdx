## How can I pay my shipping invoice?

To pay your shipping invoice, please follow the steps mentioned below:
* Click on the 'Finances' section from the left menu on the seller panel.
* Click on 'Bills' from the dropdown menu.
* Here you will see all your invoices.
* Click on the invoice you want to pay.
* Click on the 'Pay Now' button.
* You will be redirected to the payment gateway page where you can complete the payment.

## How to print eway bill number on shipping label?

Eway Bill Number: Under GST Act, It is mandatory to enter Ewaybill number for shipping of goods from one state to another with value above Rs 50000.

To enter Eway Bill Number in Shiprocket:
* While creating the order, there is an option to enter the Eway Bill Number.
* You can enter it in the designated field.
* The Eway Bill Number will be printed on the shipping label.

## **How to check invoices in your Shiprocket account?**

To view generated invoices, follow these steps:

* Go to the **Finances** section from the left menu on the seller panel.
* Click on **Bills** from the Finances dropdown.
* Here you can view all your invoices - Freight, COD, VAS, and Recharge invoices.

## **How to check invoices in the Shiprocket panel?**

To check invoices, follow these steps:

* Go to the **Finances** section from the left menu on the seller panel.
* Select **Bills** from the dropdown menu.
* Here you can view and download all your invoices.

## What is State-Level Invoicing or state gst?

State-Level Invoicing lets you receive a single consolidated invoice for all shipments from a selected state, making it easier to manage GST and file returns.

---

## How can I enable State-Level Invoicing?

Go to Seller Panel → Settings → Company Setup → Billing-Invoice-GST.
From there, choose your preferred invoicing method and add your state-specific GSTIN.

---

## What if I don't add a GST number for a state?

If no GST number is provided for a state, invoices will be generated using central GST for that state.

---

## Can I still get pickup-based invoices?

Yes. The pickup-based invoicing option is still available.
To continue using it, check the box "Enable Pickup Based Invoicing" in the Billing-Invoice-GST settings.

---

## Why can't I edit my Billing Address? How do I edit my billing address?

As per government guidelines, the address mentioned on invoices must match the GST-registered address. Therefore, the billing address cannot be edited directly. Please contact support if you need to update your billing address.

---

## Why is buyer details not visible on downloaded invoice?

Buyer details are hidden from invoice by default to safeguard buyer privacy.

You can make this visible by going to Settings > Company Setup > Billing, Invoice & GSTIN and enabling the option to show buyer details on invoice.

---

## How to add GST number? How to show GST on invoice?

To add your GST number, go to Settings > Company Setup > Billing, Invoice, & GSTIN. Enter your GSTIN and save. Once added, your GST number will be displayed on all future invoices.

---

## How to edit invoice template in Shiprocket? How change invoice preferences?

You can edit invoice preferences under the 'Billing, Invoice & GSTIN' section in Settings > Company Setup. Here you can customize invoice template, add logo, toggle buyer details visibility, and more.

## How to download invoices from Shiprocket

Go to Finances >> Invoices from the left menu. Select the billing month and click Download to get the invoice in PDF format. Invoices are generated monthly.

---

## How to update my GST number on Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> Company >> Tax & Compliance from the left menu. Enter your GSTIN number and save. The GST number will be reflected on all future invoices.

---

## How to get a GST invoice for my shipping charges

Shiprocket automatically generates GST-compliant invoices for all shipping charges. Go to Finances >> Invoices to download monthly freight invoices that include GST breakup.

## How can I pay my shipping invoice?

Shipping invoices on Shiprocket are paid from your wallet balance. Ensure your wallet has sufficient balance:
1. Charges are deducted automatically for each shipment
2. For postpaid customers, invoices are raised and must be paid within the credit period
3. Go to Finances >> Invoices to view pending invoices and pay manually if needed

---

## How to update GST number on Shiprocket?

To update your GST number:
1. Go to Settings >> Company Details or Account Settings
2. Find the GST/Tax section
3. Enter your GSTIN
4. Upload required documents
5. Save the changes

GST invoices will be generated with the updated GSTIN.

---

## How to download GST invoice on Shiprocket?

To download GST invoice:
1. Go to Finances >> Invoices from the left menu
2. Find the invoice for the required period
3. Click Download
Invoices include GST breakup as per tax requirements.

---

## What GST number should I use for Shiprocket transactions?

For GST purposes, Shiprocket's invoices are issued by Shiprocket Pvt Ltd. Check the invoice for Shiprocket's GSTIN. You should provide your own registered GSTIN to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).

---

## How to register GST number on Shiprocket for ITC credit?

To register your GST number for Input Tax Credit:
1. Go to Account Settings >> Company Details
2. Enter your GSTIN and company details
3. Upload GST certificate
4. Save
Once registered, all future invoices will include your GSTIN for ITC claims.

---

## How to get B2B invoice from Shiprocket for GST filing?

Shiprocket generates B2B invoices automatically once your GSTIN is registered. Download from Finances >> Invoices. These invoices are eligible for ITC claims in GSTR-2B.

---

## What is credit note in Shiprocket?

A credit note is issued by Shiprocket when:
1. A dispute is resolved in your favor (overcharged weight, incorrect rate)
2. A refund is applicable for a service cancellation
3. Billing error correction

Credit notes reduce the outstanding invoice amount or credit your wallet.

---

## How to download credit note on Shiprocket?

Credit notes are available in Finances >> Invoices section alongside regular invoices. Download them for your accounting records.

---

## What is the invoice settlement cycle on Shiprocket?

Invoice settlement:
- Prepaid accounts: Charges deducted from wallet per shipment
- Postpaid accounts: Monthly invoice raised, payable within credit period (typically 7-15 days)

---

## How to view invoice history on Shiprocket?

All past invoices are available in Finances >> Invoices. Filter by date range to find specific invoices. Each invoice shows the period, total amount, GST breakdown, and payment status.

---

## What happens if I don't pay freight invoice on time on Shiprocket?

Late invoice payment on postpaid plans may result in:
1. Account suspension or shipping restrictions
2. Late payment charges
3. Reduction of postpaid credit limit

Ensure timely payment to avoid service disruption.

---

## How to raise invoice dispute on Shiprocket?

To raise an invoice dispute:
1. Go to Finances >> Invoices
2. Find the disputed invoice
3. Click on specific line items to dispute
4. Provide supporting evidence (order details, weight proof, etc.)
5. Submit the dispute

Shiprocket typically resolves disputes within 7-10 business days.

---

## What is the GST rate on Shiprocket services?

GST rate on Shiprocket shipping and related services is 18% (IGST for interstate, CGST+SGST for intrastate). This is reflected in your monthly freight invoice.

---

## How to get HSN/SAC code for Shiprocket invoice?

Shiprocket's freight services typically use SAC code 996812 (domestic courier services) and 996511 (international). These are mentioned on the freight invoice for GST filing purposes.

---

## Can I get a consolidated annual invoice from Shiprocket for GST filing?

Monthly invoices are available for download. For a consolidated view, download all monthly invoices for the financial year from Finances >> Invoices. Shiprocket does not currently provide a single consolidated annual invoice.

---

## How to check if invoice is paid or unpaid on Shiprocket?

In Finances >> Invoices, each invoice shows its payment status (Paid/Unpaid). Paid invoices show the payment date and mode. Unpaid invoices show the due date.

---

## What is advance payment invoice on Shiprocket?

For some high-volume or special arrangements, Shiprocket may raise advance payment invoices. These are prepayments for services to be used in future billing cycles.

---

## How to get duplicate invoice copy from Shiprocket?

All invoices are available for re-download from Finances >> Invoices at any time. There is no need to request a duplicate copy separately.

---

## What is the e-invoicing process on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket complies with e-invoicing requirements under GST for applicable transactions. E-invoices with IRN (Invoice Reference Number) are generated for B2B transactions above the threshold limit.

---

## How to change billing details on Shiprocket?

To update billing details (company name, address, GSTIN):
1. Go to Account Settings >> Company Details
2. Update the required fields
3. Save changes

Updated billing details will reflect in future invoices.

---

## What are debit notes in Shiprocket?

Debit notes may be issued for:
1. Additional charges discovered post-invoice (weight discrepancy, miscellaneous fees)
2. Corrections that increase the invoice amount

These are added to your outstanding payable amount.

---

## How to reconcile Shiprocket invoices with my accounts?

For invoice reconciliation:
1. Download monthly freight invoices and passbook from Finances
2. Match invoice amounts with passbook deductions
3. Verify COD credits against COD remittance report
4. Raise disputes for any unresolved discrepancies

---

## Does Shiprocket provide TDS certificate?

For TDS deductions applicable to Shiprocket payments, contact Shiprocket's finance team or your account manager. TDS compliance requirements depend on your business entity type.

---

## Can I change my invoice address on Shiprocket without changing my account address?

Your invoice address is typically linked to your company details in Shiprocket settings. Update your company address in Account Settings >> Company Details to change the invoice address.

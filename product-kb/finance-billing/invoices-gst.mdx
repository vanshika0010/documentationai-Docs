## How can I pay my shipping invoice?

Your shipping invoice is automatically paid from your wallet. Ensure your wallet has sufficient balance.

For Postpaid accounts:
1. Invoice is raised monthly
2. Pay via wallet or bank transfer within the credit period
3. Go to Finances >> Invoices to view outstanding invoices

---

## How to update GST number on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Settings or Account Details
2. Find the GST/Tax section
3. Enter your GSTIN
4. Upload required documents if prompted
5. Save changes

GST invoices will reflect updated GSTIN for ITC.

---

## How to download GST invoice/freight invoice from Shiprocket?

1. Go to Finances >> Invoices from the left menu
2. Find the invoice for the required period
3. Click Download to save as PDF

---

## What GST number does Shiprocket use?

Shiprocket's invoices are issued by Shiprocket Pvt Ltd with their GSTIN. Your registered GSTIN will appear on invoices for ITC purposes once added to your account.

---

## How to register GST number on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Account Settings >> Company Details
2. Enter your GSTIN
3. Upload GST certificate
4. Save

Once registered, GST invoices will be generated with your GSTIN.

---

## How to get B2B invoice for GST filing from Shiprocket?

B2B invoices are automatically generated once your GSTIN is registered in Shiprocket. Available in Finances >> Invoices for download each month.

---

## What is a credit note on Shiprocket?

A credit note is issued by Shiprocket when:
1. A dispute is resolved in your favor
2. Overcharged weight is corrected
3. Service refund is applicable

Credit notes reduce outstanding amounts or credit your wallet.

---

## How to download credit note from Shiprocket?

Credit notes are available in Finances >> Invoices alongside regular invoices. Download as needed for accounting records.

---

## What is invoice settlement cycle on Shiprocket?

Prepaid: Charges deducted from wallet per shipment.
Postpaid: Monthly invoice raised, payable within credit period (7-15 days typically).

---

## How to view invoice history on Shiprocket?

All past invoices available in Finances >> Invoices. Filter by date range to find specific periods.

---

## What happens if I don't pay freight invoice on time?

Late payment on postpaid may result in:
1. Account shipping restrictions
2. Late payment charges
3. Credit limit reduction

---

## How to raise invoice dispute on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Finances >> Invoices
2. Find disputed invoice
3. Click on specific line items
4. Provide supporting evidence
5. Submit dispute

Resolution: 7-10 business days.

---

## What is the GST rate on Shiprocket services?

18% GST on shipping and related service charges.

---

## What HSN/SAC code does Shiprocket use?

Shiprocket's freight services use SAC code 996812 (domestic courier services). Mentioned on freight invoices for GST filing.

---

## Can I get consolidated annual invoice from Shiprocket?

Monthly invoices available for download. Download all monthly invoices for the financial year from Finances >> Invoices for year-end reconciliation.

---

## How to check if invoice is paid/unpaid on Shiprocket?

In Finances >> Invoices, each invoice shows payment status (Paid/Unpaid) and due date for unpaid invoices.

---

## What are debit notes on Shiprocket?

Debit notes are issued for additional charges discovered post-invoice (e.g., weight discrepancy corrections). Added to your outstanding payable amount.

---

## How to reconcile Shiprocket invoices?

1. Download monthly freight invoices from Finances
2. Download passbook report
3. Match invoice amounts with wallet deductions
4. Verify COD credits against COD remittance report
5. Raise disputes for unresolved discrepancies

---

## What is e-invoicing on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket complies with GST e-invoicing requirements. E-invoices with IRN (Invoice Reference Number) are generated for applicable B2B transactions.

---

## How to change billing details on Shiprocket?

1. Go to Account Settings >> Company Details
2. Update company name, address, GSTIN
3. Save changes

Updated details reflect in future invoices.

---

## Does Shiprocket provide TDS certificate?

For TDS deductions on Shiprocket payments, contact Shiprocket's finance team. TDS compliance depends on your business entity type.

---

## What is the minimum amount for e-invoicing on Shiprocket?

E-invoicing threshold follows government GST e-invoicing regulations. Shiprocket generates e-invoices for applicable transactions as per current regulations.

---

## How to get a refund for wrong charges on Shiprocket invoice?

1. Raise a dispute in Finances >> Invoices
2. Provide order details and supporting documents
3. If dispute is valid, Shiprocket issues a credit note
4. Credit is applied to your wallet or next invoice

---

## What is the invoice email address for Shiprocket?

Invoices are sent to the registered email address on your Shiprocket account. Update your email in Account Settings to ensure you receive invoice notifications.

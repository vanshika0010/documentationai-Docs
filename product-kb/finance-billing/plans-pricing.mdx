## What plans does Shiprocket offer

Shiprocket offers multiple plans including Lite, Essential, Pro, and Enterprise. Plans differ in features like number of pickup locations, custom label support, buyer notification customization, and access to premium couriers. Check the Shiprocket website for current pricing.

---

## How to upgrade my Shiprocket plan

Go to Settings >> Plans & Billing from the left menu. Browse available plans and click 'Upgrade' on the plan you want. Complete the payment to activate the new plan.

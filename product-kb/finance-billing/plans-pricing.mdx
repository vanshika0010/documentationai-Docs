## Where can I see the shipping rates or charges for all couriers or a single courier?

We have a rate card that provides the shipping rates of all couriers in one place. Follow these steps to check the shipping rates:
* Navigate to the "Rate Card" option in Settings.
* You will find the rates of all the couriers.
* You can look at the excel sheet and check rates for each courier based on zones, weight slabs.

When the weight discrepancy is resolved, the on-hold charges will automatically be set to zero.

## What do we mean by shipment forward charges and RTO charges?

Forward shipment and RTO charges are incurred on shipments, shipped through different courier partners.
Shipment forward charges: When an order is being shipped from pickup location to the delivery address.
RTO charges: When the order is being returned to the pickup location. The charges are applied for the reverse shipping.

## What is chargeable weight?

Chargeable weight is used to calculate the shipping cost and is calculated as the maximum of the following:
* Actual weight
* Volumetric weight

Volumetric weight = (L x W x H cm) / 5000

---

## What is dead weight?

Dead weight refers to the actual physical weight of the shipment as measured by a scale. It is one of the two weights used to determine the chargeable weight, along with Volumetric weight.

---

## How is Freight Charge calculated on Shiprocket?

Freight Charge = Chargeable Weight x Rate per kg for that zone + Fuel Surcharge + COD charge (if applicable)

Chargeable Weight = Max(Dead weight, Volumetric weight)

---

## What is fuel surcharge on Shiprocket?

Fuel surcharge is an additional charge levied by courier partners over and above the base shipping rate. It is typically a percentage of the base rate and varies by courier. Fuel surcharges are reflected in the shipping rate shown in the Rate Calculator.

---

## How to check if a pincode is serviceable on Shiprocket?

You can check pincode serviceability using the Rate Calculator or Serviceability Checker on the Shiprocket dashboard. Enter the origin and destination pincodes to see which couriers service that route.

---

## What are ODA charges on Shiprocket?

ODA (Out of Delivery Area) charges are additional fees applied for deliveries to remote or difficult-to-access locations. These are charged by courier partners and are reflected in the shipping invoice.

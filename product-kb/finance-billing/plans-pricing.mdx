## Where can I see the shipping rates or charges for all courier partners on Shiprocket?

You can check shipping rates using the Rate Calculator on the Shiprocket dashboard. Go to Dashboard >> Rate Calculator, enter origin pincode, destination pincode, and weight to see rates for all available couriers.

## What are the different plans available on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket offers multiple plans including Lite, Essential, Growth, and Enterprise. Each plan offers different shipping rates, features, and support levels. Check the Shiprocket pricing page or contact your account manager for current plan details.

---

## How to upgrade my Shiprocket plan?

To upgrade your plan:
1. Go to account settings or billing section
2. View available plan options
3. Select the plan you want to upgrade to
4. Complete payment
Alternatively, contact your Shiprocket account manager for plan upgrade assistance.

## Are there any hidden charges on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket's charges include:
- Freight charges (base rate + fuel surcharge + other applicable surcharges)
- COD handling charges (for COD orders)
- Weight discrepancy charges (if courier records higher weight)
- RTO charges (for returned orders)
- Plan subscription fee

All charges are itemized in your monthly freight invoice.

---

## How is the shipping rate calculated on Shiprocket?

Shipping rate = Base rate × Chargeable weight + Fuel surcharge + Other applicable surcharges

Chargeable weight = Higher of (Dead weight vs Volumetric weight)
Volumetric weight = (L × W × H) / 5000

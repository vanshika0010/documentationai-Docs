## Where can I see the shipping rates or charges for all couriers or a single courier?

We have a rate card that provides the shipping rates of all couriers in one place. Follow these steps to check the shipping rates:
* Navigate to the "Rate Card" option in Settings.
* You will find the rates of all the couriers.
* You can look at the excel sheet and check rates for each courier based on zones, weight slabs.

## How to Set On-Hold Charges to Zero

When the weight discrepancy is resolved, the on-hold charges will automatically be set to zero.

## What do we mean by shipment forward charges and RTO charges?

Forward shipment and RTO charges are incurred on shipments, shipped through different courier partners.
Shipment forward charges: When an order is being shipped from pickup location to the delivery address.
RTO charges: When the order is being returned to the pickup location. The charges are applied for the reverse shipping.

---

## Why did you apply additional charges for weight and RTO on my account?

Weight Charges: You will see this charge when there's a difference between the entered and charged weight. Shiprocket charges based on the actual weight reported by the courier.
RTO Charges: RTO charges are applied when an order is returned to the origin. The charge depends on the weight slab and zone.

## What is a UPI mandate?

A UPI mandate lets you set up automatic monthly payments through your UPI app. Once enabled, your subscription fee is auto-deducted on the billing date, so you never miss a payment.

## How do I subscribe to Rs99 or Rs199 plan?

When you're booking a shipment, you'll see an option to Upgrade & Get Discounted Price on the Ship Now page. You can subscribe to the Rs99 or Rs199 plan from there.

## Why are there additional charges for weight and RTO on my account?

* When there is a difference between the weight entered by the seller and the actual weight shared by the courier partner, then the additional weight charges will be applied.
* RTO charges are applied when an order is returned to the origin.

---

## Will I be charged for RTO?

Yes, you will be charged for your RTO shipments. RTO rates depend on your shipment weight and zone. Check the Rate Calculator or your Rate Card in Settings for specific RTO charges.

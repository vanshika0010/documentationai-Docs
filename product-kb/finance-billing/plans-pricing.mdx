<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What plans does Shiprocket offer?">

Shiprocket offers multiple plans including Lite, Essential, Pro, and Enterprise. Each plan differs in features like number of pickup locations, custom label support, buyer notification customization, and access to premium couriers. Check the Shiprocket website for current plan pricing.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to upgrade my Shiprocket plan?">

Go to Settings >> Plans & Billing from the left menu. Browse the available plans and click 'Upgrade' on the plan you want. Complete the payment to activate the new plan.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What is Shiprocket Trends? How does Shiprocket trends help you grow your business?

Shiprocket Trends is a data insights platform that helps e-commerce sellers make smarter business decisions. It shows market-wide trends based on data from Shiprocket's network of 1+ lakh sellers.

Key benefits:
1. Discover trending products before they peak
2. Identify high-demand categories and regions
3. Benchmark your performance against market
4. Use AI-powered insights for product strategy
5. Track seasonal demand patterns

Access at trends.shiprocket.in

---

## What data does Shiprocket Trends show?

Shiprocket Trends shows:
1. Trending products by category
2. Top selling categories nationwide
3. City/state-wise demand patterns
4. Buyer behavior trends
5. Seasonal and event-based demand spikes
6. RTO trends by category
7. Price point analysis

## Is Shiprocket Trends free to use?

Shiprocket Trends offers both free and paid tiers. Basic trend data may be available for free, with advanced analytics and AI features in paid plans. Check trends.shiprocket.in for current pricing.

## Who can use Shiprocket Trends?

It's useful for D2C brands, eCommerce sellers, marketers, analysts, and product managers who want to:
1. Discover what's trending in Indian e-commerce
2. Identify new product opportunities
3. Time product launches with demand peaks
4. Optimize inventory planning
5. Make data-driven business decisions

---

## Does Shiprocket Trends require a Shiprocket account?

A Shiprocket account helps get personalized insights, but the platform may be accessible to non-Shiprocket users for market-level data. Check trends.shiprocket.in for current access requirements.

## Can new sellers use Shiprocket Trends?

Yes. Shiprocket Trends is particularly valuable for new sellers who:
1. Don't have their own historical data yet
2. Need market intelligence to decide what to sell
3. Want to enter a category with confidence
4. Need to understand competition and demand patterns

---

## Is the data on Shiprocket Trends updated in real-time?

Shiprocket Trends data is updated regularly (daily/weekly) based on actual order data from the Shiprocket network. Check the platform for data freshness indicators.

---

## How does Shiprocket Trends help in product selection?

Shiprocket Trends helps in product selection by:
1. Showing which products are gaining order volume
2. Identifying products with rising search/order trends
3. Highlighting emerging categories before they peak
4. Showing demand by geography to target specific markets
5. Revealing price points that are winning

---

## Can I use Trends data for inventory planning?

Yes. Trends seasonal demand charts and category forecasts can directly inform:
1. Pre-season inventory stocking
2. Identifying slow-moving vs fast-moving products
3. Planning promotions around demand peaks
4. Reducing overstock of declining products

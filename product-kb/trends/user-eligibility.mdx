## Who can access Shiprocket Trends

Shiprocket Trends is available to registered Shiprocket sellers. Access may be plan-dependent. Check the Trends section in your Shiprocket panel or contact support to confirm eligibility.

---

## Is Shiprocket Trends free to use

Basic Trends access may be included in certain Shiprocket plans. Advanced features and deeper market intelligence may require a separate subscription. Check the current plan details for Trends access.

## How to search for a specific order in Shiprocket

Use the search bar at the top of the Orders page to search by Order ID, AWB number, customer name, phone number, or email address. You can also use the filter options to narrow results by date range, status, channel, or courier.

---

## How to filter orders by status in Shiprocket

Go to the Orders section and use the status tabs (New Orders, Ready to Ship, In Transit, Delivered, RTO, Cancelled) or use the filter icon to select specific statuses. You can combine multiple filters for an advanced search.

## How to use the Search feature on the orders page?

* To use the Search feature on the Orders page on your Shiprocket account, follow these steps:
   * Go to the Orders tab from your Shiprocket panel.
   * You will find a search bar at the top of the Orders page.
   * You can use this search bar to search orders by: Order ID, AWB Number, Product Name, Channel Order ID, Buyer Name, Buyer Email, Buyer Phone Number.
   * Type in the search term in the search bar and press Enter.
   * The search results will display the matching orders.

---

## What filters are available on the orders page?

Filters available on the Orders page:
1. Order Status (New, Ready to Ship, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled, etc.)
2. Date Range (Created date, Updated date)
3. Sales Channel
4. Courier Partner
5. Payment Mode (COD, Prepaid)
6. Zone
7. Pincode

---

## How to filter orders by status on Shiprocket?

To filter orders by status:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu
2. Click on the status tabs at the top (New, Ready to Ship, Shipped, etc.)
3. Or use the Filter option to select specific statuses
4. The filtered orders will be displayed

---

## How to filter orders by date range?

To filter orders by date range:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu
2. Click on the date filter/date picker
3. Select the start and end date
4. Apply the filter
Orders created/updated within the selected date range will be shown.

---

## How to filter orders by channel on Shiprocket?

To filter orders by channel:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu
2. Click on the Filter option
3. Select the Channel filter
4. Choose the specific channel (Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
5. Apply filter

---

To search by AWB number:

## What is the New orders tab on Shiprocket?

The New orders tab shows all orders that have been received but not yet processed for shipping. These orders need to be assigned a courier and processed for pickup.

---

## What is the Ready to Ship tab on Shiprocket?

The Ready to Ship tab shows orders that have been processed, labels generated, and are waiting for courier pickup. These orders are ready for dispatch.

---

## What is the Shipped tab on Shiprocket?

The Shipped tab shows orders that have been picked up by the courier and are currently in transit to the delivery address.

---

## What is the Delivered tab on Shiprocket?

The Delivered tab shows orders that have been successfully delivered to the buyers.

---

## What is the Cancelled tab on Shiprocket?

The Cancelled tab shows orders that have been cancelled either by the buyer, seller, or due to other reasons.

---

## What is the NDR/Failed Delivery tab on Shiprocket?

The NDR tab (also called Delivery Boost) shows orders where delivery attempts failed. You can take action on these orders (reattempt or RTO) from this tab.

---

## What is the RTO tab on Shiprocket?

The RTO tab shows orders that are being returned to origin (seller's pickup address) after failed delivery attempts.

---

## How to find orders with pending invoices on Shiprocket?

To find orders with pending invoice issues:

## How to export/download orders list from Shiprocket?

To export orders:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu

## What is the difference between Order ID and Channel Order ID on Shiprocket?

Order ID: The unique identifier assigned by Shiprocket to each order.

Channel Order ID: The original order ID from the sales channel (e.g., Amazon Order ID, Shopify Order ID). Both can be used to search for orders on Shiprocket.

## What does Order Sync mean on Shiprocket?

Order Sync is the process of importing orders from connected sales channels (Shopify, Amazon, etc.) into your Shiprocket account. Orders sync automatically when channels are connected, or can be triggered manually.

---

## How to find orders with failed sync on Shiprocket?

Orders that failed to sync appear in the Unprocessable Orders tab. Common reasons: missing product details, invalid pincode, missing buyer information.

## What is the difference between the orders page and the shipments page on Shiprocket?

Orders page: Shows all orders including unprocessed ones. Allows order management before shipping.

Shipments page: Shows only orders that have been assigned AWBs and processed for shipping.

## Can I assign two awbs on one order? Can I ship two boxes or packages for one order?

Yes, this is possible through the Multi Packet Shipment (MPS) feature. With MPS:
1. Each box gets a separate AWB linked to the parent order
2. All boxes are picked up together
3. Total weight = sum of all individual package weights
4. Contact Shiprocket support or your account manager to enable MPS for your account.

## What is the difference between an order and a shipment on Shiprocket?

Order: The customer's purchase request including product details, buyer info, and payment mode.

Shipment: The physical package assigned an AWB number for courier pickup and delivery. An order can have one or multiple shipments (via MPS).

## How do I process or ship my orders in bulk?

To process orders in bulk on Shiprocket:
1. Go to Orders >> New tab
2. Select the orders you want to process using the checkboxes
3. Click on 'Ship' or 'Process' from the bulk action options
4. The system will automatically select the best courier based on your configured rules, or you can manually select
5. Review and confirm
6. Labels will be generated for all selected orders
7. Download the bulk label PDF for printing

You can also use the Bulk Upload CSV method to process orders created outside the panel.

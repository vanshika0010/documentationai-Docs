## I am not able to hide my email from invoice

To hide your email from the invoice:
1. Go to Settings >> Company Setup >> Billing, Invoice & GSTIN
2. Look for invoice customization options
3. Toggle off the email visibility option on invoice
4. Save settings

If the option is not available, raise a support ticket for assistance.

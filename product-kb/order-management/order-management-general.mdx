## What are the different order statuses in Shiprocket

Key order statuses:

* New
* Ready to Ship (AWB assigned)
* Pickup Scheduled
* Pickup Pending
* In Transit
* Out for Delivery
* Delivered
* RTO Initiated
* RTO In Transit
* RTO Delivered
* Cancelled
* Lost

## Can I get delivery person's contact number?

Shiprocket does not provide the delivery agent's personal contact number directly to sellers for privacy reasons. However:
1. The buyer can communicate with the delivery agent when they're out for delivery
2. If there's a delivery issue, raise an NDR or contact Shiprocket support
3. Some couriers provide their local hub contact number on the tracking page

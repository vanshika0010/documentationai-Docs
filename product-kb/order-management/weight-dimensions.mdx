## What are the conditions for Multi Packet Shipment (MPS)?

The conditions for MPS are as follows:
1. If MPS is enabled in your account, you can ship multiple packets/boxes under a single order with separate labels for each box.
2. Each packet is assigned a unique AWB linked to the parent order.
3. All packets must be picked up together.
4. Total weight is the sum of all individual packet weights.

---

## How to enable Multi Packet Shipment (MPS) on Shiprocket?

To enable MPS:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager or raise a support ticket
2. MPS needs to be enabled for your account
3. Once enabled, you can add multiple packets when creating an order

## How is chargeable weight calculated on Shiprocket?

Chargeable weight = Maximum of (Dead Weight, Volumetric Weight)

Volumetric Weight = (Length cm x Width cm x Height cm) / 5000

If volumetric weight > dead weight, volumetric weight is charged. Otherwise, dead weight is charged.

---

## What is volumetric weight on Shiprocket?

Volumetric weight is calculated based on the package dimensions:
Volumetric Weight = (L x W x H) / 5000 (in kg)

This is used when the package is large but light, to charge based on the space it occupies.

---

## How to avoid weight discrepancy charges on Shiprocket?

To minimize weight discrepancy charges:
1. Enter accurate weight and dimensions when creating orders
2. Use a calibrated weighing scale
3. Measure dimensions precisely (L x W x H)
4. Include packaging weight in your measurement
5. If you consistently face discrepancies, review your measurement methodology

---

## What is the weight discrepancy process on Shiprocket?

When a courier reports a different weight than what was entered:
1. Shiprocket receives the courier's weight update
2. Weight discrepancy charge is applied (difference in freight)
3. You receive a notification about the discrepancy
4. You can accept or dispute the discrepancy with proof
5. Upload proof (photo of package on weighing scale with AWB) to dispute

## Why are correct weight and dimensions important

Shipping charges use whichever is higher — actual weight or volumetric weight.

**Volumetric weight** = (Length cm × Breadth cm × Height cm) ÷ 5000

Incorrect values lead to weight discrepancy charges from couriers. Always enter accurate measurements.

---

## What happens if I enter wrong weight or dimensions

If the courier measures a different weight or volumetric weight than what you declared, you will be charged the difference as a weight discrepancy. You can dispute this from the Weight Reconciliation section.

## How to create an order manually on Shiprocket

Go to Orders >> New Orders and click 'Create Order.' Fill in:

* Customer details (name, phone, address, pincode)
* Product details (name, SKU, quantity, price)
* Package details (weight, dimensions)
* Payment mode (COD or Prepaid)

Click 'Create Order' to save.

---

## What is the minimum information required to create an order

Minimum required: customer name, phone number, delivery address, pincode, city, state, at least one product (name and price), package weight, and payment type (COD or Prepaid).

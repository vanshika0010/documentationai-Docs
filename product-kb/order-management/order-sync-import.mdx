## How does Sync order work?

* If your sales channel is integrated with Shiprocket, your orders will automatically sync with Shiprocket. You can also manually sync orders by clicking on the 'Sync Orders' button on the Orders page.
* If your sales channel is not integrated with Shiprocket, you can import orders using a CSV file. Click on 'Import Orders' on the Orders page and upload your CSV file.

---

## How to import orders via CSV on Shiprocket?

To import orders via CSV:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu
2. Click on 'Import Orders'
3. Download the CSV template
4. Fill in the order details in the template
5. Upload the completed CSV
6. Review the import summary
7. Confirm import

Make sure all required fields are filled correctly to avoid import errors.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to upload orders in bulk on Shiprocket?">

Go to Orders >> New Orders and click 'Import.' Download the sample template, fill in order details, and upload the CSV. Orders appear in the New Orders section. Maximum 5,000 orders per file.

## What are unprocessable orders in Shiprocket

Unprocessable orders are orders that cannot be processed due to missing or incorrect information such as incomplete address, invalid pincode, missing product weight/dimensions, or zero order value. They appear in the Unprocessable Orders section and require correction before they can be shipped.

---

## How to fix unprocessable orders

Go to New Orders >> Unprocessable Orders. Click on the order to see the error reason. Edit the required fields (address, weight, dimensions, etc.) and save. The order will move to the processable queue.

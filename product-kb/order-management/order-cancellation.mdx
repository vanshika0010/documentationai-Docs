## How to cancel an order in Shiprocket

Go to the order details and click 'Cancel Order.' Provide a cancellation reason and confirm. Orders already picked up by the courier cannot be cancelled — initiate RTO instead.

---

## Can I cancel a shipped order

Once an order is picked up by the courier, you cannot cancel it from the Shiprocket panel. You can raise a support ticket to request the courier to RTO the shipment, but this is not guaranteed.

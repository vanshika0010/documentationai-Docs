## what to add in order ID and channel ID on bulk upload csv or excel file?

For bulk upload CSV:
* Order ID: Enter your own unique order identifier (from your system/platform). This will be the reference ID in Shiprocket.
* Channel ID: Enter the Channel ID of the sales channel you're uploading orders for. Get your Channel ID from Settings >> Channels in the Shiprocket panel.

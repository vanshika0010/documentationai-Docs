## How to update the delivery address for an order

For orders that haven't been assigned a courier yet, click 'Edit Order' in the order details to update the delivery address. For orders with an AWB already generated, contact Shiprocket support — courier coordination is required.

---

## How to update the buyer's phone number on an order

Go to the order details page, click 'Edit Order,' update the phone number, and save. This is only possible before the order is assigned to a courier.

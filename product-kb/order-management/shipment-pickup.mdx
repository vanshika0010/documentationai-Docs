## How to schedule a pickup for my orders

After assigning a courier (AWB generation), go to Ready to Ship. Select orders and click 'Generate Pickup.' Choose pickup address, expected number of packages, and date. The courier will arrive on the scheduled date.

---

## My pickup was not done. What should I do

If the courier didn't come for pickup on the scheduled date, try rescheduling the pickup from the Ready to Ship section. If the issue persists, raise a support ticket with the pickup request details.

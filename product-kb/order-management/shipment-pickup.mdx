## How to check delayed order?

To check delayed orders on Shiprocket:
1. Go to Orders from the left menu
2. Click on Shipped tab
3. Use filters to find orders that are past expected delivery date
4. Alternatively, go to Dashboard >> Reports for delay analysis

---

## How to schedule a pickup for my orders on Shiprocket?

To schedule pickup:
1. Process your orders (assign courier, generate labels)
2. Orders move to Ready to Ship tab
3. Click 'Request Pickup' or it may be scheduled automatically
4. The courier will collect the orders from your pickup address on the scheduled date/time

---

## What is the pickup cutoff time on Shiprocket?

Pickup cutoff time varies by:
* Courier partner
* Your location/pickup address pincode
* Day of the week

Orders processed before the cutoff get same-day pickup. After cutoff, pickup is scheduled for the next business day. Check courier-specific cutoff times in the Rate Calculator.

---

## How to add a new pickup address on Shiprocket?

To add a pickup address:
1. Go to Settings >> Manage Warehouses
2. Click 'Add New Warehouse'
3. Fill in the warehouse details (name, address, pincode, contact)
4. Set it as primary if needed
5. Save

---

## How to change the pickup address for an order on Shiprocket?

To change pickup address:
1. Find the order in New or Ready to Ship tab
2. Click on the order or the three dots menu
3. Select 'Edit Order'
4. Update the pickup address
5. Save

This is only possible before the order is picked up.

---

## How to track a shipment on Shiprocket?

To track a shipment:
1. Find the order in Orders tab
2. Click on the AWB number or tracking icon
3. You'll see the real-time tracking status
4. You can also track directly on the courier's website using the AWB number

---

## What does 'Pickup Scheduled' status mean on Shiprocket?

Pickup Scheduled means a pickup request has been sent to the courier and they have confirmed the pickup for a specific date/time slot.

---

## What does 'Pickup Awaited' status mean on Shiprocket?

Pickup Awaited means the order has been processed and is ready for pickup, but the courier hasn't picked it up yet.

---

## What should I do if the courier doesn't pick up my order?

If the courier doesn't pick up on the scheduled date:
1. Wait until end of business day
2. Raise a support ticket with the AWB number and pickup date
3. Shiprocket will coordinate with the courier for the missed pickup

---

## How to cancel a pickup request on Shiprocket?

To cancel a pickup request:
1. Contact Shiprocket support immediately
2. Provide the AWB number and reason for cancellation
3. If pickup hasn't happened yet, it can usually be cancelled

---

## What is the difference between Pickup and Drop shipment on Shiprocket?

Pickup: Courier comes to your address to collect the shipment. Standard method.

Drop/Self Drop: You drop the shipment at the courier's facility. Available with some couriers for specific locations.

---

## How to enable drop shipping (self-drop) on Shiprocket?

Self-drop/drop shipping at courier facility is available for specific couriers. Contact Shiprocket support to check availability and enable this option for your account.

---

## What is the manifest in Shiprocket?

A manifest is a document listing all orders scheduled for courier pickup on a specific date. It includes order IDs, AWBs, product details, and buyer information. The courier agent signs the manifest upon pickup.

---

## How to generate a manifest for pickup on Shiprocket?

To generate a manifest:
1. Go to Orders >> Ready to Ship tab
2. Select orders for pickup
3. Click 'Print Manifest' or 'Generate Manifest'
4. Download and print the manifest
5. Hand over to the courier agent during pickup

---

## What is the 'In Transit' status on Shiprocket?

In Transit means the shipment has been picked up by the courier and is on its way to the delivery address. The courier is transporting the package through their network.

---

## How to handle shipments that are stuck in transit on Shiprocket?

For shipments stuck in transit:
1. Check the last tracking update
2. If no update for 3+ days, raise a support ticket
3. Provide AWB number and last tracking location
4. Shiprocket will investigate with the courier

---

## How to change the courier after an order is processed on Shiprocket?

Courier reassignment after processing:
1. The order must not have been picked up yet
2. Go to the specific order
3. Click on 'Reassign Courier' option
4. Select the new courier
5. Confirm

Note: A new label will be generated with the new courier's AWB.

---

## What happens if the courier loses my shipment?

If a shipment is lost:
1. Raise a support ticket with the AWB number
2. Shiprocket will file a claim with the courier
3. If the shipment is insured (Shiprocket Secure), you can claim the insured value
4. Uninsured shipments are subject to the courier's liability limits

---

## How to enable shipment insurance on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket Secure is the shipment insurance feature:
1. Go to Settings >> Shiprocket Secure or Value Added Services
2. Enable Shiprocket Secure
3. Pay the insurance premium (% of order value)
4. Shipments are covered against loss and damage

---

## What is the Expected Delivery Date (EDD) on Shiprocket?

EDD (Expected Delivery Date) is the estimated date by which the shipment will be delivered. It's calculated based on:
* Origin and destination pincodes
* Courier partner's transit time
* Order processing date

EDD is shown in the order details and can be displayed to buyers on the tracking page.

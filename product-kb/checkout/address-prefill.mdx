## How does Shiprocket Checkout prefill addresses?

Shiprocket has delivered orders to over 25 Cr. customers so far. We prefill the addresses on the basis of our past delivery data for all the orders we have shipped to the customers. When a customer verifies their phone number at checkout, their address is automatically fetched from Shiprocket's database based on past deliveries. This makes the checkout process quick and hassle-free.

## Can OTP verification step be eliminated from the shiprocket checkout?

OTP verification helps us pre-fill the address based on the customer mobile number. If OTP step is removed, address pre-fill can't be done and we will not be able to provide the strong address intelligence and ease of one click checkout can't be provided.

---

## Do I only get delivered addresses on Address prefil on Shiprocket checkout?

Yes, only delivered addresses are pre-filled by shiprocket checkout.

## Can I collect the exit responses on the checkout when the customer closes the checkout page?

Yes, exit responses can be collected on the checkout when the customer exitst the checkout. This helps in collecting the feedback from the customer when they abandon the checkout and the complete data is available on the checkout dashboard to review this data so that those customers can be converted later by addressing their concerns as per the feedback received.

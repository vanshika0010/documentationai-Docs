<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Shiprocket Checkout prefill addresses?">

Shiprocket has delivered orders to over 25 crore customers. We prefill addresses based on past delivery data. When a customer verifies their phone number at checkout, their address is automatically fetched from Shiprocket's database based on past deliveries.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can OTP verification be eliminated from Shiprocket Checkout?">

OTP verification helps pre-fill the address based on the customer's mobile number. If OTP is removed, address pre-fill can't be done and one-click checkout can't be provided.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the different apps listed on Shopify by Shiprocket Checkout?">

Shiprocket checkout has two apps on Shopify: Shiprocket Smart COD (helps set custom rules to block COD and set shipping visibility) and Shiprocket Smart Address (helps pre-fill addresses with OTP verification to increase conversion by reducing checkout time).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

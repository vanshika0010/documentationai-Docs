## How does Shiprocket Checkout prefill addresses

Shiprocket has delivered orders to over 25 crore customers. When a customer verifies their phone number at checkout, their address is automatically fetched from Shiprocket's database based on past deliveries, making checkout quick and hassle-free.

---

## Can OTP verification be eliminated from the Shiprocket Checkout

OTP verification is what enables address pre-fill based on the customer's mobile number. If the OTP step is removed, address pre-fill and one-click checkout cannot be provided.

---

## What are the different apps listed on Shopify by Shiprocket Checkout

Shiprocket Checkout has two apps on Shopify:

* **Shiprocket: Smart COD** — helps set custom rules to block COD and set shipping visibility on Shopify checkout.
* **Shiprocket: Smart Address** — helps pre-fill addresses with OTP verification to increase conversion by reducing checkout time.

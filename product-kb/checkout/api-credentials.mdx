## Why are abandoned carts not being pushed to my Shopify store? What could be the reason?

If abandoned carts are not being pushed to your Shopify store, here are some possible reasons:
1. The Shopify integration is not configured correctly. Please check the integration settings in the checkout dashboard.
2. The webhook for abandoned cart is not configured correctly. Please check the webhook settings in the checkout dashboard.
3. The checkout session is not being abandoned correctly.
4. There could be a sync issue between Shiprocket Checkout and Shopify.

Please reach out to the checkout support team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for further assistance.

## How can I block high risk RTO customers from placing a COD order on Checkout?

Shiprocket checkout provides a feature to block high risk RTO customers from placing a COD order. Follow the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "Disable COD" as the rule type.
3. Choose criteria from a list of parameters like invoice value, product sku, destination pincodes, customer RTO history, UTM parameters etc.
4. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next".
5. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

## Can I set free shipping on Checkout based on certain conditions?

Yes, you can set free shipping on Checkout based on conditions. Follow the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Click on Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "Shipping charges" as the rule type.
3. Choose criteria from a list of parameters like invoice value, product sku, destination pincodes, UTM sources, customer type, etc.
4. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and set the shipping charges as 0 (free shipping).
5. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

## Can shiprocket checkout show EDD (Estimated Delivery Date) on the checkout page?

Yes, Shiprocket checkout shows Estimated delivery date on the checkout to provide the customers with the estimated delivery date. The delivery date is calculated based on pickup pincode and destination pincode. It takes into account the dispatch time for the order as well.

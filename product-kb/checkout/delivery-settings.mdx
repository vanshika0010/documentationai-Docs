## How does Shiprocket Checkout calculate and show Delivery Date

The delivery date is calculated based on pickup pincode, destination pincode, order dispatch time, and the courier partners configured on the seller dashboard.

---

## Can I show EDD in days instead of a specific date

Yes, EDD can be shown in either days or a specific date format based on your requirement.

---

## How to set shipping charges for different delivery types in checkout

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings. Scroll to shipping charges and fill in fixed amounts for Rush, Express, and Standard delivery:

* Rush: EDD ≤ 3 days
* Express: EDD ≤ 6 days
* Standard: EDD > 6 days

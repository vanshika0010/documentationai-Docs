<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Shiprocket Checkout show Delivery Date on Checkout?">

The delivery date is calculated based on pickup pincode, destination pincode, dispatch time, and the courier partners configured on your seller dashboard.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I disable delivery date from Shiprocket Checkout and show it in days instead?">

Yes, EDD can be shown in both days or date format based on your requirement.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to set shipping charges for different delivery types in checkout?">

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings from the left menu, scroll to shipping charges and fill in fixed amounts for Rush, Express, and Standard delivery. Rush is EDD ≤ 3 days, Express is EDD ≤ 6 days, Standard is EDD > 6 days.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

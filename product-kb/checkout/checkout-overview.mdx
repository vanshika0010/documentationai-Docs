<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Checkout?">

Shiprocket Checkout refers to the process of completing an order on an ecommerce platform. It is designed to make the checkout process smooth and easier, reducing checkout time by pre-filling the address based on the customer's phone number and showcasing all details on a single page.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the address prefill rate on checkout?">

Shiprocket Checkout address prefill rate is approximately 85%. Shiprocket has been in the delivery system for over 11 years, currently delivering 2 crore orders per month.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Fastrr?">

Fastrr is now called Shiprocket Checkout.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

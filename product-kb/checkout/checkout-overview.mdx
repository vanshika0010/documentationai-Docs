## What is Checkout

Shiprocket Checkout makes the purchase completion process smooth and fast by pre-filling the customer's address based on their phone number and presenting all details on a single page, resulting in an increased conversion rate.

---

## What is the address prefill rate on checkout

Shiprocket Checkout's address prefill rate is approximately 85%. Shiprocket has been in the delivery system for over 11 years, currently delivering 2 crore orders per month.

---

## What is Fastrr

Fastrr is now called Shiprocket Checkout.

---

## What is the billing cycle for Shiprocket Checkout services

Monthly invoices are generated for the checkout services on the Shiprocket dashboard and can be downloaded from the invoice section.

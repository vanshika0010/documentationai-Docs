## What is Checkout?

Shiprocket Checkout refers to the process of completing an order on an ecommerce platform. It is designed to make the checkout process smooth and easier, reducing the checkout time by pre-filling the address based on the customer's phone number and showcasing all details on a single page. This results in an increased conversion rate.

---

## What is the address prefill rate on checkout?

Shiprocket Checkout address prefill rate is approximately 85%. Shiprocket has been in the delivery system for the past 11 years currently, delivering 2cr. order per month. This extensive database enables us to accurately prefill addresses for most of the customers.

---

## What is the criteria to mark a customer as high RTO on checkout?

Shiprocket checkout takes into account a number of factors like destination pincode, past RTO history, etc which helps in predicting the chances of RTO by the customer based on which customer profiling is done on shiprocket

## What is the alternate solution to COD form offered by shopify on Shiprocket Checkout?

Shiprocket offers a checkout by the name “OTPless Checkout” which is an alternate to COD form for Shopify websites. Multiple features are available with this checkout like:
Single-sign on (SSO): This helps in pre-filling the address on the checkout.
Estimated Delivery Date (EDD): Accurate delivery date is shown on the checkout.
Checkout customisation: Checkout can be customised with the theme color of the website to make it more attractive.

## What is the billing cycle for shiprocket checkout services?

Monthly invoices are generated for the checkout services on the shiprocket dashboard and can be downloaded from the invoice section in shiprocket dashboard.

## What is the use of the Reports section on the checkout dashboard?

The Reports section on Checkout dashboard allows you to download various reports, such as order details, refunds, and checkout funnel performance. These reports can be sent via email to designated users. Follow these steps to generate a report:

* Go to Checkout Dashboard → Reports → Generate New Report
* Select the desired report type and specify the email recipient.
* Select the date range for which data has been requested.

Note: Reports can only be sent to users registered in the User Management section of the Checkout Dashboard.

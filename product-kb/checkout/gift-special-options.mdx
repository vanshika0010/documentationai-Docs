## How can I add a gift card option on Checkout

Shopify gift cards are supported on Shiprocket Checkout. Email checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com with your domain name to activate. The team will activate it within 12–24 working hours, and the gift card option will appear above the payment options on checkout.

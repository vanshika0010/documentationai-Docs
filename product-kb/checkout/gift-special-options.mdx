  <Accordion title="How can I add a gift card option on Checkout?">

Shopify gift cards are supported on Shiprocket Checkout. Email checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com with your domain name to activate gift cards. The team will activate it within 12-24 working hours, and the gift card option will appear above payment options on the checkout.

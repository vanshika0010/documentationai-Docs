## How is Shiprocket Checkout billing calculated

Shiprocket Checkout charges a small percentage commission on the invoice value of each order placed through it. No onboarding or integration fees are charged. Monthly invoices are available for download on the Shiprocket dashboard.

---

## Where can I view my Checkout invoices

Monthly invoices for Checkout services can be downloaded from the invoice section in the Shiprocket dashboard.

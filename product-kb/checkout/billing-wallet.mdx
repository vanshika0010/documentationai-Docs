## I want to charge advance payment on COD orders on checkout? How to enable that?

Yes, you can enable partial COD or advance payment collection on shiprocket checkout. You need to contact the checkout support team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com to enable this feature on checkout.

## How can I enable dynamic pricing on checkout based on customer type?

Dynamic pricing can be configured on the checkout based on customer type (new vs repeat customers) by following below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select the rule type from the list of rules like Prepaid Discount, COD charges, etc.
3. Choose "Customer type" as criteria under the rule type selected.
4. Select the customer type you want to apply the rule for - New Customer or Repeat Customer.
5. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the discount value for the selected customer type.
6. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

## How will I be charged for Shiprocket Checkout? How does billing work?

Shiprocket checkout doesn't charge any onboarding and integration fee on its platform. It charges a small percentage commission on the invoice value of the order placed through it. Monthly invoices are generated for the checkout services on the shiprocket dashboard and can be downloaded from the invoice section in shiprocket dashboard.

## How can I view the payment success rate on my checkout?

You can view the payment success rate on your checkout by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Analytics from the left menu.
* Click on the "Payment" tab to view the payment success rate on your checkout.

## Can I charge different shipping fees for different products or categories on checkout?

Yes, you can charge different shipping fees for different products or categories on checkout. Follow the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Click on Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "Shipping charges" as the rule type.
3. Choose "Product SKU" or "Product Collection" as criteria.
4. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the shipping charges for the selected products or categories.
5. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

---

## How to create cash on delivery (COD) discount / incentive to convert COD to prepaid orders?

You can create COD to prepaid incentives on the checkout to convert COD orders to prepaid by following below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "COD to prepaid discount" as the rule type.
3. Enter the flat or percentage discount for converting COD to prepaid.
4. Click on "Next" and click on "Save" to save the rule.

## How to add more users to the checkout dashboard?

You can add more users to the checkout dashboard by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Settings >> User Management from the left menu.
* Click on the "Add User" button.
* Enter the user's email address and select the role for the user.
* Click on "Add User" to add the user to the checkout dashboard.

## I want to offer freebie gifts on prepaid orders from checkout. How can I do that?

You can offer freebie gifts on prepaid orders by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Discounts >> Create >> Buy X Get Y.
2. Select Automatic discount method.
3. Under Discount Configuration, select "Get Products for Free" as discount type.
4. Configure the criteria - select "Payment Mode" and choose "Prepaid" payment methods only.
5. Set the free product under "Y" section.
6. Click on Save to save the discount.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is Shiprocket Checkout billing calculated?">

Shiprocket Checkout charges a small percentage commission on the invoice value of each order placed through it. No onboarding or integration fees are charged. Monthly invoices are generated and available for download on the Shiprocket dashboard.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I view my Checkout invoices?">

Monthly invoices for Checkout services can be downloaded from the invoice section in the Shiprocket dashboard.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

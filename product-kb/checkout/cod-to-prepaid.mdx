<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Shiprocket Checkout reduce RTO for a website/store?">

Shiprocket Checkout helps reduce RTO via: OTP verification, ability to disable COD based on customer risk profile, COD charges to incentivize prepaid, prepaid discounts, and prepaid-only automatic and coupon-based discounts.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is COD to Prepaid (C2P) and Aftersell feature available on WooCommerce?">

No, currently these features are only available with the Shopify platform.

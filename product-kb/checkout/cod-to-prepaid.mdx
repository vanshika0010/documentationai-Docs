## How does Shiprocket Checkout reduce RTO for a website/store

Shiprocket Checkout helps reduce RTO through:

1. OTP verification — to verify users based on phone number.
2. Disable COD — based on customer risk profile.
3. COD Charges — to incentivise prepaid orders.
4. Prepaid Discounts — to decrease COD orders.
5. Prepaid Only Discounts — automatic and coupon-based.

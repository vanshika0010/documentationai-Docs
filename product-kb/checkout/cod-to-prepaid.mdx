## How does shiprocket checkout reduce RTO for a website/store?

Shiprocket checkout helps you reduce RTO in many ways:
1. OTP verification - To verify users based on phone number
2. Disable COD - Based on customer risk profile and offers prepaid payment methods only
3. COD Charges - To incentivise customers on prepaid orders by charging extra on COD orders.
4. Prepaid Discounts - Offer prepaid discounts to decrease COD orders.
5. Prepaid Only Discounts: Offer prepaid only automatic and coupon based discounts on the checkout

---

## Will shiprocket checkout charge for both COD and Prepaid order if customer has paid through COD to Prepaid feature after placing a COD order on thank you page of the shopify?

No. shiprocket checkout will only charge for prepaid order if customer pays on thank you page using COD to Prepaid (C2P) feature and COD order will be cancelled on shopify.

---

## Is COD to Prepaid (C2P) and Aftersell feature available on woocommerce in Shiprocket Checkout?

No, currently these features are only available with shopify platform.

---

## How can I increase my checkout conversion using Shiprocket Checkout?

Shiprocket checkout helps in increasing the conversion as it makes the checkout process smooth and easier by reducing the checkout time by pre-filling the address based on customer phone number and its 1-page checkout showcases all details on a single page, thus, increasing the conversion.

---

## How to add COD to prepaid discount on my orders from checkout?

You can add COD to prepaid discount to help you convert your COD orders to prepaid on Shiprocket Checkout. To add COD to prepaid discount, follow these steps,
* Go to Settings > Shipping Settings from the left menu.
* Write a fixed or percentage discount under COD to prepaid discount.

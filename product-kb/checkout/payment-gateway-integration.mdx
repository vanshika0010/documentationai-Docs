## Does Shiprocket Checkout support all payment modes

Yes. Shiprocket Checkout supports UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Netbanking, Wallets, BNPL, and EMI options.

---

## Does Shiprocket Checkout have a payment gateway switch option for downtime

Yes. If one payment gateway is facing downtime, Shiprocket Checkout automatically switches to the secondary gateway so customers can complete checkout hassle-free.

---

## Does Shiprocket Checkout support Paytm or CCAvenue payment gateways

No, Shiprocket Checkout doesn't support Paytm or CCAvenue payment gateways.

---

## How to create a discount on checkout

Go to Settings >> Discounts >> Create. Choose your discount type (Amount off Products, Amount off Cart, Tiered Discount, Buy X Get Y, Bundle, Upselling, Bank Offers) and method (Automatic or Coupon Code). Fill in configuration, criteria, functionality, and validity details. Once created, enable or disable from the All Discounts section.

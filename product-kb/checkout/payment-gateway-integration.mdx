## Does Shiprocket Checkout support all payment modes?

Yes, Shiprocket Checkout support all payment modes. UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Netbanking, Wallets, BNPL and EMI options.

## I don’t have a payment gateway. Can I still be onboarded on checkout?

Yes, you can be onboarded on Shiprocket Checkout. We will help you get onboarded on our payment partner Billdesk and Easebuzz.

## Does shiprocket checkout has payment gateway switch option in case of a downtime of a payment gateway?

Yes, shiprocket checkout provides payment gateway switch option if one payment gateway is facing downtime and customer can complete the checkout hassle free.

## What happens if OTPs are not getting delivered on time in checkout?

OTP's are triggered on the SMS once a customer enters their phone number and if no OTP is received for 30 seconds, the customer can again ask for an OTP on SMS or WhatsApp to login. Also, a proper mechanism is setup for the OTP with multiple service providers where if OTP is not being delivered, it automatically switches to the next service provider.

## How do I configure bank offers on Shiprocket Checkout?

Bank offers can be configured on the shiprocket checkout by creating a bank offer discount on the dashboard. The steps are as follows:-
1. Create the bank offers on the payment gateway dashboard.
2. Now, using PG Discount ID created on the PG dashboard for each bank offer, create a bank offer discount on the shiprocket dashboard.
3. The discount can be made for cards, netbanking, wallets & EMI payment options for specific banks as well.

## How shiprocket checkout helps in increasing the prepaid orders for my website?

Shiprocket checkout helps in increasing the prepaid orders by various methods:-
1. Prepaid only discount : Discounts can be offered only on prepaid methods which will increase prepaid orders as customers get additional benefit on paying online.
2. Additional COD charges : COD charges can be configured on the checkout which will help us charge more on COD orders and customers can get additonal benefit on paying online.
3. UPI 1st Checkout: Checkout is designed in such a way that it promotes UPI 1st payment method and payments can be done easily with UPI method as UPI apps are available on the checkout only so that customer can select and are re-directed to the payment apps for payments.
4. Payment Layout: The checkouts payment layout is designed in such a way that it shows the clear difference of prepaid and cod amounts, thus, helping customers choose prepaid payment method with clear indication of the amount saved by paying only.
5. COD to Prepaid: Once customer has placed COD order, we can again convert the customer to prepaid order by showing the amount that can be saved if online payment is done and customer can pay now, which helps in converting COD customer to prepaid on the thank you page.
6. Prepaid only Freebie: Offer freebie gifts on prepaid payment methods only to promote prepaid orders and discourage customers from selecting COD method.

## How to enable UPI payment on Shiprocket Checkout?

Before activating the UPI on checkout, make sure UPI payment option is activated by the payment gateway which is processing the payments on the checkout. Once active, the UPI can be enabled on the checkout by following the below steps:
Checkout Dashboard >> Settings >> Payment >> Add new gateway >> Select Payment Gateway >> Select UPI

## How to create a discount on checkout?

To create a discount on Shiprocket Checkout, follow these steps:
* Go to settings from the left menu.
* Click on Discounts.
* Click on Create and select your discount type and discount method. These are discount types available to choose from-
** Amount of products - Discount offering fixed or percenatge amount discount on the price of specific product or collections.
** Amount off cart - Discount that applies a fixed or percenatge amount off the total value of items in the shopping cart.
** Tiered Discount - Discount configuration where the percentage or amount off increases based on the total purchase value or quantity of items.
** Buy X get Y - Promotional offer where customers receive a free or discounted product (Y) when they purchase products of certain amount or a certain quantity of another product (X).
** Bundle - A discount applied when multiple products are purchased together as a bundle or set, usually at a reduced price.
** Upselling - A strategy offering discounts on higher-priced products to encourage customers to spend more than they originally intended.
** Bank Offers - Special discounts or cashback promotions provided in partnership with banks, typically tied to credit card or payment gateway usage.
* Based on the type of discount and method you select, fill in the remaining details for configuration, criteria, functionality and discount validity.
* Once created, your discount will reflect in the All discounts section. You can enable or disable the discount or edit the details if you want.

## how to change the conditions for prepaid discount on shiprocket checkout?

Prepaid discount can be made based on multiple criteria's like invoice value, destination pincode, product sku, etc. You can setup prepaid discount by following below mentioned simple steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to "Settings" from the left navigation bar >> Shipping Settings >> Custom Rules >> Create Rule >> Prepaid Discount
2. Choose criteria from a list of parameters like invoice value, product sku, product collection, destination pincodes, etc on which you want to give prepaid discount.
3. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the "Flat discount" or "Percentage discount" for different prepaid options available on the checkout.
4. Click on "Apply max value" if you wish to give maximum discount when both "Flat discount" and "Percentage discount" values are configured on the checkout.
5. Click on "Next" and prepaid discount is created on your checkout.

## Can I change delivery time for different delivery methods on shiprocket checkout?

Shiprocket checkout helps in showing different delivery methods like Rush, Express & Standard on the checkout. Each delivery method has its own delivery TAT:
1. Rush Delivery: Delivery TAT is less than 2 days.
2. Express Delivery: Delivery TAT varies from 2 to 5 days.
3. Standard Delivery: Delivery TAT is greater than 5 days.

You can setup the pickup dispatch time on the checkout dashboard by following the below steps:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Default Values
2. In default values, you will find a section of "Pickup Configuration".
3. You can configure the "Pickup Configuration" based on "Cut-off time" and add pickup time for both "TAT after cut-off time" and "TAT before cut-off time".
4. Once you enter the values, scroll down the page and click on the "Save and proceed" button.

## my UPI payment is not working on shiprocket checkout, what could be the issue?

There could be multiple reasons for UPI not working on the checkout and some of the reasons are:
1. Wrong credentials are updated on the checkout dashboard for the payment gateway.
2. UPI services are not activated by the payment gateway for your account.
3. Currently, payment gateway is facing downtime and UPI services are not available.

These are some of the possible reasons for UPI failure with other reasons as well.

## Why is the prepaid option not showing at Checkout?

If prepaid option is not shown on the checkout, it could be due to multiple reasons like:
1. Payment gateway is not configured in payments section on checkout dashboard.
   To check this, Open checkout dashboard >> Settings >> Payment
2. Wrong credentials has been updated for the payment gateway.
3. Payment gateway is throwing error while loading the prepaid options on the checkout.

Please reachout to Checkout support team for more details related to payment gateway.

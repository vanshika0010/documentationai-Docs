## Which bundle app is compatible with shiprocket checkout?

Shiprocket checkout is compatible with Frequently Bought Together (FBT) and other bundle apps available on Shopify. For specific app compatibility, please reach out to the checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com.

---

## Is Shiprocket Checkout compatible with Shopify's native discount codes?

Yes, Shiprocket Checkout is compatible with Shopify's native discount codes. You can import the coupon code discounts from Shopify to the checkout dashboard.

---

## Does Shiprocket Checkout support integration with Klaviyo?

Yes, Shiprocket checkout supports integration with Klaviyo. Please reach out to the checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for the integration process.

---

## Is Shiprocket Checkout compatible with Shopify markets or international storefronts?

Currently, Shiprocket Checkout is optimized for domestic (India) orders. International storefront support may be limited. Please reach out to the checkout support team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for specific requirements.

---

## Does Shiprocket Checkout work with headless Shopify stores?

Yes, Shiprocket Checkout can work with headless Shopify stores through server-to-server (S2S) API integration. Please reach out to the checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for the integration process.

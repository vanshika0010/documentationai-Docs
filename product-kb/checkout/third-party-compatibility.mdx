## What loyalty providers can I plug into Shiprocket Checkout

Shiprocket Checkout supports integration with PopCoins, Flits, Easyrewardz, Capillarytech, Uloyal.io, and Twidpay.

---

## Are existing Shopify apps compatible with Shiprocket Checkout

Yes, Shiprocket Checkout is compatible with most third-party Shopify apps including review apps, loyalty apps, and upsell apps.

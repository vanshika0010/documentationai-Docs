## Will shiprocket checkout provide RTO refund for orders which are placed though it?

RTO refund is not provided by shiprocket checkout. RTO refund is provided by shiprocket logistics platform for the orders placed through it.

---

## How can I initiate a refund for an order placed through Shiprocket Checkout?

Refunds for prepaid orders placed through Shiprocket Checkout need to be initiated from the payment gateway dashboard. The refund process depends on the payment gateway used for the order. For assistance, contact the checkout support team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com.

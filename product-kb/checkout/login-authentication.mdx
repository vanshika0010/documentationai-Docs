## Does shiprocket checkout provide an alternative to kiwkpass on websites?

Yes, shiprocket checkout provides Fastrr Login as an alternative to Kiwkpass on websites. Fastrr Login provides seamless OTP based login with address pre-fill on the website. Please reach out to the checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com to activate Fastrr Login on your website.

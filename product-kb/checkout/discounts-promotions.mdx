## What types of discounts can I provide on Shiprocket Checkout

All types: amount off cart, amount off product, bundle, bank offers, Buy X Get Y, Aftersell offers, and Tiered Discounts. These can be automatic or coupon-based.

---

## Will my Shopify discounts work on Shiprocket Checkout

Yes, all Shopify discounts work on Shiprocket Checkout.

---

## How to add a prepaid discount on my orders from checkout

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings. Write a fixed or percentage discount under the prepaid discount field. You can choose to apply the maximum of fixed or percentage.

---

## How to add extra charges on COD orders on checkout

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings. Write a fixed or percentage value under COD charges.

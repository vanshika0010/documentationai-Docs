<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many types of discounts can I provide on Shiprocket Checkout?">

All types of discounts are available: amount off cart, amount off product, bundle, bank offers, Buy X Get Y, Aftersell offers, and Tiered Discounts. These can be automatic or coupon-based.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my Shopify discounts work on Shiprocket Checkout?">

Yes, all Shopify discounts will work on Shiprocket Checkout.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to add prepaid discount on my orders from checkout?">

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings from the left menu. Write a fixed or percentage discount you want to apply. You can choose to apply the maximum of fixed or percentage discount.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to add extra charges on COD orders on checkout?">

Go to Settings > Shipping Settings from the left menu. Write a fixed or percentage value under COD charges. You can choose to apply the maximum of fixed or percentage.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

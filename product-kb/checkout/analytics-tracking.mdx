## What analytics does Shiprocket Checkout provide

Shiprocket Checkout provides a powerful reporting and analytics dashboard with insights on checkout performance including campaign performance, payment success rates, discount coupon burn rate, and checkout funnel analysis.

---

## What tracking platforms is Shiprocket Checkout compatible with

Shiprocket Checkout can be integrated with GA4, Google Ads, Facebook, MoEngage, CleverTap, WebEngage, Engage360, and Snapchat.

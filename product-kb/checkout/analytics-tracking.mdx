## How will I track my conversion on Shopify if I use Shiprocket Checkout?

Shiprocket checkout is deeply integrated with Facebook Pixel and Google Ads which helps in tracking conversions on Shopify using the same pixel and google ads tag used on the website. No additional configuration is required for tracking conversions on Shopify.

---

## What type of events does Shiprocket Checkout track?

Shiprocket checkout tracks the following events:
* Phone Number Added - Shared once the customer has entered his mobile number and requested OTP.
* Payment Initiated - Shared at the time of prepaid payment initiation only.
* Order Placed - Shared once an order is created, Prepaid or COD.
* These events are shared with the tracking platforms configured on the checkout dashboard.

---

## How to setup Google Ads conversion tracking on Checkout?

To setup Google Ads conversion tracking on Checkout, follow these steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Settings > Tracking Info from the left menu.
* Click on the Google Ads tab.
* Enter the Conversion ID and Conversion Label from your Google Ads account.
* Click on Save to save the configuration.

---

## How to integrate Facebook Pixel with Shiprocket Checkout?

To integrate Facebook Pixel with Shiprocket Checkout, follow these steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Settings > Tracking Info from the left menu.
* Click on the Facebook tab.
* Enter the Facebook Pixel ID from your Facebook Business Manager account.
* Click on Save to save the configuration.

---

## How to setup Snapchat Pixel on Checkout?

To setup Snapchat Pixel on Checkout, follow these steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Settings > Tracking Info from the left menu.
* Click on the Snapchat tab.
* Enter the Snapchat Pixel ID from your Snapchat Business Manager account.
* Click on Save to save the configuration.

  <Accordion title="What analytics does Shiprocket Checkout provide?">

Shiprocket Checkout provides a powerful reporting and analytics dashboard with insights on checkout performance including campaign performance, payment success rates, discount coupon burn rate, and checkout funnel analysis.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What tracking platforms is Shiprocket Checkout compatible with?">

Shiprocket Checkout can be integrated with GA4, Google Ads, Facebook, MoEngage, CleverTap, WebEngage, Engage 360, and Snapchat.

  </Accordion>

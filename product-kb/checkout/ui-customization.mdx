## How to change the theme color of my Shiprocket Checkout

The theme color can be changed from the Checkout Dashboard. Go to Settings > Branding and update the theme color to match your website brand colors.

---

## Can I add my brand logo to the checkout page

Yes. Go to Settings > Branding on the checkout dashboard and upload your brand logo.

---

## How to customize the size of buttons in Shiprocket Checkout

Button size customization can be done by the checkout integration team. Raise a request at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com.

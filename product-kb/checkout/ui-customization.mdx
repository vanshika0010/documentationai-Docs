<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I customize the look and feel of my checkout page?">

Yes, Shiprocket Checkout allows you to customize the color theme, logo, font style, and button text to match your brand identity. The checkout UI is fully customizable.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I add my brand logo to the checkout page?">

You can add your brand logo from the checkout dashboard under Settings > Branding. Upload your logo and it will appear on your checkout page.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the theme color of my Shiprocket Checkout?">

Yes, you can change the theme color from Settings > Branding on the checkout dashboard to match your website's brand colors.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

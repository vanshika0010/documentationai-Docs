<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Shiprocket Checkout help reduce RTO?">

Shiprocket Checkout reduces RTO through address pre-fill (reducing wrong address deliveries), OTP verification, COD controls (charges or blocking), and RTO risk scoring to identify high-risk buyers.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I block COD for specific pincodes or customers using Checkout?">

Yes. You can create custom rules in Settings > Shipping Settings > Custom Rules to block COD for specific pincodes, invoice values, or based on customer RTO history.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

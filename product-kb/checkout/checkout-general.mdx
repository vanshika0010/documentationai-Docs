## How does Shiprocket Checkout help reduce RTO

Shiprocket Checkout reduces RTO through address pre-fill (reducing wrong address deliveries), OTP verification, COD controls (charges or blocking high-risk buyers), and RTO risk scoring.

---

## Can I block COD for specific pincodes or customers using Checkout

Yes. Go to Settings > Shipping Settings > Custom Rules > Create Rule to block COD for specific pincodes, invoice values, or based on customer RTO history.

---

## How does the checkout conversion funnel work

The checkout funnel tracks customer drop-off at each stage: visit > OTP verification > address confirmation > payment selection > order placement. The Checkout Dashboard shows where customers abandon the process so you can optimize accordingly.

## How to create a tiered discount on Checkout?

Facing an issue with tiered discount. Don't woory. Follow these detailed steps on your Checkout dashboard to create a tiered discount:
1. Go to Settings: Navigate to the settings section from the left menu.
2. Select Discounts: Click on the Discounts option.
3. Create Discount: Click on Create and choose your discount type - Tiered Discount.
4. Select Discount Method: Choose from "Automatic discount" or "Coupon Code" as per your requirement.
5. Set Configuration: Under "Discount Configuration", choose the type of discount you want to configure: "Flat" or "Percentage".
   - Type of Tiered: Select from 2 types of tiered options: "Cart value" or "Quantity" and add tiered by clicking on "Add tier".
6. Set Criteria: Fill in the necessary details for configuration.
7. Functionality: Fill in the necessary details like Payment Modes, Combination, Capping.
8. Save and Enable: Once created, your discount will appear in the All Discounts section.

## How to create a discount on checkout for prepaid orders?

To add a prepaid discount value on Shiprocket Checkout:
* Go to Settings > Shipping Settings from the left menu.
* Write a fixed or percentage discount you want to apply to your order.
* You can choose to apply the maximum of fixed or percentage discount on the order.

## how can I create rules for checkout based on pincode?

You can create rules based on pincodes on the checkout by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select the rule type from the list of rules like Shipping visibility, Prepaid discount, COD charges, etc.
3. Choose "Destination pincodes" as criteria under the rule type selected.
4. Enter the pincodes for which you want to apply the rule.
5. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the discount value or shipping visibility settings.
6. Click on "Next" and the rule is created on your checkout.

## How can I use buy X get Y feature on Checkout?

You can use the Buy X Get Y feature on Shiprocket Checkout by following these steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings: Navigate to the settings section from the left menu.
2. Select Discounts: Click on the Discounts option.
3. Create Discount: Click on Create and choose "Buy X get Y" as the discount type.
4. Select Discount Method: Choose from "Automatic discount" or "Coupon Code" as per your requirement.
5. Configure Details: Fill in the necessary details for X (quantity or amount) and Y (free or discounted product).
6. Set Criteria and Functionality details.
7. Save and Enable.

## Can I restrict checkout to certain locations?

Yes, you can restrict checkout to certain locations using custom rules on the Shiprocket Checkout dashboard. Follow the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Click on Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "Shipping visibility" as the rule type.
3. Choose "Destination pincodes" as criteria.
4. Enter the pincodes that you want to allow or restrict for checkout.
5. Click on "Next" and select the delivery methods you want to show or hide for those pincodes.
6. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

## How to create custom rules on Checkout?

Custom rules can be created on the checkout dashboard by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Click on Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select the rule type from the list of rules like Shipping visibility, Prepaid discount, COD charges, etc.
3. Choose criteria from a list of parameters like invoice value, product sku, destination pincodes, UTM sources, etc.
4. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the respective values for the selected rule type.
5. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

## How can I block high risk RTO customers from placing orders on checkout?

Shiprocket checkout provides a feature to block high risk RTO customers from placing orders by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
1. Go to Settings >> Shipping settings >> Custom rules >> Create rule.
2. Select "Disable COD" as the rule type.
3. Choose criteria from a list of parameters like invoice value, product sku, destination pincodes, customer RTO history etc.
4. After selecting the criteria, click on "Next" and enter the respective values for the selected rule type.
5. Click on "Save" to save the rule.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much time will it take for Shiprocket Checkout to be integrated in my store?">

Shiprocket Checkout integration is quick and can go live the same day. Steps: onboarding documentation, integration on preview theme, payment gateway and settings configuration, then publish. Integration typically takes 1 day once all details are provided.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to change my Payment Gateway for checkout?">

No. Shiprocket Checkout supports Cashfree, PayU, Snapmint, Simpl, Phonepe, Billdesk, Eazebuzz, Cred, and Razorpay. We can seamlessly integrate your existing gateway.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What website platforms can Shiprocket Checkout be integrated with?">

Shiprocket Checkout can be integrated with Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom websites.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What payment gateways can I integrate with Shiprocket Checkout?">

Shiprocket Checkout supports: Billdesk, Phonepe, Simpl, Cashfree, Razorpay, PayU, Easebuzz, Cred, Snapmint, Setu, and Decentro.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much do I pay for Shiprocket Checkout?">

Shiprocket Checkout doesn't charge any onboarding or integration fees. It charges a small percentage commission on the invoice value of orders placed through it.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How long will Shiprocket Checkout integration take

Shiprocket Checkout integration is quick and can go live the same day. Steps:

1. Onboarding documentation and store access.
2. Integration on preview/demo theme.
3. Payment gateway setup, shipping rules, discount configuration.
4. Testing and publishing the live theme.

---

## Do I have to change my payment gateway for checkout

No. Shiprocket Checkout supports Cashfree, PayU, Snapmint, Simpl, Phonepe, Billdesk, Easebuzz, Cred, and Razorpay.

---

## What website platforms can Shiprocket Checkout be integrated with

Shiprocket Checkout can be integrated with Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom websites.

---

## What payment gateways can I integrate with Shiprocket Checkout

Supported payment gateways: Billdesk, Phonepe, Simpl, Cashfree, Razorpay, PayU, Easebuzz, Cred, Snapmint, Setu, and Decentro.

---

## How much do I pay for Shiprocket Checkout

Shiprocket Checkout does not charge any onboarding or integration fee. It charges a small percentage commission on the invoice value of each order placed through it.

## Will Shiprocket Checkout support affiliate marketing tracking?

Yes, Shiprocket checkout supports all kinds of affiliate marketing tracking.

---

## Does shiprocket checkout support shipping international orders?

No, currently shiprocket checkout doesn't support shipping of international orders.

---

## How can I reach out to the shiprocket checkout team for any query?

You can reach out to the shiprocket checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for any queries.

---

## How do I contact Shiprocket Checkout customer support?

If you have any query or you’re facing any issues, write an email to checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com. Our support team will get back to you to resolve the issue.

---

## how to enable partial cod or partial payment on shiprocket checkout?

Partial COD can be activated on the checkout in one working day by following the below steps: 

* Reach out to the checkout team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com. 
* Team will make the below changes which are required for the changes on the checkout.
  * Activate partial payment option on the checkout.
  * Change shipping label type to "Label 3" on shiprocket platform if shipping is done using shiprocket.
* Once the above changes are done, the partial cod method is activated on the checkout.

---

## At what time, can I reach checkout support?

You can reach out to the checkout support team anytime and raise a ticket at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com. In case you need a call back, then you can reach out from 10 am to 7 pm (working hours) from Monday to Saturday. Team will assist you and help you resolve your queries.

---

## How can I add option to avail Gift card in payment methods on Checkout?

Shopify gift cards are supported on shiprocket checkout and can be activated by following the below mentioned steps:
1. Email at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com to activate gift cards on checkout providing relevant details like domain name.
2. Checkout team will get this activated within 12-24 working hours and the gift card option will be shown above payment options on the checkout.

You can visit fastrr-checkout.myshopify.com demo store to experience the gift card flow via shiprocket checkout.

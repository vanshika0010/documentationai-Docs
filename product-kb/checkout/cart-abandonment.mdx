## Will my abandoned checkout marketing campaign be affected after I start using Shiprocket Checkout

No. Shiprocket Checkout pushes abandoned carts back to Shopify's abandoned checkouts section, so your existing marketing automations continue working as before.

---

## Can I show FOMO messages on the checkout page when the customer tries to exit

Yes. FOMO messages like "Products may get out of stock" or "You may miss savings of Rs. X" can be shown when the customer tries to exit, helping reduce checkout abandonment.

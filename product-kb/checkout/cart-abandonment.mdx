<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my abandoned checkout marketing campaign be affected after I start using Shiprocket Checkout?">

No. Shiprocket Checkout pushes abandoned carts back to Shopify abandoned checkouts so your existing processes work as they are.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I show FOMO messages on the checkout page when the customer tries to exit?">

Yes, FOMO messages like 'Products may get out of stock' or 'You may miss on savings of Rs. X' can be shown when the customer tries to exit, helping reduce checkout abandonment.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

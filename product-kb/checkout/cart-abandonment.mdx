## Will my abandoned checkout marketing campaign be affected after I start using Shiprocket Checkout?

No, Shiprocket Checkout pushes your abandoned cart back to Shopify abandoned checkouts so that nothing breaks and your processes work as they are.

---

## How is Shiprocket checkout different from Shiprocket Engage?

Shiprocket checkout helps in pre-order journey by increasing the website conversions and provides a smooth checkout experience to the customers. Whereas, shiprocket engage helps in post order journey after the order is placed on the website along with abandoned cart recovery program.

---

## Can I show FOMO messages on the checkout page when the customer tries to exit it?

Yes, FOMO messages like “Products may get out of stock”, “You may miss on savings of Rs. X” can be shown on the checkout page when the customer tries to exit it which helps in reducing the checkout abandonment.

---

## How can I identify pending orders on Shiprocket Checkout where the payment has been received, but the order has not been successfully placed in the store?

You can track all such orders in the "Pending Orders" section on the Checkout Dashboard. Simply follow these steps:
* Checkout Dashboard → Orders → Pending Orders
* Abandoned carts are sent to Shopify in real time once a customer abandons their session.

---

## Why shopify default cart value features not working with Checkout?

It seems that the default cart value features from Shopify may not work seamlessly with Shiprocket Checkout because orders are processed through the Shiprocket system rather than directly through Shopify. This means that certain Shopify-specific features, including cart value requirements, may not be applicable. Since shopify checkout button is replaced with shiprocket checkout button, so handling needs to be done separately by 3rd party checkouts to handle such cases.

## How to integrate webhooks with Checkout

Go to Checkout Dashboard >> Settings >> Webhooks >> Add Webhook. Configure:

* **Service Provider** — the external service that sends or receives the webhook.
* **Webhook Type** — Real Time or Abandoned Cart.
* **Time (seconds)** — available for Abandoned Cart webhook type only.

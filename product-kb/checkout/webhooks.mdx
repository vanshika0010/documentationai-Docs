## Where can I manage and configure my webhooks on Shiprocket Checkout?

You can manage and configure your webhooks on Shiprocket Checkout by following the below steps on your Checkout dashboard:
* Go to Settings >> Webhooks from the left menu.
* You can view all the configured webhooks and their status here.
* To add a new webhook, click on "Add webhook" button.
* Configure the webhook by entering the Service Provider, Webhook Type, and Time (for abandoned cart webhooks).
* Click on "Save" to save the webhook configuration.

## My abandoned cart webhooks are not triggering. What could be the reason?

If your abandoned cart webhooks are not triggering, here are some possible reasons:
1. The webhook URL is not correct or accessible.
2. The webhook is disabled. Check the webhook status in the Webhooks section.
3. The webhook time configuration is not set correctly.
4. The checkout session is not being abandoned correctly.

Please reach out to the checkout support team at checkoutsupport@shiprocket.com for further assistance.

  <Accordion title="How can I increase the chances that a customer will come back and order again?">

To encourage repeat purchases: offer discounts or loyalty points on second purchases, send personalized messages or product recommendations, make sure their first delivery is on time and hassle-free, and include a small thank-you note or freebie in the package.

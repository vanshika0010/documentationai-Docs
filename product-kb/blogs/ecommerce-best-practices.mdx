## How can I increase the chances that a customer will come back and order again

To encourage repeat purchases:

* Offer discounts or loyalty points on second purchases
* Send personalized messages or product recommendations
* Make sure their first delivery is on time and hassle-free
* Include a small thank-you note or freebie in the package

Delight your customers once, and many will come back on their own.

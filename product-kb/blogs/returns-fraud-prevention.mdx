<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is return fraud, and why should I be concerned as a seller?">

Return fraud happens when someone takes advantage of the return process — for example, by using a product and then returning it, or falsely claiming an item wasn't delivered. As a seller, this can lead to serious financial losses, hurt your brand reputation, and make your genuine customers bear the burden of stricter return rules or higher prices.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can you explain the different types of return fraud I should be aware of?">

Yes, there are several types of return fraud. For instance, wardrobing is when a buyer uses a product (like clothing) and returns it after use. Bricking involves tampering with electronics before returning them. Some buyers return empty boxes or create fake receipts. There's also arbitrage, where cheaper products are returned in place of expensive ones.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I safeguard my business against return fraud?">

To protect your store, start by setting a clear return policy. Use RMA (Return Merchandise Authorisation) systems that require approval before accepting returns. Always track your returns, inspect items thoroughly before issuing refunds, and monitor high-risk customer behavior or frequent return patterns.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What exactly is an RMA system, and how does it help me?">

An RMA (Return Merchandise Authorisation) system helps streamline your return process. Instead of allowing returns without any structure, customers must first request approval. This system gives you better control, allows you to track each return, and ensures that only valid returns are accepted.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I choose the right courier partner for my shipments?">

Selecting the right courier partner depends on your business needs. Think about how fast your customers expect their orders, whether the courier covers your delivery areas, and how competitive their rates are. Also check for features like COD support, tracking updates, and good pickup service.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is it important to have accurate delivery addresses?">

Incorrect or incomplete addresses are one of the biggest reasons orders don't get delivered. A wrong pin code, missing landmark, or incorrect contact number can lead to delivery failures, which means added return costs for you and a poor experience for your customer.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can automation help me manage my online store better?">

Automation can be a game-changer for your business. It helps you sync orders automatically from marketplaces, generate shipping labels without manual effort, auto-assign the best courier partner for each shipment, and track NDRs and returns more efficiently.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What types of fraud should I be aware of as an online seller?">

Unfortunately, fraud is quite common in eCommerce. You might face fake COD orders, return fraud, chargebacks where buyers falsely claim they didn't receive the order, and payment fraud involving stolen cards or suspicious transactions.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What is return fraud, and why should I be concerned as a seller

Return fraud happens when someone takes advantage of the return process — for example, by using a product and then returning it, or falsely claiming an item wasn't delivered. As a seller, this can lead to serious financial losses, hurt your brand reputation, and make your genuine customers bear the burden of stricter return rules or higher prices.

---

## Can you explain the different types of return fraud I should be aware of

Yes, there are several types of return fraud:

* **Wardrobing** — the buyer uses a product (like clothing) and returns it after use.
* **Bricking** — tampering with electronics before returning them.
* **Empty box returns** — returning empty boxes or creating fake receipts.
* **Arbitrage** — returning cheaper products in place of expensive ones.

Being aware of these tactics can help you spot suspicious activity early.

---

## How can I safeguard my business against return fraud

To protect your store:

* Set a clear return policy.
* Use an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorisation) system that requires approval before accepting returns.
* Always track your returns and inspect items before issuing refunds.
* Monitor high-risk customer behavior or frequent return patterns.

---

## What exactly is an RMA system, and how does it help me

An RMA (Return Merchandise Authorisation) system helps streamline your return process. Customers must first request approval before sending back a product. This gives you better control, allows you to track each return, and ensures that only valid returns are accepted — saving time and avoiding losses.

## How do I choose the right courier partner for my shipments

Selecting the right courier partner depends on your business needs. Consider delivery speed, coverage area, competitive rates, and features like COD support, tracking updates, and good pickup service. Comparing performance across pin codes can help you identify which courier performs best where.

---

## Why is it important to have accurate delivery addresses

Incorrect or incomplete addresses are one of the biggest reasons orders don't get delivered. A wrong pin code, missing landmark, or incorrect contact number can lead to delivery failures, which means added return costs for you and a poor experience for your customer.

---

## How can automation help me manage my online store better

Automation can help you:

* Sync orders automatically from marketplaces
* Generate shipping labels without manual effort
* Auto-assign the best courier partner for each shipment
* Track NDRs and returns more efficiently

This means less time doing repetitive tasks and more time focusing on sales and growth.

## What types of fraud should I be aware of as an online seller

Common types of fraud include:

* **Fake COD orders** — customers place orders with no intention of receiving them.
* **Return fraud** — sending back used or tampered products.
* **Chargebacks** — buyers falsely claiming they didn't receive the order.
* **Payment fraud** — involving stolen cards or suspicious transactions.

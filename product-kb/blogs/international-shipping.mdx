## I'm new to international shipping — what should I prepare before sending an order abroad

You'll typically need:

* A commercial invoice with product details, value, and buyer info
* HS codes for customs classification
* A valid KYC from the buyer in some countries
* Properly packed products with clear labels

Also check if your product is allowed in the destination country to avoid clearance issues.

---

## What is a commercial invoice, and why is it required for international shipments

A commercial invoice is like your product's passport. It includes product description, quantity, price, seller and buyer details, and the HS code. Customs officials use it to calculate duties and decide whether your shipment can be cleared. Always fill it out carefully — errors can delay or block delivery.

---

## Who pays the customs duties and taxes — the buyer or the seller

That depends on your shipping terms:

* **DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid)** — the buyer pays duties at delivery.
* **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** — you cover the taxes upfront.

Clearly mention this on your store so the buyer knows what to expect.

---

## What are HS codes, and how do they affect my international shipments

HS (Harmonized System) codes classify products for customs. These codes determine how much duty is charged and whether the item is allowed into a country. You can usually find the correct HS code based on your product type.

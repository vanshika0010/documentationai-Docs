## I'm new to international shipping—what should I prepare before sending an order abroad?

Great question! Shipping internationally involves a bit more paperwork, but it's manageable. You'll typically need:

A commercial invoice with product details, value, and buyer info

HS codes for customs classification

A valid KYC (like PAN or Aadhar) from the buyer in some countries

Properly packed products with clear labels

You should also check if your product is allowed in the destination country to avoid clearance issues.

---

## What is a commercial invoice, and why is it required for international shipments?

A commercial invoice is like your product’s passport. It includes details such as product description, quantity, price, seller and buyer details, and the HS code. Customs officials use it to calculate duties and decide whether your shipment can be cleared. Always fill it out carefully—errors here can delay or block your delivery.

---

## Who pays the customs duties and taxes—the buyer or the seller?

That depends on how you set up your shipping terms:

With DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid), the buyer pays the duties at delivery.

With DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), you cover the taxes upfront.

It's important to clearly mention this on your store so the buyer knows what to expect. Surprises at customs can lead to refusals or bad experiences.

## How do I calculate the shipping charges for an international order?

You can use a shipping rate calculator by entering:

The origin and destination countries

Package dimensions and weight

Shipment purpose (gift, commercial, sample)

Shipping charges vary based on distance, delivery speed, and package weight (actual or volumetric). It's best to check charges in advance to price your products right.

---

## Can I ship items marked as gifts or samples? Are there special rules for those?

Yes, you can ship gifts and samples, and they may even get duty exemptions depending on the country. However, you still need to provide a commercial invoice with a declared value and product details. Avoid under-declaring the value, as that may cause clearance issues.

---

## What are HS codes, and how do they affect my international shipments?

HS (Harmonized System) codes classify products for customs. For example, clothing, electronics, and cosmetics all have specific HS codes. These codes determine how much duty is charged and whether the item is allowed into a country. You can usually find the correct HS code based on your product type, or consult a logistics expert to help you pick the right one.

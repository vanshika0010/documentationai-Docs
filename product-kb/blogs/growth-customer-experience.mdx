## What does an omnichannel strategy mean, and do I really need it

An omnichannel strategy means selling on multiple platforms — your website, marketplaces like Amazon or Flipkart, social media, and even offline — while managing everything from a single system. It helps you reach more customers and provide a seamless experience across all touchpoints.

## What role does good customer communication play in my store's growth

A big one! Sending timely updates about orders, proactively handling issues, and responding quickly to questions creates a positive customer experience. Happy customers are more likely to leave good reviews, refer friends, and become repeat buyers — all of which fuel your growth.

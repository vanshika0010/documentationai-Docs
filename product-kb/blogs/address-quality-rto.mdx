## What's the best way to ensure customers enter correct addresses?

You can guide customers during checkout by:

Using address auto-fill or validation tools

Allowing dropdowns for states and cities to reduce manual errors

Asking for landmarks or alternate phone numbers

Showing a real-time serviceability check for the entered pin code

These steps reduce delivery issues significantly.

## How can I avoid mistakes while packing and shipping orders?

Mistakes often happen when orders are managed manually. You can avoid them by:

Using barcode scanning for picking and packing

Validating addresses before dispatch

Automating label generation so nothing gets missed

Keeping your inventory system updated to prevent overselling

Automation makes your operations smoother and helps ensure accuracy every step of the way.

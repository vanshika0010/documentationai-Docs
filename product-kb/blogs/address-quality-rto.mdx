<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the best way to ensure customers enter correct addresses?">

You can guide customers during checkout by using address auto-fill or validation tools, allowing dropdowns for states and cities to reduce manual errors, asking for landmarks or alternate phone numbers, and showing a real-time serviceability check for the entered pin code.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I really reduce my RTOs just by improving address quality?">

Absolutely. A lot of RTOs (Return to Origin) happen because the courier couldn't reach the customer due to a wrong address or phone number. By validating addresses before dispatch and asking for accurate contact details, you can avoid many failed delivery attempts.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

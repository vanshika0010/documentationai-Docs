## What's the best way to ensure customers enter correct addresses

You can guide customers during checkout by:

* Using address auto-fill or validation tools
* Allowing dropdowns for states and cities to reduce manual errors
* Asking for landmarks or alternate phone numbers
* Showing a real-time serviceability check for the entered pin code

---

## Can I really reduce my RTOs just by improving address quality

Absolutely. A lot of RTOs (Return to Origin) happen because the courier couldn't reach the customer due to a wrong address or phone number. By validating addresses before dispatch and asking for accurate contact details, you can avoid many failed delivery attempts and the cost that comes with them.

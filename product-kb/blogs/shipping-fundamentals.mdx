  <Accordion title="Can I deliver to remote or hard-to-reach locations using Shiprocket?">

Yes, you can! Shiprocket works with courier partners like India Post to help you deliver even to the most remote areas such as tier 3 and 4 cities, tribal regions, and hilly zones.

  </Accordion>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the main things that affect my shipping cost?">

Shipping charges are usually based on the actual weight and volumetric weight of your package, the distance between pickup and delivery, the courier partner's pricing, and whether it's a COD or prepaid shipment.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is volumetric weight, and how is it calculated?">

Volumetric weight considers the space your parcel takes up. It's calculated as: (Length × Breadth × Height) / Volumetric Divisor. Make sure your packaging isn't too bulky for what's inside — it can help reduce shipping costs.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I reduce delays in my shipments?">

To keep deliveries on time: always update your inventory and avoid stockouts, get shipments ready before pickup slots, enter accurate weights and dimensions, double-check delivery addresses, and use an automated platform to reduce manual errors.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

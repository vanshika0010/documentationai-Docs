## Can I deliver to remote or hard-to-reach locations using Shiprocket

Yes, Shiprocket works with courier partners like India Post to help you deliver even to the most remote areas — such as tier 3 and 4 cities, tribal regions, and hilly zones.

## What are the main things that affect my shipping cost

Shipping charges are usually based on:

* The actual weight and volumetric weight of your package (whichever is higher)
* The distance between pickup and delivery (local, zonal, national)
* The courier partner's pricing
* Whether it's a COD or prepaid shipment

---

## What is volumetric weight, and how is it calculated

Volumetric weight considers the space your parcel takes up. It's calculated as:

**(Length cm × Breadth cm × Height cm) ÷ 5000**

If your package is large but not very heavy, you might be charged by volumetric weight. Avoid overly bulky packaging to reduce shipping costs.

## How can I reduce delays in my shipments

To keep deliveries on time:

* Always update your inventory and avoid stockouts
* Get shipments ready before pickup slots
* Enter accurate weights and dimensions
* Double-check delivery addresses
* Use an automated platform to reduce manual errors

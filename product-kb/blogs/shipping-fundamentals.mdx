## Can I deliver to remote or hard-to-reach locations using Shiprocket?

Yes, you can! Shiprocket works with courier partners like India Post to help you deliver even to the most remote areas—such as tier 3 and 4 cities, tribal regions, and hilly zones. This allows you to expand your customer base without worrying about serviceability.

## What are the main things that affect my shipping cost?

Shipping charges are usually based on:

The actual weight and volumetric weight of your package

The distance between pickup and delivery (local, zonal, national)

The courier partner's pricing

Whether it's a COD or prepaid shipment

Be sure to pack efficiently and measure your parcels correctly to avoid surprise costs.

---

## What is volumetric weight, and how is it calculated?

Volumetric weight considers the space your parcel takes up. If your package is large but not very heavy (like a big box of shoes), you might be charged by volume. It's calculated as:
(Length × Breadth × Height) / Volumetric Divisor
Make sure your packaging isn't too bulky for what's inside—it can help reduce shipping costs.

## Can I schedule deliveries or request pickups on weekends?

Some courier partners do support scheduled deliveries or weekend pickups, but it depends on their availability in specific areas. You can check this through your panel or directly with the courier. For smoother operations, try to schedule pickups in advance.

---

## What should I keep in mind when shipping heavy or oversized items?

Yes, you can ship heavy or oversized items, but check each courier's weight and dimension limits. You may need special packaging or pickup arrangements. Oversized parcels may also attract higher shipping charges or special handling fees.

## How can I reduce delays in my shipments?

To keep deliveries on time:

Always update your inventory and avoid stockouts

Get shipments ready before pickup slots

Enter accurate weights and dimensions

Double-check delivery addresses

Use an automated platform to reduce manual errors

Every small step helps make the delivery process smoother.

Some of the most helpful tools include:

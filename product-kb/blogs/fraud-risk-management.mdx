## Are there any signs that suggest an order might be fraudulent

Yes, here are a few red flags to watch for:

* Unusual order volumes from new customers
* Mismatched contact details (email vs. name)
* Frequent order-and-cancel behavior
* High-value orders from suspicious locations

If something feels off, take a moment to verify the details before dispatching.

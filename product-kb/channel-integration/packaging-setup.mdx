## Why do I need to upload package images

Uploading photographs of your package is recommended (not required). It allows Shiprocket to determine the size and shape of your package. Upload a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 5 images from various angles.

---

## Can I use my own packaging

Yes. Mention 'Self' in the Packaging Material column, or leave the cell empty. Ensure your packaging is secure, correctly labeled, complies with size/weight restrictions, and uses adequate cushioning for fragile items.

---

## How to manage packages

Go to Setup & Manage >> Packaging from the left menu bar. Three tabs are available:

* **All Packaging** — shows all packages (yours + Shiprocket's).
* **Own Packaging** — shows packages you created.
* **Shiprocket Packaging** — shows packages purchased from Shiprocket.

---

## Can I buy packaging material from Shiprocket

Yes. Go to Setup & Manage >> Packaging and click 'Buy Packaging' at the top-right corner. You'll be redirected to the packaging website.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I need to upload package images?">

Uploading photographs of your package is recommended (not required) so we can determine the size and shape. Upload a minimum of 3 and maximum of 5 package images from various angles.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own packaging?">

Yes. If you don't want to use our packaging, simply mention 'Self' in the Packaging Material column or leave the cell empty. Ensure your packaging is secure, correctly labeled, complies with size/weight restrictions, and uses adequate cushioning for fragile items.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy packaging material from Shiprocket?">

Yes. Go to Setup & Manage >> Packaging and click 'Buy Packaging' at the top-right corner to be redirected to the packaging website.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## why is my channel disconnected? how to fix the issue?

Your channel might be disconnected because of authentication error. It might be possible that your API keys have changed or password has been updated due to which you are getting this issue. To fix the issue, try to reconnect or reauthorize your channel from the troubleshooting option.

---

## Channel connected but orders are not getting synced.

There could be a few reasons for this issue. Firstly, ensure that the Order Sync is turned On for your integrated channel. If it's already On, also check the 'Status to fetch' field this should be same as your sales channel status. try manually triggering the order synchronization process by clicking on the 'Sync Order' button located at the top right corner of the orders page. This will prompt Shiprocket to fetch the latest orders from your integrated sales channels.

## How to integrate the Razorpay with Shiprocket?

To integrate the Razorpay, follow these steps on Shiprocket website:
1. Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu bar.
2. You will be directed to the Sales Channel page.
3. To add the new channel click on 'Connect New Channel' button located at top-right corner.
4. Search 'Razorpay' in the search bar and click on the 'Add' button.
5. Click on "Connect to Razorpay" button.
6. You'll be diverted to the Razorpay seller login page. Log in with your credentials.
7. A page 'Authorize Shiprocket' will open — click "Authorize".
8. You will be redirected to the Shiprocket channel page where you can edit your preferences.
9. To pull orders in Shiprocket, you need to activate Shiprocket for your pages created in Razorpay panel

## What's a Map Payment Statuses Feature?

The map payment statuses feature helps you map new and existing payment gateway/statuses of your channel against Shiprocket's COD and Prepaid payment statuses.
To enable this follow the below steps on Shiprocket website:
1. Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu.
2. Click on the 'Edit' button for the channel you wish to map payment statuses.
3. Locate the 'Map Payment Statuses' header. Turn the toggle on.
4. You can enter multiple statuses separated by ",".
5. Click on 'Update Channel & Test Connection'.

## Can i delete a channel?

No, once a channel is integrated, you cannot delete it. You can only enable or disable the channel.

---

## How do I bulk import orders from sales channels?

Shiprocket automatically syncs/imports all your pending orders from the channels those are integrated into the panel.
For all the channels integrated, Order Sync Automatically runs every 30 minutes.
But still in case you want to run order sync manually, you can Go to Orders Tab in the left menu and click the 'Sync orders' button present in the top-right corner of the orders page.
Just make sure that for your integrated channel, the Order Sync is On

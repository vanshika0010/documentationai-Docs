## why is my channel disconnected? how to fix the issue?

Your channel might be disconnected because of authentication error. It might be possible that your API keys have changed or password has been updated due to which you are getting this issue. To fix the issue, try to reconnect or reauthorize your channel from the troubleshooting option.

---

## Channel connected but orders are not getting synced.

There could be a few reasons for this issue. Firstly, ensure that the Order Sync is turned On for your integrated channel. If it's already On, also check the 'Status to fetch' field this should be same as your sales channel status. try manually triggering the order synchronization process by clicking on the 'Sync Order' button located at the top right corner of the orders page. This will prompt Shiprocket to fetch the latest orders from your integrated sales channels.

## How to integrate the Razorpay with Shiprocket?

To integrate the Razorpay, follow these steps on Shiprocket website:
1. Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu bar.
2. You will be directed to the Sales Channel page where you can view your currently integrated channels. (if already integreated).
3. To add the new channel click on 'Connect New Channel' button located at top-right corner of the page.
4. Search 'Razorpay' in the search bar at top-right corner of the page.
5. Next, click on the 'Add' button. Follow the below steps to proceed further:
    1. To proceed, please click on the "Connect to Razorpay” button on your screen.
    2. You'll be diverted to the Razorpay seller login page. Log in to your Razorpay account by entering your email address/username and password.
    3. Once logged in, a page ‘Authorize Shiprocket’ will open where you can verify your account integration with Shiprocket by clicking “Authorize”.
    4. Now, you will be redirected to the Shiprocket channel page. Here you can edit your Razorpay channel to modify it as per your preferences.
    5. To pull orders in Shiprocket, you need to activate Shiprocket for your pages created in Razorpay panel

## How to Integrate Prestashop with Shiprocket?

* Follow the below steps to integrate your Prestashop with Shiprocket:
* Part A: Setting has to be made at Prestashop end.
   * Login to your Prestashop Admin Panel. 
   * Go to ‘Advanced Parameters’ >> ‘Webservice’ from left menu.
   * Make sure the option ‘Enable PrestaShop’s Web Service’ should be marked ‘Yes’.
   * Click on Sign (+) to add new API.
   * Enter Key value and click to Generate. 
   * Enter Key Description i.e. (API key, API name, other information) 
   * Enable Status to Yes. 
   * Check all Permissions. 
   * Click on Save.
* Part B: Setting has to be made at Shiprocket end.
   * Go to ‘Setup & Manage’ >> ‘Channels’ from the left menu on Shiprocket website.
   * Click on the ‘Add New Channel’ button.
   * Click on ‘Prestashop’ >> ‘Add’.
   * Switch ‘On’ the Order Sync icon.
   * Enter Prestashop Store URL & Prestashop API Key (which has been already generated in Step A)
   * Click ‘Save Channel & Test Connection’.

## What's a Map Payment Statuses Feature?

* The map payment statuses feature helps you map new and existing payment gateway/statuses of your channel against Shiprocket's COD and Prepaid payment statuses. 
* To enable this follow the below steps on Shiprocket website:
   * Go to ‘Setup & Manage’ >> ‘Channels’ from the left menu.
   * Now, click on the 'Edit' button for the channel you wish to map payment statuses. It will redirect you to a new page.
   * In this page, locate the 'Map Payment Statuses' header. Turn the toggle on to turn on this feature.
   * You can enter multiple statuses separated by ",". 
   * Click on 'Update Channel & Test Connection'.

## What Is a Push Order Status Mapper?

* A Push Order Status Mapper helps you match your channel or website statuses with the default statuses of Shiprocket. By turning it on, you can select or create an order status which you want to push on your channel. When the Push Order Status Mapper is disabled, default push status mapping will work on your channel. 
* Follow the below steps on Shiprocket website:
   * Go to ‘Setup & Manage’>>’Channels’ from the left menu.
   * Now, click on ‘Edit’ button for the channel you wish to push order statuses. It will redirect you to a new page.
   * On this page locate the ‘Push Order Status for Shipment Tracking’ header.
   * Turn on the toggle button. Now you can see the multiple statuses.
   * Now, mapped the statuses from the drop-down.
   * Click on 'Update Channel & Test Connection'.
* Note: Make sure you only mention the statuses you have on your channel.

## How do I integrate Unicommerce with Shiprocket?

* Steps to be performed in Shiprocket Account:
   * Go to Settings -> API from the left menu.
   * Next, select Configure and click on the ‘Create an API user’ button. 
   * A pop-up will appear To create an external API, enter an email id (different from your registered email id), and password. Click on ‘Generate API Credentials’ button to proceed.
   * Note: Only 25 API users are allowed to create an external API.
* Steps to be taken in your Unicommerce Account:
   * Login to your Unicommerce account and Go to settings > Shipping Provider.
   * Next, click on Add Shipping Provider from the top right corner.
   * Now search Shiprocket from the search bar and select Shiprocket. 
   * Go to ‘Settings’ tab, Enter the shipping provider name, select your desirable serviceability & shipping methods. Click on ‘Save & Next’.
   * Then, click on ‘Connectors’ tab, Add API user credentials as Email id & Password of your Shiprocket account. 
   * Click on ‘Connect’
   * Now go to ‘Tools’ > select ‘Import’
   * Select Import Type and Import Options from the drop-down given, then upload the pincode that you have downloaded from Shiprocket Panel. Finally, Click on ‘upload file’ button.

## What Is a Pull Order Status Mapper?

* A Pull Order Status Mapper allows you to match your eCommerce channel or website statuses with the Shiprocket's order processing status. Follow the below steps on Shiprocket website:
   * Go to ‘Setup & Manage’ >> ‘Channels’ from the left menu.
   * Now, click on 'Edit' button for the channel you wish to pull order statuses. It will redirect you to a new page.
   * On this page locate the 'Pull Order Statuses' header.
   * You can enter the multiple statuses separated by “,”. For example Status to fetch, PULL Prepaid orders, PULL COD orders, etc.
   * Click on 'Update Channel & Test Connection'.
* Note: By default, we import unfulfilled orders for Amazon. If you are connecting any other channel, please mention all your order statuses which you want to map with Shiprocket's order processing status.

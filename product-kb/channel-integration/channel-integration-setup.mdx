## Why is my channel disconnected? How to fix the issue

Your channel might be disconnected due to an authentication error — API keys may have changed or the password was updated. To fix the issue, try to reconnect or reauthorize your channel from the troubleshooting option.

---

## Channel connected but orders are not getting synced

There could be a few reasons:

1. Ensure Order Sync is turned On for your integrated channel.
2. Check the 'Status to fetch' field — it should match your sales channel's order status.
3. Manually trigger order sync by clicking the 'Sync Order' button at the top-right corner of the orders page.

## What is a Map Payment Statuses feature

The Map Payment Statuses feature helps you match your channel's payment gateway statuses with Shiprocket's COD and Prepaid payment statuses.

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels.
2. Click 'Edit' for the channel you wish to configure.
3. Locate the 'Map Payment Statuses' header and turn the toggle On.
4. Enter statuses separated by commas.
5. Click 'Update Channel & Test Connection.'

---

## Channel Integration: Importance of adding brand name

Adding your brand name is important because Shiprocket uses it in all buyer-facing communications (Email, SMS, Post-Ship page, and Label). It helps buyers recognize your brand, which reduces RTO.

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels.
2. Click the Edit button for your channel.
3. Add your brand name in the 'Communication Brand Name' field and upload your brand logo.
4. Click 'Update Channel and Test Connection.'

---

## What is a Push Order Status Mapper

A Push Order Status Mapper helps you match your channel or website statuses with Shiprocket's default statuses. When disabled, default push status mapping applies.

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels.
2. Click 'Edit' for the channel you wish to configure.
3. Locate 'Push Order Status for Shipment Tracking' and turn the toggle On.
4. Map the statuses from the dropdowns.
5. Click 'Update Channel & Test Connection.'

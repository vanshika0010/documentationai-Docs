<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I integrate Shopify store with Shiprocket?">

To connect your Shopify store: Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels, click 'Connect New Channel' and then 'Add' for the Shopify channel. You will be redirected to Shopify — log in and install the Shiprocket app. After installation, you'll be redirected back to Shiprocket with a success confirmation.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect my Shopify store twice in one Shiprocket account?">

No, you cannot connect your Shopify store twice in one Shiprocket account.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect my Shopify store to multiple Shiprocket accounts?">

Yes, you can connect your Shopify store to multiple Shiprocket accounts.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit how orders are fetched from Shopify to Shiprocket?">

Yes. In Edit Channel settings, you can choose: Fetch all orders (all Unfulfilled orders), Fetch no orders, or Custom preferences. Under Custom Preferences, you can set conditions to fetch or exclude orders based on payment status, Shopify tags, or a combination of both. If an order matches both Fetch and Don't Fetch conditions, Don't Fetch takes priority.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if an order is cancelled in Shopify?">

If an order is cancelled in Shopify, it will automatically appear as 'Cancelled' in Shiprocket. This cannot be changed.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

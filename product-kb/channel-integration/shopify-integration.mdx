## How do I integrate Shopify store with Shiprocket

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels from the left menu.
2. Click 'Connect New Channel' and then 'Add' for the Shopify channel.
3. You will be redirected to Shopify — log in and install the Shiprocket app.
4. After installation, you'll be redirected back to Shiprocket with a success confirmation.

---

## How to retrieve old orders from Sales Channels (Amazon, Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.)

Once you connect any sales channel to Shiprocket, we pull orders from the last 30 days.

---

## Can I connect my Shopify store twice in one Shiprocket account

No, you cannot connect your Shopify store twice in one Shiprocket account.

---

## Can I connect my Shopify store to multiple Shiprocket accounts

Yes, you can connect your Shopify store to multiple Shiprocket accounts.

---

## Can I edit how orders are fetched from Shopify to Shiprocket

Yes. In Edit Channel settings, you can choose:

1. **Fetch all orders** – All Unfulfilled orders in Shopify will be fetched.
2. **Fetch no orders** – No orders will be fetched from Shopify.
3. **Custom preferences** – Fetch orders based on specific conditions (payment status, Shopify tags).

If an order matches both Fetch and Don't Fetch conditions, Don't Fetch takes priority.

---

## What happens if an order is cancelled in Shopify

If an order is cancelled in Shopify, it will automatically appear as "Cancelled" in Shiprocket. This cannot be changed.

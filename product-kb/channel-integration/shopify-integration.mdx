## How to integrate the channel?

To integrate the channels, follow these steps on Shiprocket website:
1. Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu bar.
2. You will be directed to the Sales Channel page where you can view your currently integrated channels.
3. To add the new channel click on 'Connect New Channel' button located at top-right corner of the page.
4. You will be taken to a page where you can add new channels. You can also search for the specific channel you want to add using the search bar.

---

## How to Retrieve Old Orders from Sales Channels(Amazon, Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, OpenCart,etc)?

Once you connect any sales channel (such as Amazon, Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, etc.) to Shiprocket, we pull the orders from the last 30 days.

## How do I integrate Shopify store with Shiprocket?

To connect your Shopify store to Shiprocket, follow these steps on the Shiprocket website:
* Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu.
* Click on the 'Connect New Channel' in the top corner and then click on 'Add' for the Shopify channel. OR directly click 'Connect' CTA for Shopify.
* You will be redirected to Shopify. If not already logged in, log in to your Shopify account.
* Install the Shiprocket app in Shopify.
* After installation, you will be redirected back to Shiprocket with a success pop-up confirming the integration.

Yes, split shipment is allowed with Shopify, but only in SRF.

No, you cannot connect your Shopify store twice in one Shiprocket account.

## can I connect my shopify store to multiple shiprocket accounts?

Yes, you can connect your Shopify store to multiple Shiprocket accounts.

## What default settings are applied when I integrate my Shopify store?

Upon integration, the following default settings are applied:
* Store URL and Store Name are fetched from Shopify.
* Orders with 'Unfulfilled' and 'Unshipped' status in Shopify are fetched to Shiprocket. (This can be changed later.)
* Any changes in order details on Shopify are automatically reflected in Shiprocket. (This cannot be changed.)
* If an order is cancelled in the Shopify store, it will be marked as cancelled in Shiprocket. (This cannot be changed.)
* Self-fulfilled orders in Shopify will be marked as "Self-Fulfilled" in Shiprocket. (This cannot be changed.)
* Order payments: If the remaining amount is less than 1, the order will be classified as Prepaid; otherwise, it will be marked as COD. (This cannot be changed.)
* Shiprocket statuses are mapped to Shopify Delivery Status (This can be customized later).

---

## What happens if an order is canceled in Shopify?

If an order is canceled in Shopify, it will automatically appear as "Canceled" in Shiprocket. This cannot be changed.

---

## What happens if an order is self-fulfilled in Shopify?

If an order is self-fulfilled in Shopify, it will be tagged as “Self-Fulfilled” in Shiprocket. This cannot be changed.

---

## What happens to the 'Return' and 'RTO Delivered' statuses in Shopify?

If a return is created in Shiprocket, a 'Return' tag will appear in Shopify. If an order status is RTO Delivered in Shiprocket, the 'RTO Delivered' tag will appear in Shopify.

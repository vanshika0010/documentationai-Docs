## What is a Pull Order Status Mapper

A Pull Order Status Mapper lets you match your eCommerce channel statuses with Shiprocket's order processing status.

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels.
2. Click 'Edit' for the channel you wish to configure.
3. Locate the 'Pull Order Statuses' header.
4. Enter statuses separated by commas.
5. Click 'Update Channel & Test Connection.'

Note: By default, we import Unfulfilled orders for Amazon.

## Can I connect to the Amazon USA channel

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels.
2. Search for the Amazon US channel and click it.
3. Click 'Connect to AMAZON.'
4. Log in to your Amazon account and click 'Confirm' on the 'Authorise Shiprocket Selling Partner' page.
5. You'll be redirected back to the Shiprocket channel page.

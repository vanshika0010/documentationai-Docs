<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a Pull Order Status Mapper?">

A Pull Order Status Mapper lets you match your eCommerce channel statuses with Shiprocket's order processing status. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels, click Edit for your channel, locate 'Pull Order Statuses,' enter statuses (comma-separated), then click 'Update Channel & Test Connection.' By default, we import Unfulfilled orders for Amazon.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect to Amazon USA channel?">

Yes. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels, search for Amazon US, click it, then click 'Connect to AMAZON.' Log in to your Amazon account, authorize Shiprocket on the 'Authorise Shiprocket Selling Partner' page, and you'll be redirected back to the Shiprocket channel page.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

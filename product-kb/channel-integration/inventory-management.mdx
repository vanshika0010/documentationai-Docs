## How to integrate Omuni with Shiprocket

1. Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Channels' from the left menu bar.
2. Click 'Connect New Channel' button at the top-right corner.
3. Search 'Omuni' in the search bar and click 'Add.'
4. Enter Omuni Username, Omuni Password, Omuni API Token, Brand Name.
5. Enable toggles for Order Status Tracking and Inventory Sync.
6. Click 'Save Channel & Test Connection.'

## How Do I integrate Magento (version 1) with Shiprocket

**Step A — Magento:**
1. Login to Magento admin panel.
2. Go to System > Web Services > SOAP/XML-RPC – Users.
3. Click 'Add New User,' fill in all required info, and click 'Save User.'
4. Go to SOAP/XML-RPC – Roles, click 'Add New Role,' enter role name as 'superadmin,' select Resource Access 'All,' and save.
5. Assign the role to your user.

**Step B — Shiprocket:**
1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels >> Add New Channel.
2. Click on Magento >> Add.
3. Enter Magento order statuses to fetch, toggle Order Sync On.
4. Fill in Store URL, API Key & API Username.
5. Click 'Save Channel & Test Connection.'

## What is a custom channel and how do I use it

A custom channel is created to manage offline/store orders or orders from channels not authenticated with Shiprocket.

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels >> Add New Channel.
2. Select 'Manual' >> 'Add.'
3. Enter Channel Name, Communication Brand Name, and Brand Logo.
4. Click 'Save Channel & Test Connection.'

Note: Orders do not come in automatically via a custom channel. You must import orders manually via the Bulk Import Order Sheet.

---

## Can I delete a channel

No, once a channel is integrated, you cannot delete it. You can only enable or disable the channel.

---

## How do I bulk import orders from sales channels

Shiprocket automatically syncs pending orders from all integrated channels every 30 minutes. To manually trigger a sync, go to the Orders tab from the left menu and click the 'Sync Orders' button at the top-right corner. Ensure Order Sync is On for your integrated channel.

  <Accordion title="How to add and search customer details in Shiprocket?">

Go to Setup & Manage >> Customers from the left menu and click 'Add Customer.' Fill in the customer's name, email, phone, and address details, then click 'Save Details.' To search, use the search bar and look up customers by name, email, phone number, or pincode.

## How to add and search customer details in Shiprocket

Go to Setup & Manage >> Customers from the left menu and click 'Add Customer.' Fill in the customer's name, email, phone, and address details, then click 'Save Details.'

To search, use the search bar at the top-left of the Customers page — you can search by name, email, phone number, or pincode.

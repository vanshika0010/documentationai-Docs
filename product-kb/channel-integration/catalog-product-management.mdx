## How to add a product to the catalog?

Go to Setup & Manage >> Catalogue >> All Products from the left menu. Click "Add Product" at the top-right corner. In the pop-up window, enter the required information (SKU, Product Name, Category, Tax Class, Initial Quantity, Type, HSN) and any additional details like dimensions, weight, EAN, UPC, MRP. Click "Add Product" to save.

---

## How to add a category?

Go to Setup & Manage >> Catalogue >> Categories. Click "Add New Category." Enter the Category name, Code, HSN, and Tax Class Code, then save.

---

## What is a SKU and what are its uses?

SKU stands for "Stock Keeping Unit" — a unique alphanumeric code assigned to each distinct product in a retailer's inventory. SKUs are used for:
1. Inventory management — accurate counting and tracking of stock levels.
2. Order fulfillment — ensuring correct items are picked and shipped.
3. Warehousing — organizing and locating products efficiently.
4. Sales analysis — identifying popular products and trends.
5. Product variants — differentiating items by size, color, or configuration.

## How to import the weight and dimensions of products from my channel?

Go to Setup & Manage >> Catalogue >> Channel Products. Click the upload icon at the top-right, download the sample product file, fill in your products' information (Channel Name, Product Name, SKU Code, Weight, Dimensions), then select the updated file using "Browse" and click "Upload Products."

## How to integrate Amazon as a channel to Shiprocket?

Step A — Amazon Seller Panel:
1. Go to Permissions >> Third-Party Developer and Apps >> Visit Manage your Apps.
2. Generate an MWS Auth token using Developer Name: KartRocket and Developer Account Number: 1469-7463-9584.
3. Save the Merchant ID and MWS Auth Token.

Step B — Shiprocket Panel:
1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels >> Add New Channel.
2. Select Amazon, click "Connect to AMAZON."
3. Log in to your Amazon Seller Central account and confirm authorization.
4. You'll be redirected back to the Shiprocket channel page.

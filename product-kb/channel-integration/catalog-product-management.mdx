<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to add the product to the catalog?">

To add a product to the catalog:
* Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Catalogue' from the left menu.
* Click on 'All Products' from the left side of the page.
* Click on the 'Add Product' button at the top-right corner.
* In the pop-up window, enter SKU, Product Name, Category, Tax Class, Initial Quantity, Type, and HSN.
* You can also add additional information like dimensions, weight, EAN, UPC, ISBN, color, brand, size, image URL, MRP, and cost price.
* Finally, click on the 'Add Product' button.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is SKU and what are its uses?">

SKU stands for 'Stock Keeping Unit.' It is a unique alphanumeric code assigned to each distinct product in a retailer's inventory. SKUs are used for inventory management, order fulfillment, warehousing, sales analysis, pricing and promotions, supply chain management, product variants, and e-commerce product listings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to import the weight and dimensions of each product from my channel?">

Go to 'Setup & Manage' >> 'Catalogue' >> 'Channel Products' from the left menu. Click on the 'upload' icon at the top-right corner, download the sample product file, fill in your products' information (Channel Name, Product Name, SKU Code, Weight, Dimensions), then select the updated file using 'Browse' and click 'Upload Products.'

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

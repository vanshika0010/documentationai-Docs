<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My WooCommerce orders are not synced with Shiprocket. What should I do?">

Ensure Order Sync is turned on for your integrated channel and that the 'Status to fetch' field matches your WooCommerce order status. If the issue persists, create a support ticket.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I integrate WooCommerce with Shiprocket?">

Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels, click 'Add New Channel,' find WooCommerce and click 'Add.' Enter your WooCommerce store URL and click 'Connect to WooCommerce.' You'll be redirected to approve the connection. Once approved, click 'Update Channel & Test Credentials.' Ensure REST API is enabled in your WooCommerce plugin settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to delete old WooCommerce connection?">

You cannot delete a channel once connected. However, you can deactivate it by going to Setup & Manage >> Channels and toggling the channel status.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

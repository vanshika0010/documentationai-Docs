## My WooCommerce orders are not synced with Shiprocket. What should I do

1. Ensure Order Sync is turned On for your integrated channel.
2. Check the 'Status to fetch' field — it should match your WooCommerce order status.
3. If the issue persists, create a support ticket.

---

## How do I integrate WooCommerce with Shiprocket

1. Go to Setup & Manage >> Channels >> Add New Channel.
2. Find WooCommerce and click 'Add.'
3. Enter your WooCommerce store URL and click 'Connect to WooCommerce.'
4. You'll be redirected to WooCommerce — click 'Approve' to grant Shiprocket permissions.
5. After approval, click 'Update Channel & Test Credentials.'

Note: Ensure REST API is enabled in your WooCommerce plugin (WooCommerce >> Settings >> API tab).

---

## How to delete old WooCommerce connection

You cannot delete a channel once it's connected. You can deactivate it by toggling the channel status in Setup & Manage >> Channels.

  <Accordion title="How many couriers does Shiprocket support?">

Shiprocket supports 25+ courier partners including Delhivery, Ecom Express, DTDC, Bluedart, Xpressbees, Shadowfax, Amazon Shipping, India Post, and many more.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I check if a pincode is serviceable?">

You can check pincode serviceability using the Serviceability Checker in your Shiprocket panel. Go to Dashboard and use the Rate/Serviceability calculator, enter the origin and destination pincodes to see which couriers service that route.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

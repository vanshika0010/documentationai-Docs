## Can I use my own courier? How can I add it?

No, you cannot directly add your own courier. Please connect with the Shiprocket team if you have a specific courier partner requirement. Shiprocket continuously adds new courier partners based on seller needs.

---

## How many couriers does Shiprocket support?

Shiprocket supports 25+ courier partners including Delhivery, Ecom Express, DTDC, Bluedart, Xpressbees, Shadowfax, Amazon Shipping, India Post, and many more.

---

## How to check which couriers serve a specific pincode?

Use the Rate Calculator or Serviceability Checker in your Shiprocket dashboard. Enter origin pincode, destination pincode, and weight to see all serviceable couriers for that route.

---

## What is Shiprocket Recommended courier?

Shiprocket Recommended is an auto-selection mode where Shiprocket's algorithm picks the best courier for each order based on performance data, cost, and serviceability. You can enable this from Courier Priority settings.

---

## How to manage dangerous goods on Shiprocket?

For dangerous goods (DG), you need to mark orders as DG when creating them. Only couriers that support dangerous goods will be shown for such orders. Ensure your products are classified correctly and you have the required documentation.

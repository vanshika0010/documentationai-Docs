## How many couriers does Shiprocket support

Shiprocket supports 25+ courier partners including Delhivery, Ecom Express, DTDC, Bluedart, Xpressbees, Shadowfax, Amazon Shipping, India Post, and many more.

---

## How do I check if a pincode is serviceable

Use the Serviceability Checker in your Shiprocket panel. Go to Dashboard and use the Rate/Serviceability calculator — enter origin and destination pincodes to see which couriers service that route.

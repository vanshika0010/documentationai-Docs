<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an AWB number?">

AWB stands for Air Waybill. It is a unique tracking number assigned to each shipment by the courier partner. This number is used to track the shipment throughout its journey from pickup to delivery.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to assign a courier and generate AWB?">

After creating an order, go to the New Orders section and click 'Ship Now' or select orders and click 'Bulk Ship.' Select the courier from the recommended options and click 'Assign.' An AWB number will be generated and the label will be available for download.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my AWB not getting generated?">

AWB generation may fail due to: insufficient wallet balance, the courier's API being temporarily down, the delivery pincode not being serviceable, or incorrect package weight/dimensions. Check your wallet balance and try re-assigning the courier.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

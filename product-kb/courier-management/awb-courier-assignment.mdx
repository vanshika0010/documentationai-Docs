## till what status can I reassign a courier?

You can reassign the courier until the status reaches 'Out for Pickup' or 'Pickup Done.' Once the courier has collected the shipment, reassignment is not possible from the panel. Contact Shiprocket support for assistance after that stage.

---

## What is an AWB number?

AWB stands for Air Waybill. It is a unique tracking number assigned to each shipment by the courier partner. This number is used to track the shipment throughout its journey from pickup to delivery.

---

## How to assign a courier and generate AWB?

After creating an order, go to the New Orders section and click 'Ship Now.' Select the courier from the recommended options and click 'Assign.' An AWB number will be generated and the label will be available for download.

---

## Why is my AWB not getting generated?

AWB generation may fail due to:
* Insufficient wallet balance
* The courier's API being temporarily down
* The delivery pincode not being serviceable by the selected courier
* Incorrect package weight or dimensions
* The courier not supporting COD for COD orders

Check your wallet balance and try re-assigning the courier.

---

## Can I generate AWB for multiple orders at once?

Yes. Go to New Orders, select multiple orders using checkboxes, and click 'Bulk Ship.' The system will auto-assign couriers and generate AWBs for all selected orders simultaneously.

---

## How to cancel an AWB?

Go to the order details and click 'Cancel Order.' This will cancel the AWB and release the shipment. Note that once the shipment is picked up by the courier, AWB cancellation is not possible from the panel.

---

## Can I have the same AWB assigned to multiple packages?

No. Each AWB is unique to one shipment/package. If you need to ship multiple packages for one order, you need to generate separate AWBs for each package.

---

## What does it mean when the AWB status shows 'Pickup Exception'?

Pickup Exception means the courier attempted to collect the shipment but was unable to do so. Common reasons include: seller not available, package not ready, address issues, or courier operational problems. Reschedule the pickup from your Shiprocket panel.

---

## How long is an AWB valid?

An AWB is valid until the shipment is delivered or returned. If an order is cancelled, the AWB becomes void. There is no time-based expiry for AWBs on Shiprocket.

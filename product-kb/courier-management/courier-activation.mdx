## Are there any couriers that are deactivated? What are the conditions for that?

For new sellers, couriers are initially deactivated if:
* KYC is not completed.
* The seller does not meet the courier's eligibility criteria.
* The courier has specific restrictions for certain product categories.

To activate a courier, go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection >> Deactivated tab and click on Activate.

---

## How to activate all couriers at once?

You can activate all couriers at once by going to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection and clicking on the 'Activate All' button. However, some couriers may require KYC or special approval before activation.

---

## Why is a specific courier not showing for my orders?

A courier may not appear due to:
* The courier not being activated in your account.
* The delivery pincode being outside the courier's serviceability.
* The package weight or dimensions exceeding the courier's limits.
* The courier being blocked by your courier rules.
* Insufficient wallet balance.
* The courier not supporting COD for COD orders.

## How to activate Bluedart on Shiprocket?

To activate Bluedart on Shiprocket:
1. Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection >> Deactivated tab.
2. Find Bluedart and click on Activate.
3. You will need to complete the Bluedart KYC process.
4. Required documents: Company Name, PAN Number, Contact Person's Number, Pickup Address with PIN Code, Billing Address, Company URL/Website, Product Category.
5. Upload the required documents and wait for approval (6-7 business days).

---

## How to deactivate a courier on Shiprocket?

To deactivate a courier:
1. Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection.
2. Find the courier you want to deactivate.
3. Toggle the courier status to deactivate it.

---

## Why is Amazon Shipping not visible in my courier list?

Amazon Shipping is only available for Amazon channel orders. It will only appear when you are shipping an order that came from the Amazon sales channel integration.

---

## How to check which couriers are active on my account?

Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection. The Active tab shows all currently active couriers on your account.

---

## Can I activate a courier for specific channels only?

Yes, you can restrict couriers to specific channels using Courier Rules. Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Rules and create a rule with the channel as the condition.

---

## How to activate India Post on Shiprocket?

India Post is available on Shiprocket for remote area deliveries. To activate India Post, go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection and activate it. No special KYC is required for India Post activation.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to activate a courier on Shiprocket?">

Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection from the left menu. Find the courier you want to activate in the Deactivated section and click Activate. Some couriers (like Bluedart) require KYC verification before activation.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a specific courier not showing for my orders?">

A courier may not appear for your order due to: the courier not being activated in your account, the delivery pincode being outside the courier's serviceability, the package weight/dimensions exceeding the courier's limits, or the courier being blocked by your courier rules.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

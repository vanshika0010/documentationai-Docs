## How to activate a courier on Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> Courier >> Courier Selection from the left menu. Find the courier you want to activate in the Deactivated section and click Activate. Some couriers (like Bluedart) require KYC verification before activation.

---

## Why is a specific courier not showing for my orders

A courier may not appear due to:

* The courier not being activated in your account.
* The delivery pincode being outside the courier's serviceability.
* The package weight or dimensions exceeding the courier's limits.
* The courier being blocked by your courier rules.

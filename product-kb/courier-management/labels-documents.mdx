<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to download shipping labels in bulk?">

Go to the Orders section, select the orders you want to print labels for using the checkboxes, then click 'Bulk Actions' >> 'Download Labels.' All labels will be downloaded as a single PDF.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a manifest and how do I generate it?">

A manifest is a document listing all shipments handed over to the courier in a single pickup. To generate: select orders that are ready for pickup, click 'Generate Manifest.' The manifest serves as proof of handover to the courier.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to download invoice for an order?">

Go to Orders, find the specific order, click on it to open order details, and click 'Download Invoice.' You can also bulk download invoices by selecting multiple orders and choosing 'Download Invoice' from bulk actions.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

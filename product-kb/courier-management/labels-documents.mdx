## How to download shipping labels in bulk

Go to the Orders section, select the orders you want to print labels for using the checkboxes, then click Bulk Actions >> Download Labels. All labels will be downloaded as a single PDF.

---

## What is a manifest and how do I generate it

A manifest is a document listing all shipments handed over to the courier in a single pickup. Select orders that are ready for pickup and click 'Generate Manifest.' The manifest serves as proof of handover to the courier.

---

## How to download an invoice for an order

Go to Orders, find the specific order, click on it to open order details, and click 'Download Invoice.' You can also bulk download invoices by selecting multiple orders and choosing 'Download Invoice' from bulk actions.

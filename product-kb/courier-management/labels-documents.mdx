## Is it possible to reassign my shipment to a different courier partner once the manifest has been generated?

* Yes, it is possible to reassign your shipment to a different courier partner even after the manifest has been generated, as long as the shipment status has not reached 'Pickup Done' or a later stage.
* To reassign, locate the order in your Shiprocket panel, select the 'Reassign Courier' option, choose a new courier, and generate a new AWB.
* Note: Reassigning a courier after manifest generation may result in delays as you will need to generate a new manifest.

## How to download shipping labels in bulk?

Go to the Orders section, select the orders you want to print labels for using the checkboxes, then click Bulk Actions >> Download Labels. All labels will be downloaded as a single PDF.

---

## How to download a manifest?

Go to the Manifests section from the Tools menu. Find the manifest for the required date and click Download. The manifest will be downloaded as a PDF.

---

## How to download invoice for an order?

Go to Orders, find the specific order, click on it to open order details, and click 'Download Invoice.' You can also bulk download invoices by selecting multiple orders and choosing 'Download Invoice' from bulk actions.

---

## What is a shipping label and what information does it contain?

A shipping label is a document attached to the package that contains all the information needed for delivery:
* Sender and recipient details
* AWB (tracking) number and barcode
* Order details and product description
* Weight and dimensions
* Payment type (COD/Prepaid)
* Courier name
* Delivery address and pincode

---

## How to print labels for multiple orders at once?

Select the orders in the New Orders or Ready to Ship section using checkboxes. Click Bulk Actions >> Download Labels to download all labels in a single PDF for bulk printing.

---

## What is an E-way bill and when is it required?

An e-way bill is a document required for movement of goods worth more than ₹50,000 under GST. It is electronically generated and must accompany the shipment. You can add the e-way bill number when creating orders in Shiprocket using the `ewaybill_no` field.

---

## Can I generate labels before scheduling pickup?

Yes. Once you assign a courier and generate an AWB, the shipping label is immediately available for download. You don't need to wait for pickup scheduling to print and attach labels to your packages.

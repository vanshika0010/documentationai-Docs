## How to customize shipping labels

Go to Settings >> Label Customization from the left menu. You can add your brand logo, choose label size, and configure which information to display on the label. All new labels will use the customized format.

---

## What sizes of shipping labels are available

Shiprocket supports A4, A5, and 4x6 inch thermal labels. Select your preferred size in the Label Customization settings.

---

## Can I add custom fields to my shipping label

Yes. Custom fields like product name, HSN code, or other order-specific information can be added through the Label Customization settings.

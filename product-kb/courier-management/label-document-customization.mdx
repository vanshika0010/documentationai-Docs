<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to customize shipping labels?">

Go to Settings >> Label Customization from the left menu. You can add your brand logo, choose label size, and configure which information to display on the label. Save your settings and all new labels will use the customized format.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What sizes of shipping labels are available?">

Shiprocket supports multiple label sizes including A4, A5, and 4x6 inch thermal labels. You can select your preferred size in the Label Customization settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add custom fields to my shipping label?">

Yes, you can add custom fields like product name, HSN code, or any other order-specific information to your label through the Label Customization settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

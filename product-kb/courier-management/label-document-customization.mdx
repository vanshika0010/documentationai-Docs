## why am I getting a different format label for amazon courier?

For Amazon courier, you can only use the Amazon standard label format. Customization of Amazon courier labels is not supported as Amazon mandates their own label format for all shipments.

---

## How to customize shipping labels on Shiprocket?

Go to Settings >> Label Customization from the left menu. You can:
* Add your brand logo
* Choose label size (A4, A5, 4x6 thermal)
* Show/hide Shiprocket branding
* Configure which fields to display (product details, HSN, dimensions, etc.)
* Add custom fields
* Configure barcode/QR code options

---

## What label sizes are available on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket supports:
* A4 label
* A5 label
* 4x6 inch thermal label

Select your preferred size in Settings >> Label Customization.

---

## Can I add my company logo to shipping labels?

Yes. Go to Settings >> Label Customization and upload your brand logo. The logo will appear on all shipping labels generated for your orders.

---

## How to generate a manifest on Shiprocket?

A manifest is a document listing all shipments handed over to the courier in a single pickup. To generate a manifest:
1. Select orders that are ready for pickup.
2. Click on 'Generate Manifest'.
3. The manifest will be generated and can be downloaded as a PDF.
The manifest serves as proof of handover to the courier.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to schedule a pickup in Shiprocket?">

After assigning a courier to your order, you can schedule a pickup by clicking 'Schedule Pickup' from the order details page. Select the pickup address, date, and expected number of shipments. The courier will arrive at your location on the scheduled date.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the pickup timings for couriers?">

Pickup timings vary by courier and location. Generally, pickups are scheduled for the next business day. Some couriers offer same-day or time-slotted pickups in select cities.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to schedule a pickup in Shiprocket

After assigning a courier to your order, click 'Schedule Pickup' from the order details page. Select the pickup address, date, and expected number of shipments. The courier will arrive at your location on the scheduled date.

---

## What are the pickup timings for couriers

Pickup timings vary by courier and location. Generally, pickups are scheduled for the next business day. Some couriers offer same-day or time-slotted pickups in select cities.

## What is the minimum time possible for a pickup?

Shiprocket has launched instant pickup by ShadowFax where a pickup can happen within 1 hour of scheduling. For other couriers, pickups are generally scheduled for the next business day.

---

## How to schedule a pickup in Shiprocket?

After assigning a courier to your order (AWB generation), go to Ready to Ship. Select orders and click 'Generate Pickup.' Choose pickup address, date, and expected number of shipments. The courier will arrive at your location on the scheduled date.

---

## What are the pickup timings for couriers?

Pickup timings vary by courier and location. Generally, pickups are scheduled for the next business day. Some couriers offer same-day or time-slotted pickups in select cities. Shadowfax Instant offers 3-hour pickup windows (morning, afternoon, or evening).

---

## What happens if the courier doesn't come for pickup?

If the courier doesn't arrive on the scheduled pickup date, you can reschedule the pickup from the Ready to Ship section. If the issue persists, raise a support ticket with the pickup request details.

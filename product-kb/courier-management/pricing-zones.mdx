## How to set courier priority?

Follow the listed below steps to set courier priority on Shiprocket website:
* Click on the 'Settings' option from the left menu.
* Navigate to the courier category and click on the 'Courier Priority' option.
* In the Courier Priority page, you can see the list of active couriers along with their delivery time and estimated cost.
* To enable a courier, toggle the 'Enabled' button to activate it or disable as needed.
* To set the priority, drag the couriers by clicking and holding the 'drag icon' to arrange them in the order of priority you want.
* Once you're done, click on the 'Save' button to save your courier priority setting.

## How to set Courier Rules?

Courier Rules is a great feature for those who want to split their courier assignment based on specific conditions. This saves you from a lot of hassles. Give it a try!

Follow the steps below to set the Courier Rules on Shiprocket website:
* Click on the 'Settings' option from the left menu.
* Navigate to the courier category and click on the 'Courier Rules' option.
* In the Courier Rules page, click on the 'Create Rule' button.
* First, name your rule, e.g., 'Assign BlueDart to Delhi COD Orders'.
* Next, define the conditions for applying this rule by clicking 'Add Condition'.
* Choose the order attributes to set as the condition. These attributes can be related to Payment Mode, Weight, Zone, Order Value, State, Pincode, City, Dangerous Goods, Product SKU, or AWB Assigned Time.
* Set the value for the selected condition and click 'Apply'.
* After setting the conditions, choose the courier to be assigned when these conditions are met.
* Optionally, you can set an Alternate Courier in case the primary courier is unavailable.
* Finally, click 'Save' to activate your new courier rule.

## What is Courier Priority?

Courier priority enables you to set a priority list of courier partners based on cost, courier rating, and delivery speed or the custom courier priority of your choice. If you want us to decide, then you can choose the 'Shiprocket Recommended' option. This setting helps you to ship orders through your preferred courier partners every time.

## I can find the courier serviceable in the rate calculator but it is not showing up when I ship the order.

There could be multiple reasons why a courier service is not showing up as serviceable when you are shipping the order compared to when you check in the rate calculator. Some of these reasons include:
* The courier may not be active. Please check if the courier is active in your courier settings.
* Insufficient wallet balance to assign the courier.
* The order may have a COD payment mode while the courier may not support COD.
* The courier may not be supporting the weight of the order.
* The courier may have specific product restrictions.
* There may be operational constraints or the courier may have stopped operations in that region.

## How to Download Bulk LM Labels?

To download Bulk LM lables, follow these steps on your Shiprocket website:
1. Go to the Bulk Action section under Tools.
2. Select the Tracking (AWB) Number option.
3. Enter or paste the list of tracking numbers for the shipments you want to download the LM labels for.
4. Click on the Download LM Labels button to initiate the download.
5. The LM labels for the selected tracking numbers will be downloaded in bulk.

## Explaining shipping zones: How shipment zones affect order fulfillment and rates?

Courier companies have divided all serviceable pin codes into multiple shipping zones. Each shipping zone has multiple sets of pin codes and a definite definition. Your shipping metrics like Freight (weight x rate), Fuel Surcharge, COD Charges, and more are affected based on the zones.

Shipping Zone Categories:
* Local: Shipments delivered within the same city
* Zonal A: Shipments delivered within the same state
* Zonal B: Shipments delivered across states but within the same geographical zone
* National A: Shipments delivered anywhere across India
* National B: Shipments delivered to specific distant locations
* Special: Shipments to J&K, North East states, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep

## Can Shiprocket pick shipments for multiple courier partners in one go?

Yes. One Shiprocket rider can collect shipments assigned to different courier partners in a single pickup.
You do not need to coordinate separately with each courier.

If your pincode is eligible:

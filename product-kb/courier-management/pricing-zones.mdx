## How is the shipping rate calculated

Shipping rates are calculated based on:

* Weight (actual or volumetric, whichever is higher)
* Zone (distance between pickup and delivery pincode): Within City, Within State, Metro to Metro, Rest of India
* Courier partner's pricing
* Payment type (COD or Prepaid)

---

## How to check shipping rates or compare courier charges

Use the Rate Calculator in your Shiprocket panel. Enter origin pincode, destination pincode, and package weight to see rates across all active couriers.

---

## What is an ODA pincode

ODA (Out of Delivery Area) pincodes are locations that are difficult to service and may incur additional charges or have limited courier options. Check ODA pincodes for each courier from the Courier Management settings.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the shipping rate calculated?">

Shipping rates are calculated based on the weight of the shipment (actual or volumetric, whichever is higher) and the zone (distance between pickup and delivery pincode). Zones are typically: Within City, Within State, Metro to Metro, Rest of India.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to check shipping rates or compare courier charges?">

You can use the Rate Calculator in your Shiprocket panel to compare shipping rates across couriers. Go to the Dashboard and find the Rate Calculator tool, enter origin pincode, destination pincode, and package weight to see rates.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an ODA pincode?">

ODA stands for Out of Delivery Area. These are pincodes that are difficult to service and may incur additional charges or have limited courier options. You can check ODA pincodes for each courier from the Courier Management settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What are the shipping zones in Shiprocket?

Shiprocket categorizes shipments into the following zones based on origin and destination:
* Within City (Local): Same city deliveries
* Within State (Zonal A): Same state deliveries
* Metro to Metro (Zonal B): Between metro cities
* Rest of India (National): All other inter-state deliveries
* Special Zones: J&K, North East, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep

---

## How to check the shipping rate for a specific courier?

You can check shipping rates by using the Rate Calculator available on the Shiprocket dashboard. Go to Dashboard >> Rate Calculator, enter the origin pincode, destination pincode, weight, and dimensions to see rates across all active couriers.

---

## What is COD charge and how is it calculated?

COD (Cash on Delivery) charge is an additional fee charged for COD shipments. It is calculated as a percentage of the order value or a flat fee, depending on the courier partner. The COD charge is added to the shipping rate.

---

## How to add additional charges for COD orders?

You cannot add additional charges for COD orders directly on Shiprocket. The COD charges are determined by the courier partner. However, you can configure COD charges on your checkout page using Shiprocket Checkout.

---

## What is the minimum weight for a shipment on Shiprocket?

The minimum chargeable weight for most courier partners on Shiprocket is 0.5 kg (500 grams). Even if your shipment weighs less, you will be charged for the minimum weight.

---

## How to get a better shipping rate on Shiprocket?

You can get better shipping rates on Shiprocket by:
1. Shipping higher volumes - higher volume leads to better negotiated rates.
2. Using the right packaging to minimize volumetric weight.
3. Choosing the right courier for each route based on performance and cost.
4. Upgrading to higher Shiprocket plans which offer better rates.
5. Reaching out to your Shiprocket account manager for custom rate negotiations.

---

## What is an ODA (Out of Delivery Area) pincode?

ODA (Out of Delivery Area) pincodes are locations that are considered difficult to service by courier partners. Shipments to ODA pincodes may attract additional surcharges or may have limited courier options. You can check ODA pincodes for each courier in the courier settings.

---

## How does the fuel surcharge affect my shipping cost?

Fuel surcharge is an additional charge levied by courier partners to offset the cost of fuel. It is typically calculated as a percentage of the base shipping rate. The fuel surcharge may vary by courier and is subject to change.

---

## What are the extra charges I should be aware of in Shiprocket?

Extra charges in Shiprocket include:
* COD charges - for cash on delivery shipments
* Fuel surcharge - additional fuel cost charge
* ODA charges - for out of delivery area pincodes
* Weight discrepancy charges - if courier measures higher weight
* Re-attempt charges - for additional delivery attempts
* RTO charges - for return to origin shipments

---

## How to check if a pincode is serviceable on Shiprocket?

You can check pincode serviceability by using the Serviceability Checker available on the Shiprocket dashboard. Go to Dashboard >> Serviceability Checker, enter the origin pincode, destination pincode, and weight to see which couriers service that route.

---

## What is special zone surcharge?

Special zone surcharge is an additional charge for shipments to special zones like J&K, North East states, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, and Lakshadweep. These zones are considered difficult to service and attract higher shipping rates.

---

## How to check the estimated delivery time for a shipment?

You can check the estimated delivery time using the Rate Calculator on the Shiprocket dashboard. The ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) is shown along with the shipping rate for each courier.

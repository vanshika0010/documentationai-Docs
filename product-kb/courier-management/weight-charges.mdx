## What is Dead Weight and Volumetric Weight

Dead weight is the actual physical weight of the package as measured on a scale.

Volumetric weight is calculated as: **(Length cm × Breadth cm × Height cm) ÷ 5000**

The higher of the two values is used for billing. Always ensure your package dimensions are entered accurately to avoid unexpected charges.

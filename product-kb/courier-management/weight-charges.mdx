## What is Dead Weight and Volumetric Weight?

Dead Weight is the actual physical weight of the parcel.
Volumetric Weight is calculated as: (Length in cm * Width in cm * Height in cm) / 5000

The chargeable weight is the higher of the actual dead weight or the volumetric weight.

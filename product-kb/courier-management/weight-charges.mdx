  <Accordion title="What is Dead weight and Volumetric Weight?">

Dead weight is the actual physical weight of the package as measured on a weighing scale. Volumetric weight is calculated based on the package dimensions: (Length × Breadth × Height) / 5000. The higher of the two values (dead weight vs. volumetric weight) is used for billing.

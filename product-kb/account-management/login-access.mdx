## Can I Edit the module access of my account's users?

Yes, you can edit access for sub users of your account. Follow these steps:
1. Go to 'Settings' >> 'Manage Users'
2. Click on the user whose access you want to edit
3. Update the module permissions
4. Save the changes

---

## How to login to Shiprocket panel?

To login to Shiprocket:
1. Go to app.shiprocket.in
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click Login
5. Complete 2FA if enabled on your account

---

## What to do if I'm locked out of my Shiprocket account?

If you're locked out:
1. Click 'Forgot Password' on the login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Follow the password reset instructions sent to your email
4. If you don't have access to your registered email, contact support@shiprocket.in

---

## How to login to Shiprocket mobile app?

To login to the Shiprocket mobile app:
1. Download the Shiprocket app (iOS/Android)
2. Open the app
3. Enter your registered email and password
4. Complete OTP verification if required
5. You're logged in

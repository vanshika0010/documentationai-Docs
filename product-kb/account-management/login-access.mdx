## How to reset my Shiprocket password

Go to app.shiprocket.in and click 'Forgot Password.' Enter your registered email address. Check your email and follow the reset link to create a new password.

---

## How to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> Security from the left menu. Enable 2FA and choose your preferred method (SMS OTP or Authenticator app). Follow the setup steps and save.

## Where can I find my seller id/company id?

You can check your company ID by following these steps:

1. Go to Settings from the left navigation panel.
2. Click on Company Setup or Company Profile.
3. Your Seller ID / Company ID will be displayed there.

---

## What documents are required for KYC on Shiprocket?

KYC documents required:
1. PAN Card (mandatory for all entity types)
2. Aadhaar Card (for individual/proprietorship)
3. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
4. GST Certificate (if applicable)
5. Business registration documents (for companies/LLPs)

---

## How to complete KYC on Shiprocket?

To complete KYC:
1. Go to Settings >> KYC Verification
2. Select your business entity type
3. Upload the required documents
4. Submit for verification
Shiprocket team reviews KYC within 24-48 business hours.

---

## What happens if my KYC is rejected?

If KYC is rejected:
1. You will receive an email with the rejection reason
2. Review the reason and correct the issue
3. Re-upload the correct/updated documents
4. Resubmit for verification

Common rejection reasons: blurry documents, mismatched name/address, expired documents.

---

## How long does KYC verification take on Shiprocket?

KYC verification typically takes 24-48 business hours after all documents are submitted. You will receive an email confirmation once KYC is approved.

---

## Can I start shipping before KYC is complete on Shiprocket?

You can create orders and process shipments, but COD remittance withdrawal to bank account requires completed KYC. Shipping itself may not be blocked, but financial operations require KYC.

---

## What is the GST requirement for KYC on Shiprocket?

GST is not mandatory for KYC on Shiprocket. However, providing your GSTIN enables:
1. GST invoices for claiming Input Tax Credit
2. Access to plans that require GST verification (e.g., Rs. 99 plan)

---

## How to update KYC documents on Shiprocket?

To update KYC documents:
1. Go to Settings >> KYC Verification
2. Find the document section you want to update
3. Upload the new document
4. Submit for re-verification

---

## What is entity type in Shiprocket KYC?

Entity type refers to your business structure:
1. Individual/Proprietorship
2. Partnership
3. Private Limited Company
4. Public Limited Company
5. LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)
6. Trust/Society

Choose the correct entity type to ensure the right documents are requested.

---

## What is PAN verification on Shiprocket?

PAN (Permanent Account Number) verification confirms your identity and enables financial transactions on Shiprocket, including COD remittance to bank accounts.

---

## How to verify bank account on Shiprocket?

To verify bank account:
1. Go to Settings >> Bank Details or KYC
2. Enter your bank account number and IFSC code
3. Upload a cancelled cheque or bank statement
4. Submit for verification
Shiprocket team verifies bank details within 24-48 business hours.

---

## Why is my bank account verification failing on Shiprocket?

Common reasons for bank verification failure:
1. Account number or IFSC code mismatch
2. Name on bank account doesn't match PAN/company name
3. Cancelled cheque image is blurry or incomplete
4. Wrong bank document uploaded

Correct the specific issue and resubmit.

---

## Can I add multiple bank accounts on Shiprocket?

Yes, you can add multiple bank accounts. However, COD remittance will go to the primary/verified bank account. Contact support to change the primary bank account.

---

## What is Aadhaar verification on Shiprocket?

Aadhaar verification confirms your identity for KYC purposes. It is required for Individual/Proprietorship accounts and helps verify your address and identity details.

---

## How to update my GSTIN on Shiprocket?

To update GSTIN:
1. Go to Settings >> Company Setup >> Billing, Invoice & GSTIN
2. Enter or update your GSTIN
3. Upload the GST certificate
4. Save changes

---

## How long is KYC valid on Shiprocket?

KYC verification doesn't have a specific expiry. However, if your business details change significantly (company name change, new PAN, etc.), you should update your KYC documents.

---

## What is the difference between KYC pending and KYC rejected on Shiprocket?

KYC Pending: Documents have been submitted and are awaiting review by the Shiprocket team.

KYC Rejected: Documents were reviewed but rejected due to issues. You need to correct and resubmit.

---

## What documents are needed for company (Pvt Ltd) KYC on Shiprocket?

For Private Limited Company:
1. PAN Card of the company
2. Certificate of Incorporation
3. GST Certificate (if registered)
4. Bank account details in the company's name
5. Director's PAN and Aadhaar (may be required)

---

## Can I change my entity type after KYC on Shiprocket?

Changing entity type after KYC approval requires re-verification. Contact Shiprocket support to initiate the entity type change process.

---

## What is address proof required for KYC on Shiprocket?

Accepted address proof documents:
1. Aadhaar Card
2. Passport
3. Voter ID
4. Driving License
5. Utility bills (electricity, water - recent)

---

## Why is my KYC showing under review for a long time?

If KYC is under review for more than 48 business hours:
1. Check your email for any requests for additional documents
2. Raise a support ticket with your KYC submission date
3. Contact your account manager if available

---

## Is GST mandatory for starting shipments on Shiprocket?

GST is not mandatory to start shipping on Shiprocket. However, providing GSTIN enables claiming Input Tax Credit on shipping charges and access to GST-verified seller plans.

---

## What happens to my account if KYC is not completed?

Without completed KYC:
1. COD withdrawal to bank account may be restricted
2. Some features may have limited access
3. Account may be flagged for review

Complete KYC as soon as possible for full account functionality.

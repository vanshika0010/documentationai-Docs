## What KYC documents are required for Shiprocket

Required KYC documents:

* PAN card
* GST certificate (if registered)
* Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
* A valid government ID

KYC is required for COD remittance and shipping activation.

---

## How to complete KYC on Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> KYC from the left menu. Upload the required documents. Verification typically takes 24–48 hours.

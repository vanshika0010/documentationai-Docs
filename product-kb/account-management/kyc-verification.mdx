<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What KYC documents are required for Shiprocket?">

Required: PAN card, GST certificate (if registered), bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement), and a valid government ID. KYC is needed for COD remittance and shipping activation.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to complete KYC on Shiprocket?">

Go to Settings >> KYC from the left menu. Upload the required documents. Verification typically takes 24-48 hours.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

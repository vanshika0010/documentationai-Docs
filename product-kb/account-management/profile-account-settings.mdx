## How to update company details in Shiprocket

Go to Settings >> Company from the left menu. Update your company name, address, GSTIN, PAN, and logo, then save.

---

## How to add sub-users to my Shiprocket account

Go to Settings >> Users & Teams. Click 'Add User,' enter the email, select the role (Admin, Manager, etc.), and send the invitation. The user will receive an email to set their password.

---

## How to change the role or permissions of a sub-user

Go to Settings >> Users & Teams. Click 'Edit' on the user whose role you want to change. Modify the role or toggle specific module access, then save.

## How do I get the in-person assistance or POC details or KAM details?

For getting in-person assistance connect with your KAM. Follow the below steps to connect with your KAM:
* Go to 'Settings' >> 'Company Profile'.
* You will find your KAM details in the 'My Account Manager' section.

---

## How to change or reset my password on Shiprocket?

To change or reset your password:
1. Go to the Shiprocket login page
2. Click on 'Forgot Password'
3. Enter your registered email address
4. You will receive a password reset link on your email
5. Click the link and set a new password

Alternatively, if you're logged in:
1. Go to Settings >> Profile
2. Click on Change Password
3. Enter current password and new password
4. Save changes

---

## How to update company details on Shiprocket?

To update company details:
1. Go to Settings from the left menu
2. Click on Company Setup or Company Profile
3. Update the required fields (company name, address, website, etc.)
4. Save changes

---

## How to add a sub-user to my Shiprocket account?

To add a sub-user:
1. Go to Settings >> Manage Users
2. Click on 'Add User' or 'Invite User'
3. Enter the email address and name of the new user
4. Select the role/permissions for the user
5. Send the invitation
The new user will receive an email invitation to join your account.

---

## How to manage sub-user permissions on Shiprocket?

To manage sub-user permissions:
1. Go to Settings >> Manage Users
2. Find the user whose permissions you want to modify
3. Click on Edit or the user's name
4. Update the module access and permissions
5. Save changes

---

## Can I have multiple users on one Shiprocket account?

Yes. Shiprocket supports multiple sub-users under one account. Each sub-user can be given specific permissions for different modules (orders, finance, reports, etc.).

---

## How to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on Shiprocket?

To enable 2FA:
1. Go to Settings >> Security or Profile
2. Find the Two-Factor Authentication option
3. Enable it
4. Follow the setup instructions (usually involves linking an authenticator app or phone number)
5. 2FA will be required for all future logins

---

## How to update my registered email address on Shiprocket?

To update your registered email:
1. Go to Settings >> Profile
2. Find the email field
3. Enter the new email address
4. Verify the new email (Shiprocket may send a verification email)

Note: Some email change requests may require support assistance for security reasons.

---

## How to update my phone number on Shiprocket?

To update your phone number:
1. Go to Settings >> Profile
2. Find the phone number field
3. Enter the new phone number
4. Verify with OTP
5. Save changes

---

## How to add a logo to my Shiprocket account?

To add your company logo:
1. Go to Settings >> Company Setup or Company Profile
2. Find the Logo section
3. Upload your logo (PNG/JPG format recommended)
4. Save
Your logo will appear on shipping labels and invoices.

---

## How to change my primary pickup address on Shiprocket?

To change primary pickup address:
1. Go to Settings >> Manage Warehouses
2. Find the warehouse you want to set as primary
3. Click on 'Set as Primary'
All new orders will default to this pickup address.

---

## How to deactivate a sub-user on Shiprocket?

To deactivate a sub-user:
1. Go to Settings >> Manage Users
2. Find the user to deactivate
3. Click on Deactivate or remove the user
4. Confirm the action
The user will no longer have access to your account.

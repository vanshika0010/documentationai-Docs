<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to update company details in Shiprocket?">

Go to Settings >> Company from the left menu. Update your company name, address, GSTIN, PAN, and logo, then save.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to add sub-users to my Shiprocket account?">

Go to Settings >> Users & Teams. Click 'Add User,' enter the email, select the role, and send the invitation. The user will receive an email to set their password.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How do I get training or demo for Shiprocket panel?

To get training or a demo for the Shiprocket panel:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager
2. Request a walkthrough session
3. Access video tutorials on help.shiprocket.in
4. Watch the onboarding videos available in the Help section
5. For new features, Shiprocket regularly hosts webinars - check announcements

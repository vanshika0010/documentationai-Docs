## How do I get training or demo for Shiprocket panel?

To get a training or demo:
* Contact your Shiprocket account manager
* They will schedule a walkthrough session for you
* You can also access video tutorials and help guides at help.shiprocket.in
* For new users, Shiprocket offers onboarding assistance through the panel

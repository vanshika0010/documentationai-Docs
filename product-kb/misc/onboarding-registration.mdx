## How do I register as a seller at Shiprocket? How do I start using Shiprocket?

To register as a seller on Shiprocket:
1. Go to app.shiprocket.in
2. Click on Sign Up / Register
3. Enter your email address and create a password
4. Verify your email
5. Complete your profile with:
   - Company name
   - Phone number
   - Address and pincode
6. Complete KYC verification
7. Add a pickup address
8. Recharge your wallet
9. Start creating orders!

---

## What documents are needed to register on Shiprocket?

Documents needed for Shiprocket registration and KYC:
1. PAN card (mandatory)
2. Aadhaar card
3. Bank account details (for COD withdrawal)
4. GST certificate (if applicable)
5. Business registration documents (if applicable)

KYC can be completed from Settings >> KYC Verification in your Shiprocket panel.

## How do I register as a seller at Shiprocket? How do I start shipping here?

To start shipping with Shiprocket, follow these steps:
* Fill Your Company Details: Add your company name, website address, billing and shipping address, and bank account details.
* Complete KYC Verification: Ensure that your KYC (Know Your Customer) process is completed for account verification.
* Add Products: Add your products to the Shiprocket catalogue to streamline future shipping processes.
* Add Warehouse: Provide details of your warehouse or storage locations to manage inventory efficiently.
* Explore Plans: Review and choose from Shiprocket's shipping plans to find the one that best fits your business needs.
* Start Shipping: Once everything is set up, you can start creating orders and shipping them.

## Who should use Shiprocket?

Shiprocket is designed for e-commerce businesses and online sellers who need a reliable and cost-effective shipping solution. It is particularly useful for:
* Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) looking to streamline their shipping operations.
* Online sellers on platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, and other e-commerce marketplaces.
* Direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands looking to reach customers across India.
* Businesses looking to expand their reach with pan-India shipping and international shipping options.

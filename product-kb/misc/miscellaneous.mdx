## What items are prohibited to ship via Shiprocket?

Prohibited items on Shiprocket include:
1. Hazardous materials (explosives, flammables, corrosives, radioactive)
2. Narcotics, drugs, and controlled substances
3. Weapons, ammunition, and firearms
4. Currency, negotiable instruments, and precious stones
5. Live animals and plants
6. Human remains or body parts
7. Perishable items (unless specific refrigerated courier is chosen)
8. Items prohibited by Indian or destination country laws
9. Pornographic material
10. Items subject to trade sanctions or embargoes

Check the specific courier's terms for additional prohibited items.

## What does AWB stand for in Shiprocket?

AWB stands for Air Waybill. In Shiprocket, it's the unique tracking number assigned to each shipment by the courier partner.

---

## What does RTO mean in Shiprocket?

RTO stands for Return to Origin. It means the shipment couldn't be delivered and is being returned to the seller's pickup address.

---

## What does NDR mean in Shiprocket?

NDR stands for Non-Delivery Report. It is generated when a delivery attempt fails and requires action from the seller.

---

## What does COD mean in Shiprocket?

COD stands for Cash on Delivery. The buyer pays cash to the courier agent at the time of delivery.

---

## What is Shiprocket Co-Pilot?

Shiprocket Co-Pilot is an AI-powered assistant integrated into the Shiprocket panel that helps sellers with quick answers, order tracking, and navigation guidance.

---

## What does ODA stand for in Shiprocket?

ODA stands for Out of Delivery Area. These are remote locations that attract additional charges from courier partners.

---

## What does SLA stand for in Shiprocket?

SLA stands for Service Level Agreement. It defines the expected timelines for various services like delivery, support resolution, etc.

---

## What is ECOD in Shiprocket?

ECOD stands for Early Cash on Delivery. It's a feature that allows sellers to receive their COD remittance faster (within D+2, D+3, or D+4 days) instead of waiting for the standard 7-10 day cycle.

---

## What is Shiprocket's GST number?

For Shiprocket's GSTIN, refer to your freight invoice which shows Shiprocket Pvt Ltd's complete billing and tax registration details.

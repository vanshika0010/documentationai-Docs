## What is Shiprocket Co-pilot?

Shiprocket Co-pilot is an AI-powered assistant integrated into the Shiprocket panel that helps sellers with:
1. Quick answers to shipping queries
2. Order tracking and status updates
3. Rate calculations
4. Troubleshooting common issues
5. Guided navigation through the platform

## What items are prohibited to ship via Shiprocket?

Prohibited items on Shiprocket include:
1. Hazardous materials (explosives, flammables, corrosives)
2. Narcotics and drugs
3. Weapons and ammunition
4. Perishable items (unless specific courier is chosen)
5. Currency and negotiable instruments
6. Live animals
7. Human remains
8. Items prohibited by law
9. Radioactive materials

Check specific courier terms for additional prohibited items.

## What does AWB stand for in Shiprocket?

AWB stands for Air Waybill. In Shiprocket context, it refers to the unique tracking number assigned to each shipment by the courier partner.

---

## What does RTO stand for in Shiprocket?

RTO stands for Return to Origin. It means the shipment couldn't be delivered and is being returned to the seller's pickup location.

---

## What does NDR stand for in Shiprocket?

NDR stands for Non-Delivery Report. It is generated when a delivery attempt fails due to reasons like buyer not available, wrong address, or refused delivery.

---

## What does COD stand for in Shiprocket?

COD stands for Cash on Delivery. It is a payment mode where the buyer pays cash to the courier agent at the time of delivery.

---

## What does SLA stand for in Shiprocket?

SLA stands for Service Level Agreement. It defines the expected timelines and performance standards for various services.

---

## What does ODA stand for in Shiprocket?

ODA stands for Out of Delivery Area. These are locations considered remote or difficult to service, attracting additional charges from courier partners.

---

## What is the Shiprocket API?

Shiprocket provides a comprehensive API that allows sellers to:
1. Create and manage orders programmatically
2. Generate AWBs and shipping labels
3. Track shipments
4. Manage returns
5. Access rate calculations

API documentation is available at the Shiprocket developer portal.

---

## What is Shiprocket's GST number?

For Shiprocket's GSTIN, refer to your freight invoice or contact support. The invoice will show Shiprocket Pvt Ltd's registration details.

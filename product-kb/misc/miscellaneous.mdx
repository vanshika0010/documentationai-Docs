<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What items are prohibited from shipping on Shiprocket?">

Prohibited items include: hazardous materials, flammable liquids, weapons and ammunition, live animals, perishables without proper packaging, counterfeit goods, and items banned by specific couriers. Check the full prohibited items list in the Shiprocket help center.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Seller Co-pilot in Shiprocket?">

Seller Co-pilot is the seller-facing knowledge base and AI assistant in Shiprocket that helps sellers find answers to common questions about shipping, orders, and platform features without contacting support.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to raise a support ticket in Shiprocket?">

Go to Help & Support from the left menu. Click 'Create Ticket' at the top-right corner. Select the Category and Sub-category for your issue, fill in the required details, and click 'Create Ticket.' You will receive email updates on your ticket status.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to track my support ticket status?">

Go to Help & Support from the left menu. Under 'My Tickets' you can view all your open and closed tickets along with their current status and agent responses.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## how can i check for ticket with pending response?

You can check for tickets with a pending response by following these steps:
* Go to the Help & Support section from the left menu.
* Click on "My Tickets" to view all your support tickets.
* Look for tickets with "Awaiting Response" or "Pending" status.
* These are the tickets where a response is pending.

---

## How to raise a ticket on Shiprocket?

To raise a support ticket on Shiprocket:
* Go to Help & Support from the left menu.
* Click on "Raise a Ticket".
* Select the category of your issue.
* Fill in the details of your issue.
* Attach any supporting screenshots or documents if needed.
* Click Submit.
You will receive a ticket number and email confirmation.

## How to check the status of my support ticket?

To check ticket status:
* Go to Help & Support from the left menu.
* Click on "My Tickets".
* You can see the status of all your tickets here.
* Click on a specific ticket to view the full conversation history and current status.

---

## What are the different ticket status types on Shiprocket?

Ticket statuses:
* Open: Ticket has been submitted and is awaiting agent response
* In Progress: Agent is working on the issue
* Awaiting Response: Waiting for your reply
* Resolved: Issue has been resolved
* Closed: Ticket has been closed after resolution

---

## How to escalate a support ticket?

If your issue is not resolved within the expected time:
* Reply to the existing ticket requesting escalation
* Mention your ticket number and the urgency
* You can also contact your dedicated account manager if available

---

## What information should I include in a support ticket?

For faster resolution, include:
1. Order ID or AWB number (if applicable)
2. Description of the issue
3. Screenshots of error messages
4. Steps you have already tried
5. Date and time of the issue

---

## How long does Shiprocket take to respond to support tickets?

First response time:
* Standard tickets: 24-48 hours
* High priority tickets: 4-8 hours
* For immediate assistance, use the live chat option if available

---

## Can I reopen a closed support ticket?

Yes. If your issue was not fully resolved:
* Go to Help & Support >> My Tickets
* Find the closed ticket
* Reply to the closed ticket with your follow-up
* The ticket will be reopened automatically

---

## What is Shiprocket's SLA for ticket resolution?

Resolution SLAs vary by issue type:
* Billing/Wallet: 2-3 business days
* Shipment issues: 3-5 business days
* Weight disputes: 7-10 business days
* Technical issues: 1-3 business days

## Can I track the progress of my issue in the support ticket?

Yes. All updates from the support team are added as comments to your ticket. You receive email notifications for each update. Log in to the panel and go to Help & Support >> My Tickets to see real-time progress.

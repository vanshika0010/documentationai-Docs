## how can i check for ticket with pending response?

To check tickets with pending response:
1. Go to Help section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Click on Support Tickets or My Tickets
3. Filter by status "Pending" or "Awaiting Response"
4. You can see all tickets where you haven't received a response yet

---

## How to raise a support ticket on Shiprocket?

To raise a support ticket:
1. Go to Help section from the left menu
2. Click on Raise Ticket or Create Ticket
3. Select the category relevant to your issue
4. Provide order details or AWB number (if applicable)
5. Describe the issue in detail
6. Attach supporting screenshots or documents
7. Submit the ticket

---

## What are the support hours for Shiprocket?

Shiprocket customer support is available:
- Monday to Saturday: 9 AM to 9 PM
- Sunday: 10 AM to 6 PM

For critical issues, 24/7 support may be available through specific channels.

---

## How to track the status of my support ticket?

To track support ticket status:
1. Go to Help >> My Tickets
2. Find your ticket by ticket number or date
3. View current status and any responses
4. You can add comments or additional information to the ticket

---

## What is the resolution SLA for support tickets on Shiprocket?

Resolution SLAs vary by issue category:
- Wallet/billing issues: 2-3 business days
- Shipment queries: 3-5 business days
- Weight disputes: 7-10 business days

Priority tickets on higher plans may have faster SLAs.

---

## How to escalate a support ticket on Shiprocket?

To escalate an unresolved ticket:
1. Reply to the existing ticket requesting escalation
2. Contact your dedicated account manager (if on higher plan)
3. Use the escalation matrix provided in Shiprocket's help section

---

## How do I contact Shiprocket customer support?

Contacting Shiprocket support:
1. In-panel support ticket: Help section >> Raise Ticket
2. Email: support@shiprocket.com
3. Phone: Available for higher plan customers
4. Chat support: Available in panel during business hours
5. Account manager: For Enterprise/Growth plan customers

---

## How to check if my ticket has been resolved on Shiprocket?

Ticket resolution can be checked:
1. Go to Help >> My Tickets
2. Resolved tickets show status as "Closed" or "Resolved"
3. You receive email notification when ticket is resolved
4. If not satisfied with resolution, reopen the ticket

---

## What information should I include in a support ticket on Shiprocket?

For faster resolution, include:
1. Order ID or AWB number
2. Exact issue description
3. Steps you've already tried
4. Screenshots or error messages
5. Expected vs actual behavior
6. Date and time of the issue

---

## How long does Shiprocket take to respond to support tickets?

First response time:
- Standard: Within 24-48 hours
- Priority (higher plans): Within a few hours
- Urgent issues: Contact via phone or chat for faster response

---

## Can I reopen a closed support ticket on Shiprocket?

Yes. If your issue wasn't resolved satisfactorily:
1. Go to Help >> My Tickets >> Find closed ticket
2. Click Reopen or Reply to the closed ticket
3. Explain why the issue persists
4. The ticket will be reopened for further investigation

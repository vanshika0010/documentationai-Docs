## Will I be able to send messages manually to my buyers on Engage?

Yes, you can send messages manually to your buyers in Engage 360 using the broadcasting feature. Here's a simplified step-by-step guide:

1. Create and upload a Contact list. 
   - Create a contact list by selecting New Contact List from the Contact section in Engage 360. 
   - Provide a list name and upload a CSV file with your buyer's contact information. Ensure the CSV file has the correct columns, including the phone number in the first column (column A).
2. Create a template. Go to the templates section and create a broadcast template. 
3. Create a Broadcasting Template. Navigate to campaigns and create a broadcast template. Please note that only templates approved by WhatsApp can be used for broadcasting.
4. Schedule or Send the Broadcast

## What is the scope of Engage 360 on Shiprocket beyond NDR & Order messages?

Engage 360 on Shiprocket goes beyond NDR and order messages to offer a comprehensive set of features:

1. Marketing Campaigns: Allows businesses to run targeted marketing campaigns through WhatsApp, SMS, and Email.
2. Customer Segmentation: Engage 360 provides tools for segmenting your customer base, enabling personalized communication.
3. Chatbot Integration: It supports integration with chatbots for automated responses and customer support on WhatsApp.
4. Abandoned Cart Recovery: It can send automated messages to customers who left items in their online shopping cart, encouraging them to complete the purchase.
5. WhatsApp Flows: Enables the creation of interactive WhatsApp conversations and complex multi-step experiences within WhatsApp.
6. Analytics and Reporting: Offers detailed analytics and reporting features to track the performance of communication campaigns.

## how can you bulk import contacts in Engage?

To bulk import contacts in Engage 360, you'll need to prepare a CSV file with specific columns. Here's the list of columns:
1. Phone (Mandatory): The contact's phone number.
2. Name (Optional): The contact's name.
3. Email (Optional): The contact's email address.
4. Extra Attributes (Optional): Any additional information, labeled as "attribute1", "attribute2", etc.

To import these contacts:
1. Go to the 'Contacts' section in Engage 360.
2. Click on 'New Contact List' and name your list.
3. Upload the CSV file.
4. Map the columns in your CSV to the corresponding fields in Engage 360.
5. Click 'Import' to upload the contacts.

## What is the RTO Suite in Engage?

The RTO Suite in Engage 360 is a set of automated communication tools designed to reduce Return-to-Origin rates:
1. Order Confirmation: IVR calls and WhatsApp messages to confirm COD orders
2. Address Verification: Automated flow to verify and correct delivery addresses
3. COD to Prepaid: Convert COD orders to prepaid via WhatsApp payment links
4. NDR Management: Automated buyer communication for failed delivery attempts
5. Pre-delivery Check: Confirm buyer availability before delivery

## How does Engage 360 help recover abandoned carts?

Engage 360 detects cart abandonment events from connected platforms and automatically triggers WhatsApp/SMS/email messages to the customer with cart details and a recovery link. You can configure:
- Time delay before sending (e.g., 30 minutes, 2 hours, 24 hours)
- Number of follow-up messages
- Discount incentives in the message

## How to create an automated WhatsApp campaign on Engage?

To create an automated WhatsApp campaign in Engage 360:
1. Go to Campaigns >> Create Campaign
2. Select WhatsApp as the channel
3. Choose your customer segment
4. Select an approved WhatsApp template
5. Set the schedule (immediate or scheduled)
6. Review and launch
Monitor performance from the Campaign Analytics dashboard.

## What is the difference between a Journey and a Campaign in Engage?

Journey: Triggered by specific customer events (order placed, cart abandoned, order delivered). Automated, event-driven communication flow.

Campaign: Proactive broadcast messages sent to a selected segment at a scheduled time. Marketing-oriented, not event-triggered.

## What is the character limit for SMS messages on Engage?

Standard SMS messages have a 160-character limit per message. Messages longer than 160 characters are split into multiple SMS units and charged accordingly. Unicode characters (non-English) reduce the limit to 70 characters per unit.

## How does Engage handle WhatsApp's 24-hour session window?

WhatsApp allows free-form messaging within 24 hours of a customer's last message (customer service window). For messages outside this window, you must use Meta-approved message templates. Engage 360 automatically handles this by using appropriate templates for out-of-window messages.

## What is the difference between Utility and Marketing messages on WhatsApp Engage?

Utility Messages: Transactional in nature. Related to specific agreed-upon requests or transactions. Examples: order updates, delivery confirmations, NDR notifications. Lower cost per conversation.

Marketing Messages: Promotional content. Campaigns, offers, abandoned cart recovery, new product announcements. Higher cost per conversation but no restriction on content.

## What is the scope of engage 360 on shiprocket, beyond NDR and order messages?

Scope of Engage 360 on Shiprocket goes beyond NDR and order messages.

Engage 360 on Shiprocket goes beyond NDR and order messages to offer a comprehensive set of features:

1. Marketing Campaigns: Allows businesses to run targeted marketing campaigns through WhatsApp, SMS, and Email.
2. Customer Segmentation: Engage 360 provides tools for segmenting your customer base, enabling personalized communication.

## how can you bulk import contacts in engage?

To bulk import contacts in Engage 360, you'll need to prepare a CSV file with specific columns. Here's the list of columns:
1. Phone (Mandatory): The contact's phone number.
2. Name (Optional): The contact's name.
3. Email (Optional): The contact's email address.
4. Extra Attributes (Optional): Any additional information, labeled as "attribute1", "attribute2", etc.

To import these contacts:
1. Go to the 'Contacts' section in Engage 360.
2. Click on 'New Contact List' and name your list.
3. Upload the CSV file.
4. Map the columns in your CSV to the corresponding fields in Engage 360.
5. Click 'Import' to upload the contacts.

## What is Shiprocket Engage's NDR feature?

Shiprocket Engage's NDR (Non-Delivery Report) feature is a tool that helps in managing failed delivery attempts. When a delivery attempt fails, the system automatically sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer, asking them to provide a suitable time and date for re-delivery or to update their delivery address if it was incorrect.

---

## How does the RTO suite on Engage work?

The RTO Suite on Engage 360 operates as a set of automated processes to reduce Return to Origin (RTO) rates. It works through the following steps:
1. Order Confirmation: Engage 360 automatically sends a WhatsApp message and makes an IVR call to confirm COD orders.
2. Address Verification: Sends WhatsApp to verify delivery address before shipping.
3. COD to Prepaid: Sends payment link for COD-to-prepaid conversion.
4. Pre-delivery Reminder: Sends reminder before delivery.
5. Post NDR: Manages failed deliveries via WhatsApp.

## My COD order is confirmed on Engage, but the order is still showing as COD (not prepaid) in Shiprocket. Why?

The order confirmation on Engage just confirms the buyer's intent to receive the order. It doesn't automatically change the payment mode from COD to Prepaid in Shiprocket. The payment mode only changes if the buyer actually makes a prepaid payment via the payment link.

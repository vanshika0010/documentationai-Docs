1. Go to the 'Contacts' section in Engage 360.
2. Click on 'New Contact List' and name your list.
3. Upload the CSV file.

5. Click 'Import' to upload the contacts.

## How does the RTO suite on Engage work?

The RTO Suite on Engage 360 operates as a set of automated processes to reduce Return to Origin (RTO) rates. It works through the following steps:

1. Order Confirmation: When an order is placed, Engage 360 automatically sends a WhatsApp message and makes an IVR call to the buyer to confirm the order. If the buyer responds positively, it confirms the order. If there's no response or a negative one, it may flag the order for review.
2. Address Verification: Before the order is shipped, Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer to verify the delivery address. If the address is incorrect or needs updating, the buyer can provide the correct address, which is then updated in the system.
3. COD to Prepaid: To reduce RTO due to COD orders, Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer offering them the option to switch to a prepaid payment mode. If the buyer agrees, a payment link is sent to them.
4. Pre-delivery Reminder: Just before the delivery, Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer reminding them about the upcoming delivery. This helps in ensuring that the buyer is available to receive the delivery, reducing NDRs.
5. Post NDR: In case of a failed delivery, Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer providing options to either reschedule the delivery or update the delivery address.

## What is the difference between a campaign and an automation on Engage?

On Engage 360:
1. Campaign: A campaign is a targeted communication effort directed at a specific segment of your audience. It usually has a defined start and end date, and the same message is sent to all recipients in the target audience.
2. Automation: An automation, also known as a 'journey' or 'flow', is a series of automated messages sent in response to specific triggers or buyer actions. These could include events like a buyer placing an order, abandoning a cart, or completing a purchase.

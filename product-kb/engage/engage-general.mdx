## What is the scope of engage 360 on shiprocket, beyond NDR and order messages?

Scope of Engage 360 on Shiprocket goes beyond NDR and order messages.

Engage 360 on Shiprocket goes beyond NDR and order messages to offer a comprehensive set of features:

1. Marketing Campaigns: Allows businesses to run targeted marketing campaigns through WhatsApp, SMS, and Email.
2. Customer Segmentation: Engage 360 provides tools for segmenting your customer base, enabling personalized communication.
3. Chatbot Integration: It supports integration with chatbots for automated responses and customer support on WhatsApp.
4. Abandoned Cart Recovery: It can send automated messages to customers who left items in their online shopping cart, encouraging them to complete the purchase.
5. WhatsApp Flows: Enables the creation of interactive WhatsApp conversations and complex multi-step experiences within WhatsApp.
6. Analytics and Reporting: Offers detailed analytics and reporting features to track the performance of communication campaigns.

## how can you bulk import contacts in engage?

To bulk import contacts in Engage 360, you'll need to prepare a CSV file with specific columns. Here's the list of columns:
1. Phone (Mandatory): The contact's phone number.
2. Name (Optional): The contact's name.
3. Email (Optional): The contact's email address.
4. Extra Attributes (Optional): Any additional information, labeled as "attribute1", "attribute2", etc.

To import these contacts:
1. Go to the 'Contacts' section in Engage 360.
2. Click on 'New Contact List' and name your list.
3. Upload the CSV file.
4. Map the columns in your CSV to the corresponding fields in Engage 360.
5. Click 'Import' to upload the contacts.

## What is the limit on template creations?

On Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, there is a limit on the number of message templates you can create. The limit is 6000 templates per WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). However, the specific limit might vary based on your account's status and Meta's policies.

## My seller is not willing to opt for Engage services. What can I tell my seller about the WABA number used to send messages?

For the default (non-WABA) Engage setup, the messages are sent using Shiprocket's verified WhatsApp number (6366058044). Sellers can view this number in the message preview. For Self-WABA, the seller's own business number is used.

2. There might be a configuration issue with those channels.

## My seller says the buyer confirmed the order verbally, but the order confirmation is not showing as done on Engage.

If the buyer confirmed the order verbally but the confirmation isn't reflected in Engage, it could be due to:
1. The buyer's response might not have been captured by the system.

3. The confirmation might have been given outside the Engage messaging flow.
Please check the Engage logs for that specific order and raise a support ticket if the issue persists.

## What is the difference between a call done on engage and a normal outbound call on Engage?

On Engage 360:
- A call done through Engage: This is an IVR (Interactive Voice Response) automated call triggered by specific order events (e.g., COD order confirmation). These calls are tracked and logged in Engage.
- A normal outbound call: This refers to manually placing a call to the buyer from the Engage panel, which is possible within the 24-hour window after a buyer responds to a message.

## What is Shiprocket Engage's NDR feature?

Shiprocket Engage's NDR (Non-Delivery Report) feature, also called Delivery Boost, automatically sends WhatsApp messages to buyers when a delivery fails. It helps:
1. Communicate delivery failure to the buyer
2. Verify/update the delivery address
3. Schedule re-delivery at a convenient time
4. Reduce RTO by proactively resolving delivery issues

---

## How does the RTO suite on Engage work?

The RTO Suite on Engage 360 works through automated WhatsApp communication flows:
1. Order Confirmation (COD): Sends IVR + WhatsApp to confirm genuine intent for COD orders
2. Address Verification: Sends WhatsApp to verify delivery address accuracy before shipping
3. COD to Prepaid: Sends payment link to convert COD orders to prepaid
4. Pre-delivery Check: Confirms buyer availability on the day of delivery
5. NDR Management: Automated recovery flows for failed deliveries

## How does Engage decide which orders to send confirmation messages to?

Engage sends order confirmation messages based on your configured settings. You can set rules to send to:
- All COD orders
- COD orders above a specific value
- Orders from specific channels
- Orders to specific zones/pincodes

Configure these rules in the RTO Suite settings in Engage 360.

## My COD order is confirmed on Engage, but the order is still showing as COD (not prepaid) in Shiprocket. Why?

Confirmation of intent via Engage does not automatically change the payment mode from COD to Prepaid in Shiprocket. The actual payment mode change only happens if the buyer clicks the payment link and completes the prepaid payment. If they only confirm the order, it remains COD.

## What is the difference between a campaign and an automation on Engage?

Campaign: Proactive broadcast message sent to a selected segment at a scheduled time. One-time send.

Automation/Journey: Triggered by specific customer events. Runs automatically whenever the trigger condition is met. Ongoing.

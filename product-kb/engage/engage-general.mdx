  <Accordion title="What channels does Shiprocket Engage support?">

Shiprocket Engage supports WhatsApp, SMS, and Email as communication channels for both automation workflows and marketing campaigns.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the RTO Suite in Engage?">

The RTO Suite helps reduce Return to Origin (RTO) rates by proactively communicating with buyers about their pending deliveries, confirming delivery addresses, collecting prepayment for COD orders, and running NDR recovery workflows.

  </Accordion>

## If the customer confirms the order first and then cancels the order, will the order cancellation WhatsApp/Email be sent on Engage?

The order cancellation notification flow is not a part of the default flows on Engage. We can set it up as a custom flow. Please reach out to srengage@shiprocket.com for the same.

---

## Can I send email campaigns on Engage?

Yes. Engage 360 supports email campaigns. You can send promotional emails, order update notifications, and automated email journeys. Email domain verification is required. Contact your KAM or srengage@shiprocket.com to set up email integration.

---

## How to set up email on Engage 360?

Email integration on Engage 360 is managed by Key Account Managers (KAMs). To set up email:
1. Contact your Shiprocket account manager or srengage@shiprocket.com
2. Provide your email domain details
3. Complete domain verification (SPF, DKIM, DMARC setup)
4. Once verified, email channel will be available in Engage 360

For detailed instructions, refer to the Email and SMS Integration documentation.

---

## What email features are available on Engage 360?

Engage 360 email features include:
1. Email campaigns (broadcast to segments)
2. Automated email journeys (triggered by order events)
3. HTML email templates with rich formatting
4. Email analytics (open rate, click rate, unsubscribes)
5. Personalization with dynamic variables
6. Scheduling and A/B testing

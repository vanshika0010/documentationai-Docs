## Is any report available which shows the benefits I am getting with RTO suite?

Yes. Engage 360 provides an RTO Suite analytics dashboard that shows:
1. Orders processed through RTO Suite
2. Confirmed orders vs. cancelled orders
3. COD to Prepaid conversions count and value
4. NDR recovery rate
5. Estimated RTO reduction percentage
6. Revenue saved by converting COD to prepaid

---

## What analytics are available on Engage 360?

Engage 360 provides the following analytics:
1. Campaign Analytics: Sent, delivered, read, clicked, conversions, revenue, ROAS
2. Journey Analytics: Users entered, messages sent, conversion rates
3. RTO Suite Analytics: Order confirmation rates, COD-to-prepaid conversions, NDR recovery
4. WhatsApp Quality Metrics: Delivery rate, read rate, opt-out rate
5. Audience Analytics: Segment sizes, growth trends

---

## How to download reports on Engage 360?

To download reports on Engage 360:
1. Go to the Analytics section in the Engage dashboard
2. Select the report type (Campaign, Journey, RTO Suite, etc.)
3. Set the date range
4. Click Download or Export
Reports are available in CSV format for further analysis.

## How to check delivery rate for WhatsApp messages on Engage?

Delivery rates are available per campaign and per journey in Engage 360 analytics:
1. Go to Campaigns or Journeys section
2. Click on a specific campaign/journey
3. View delivery metrics: Sent, Delivered, Read counts and percentages
4. Overall WABA delivery metrics are available in Settings >> WhatsApp

---

## Can I track revenue from Engage campaigns?

Yes. Engage 360 uses last-interaction attribution to track revenue from campaigns:
- When a customer purchases after clicking a campaign link, the revenue is attributed to that campaign
- Campaign analytics shows revenue attributed, number of orders, and ROAS
- Journey analytics also shows conversion revenue

---

## What is ROAS in Engage analytics?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) in Engage 360 is calculated as:
ROAS = Attributed Revenue ÷ Campaign Spend

For example, if you spent Rs. 1,000 on WhatsApp campaigns and generated Rs. 10,000 in attributed revenue, ROAS = 10x.

This metric helps evaluate the effectiveness of your campaigns.

---

## How to see which customers clicked on campaign links in Engage?

Engage 360 campaign analytics shows click-through data including:
- Total clicks on CTA buttons/links
- Unique click count
- Click-through rate (CTR)
For identifying specific customers who clicked, use the detailed campaign report export.

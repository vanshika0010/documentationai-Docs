## Is any report available which shows the benefits I am getting by using Shiprocket Engage?

Yes. Other than the dashboard available in the Engage panel for all modules, the sellers receive a monthly Engage performance report on the registered email id. The report will have the benefits getting accrued by using the Engage platform.

---

## Will I get detailed reports - how many IVR calls, whatsapp messages, manual calls were done along with the response on Engage?

As a seller, one can export the dump from the panel itself where all the above mentioned details are available.

## Why aren't my analytics showing any data on Engage?

If analytics aren't updating:
1. Allow some time for data to populate (may take a few hours).
2. Ensure tracking scripts are properly installed.
3. Check if the correct date range is selected.
4. Clear cache and refresh the dashboard.

---

## Why do some customers report not receiving messages on Engage?

1. They may have unsubscribed or opted out.
2. Their phone number is incorrect or inactive.
3. Messages may be blocked by their network provider.
4. WhatsApp delivery issues due to number quality or restrictions.
5. Insufficient wallet balance.

---

## What happens if a customer marks my WhatsApp message as spam on Engage?

Too many spam reports reduce your WhatsApp quality rating and can temporarily block messaging. Ensure customers opt-in before sending messages.

## How can I check the ROAS of a journey on Engage?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) for a journey is automatically calculated by Engage360 based on:
1. Total revenue generated by users who received a journey message and subsequently placed an order
2. Total cost of sending those messages

ROAS = Revenue / Cost. You can view this metric in the Journey Analytics section of Engage360.

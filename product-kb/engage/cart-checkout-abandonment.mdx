## Can the seller decide the time intervals for Abandoned Cart recovery messages on Engage?

Yes. The seller can device the time intervals for all the 4 messages that can be pushed via Abandoned cart recovery. This is accessible in the panel itself.

---

## What is the best time to send abandoned checkout recovery messages on Engage?

We recommend sending 2 messages in the first 24 hours & 2 messages in the next 48 hours of the buyer churning from the checkout. Engage allows the sellers to set-up the timings by him/herself.

---

## How can I setup Abandoned Checkout marketing via WhatsApp on Engage?

You can easily launch an Abandoned Checkout journey by following these steps:
1. Click on Journeys >Create Journeys >Create Custom Journeys
2. Fill details such as a Start trigger to be "Checkout Started" followed by other conditions, delay you require, and actions to be WhatsApp.
3. At the end, click on Save and your journey will be processed. 

You can access the Journey from the dashboard or from the Template Gallery and add Button URL to re-direct WhatsApp users to your desired Checkout page.

## How to set up abandoned cart for Shopify on Engage?

To set up abandoned cart for Shopify on Engage:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations
2. Find Shopify and enable the integration
3. The Shopify integration will start sending cart abandonment events to Engage
4. Create a Journey with "Cart Created" trigger and configure your recovery messages

---

## How to set up abandoned cart for WooCommerce on Engage?

To set up abandoned cart for WooCommerce on Engage:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations
2. Find WooCommerce and copy the webhook URL
3. Add the webhook URL in your WooCommerce settings
4. The WooCommerce integration will start sending cart abandonment events to Engage
5. Create a Journey with "Cart Created" trigger and configure your recovery messages

## What is the difference between Abandoned Cart and Abandoned Checkout on Engage?

Abandoned Cart: Triggered when a user adds products to the cart but does not proceed to checkout. Event: "Cart Created".

Abandoned Checkout: Triggered when a user starts the checkout process but does not complete the purchase. Event: "Checkout Started".

Both can be set up as separate journeys in Engage 360.

## How many follow-up messages can I send for Abandoned Cart on Engage?

You can send up to 4 messages in the Abandoned Cart recovery flow on Engage. The seller can configure the time intervals between each message.

---

## What is the cost of Abandoned Cart recovery on Engage?

The cost for Abandoned Cart WhatsApp messages on Engage is Rs. 0.99/- per marketing message (as per current WhatsApp pricing). IVR calls may have additional charges if configured.

## Can I include product images in the Abandoned Cart WhatsApp message on Engage?

Yes. You can include a header image in the WhatsApp template. For product carousel (multiple products), Self WABA with Carousel template support is required.

---

## Does Engage track conversions from Abandoned Cart recovery messages?

Yes. Engage 360 Journey analytics tracks:
- Users who entered the journey
- Messages sent/delivered/read
- Clicks on recovery links
- Orders placed (conversions)
- Revenue recovered

---

## Can I add a discount code in the Abandoned Cart message on Engage?

Yes. You can add a discount code as a variable in your WhatsApp template. Create the discount in your store and include it in the Abandoned Cart recovery message to incentivize purchase completion.

---

## How to stop Abandoned Cart messages for customers who have already purchased on Engage?

Engage 360 has an exit condition built into the Abandoned Cart journey. If a customer places an order, they are automatically removed from the journey and no further recovery messages are sent.

---

## What is the opt-out mechanism for Abandoned Cart messages on Engage?

Customers can opt out of Abandoned Cart messages by:
- Replying STOP to WhatsApp messages
- Clicking unsubscribe in email messages
Engage 360 will stop sending messages to opted-out customers automatically.

---

## Can I use email for Abandoned Cart recovery on Engage in addition to WhatsApp?

Yes. Engage 360 supports both WhatsApp and Email for Abandoned Cart recovery. You can create separate journeys for each channel or configure a multi-channel approach.

---

## Does the Abandoned Cart journey run for both new and returning customers on Engage?

Yes. The Abandoned Cart journey can be configured to run for both new and returning customers. You can add segment conditions to target specific customer types if needed.

---

## What data does Engage receive from Shopify for Abandoned Cart?

Engage 360 receives the following data from Shopify for Abandoned Cart:
- Customer phone number and name
- Cart contents (product names, quantities, prices)
- Cart total value
- Cart recovery URL
- Customer email

---

## Can I use Engage Abandoned Cart for international customers?

Yes. WhatsApp Abandoned Cart messages can be sent to international customers. Standard international WhatsApp Business conversation rates apply.

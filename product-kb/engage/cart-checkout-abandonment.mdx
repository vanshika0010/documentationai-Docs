## Can the seller decide the time intervals for Abandoned Cart recovery messages on Engage?

Yes. The seller can device the time intervals for all the 4 messages that can be pushed via Abandoned cart recovery. This is accessible in the panel itself.

## What is the best time to send abandoned checkout recovery messages on Engage?

We recommend sending 2 messages in the first 24 hours & 2 messages in the next 48 hours of the buyer churning from the checkout. Engage allows the sellers to set-up the timings by him/herself.

## How can I setup Abandoned Checkout marketing via WhatsApp on Engage?

You can easily launch an Abandoned Checkout journey by following these steps:
1. Click on Journeys >Create Journeys >Create Custom Journeys
2. Fill details such as a Start trigger to be "Checkout Started" followed by other conditions, delay you require, and actions to be WhatsApp.
3. At the end, click on Save and your journey will be processed.

You can access the Journey from the dashboard or from the Template Gallery and add Button URL to re-direct WhatsApp users to your desired Checkout page.

## How can I edit the Abandoned Checkout Messages on Engage?

With the current functionality of Engage360, you can only edit the Button URL from the message. If you want to make any other changes to the saved template, you'll have to delete the current template and create a new custom template.

## What is the difference between an abandoned cart and an abandoned checkout on Engage?

An abandoned cart occurs when a user adds items to their cart but does not proceed to checkout. An abandoned checkout happens when a user begins the checkout process but does not complete the purchase.

## My cart abandonment journey is not triggering on Engage. What should I check?

If the cart abandonment journey isn't working:
1. Ensure the Engage 360 integration with your website is active.
2. Verify if the customer has added an item to the cart but not completed checkout.
3. Check if the time delay for sending the message is correctly set.
4. Make sure the customer hasn't opted out of messages.

## How can I integrate Shiprocket Checkout in Engage 360?

1. Log in to your Engage 360 panel and navigate to the Integrations section.
2. Click on Checkout Partners and select Shiprocket Checkout.
3. Turn the Shiprocket Checkout toggle On.
4. You will start seeing last event recorded details to trigger Abandoned Checkout flows.

---

## How can I integrate Gokwik in Engage 360?

1. Navigate to Integrations on your Engage panel.
2. Click on Checkout Partners and select GoKwik.
3. Turn the toggle on and get API credentials.
4. In GoKwik: Kwik Checkout > Settings > Integrations > Re-Marketing Tools
5. Turn toggle On for "Abandoned Cart Configure Webhooks" and paste Webhook URL.

---

## How to Integrate Shopflo in Engage 360?

1. Navigate to Integrations on your Engage panel.
2. Click on Checkout Partners and select Shopflo.
3. Get API credentials: URL, Header Name, Header Value.
4. In Shopflo Settings > Webhook Configuration, enter the credentials.
5. Turn the Webhook Configuration toggle On.

## Where do I paste the Engage Webhook URL in GoKwik?

Inside your GoKwik merchant panel, navigate to Kwik Checkout > Settings > Integrations > Re-Marketing Tools and paste the Engage360 webhook URL in the "Abandoned Cart Configure Webhooks" section.

## What's the difference between Abandoned Checkout and Abandoned Cart on Engage?

Abandoned Cart: User adds product to cart but doesn't proceed to checkout.
Abandoned Checkout: User starts checkout process but doesn't complete payment.

Both require different integration setups and target different stages of the purchase funnel.

## Can the seller decide the time intervals for Abandoned Cart recovery messages on Engage?

Yes, in Engage 360, sellers can decide the time intervals for Abandoned Cart recovery messages. When creating a Chatflow for Abandoned Cart recovery, sellers can add a delay between messages using the 'Delay' component in the Chatflow builder.

---

## Does Engage 360 track the orders placed through Abandoned Cart recovery messages?

Yes, Engage 360 does track the orders placed through Abandoned Cart recovery messages. The 'Journeys' section in Engage 360 provides detailed analytics on the performance of each journey, including how many users entered the journey, how many received each message, and how many completed a purchase.

## What is the difference between Abandoned Cart and Abandoned Checkout on Engage?

On Engage 360:
- Abandoned Cart: When a customer adds items to the cart but doesn't proceed to checkout.
- Abandoned Checkout: When a customer starts the checkout process but doesn't complete the purchase.
Engage 360 handles both types of abandonment, but the specific event trigger depends on what your connected platform sends.

## Can the Abandoned Cart Journey on Engage contain product images?

Yes, if using Carousel templates (available with Self WABA), you can include product images in the Abandoned Cart message. For standard templates, you can include a single header image.

---

## Can I also set up Abandoned Cart for products available on Amazon, Flipkart, etc. using Engage?

Engage 360 Abandoned Cart recovery is designed for your own website/D2C store (Shopify, WooCommerce, etc.). Marketplace platforms like Amazon and Flipkart have their own seller communication restrictions and don't allow third-party checkout abandonment flows.

---

## Can I set a minimum cart value for Abandoned Cart messages on Engage?

Yes. When setting up the Abandoned Cart Journey in Engage 360, you can add conditions to only trigger the flow for carts above a certain value. This helps focus recovery efforts on high-value carts.

---

## How does Engage 360 integrate with Shopify for Abandoned Cart?

Engage 360 integrates with Shopify through the Shopify App Store integration. Once connected, Engage automatically receives cart abandonment events from Shopify and triggers the configured recovery flow.

---

## What happens if the customer completes the purchase before the Abandoned Cart message is sent on Engage?

Engage 360 checks order status before sending. If the customer has completed the purchase before the scheduled message time, the message will not be sent. This prevents sending irrelevant messages to customers who have already converted.

---

## Can I customize the CTA (Call to Action) button in the Abandoned Cart message on Engage?

Yes. You can customize the CTA button text in your WhatsApp template. Common CTAs include "Complete Purchase", "Return to Cart", "Get 10% Off". The button URL is typically a direct link back to the customer's cart.

---

## How many messages can I send in an Abandoned Cart journey on Engage?

You can create multiple message nodes in the Abandoned Cart journey. A typical setup is 2-3 messages with increasing urgency. More messages can decrease effectiveness and increase opt-outs.

---

## What is the average time a buyer waits before the Abandoned Cart message is sent on Engage?

The most effective timing is typically 30-60 minutes after cart abandonment for the first message, followed by a 2-hour and 24-hour follow-up. However, the best timing may vary based on your specific audience.

---

## Can I include a discount in the Abandoned Cart message on Engage?

Yes. You can include a discount code in the Abandoned Cart WhatsApp message. Create the discount on your store, add it to the template, and approve with Meta.

---

## Can Engage Abandoned Cart work for guest checkout users?

Engage 360 can capture guest checkout abandonment if the user has provided their phone number (for WhatsApp) or email. If no contact info is available, the recovery message cannot be sent.

---

## What analytics are available for Abandoned Cart campaigns on Engage?

Engage 360 provides Journey analytics including:
- Users entered
- Messages sent/delivered/read
- Clicks on recovery links
- Conversions (orders placed)
- Revenue recovered
- Recovery rate percentage

## Does Engage support multi-language Abandoned Cart messages?

Yes. You can create Abandoned Cart templates in multiple languages (with Meta approval via Self-WABA) and configure the journey to send the appropriate language based on customer preference.

---

## How do I access the Abandoned Cart journey in Engage 360?

Go to Engage 360 >> Journeys >> Create Journey. Select "Cart Abandoned" or "Checkout Abandoned" as the trigger event. Configure your message nodes, delays, and conditions, then activate the journey.

---

## Can I use email for Abandoned Cart recovery in addition to WhatsApp on Engage?

Yes. Engage 360 supports multi-channel journeys. You can create separate email and WhatsApp journeys for abandoned cart recovery, or set up a sequence where email is sent first followed by WhatsApp.

---

## How to stop sending Abandoned Cart messages to customers who have already purchased on Engage?

Engage 360 automatically detects when a customer completes a purchase and stops sending further abandonment messages. You can also configure exit conditions in the journey to remove users who have converted.

---

## Is there a limit to how many times Engage will send Abandoned Cart messages to the same customer?

You can configure re-entry rules in the journey. By default, customers won't re-enter the same journey within a short period. Configure cooldown periods to prevent message fatigue.

---

## What is the opt-out process for Abandoned Cart messages on Engage?

Customers can opt out by:
- Replying with STOP or UNSUB to WhatsApp messages
- Clicking unsubscribe in email messages
Engage 360 will stop sending messages to opted-out customers.

---

## Can Engage send Abandoned Cart messages for both mobile and desktop users?

Yes. Engage 360 captures cart abandonment events regardless of whether the user is on mobile or desktop, as long as they are identified (phone number or email captured).

---

## Does the Abandoned Cart feature work for international customers on Engage?

Yes, WhatsApp Abandoned Cart messages can be sent to international customers. Standard international WhatsApp Business conversation rates apply.

## How to connect GoKwik with Engage for Abandoned Cart recovery?

To connect GoKwik with Engage 360 for Abandoned Cart:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations
2. Find GoKwik in the checkout integrations list
3. Enable the integration and save the webhook URL
4. Configure the webhook in your GoKwik settings
5. Test the integration to confirm events are flowing
6. Build your Abandoned Cart journey using the GoKwik trigger

## How does Engage help recover abandoned carts

Engage sends automated WhatsApp/SMS/Email messages to customers who added items to their cart but didn't complete the purchase. You can set up timed sequences (e.g., 30 minutes, 2 hours, 24 hours after abandonment) with personalized messages and discount codes.

---

## How to set up abandoned cart recovery automation in Engage

Go to Engage >> Automation >> Create Workflow. Select 'Cart Abandonment' as the trigger, configure the delay time, and set up the message content. Connect your store to ensure cart data flows into Engage.

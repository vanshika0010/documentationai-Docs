## How can I setup Abandoned Checkout marketing via WhatsApp on Engage?

You can easily launch an Abandoned Checkout journey by following these steps:
1. Click on Journeys >Create Journeys >Create Custom Journeys
2. Fill details such as a Start trigger to be "Checkout Started" followed by other conditions, delay you require, and actions to be WhatsApp.
3. At the end, click on Save and your journey will be processed. 

You can access the Journey from the dashboard or from the Template Gallery and add Button URL to re-direct WhatsApp users to your desired Checkout page.

## What is the difference between an abandoned cart and an abandoned checkout on Engage?

An abandoned cart occurs when a user adds items to their cart but does not proceed to checkout. An abandoned checkout happens when a user begins the checkout process but does not complete the purchase.

## What is the pricing of Custom Chatflow in Engage?

You're charged per conversation, not per individual message.
A conversation is a 24-hour chat thread between you and your buyer. It starts (and is charged) when your message is sent to the buyer.
These are the types of conversations available: 
1. Marketing – Used for promotions, sales, and customer engagement, like product announcements, special offers, or cart abandonment reminders.
2. Utility – For important updates related to a customer's actions, such as order confirmations, delivery updates, payment reminders, or feedback requests.
3. Authentication – For verifying user identity, like sending one-time passwords (OTPs) for login or account recovery.
4. Service – For customer support and answering inquiries initiated by the buyer.

All these conversations will be buyer-initiated conversations which fall under the Service Category. Hence charges will be accordingly.

## How can I integrate Shiprocket Checkout in Engage 360?

1. Connect to Engage 360:
    - Log in to your Engage 360 panel and navigate to the Integrations section.
    - Click on Checkout Partners and select Shiprocket Checkout from the available integrations. 
    - Turn the Shiprocket Checkout toggle On to Enable receiving events for your checkout journeys.
2. Once this is done, you will start seeing last event recorded details in Engage 360 platform using which you can trigger Abandoned Checkout flows.

## How can I integrate Gokwik in Engage 360?

1. Connect to Engage 360:
    - Navigate to the Integrations section on your Engage panel.
    - Click on Checkout Partners and select GoKwik from the available integrations.
    - Turn the toggle on and click on the Down button to get API credentials. 
    - Copy the key details such as: URL

2. Login to your GoKwik Panel:
   - Make sure you have admin access for your Gokwik account
   - Go to Kwik Checkout > Settings > Integrations > Re-Marketing Tools
   - Turn the toggle On for "Abandoned Cart Configure Webhooks"
   - Copy your Webhook URL from Engage panel and paste here
   - Click "Save Changes"

3. Once this is done, you will start seeing last event recorded details in Engage 360 platform using which you can trigger Abandoned Checkout flows.

## Where do I paste the Engage Webhook URL in GoKwik?

Inside your GoKwik merchant panel, navigate to Kwik Checkout > Settings > Integrations > Re-Marketing Tools. There, find the "Abandoned Cart Configure Webhooks" section and paste the Engage360 webhook URL. Once saved, GoKwik starts pushing real-time checkout data directly into Engage360.

## What's the difference between Abandoned Checkout and Abandoned Cart on Engage?

While both Abandoned Cart and Abandoned Checkout journeys aim to recover lost sales, they are triggered by different customer behaviors, require different integration setups, and target different stages of the purchase funnel. Understanding the distinction between the two helps you set up the right automation and maximize recovery.

Abandoned Cart: Triggered when a user adds a product to the cart but does not proceed to checkout. Requires integration of Engage360's JavaScript SDK or pixel on your website's product and cart pages. Useful for platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, custom websites, etc.

Abandoned Checkout: Triggered when a user starts the checkout process but does not complete the payment. Works only if you've integrated Engage360 with a checkout system like Shiprocket Checkout, Shopify Checkout, GoKwik, or Shopflo. Provides more reliable messaging since the user has submitted contact details.

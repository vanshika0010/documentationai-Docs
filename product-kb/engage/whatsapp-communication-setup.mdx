## How can I select or customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication?

To select or customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication, you can use our predefined message list that is sent to buyers. You can choose at least one or all messages depending on your specific needs. 
Currently, we trigger messages for the following status: Picked, packed, shipped, out for delivery, Arriving early, delivery delayed and Delivered. 
This allows you to keep your buyers informed about the status of their orders and ensure a smooth communication process.
Follow the below steps to customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication on shiprocket website:
* Go to settings from the left menu.
* Navigate to Value Added Services >> Notify. 
* Go to the bottom of this page and click on the preview option available under the 'select shipment status for WhatsApp communication'. 
* Select the order status that you want to send out to your buyers and click on the 'Save' button to save your customization. 
* Once you have made your selections, save your changes and the new flow of communication will take effect.

## How can I enable Whatsapp Communication?

Follow the listed below steps to enable or disable WhatsApp communication on shiprocket website:
* Click on Notify under Value Added Services in the left panel.
* Click on the Activate Now Button to enable WhatsApp Communication.
* You can select the statuses for which you want to send the updates from the bottom of the page. Expand the shipment status bar and choose the desired statuses.
* You can also see the preview of the messages using the preview icon for each status.

---

## How can I disable Whatsapp Communication?

Follow the listed below steps to disable WhatsApp communication on shiprocket website:
* Click on Notify under Value Added Services in the left panel.
* Find the option available to deactivate.
* Click on the 'Click here' option and select the reason for deactivating the plan. 
* Then, click on the Deactivate button to disable the WhatsApp communication for your account.

## Does Engage only do WhatsApp messages or do we do IVR calls also?

The default flow includes two WhatsApp messages (including one reminder) & 3 IVR calls (including 2 reminder calls) for the order confirmation on COD orders. WhatsApp only for Address Verification & COD to Prepaid conversion.

## What is the WhatsApp Communication flow for Buyer/Customer?

The WhatsApp Communication flow for buyers on Engage includes automated messages sent at key order milestones:
1. Order Packed: When order is packed and ready for pickup
2. Order Picked: When courier picks up the order
3. Order Shipped: When order is in transit
4. Out for Delivery: When order is with the delivery person
5. Arriving Early: When order will be delivered ahead of schedule
6. Delivery Delayed: When there is a delay in delivery
7. Delivered: When order is successfully delivered

For COD orders, additional flows include Order Confirmation (IVR + WhatsApp) and NDR handling.

---

## When do buyers receive the WhatsApp messages?

Buyers receive WhatsApp messages when the shipment status changes. Messages are triggered automatically at each status milestone: Packed, Picked, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Arriving Early, Delivery Delayed, and Delivered.

## What language are WhatsApp messages sent in on Engage?

WhatsApp messages on Engage are sent in English by default. For messages in other languages, you need custom templates approved by Meta through your Self-WABA.

## Can I send WhatsApp messages to international buyers?

Yes, WhatsApp messages can be sent to international buyers as long as they have a WhatsApp account. Standard international WhatsApp conversation charges apply.

## Is there a limit on the number of WhatsApp messages I can send?

WhatsApp messaging limits depend on your WABA tier (1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 unique conversations per day). Tiers are upgraded automatically based on quality rating and message volume.

## How are WhatsApp communication charges calculated?

WhatsApp charges are based on conversations (24-hour windows). Each unique customer conversation initiated is charged as per Meta's WhatsApp Business API pricing. Charges are deducted from your Shiprocket wallet.

## What happens if my wallet balance is insufficient for WhatsApp messages?

If your wallet balance is insufficient, WhatsApp messages will stop being sent to buyers. Ensure your wallet has sufficient balance to avoid interruption in buyer communication.

## Can I send WhatsApp messages for returns/RTO?

Yes. Engage supports WhatsApp notifications for return and RTO statuses. Enable the relevant status messages from the Notify settings.

## What is the RTO reduction flow in Engage via WhatsApp?

Engage's RTO reduction flow uses WhatsApp to:
1. Confirm COD orders with buyers (order confirmation message)
2. Verify delivery address before shipment
3. Send pre-delivery confirmation to ensure buyer availability
4. Handle NDR recovery (re-attempt scheduling via WhatsApp)
5. Convert COD orders to prepaid via WhatsApp payment links

---

## How does Engage handle NDR via WhatsApp?

When an NDR is triggered, Engage automatically sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer asking them to:
* Confirm their availability for re-delivery
* Update their delivery address if incorrect
* Choose a preferred delivery time slot

Based on the buyer's response, the system triggers a re-attempt or RTO.

## Can buyers respond to WhatsApp messages sent via Engage?

Yes, if you have a WABA (WhatsApp Business Account) configured on Engage, buyers can respond to messages. Responses can be handled by automated chatflows or escalated to human agents.

## How to set up WhatsApp communication for the first time on Engage?

To set up WhatsApp communication on Engage:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Notify from the left menu
2. Click on 'Activate Now' on the WhatsApp Communication section
3. Select the order statuses for which you want to send messages
4. Ensure your wallet has sufficient balance
5. Save the settings

Messages will start going out automatically when orders reach the selected statuses.

## What is the difference between Engage Notify and Engage 360?

Notify (basic): Sends automated WhatsApp/SMS/Email order status updates to buyers. Included in the Engage basic plan.

Engage 360 (advanced): Full marketing automation platform including campaigns, chatbots, abandoned cart recovery, COD confirmation, NDR management, and custom workflows. Requires a separate subscription.

## Does WhatsApp communication support media messages (images/videos)?

Standard Engage WhatsApp messages are text-based. For media messages (images, videos), you need a Self-WABA with custom media templates approved by Meta.

## How long does it take for WhatsApp messages to be delivered after a status change?

WhatsApp messages are typically sent within a few minutes of a status update being received from the courier. Actual delivery to the buyer depends on their WhatsApp connectivity.

---

## Can I send WhatsApp messages for prepaid and COD orders both?

Yes. WhatsApp order status messages are sent for both prepaid and COD orders. For COD orders, additional flows like order confirmation and NDR recovery are also available on Engage 360.

---

## What happens to WhatsApp messages if the buyer's number is not on WhatsApp?

If the buyer's number is not registered on WhatsApp, the message delivery will fail. In this case, Engage can fall back to SMS communication if SMS is also configured in your Notify settings.

---

## Can I opt specific buyers out of WhatsApp messages?

Currently, individual buyer opt-out management is available on Engage 360. On the basic Notify plan, WhatsApp messages are sent to all buyers whose orders have the enabled statuses.

---

## What is the WhatsApp Business API quality rating?

Meta assigns a quality rating (Green/Yellow/Red) to WhatsApp Business accounts based on user feedback and message quality. A low quality rating can reduce your messaging limits. Maintain quality by sending relevant, non-spammy messages.

---

## How to check my WhatsApp Business Account quality rating?

You can check your WABA quality rating in Meta's WhatsApp Business Manager or from the Engage dashboard if you have Self-WABA configured.

---

## Is there a setup fee for WhatsApp communication on Engage?

There is no separate setup fee for the basic WhatsApp communication (Notify) on Engage. You only pay for conversations used. For Self-WABA setup (Engage 360), contact the Engage team for pricing.

If a WhatsApp message fails to deliver (e.g., number not on WhatsApp, connectivity issues), Engage can retry or fall back to SMS if configured. Failed message details are visible in the Engage Reports section.

## How to resolve WhatsApp message delivery issues on Engage?

If WhatsApp messages are not being delivered:
1. Check your wallet balance - ensure sufficient funds
2. Verify the buyer's phone number is correct and active on WhatsApp
3. Check the Engage Reports for specific error codes
4. Ensure WhatsApp Communication is enabled in Notify settings
5. Raise a support ticket if the issue persists

## What reports are available for WhatsApp communication on Engage?

Engage provides the following WhatsApp communication reports:
* Messages sent count
* Messages delivered count
* Messages read count
* Delivery failure reasons
* Conversation costs
* Status-wise breakdown

Access these from the Engage Reports/Analytics section.

Standard Notify messages use text templates. For interactive messages with buttons, lists, or quick replies, you need Engage 360 with Self-WABA and custom Meta-approved interactive templates.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Engage?">

Shiprocket Engage (Engage360) is a customer communication and marketing automation platform. It enables businesses to communicate with customers via WhatsApp, SMS, and Email for order updates, marketing campaigns, RTO reduction, and abandoned cart recovery.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to set up WhatsApp communication on Engage?">

To set up WhatsApp, you need a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). Go to Engage >> Settings >> WhatsApp and follow the WABA setup process. Connect your Facebook Business Manager account and get your number verified by Meta.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

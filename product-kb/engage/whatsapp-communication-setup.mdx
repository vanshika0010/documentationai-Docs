## How can I select or customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication?

To select or customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication, you can use our predefined message list that is sent to buyers. You can choose at least one or all messages depending on your specific needs. 
Currently, we trigger messages for the following status: Picked, packed, shipped, out for delivery, Arriving early, delivery delayed and Delivered. 
This allows you to keep your buyers informed about the status of their orders and ensure a smooth communication process.
Follow the below steps to customize the flow of WhatsApp Communication on shiprocket website:
* Go to settings from the left menu.
* Navigate to Value Added Services >> Notify. 
* Go to the bottom of this page and click on the preview option available under the 'select shipment status for WhatsApp communication'. 
* Select the order status that you want to send out to your buyers and click on the 'Save' button to save your customization. 
* Once you have made your selections, save your changes and the new flow of communication will take effect.

## What should I do if I'm migrating my WABA number from another provider to Engage360?

A: To migrate your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) to Engage360:
    1. Ensure that your Meta Business Account is verified.
    2. Disable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):
    3. Log in to Facebook Business Manager.
    4. Go to Business Settings > WhatsApp Accounts and select your WABA.
    5. Under Settings, disable 2FA.
    6. Contact your current provider to initiate the number transfer.
    7. Once the number is released, complete the Engage360 onboarding process to register the number under the new WABA.
    Important: The number will be unavailable during migration. Plan accordingly to avoid disruptions.

## How do I create journeys on Engage?

## Why is my journey not triggering on Engage?

If a journey is not triggering, it's typically due to one or more of the following reasons:

1. Missing Integration (Shiprocket Checkout): If you're using Shiprocket Checkout and haven't enabled integration in Engage360, events like cart abandonment won't flow into the platform.
2. Integration Not Enabled: Even if integrated, the toggle in Engage360 might not be turned ON.
3. Event Not Fired: The source platform (e.g., Shopify or WooCommerce) may not be sending the trigger event. Verify this by checking the "Last Event Received" status in Engage360's integration panel.
4. Journey is Paused or Inactive: Confirm the journey is live and not in draft or paused state.
5. Audience Mismatch: The user might not meet the segment conditions set in the journey.
6. WABA/Template Issue: If your WABA is disconnected or the template isn't approved, messages can't be sent even if the journey triggers.

## How is ROAS calculated on Engage?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) in Engage360 is calculated using the following formula:

ROAS = Attributed Revenue ÷ Campaign Spend

Attributed revenue is determined using a last interaction attribution model, which means Engage360 credits the revenue to the most recent campaign or automation message that a customer interacted with before making a purchase.

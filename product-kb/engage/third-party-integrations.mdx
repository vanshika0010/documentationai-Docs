## Can Engage be integrated with Interakt, Business on BOT etc?

Engage 360 is Shiprocket's own platform and does not directly integrate with other WhatsApp BSPs like Interakt or Business on Bot. Engage 360 provides the full suite of WhatsApp marketing and automation features natively.

---

## Does Engage integrate with Shopify?

Yes. Engage 360 integrates natively with Shopify for:
- Order event triggers (placed, shipped, delivered)
- Cart abandonment events
- Customer data sync
- Abandoned checkout recovery

Connect from Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations >> Shopify.

---

## Does Engage integrate with WooCommerce?

Yes. Engage 360 integrates with WooCommerce via webhook:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations >> WooCommerce
2. Copy the webhook URL
3. Add it in your WooCommerce settings
4. Configure the events to send (order placed, cart abandoned, etc.)

---

## Does Engage integrate with GoKwik?

Yes. Engage 360 supports GoKwik checkout integration for abandoned checkout recovery:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations >> Checkout Partners >> GoKwik
2. Enable the integration and copy the webhook URL
3. Paste in GoKwik: Kwik Checkout >> Settings >> Integrations >> Re-Marketing Tools >> Abandoned Cart Configure Webhooks

---

## Does Engage integrate with Shopflo?

Yes. Engage 360 supports Shopflo integration for checkout abandonment:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations >> Checkout Partners >> Shopflo
2. Enable integration and copy API credentials (URL, Header Name, Header Value)
3. Configure in Shopflo settings with the provided credentials

---

## Does Engage integrate with Shiprocket Checkout?

Yes. Shiprocket Checkout is natively integrated with Engage 360. Enable it from:
Engage 360 >> Settings >> Integrations >> Checkout Partners >> Shiprocket Checkout >> Toggle ON

---

## Does Engage integrate with Razorpay for COD to Prepaid?

Yes. Engage 360 supports Razorpay as a payment gateway for the COD to Prepaid conversion flow. When buyers receive the payment link, they can pay via Razorpay. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com or your KAM to configure the Razorpay integration.

---

## Does Engage integrate with Cashfree?

Yes. Cashfree is supported as a payment gateway for COD to Prepaid conversion on Engage 360. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com or your KAM to set it up.

---

## Can Engage be integrated with Google Analytics?

Yes. You can track Engage campaign performance in Google Analytics by adding UTM parameters to your campaign CTA links. Engage 360 also provides native analytics for campaign performance.

---

## Does Engage support Magento integration?

Engage 360 can be integrated with Magento via webhook/API. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com for Magento integration setup and documentation.

---

## Does Engage support custom platform integration via API?

Yes. Engage 360 provides webhook and API endpoints for custom platform integration. You can send order events, customer data, and cart events to Engage from any platform. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com for API documentation.

---

## Can Engage be used with Unicommerce?

Engage 360 can receive order events from Shiprocket's connected channels. If Unicommerce is connected to Shiprocket, order events flow into Engage automatically. For direct Unicommerce integration, contact the Engage team.

---

## Does Engage integrate with Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart for order events?

Engage 360 receives order events from channels integrated with Shiprocket. If you have Amazon, Flipkart, or Meesho connected to your Shiprocket account, order events from those channels can trigger Engage flows.

---

## Does Engage support Klaviyo integration?

For Klaviyo integration with Engage 360, contact srengage@shiprocket.com. The Engage team can advise on the best approach for syncing data between platforms.

---

## Does Engage support Clevertap or MoEngage integration?

Engage 360 supports webhook-based integration for sending event data to third-party analytics and engagement platforms like Clevertap and MoEngage. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com for integration setup.

## Can Engage be integrated with Interakt, Business on BOT etc?

No.

---

## Is Engage a part of Shiprocket?

Yes, Engage 360 is integrated with Shiprocket, providing seamless order management and communication tools.

---

## Does Engage 360 integrate with my website?

Yes, it works with Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom websites.

---

## Can I integrate Engage 360 with my CRM?

It supports integrations with various CRM and marketing platforms.

---

## Can I use Engage 360 with multiple stores?

Yes, if you operate multiple stores, you can integrate them within Engage 360.

---

## Do I need to code to integrate Shiprocket Checkout with Engage?

No, the integration process is 100% no-code. Engage360 was designed with user-friendliness in mind, so you won't need to write or deploy any custom scripts. Simply navigate to the Integrations section in your Engage360 settings, find Checkout Partners, and toggle Shiprocket Checkout to ON.

---

## Where can I find the integration settings in Engage?

To access integration settings, log into Engage360 and click on the Settings icon on the top right. From there, navigate to the Integrations tab in the left sidebar. You'll find all platform connections—including Shiprocket Checkout, GoKwik, Shopflo, and others—listed under Checkout Partners.

---

## What info do I need to copy from Engage for Shiprocket Checkout?

For Shiprocket Checkout, Engage360 handles most of the heavy lifting for you. Simply enabling the toggle in the Checkout Partners section is enough—there are no credentials to copy or configure manually. Once enabled, Shiprocket Checkout automatically starts sending checkout events to Engage360.

---

## How do I know if Shiprocket Checkout integration is working on Engage?

After enabling the Shiprocket Checkout integration, return to the Integrations dashboard and check for the "Last Event Recorded" line beneath the Shiprocket Checkout toggle. If a recent timestamp appears here, it confirms that checkout data is flowing successfully into Engage360.

---

## Do I need to set headers for GoKwik integration on Engage?

No, GoKwik's integration process is simple and doesn't require any additional authentication headers. All you need is the Webhook URL, which makes it quick to configure on the GoKwik side.

---

## How do I connect Shopflo to Engage?

Shopflo requires a few extra steps for a secure integration. In Engage360, enable Shopflo from the Checkout Partners section. You'll be provided with a URL, Header Name, and Header Value. In your Shopflo account, go to Settings > Webhook Configuration and enter these credentials exactly as shown.

---

## Where do I paste the credentials for Shopflo on Engage?

In Shopflo, go to Settings > Webhook Configuration, and enter the URL, Header Name, and Header Value exactly as given by Engage360. These are required for Shopflo to securely send events to Engage360.

---

## Why are headers needed for Shopflo but not GoKwik on Engage?

Shopflo follows a more secure webhook protocol that requires each request to be authenticated using headers. This adds a layer of protection to ensure only Engage360 can receive data from Shopflo.

---

## What does the "Webhook Configuration" toggle do in Shopflo for integration with Engage?

The Webhook Configuration toggle in Shopflo enables the system to start sending event data to Engage360. Until this toggle is turned ON, no checkout data—such as abandoned checkouts—will be transmitted to Engage360.

---

## Can I track last event data from Shopflo in Engage?

Yes. Just like with Shiprocket Checkout and GoKwik, Engage360 shows a "Last Event Recorded" timestamp for Shopflo. This helps verify that the webhook is correctly configured and data is flowing into Engage360.

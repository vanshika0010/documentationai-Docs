## What is the criteria for High and Low RTO risk on Engage?

High RTO Risk criteria:
1. Orders with a history of high RTO orders
2. Orders to pincodes with high RTO rates
3. COD orders from buyers who have previously rejected or not paid for COD orders
4. Orders with suspicious or unverifiable delivery addresses

Low RTO Risk criteria:
1. Prepaid orders
2. Orders to pincodes with low RTO rates
3. Orders from buyers with a good delivery history
4. Orders with accurate and verifiable delivery addresses

## What is the RTO Suite on Engage?

The RTO Suite on Engage 360 is a comprehensive set of tools designed to reduce Return to Origin (RTO) rates. It includes:
1. Order Confirmation: Automated IVR calls and WhatsApp messages to confirm COD orders
2. Address Verification: Automated WhatsApp messages to verify delivery address before shipping
3. COD to Prepaid Conversion: Automated messages with payment link to convert COD orders to prepaid
4. NDR Management: Automated WhatsApp messages to handle failed delivery attempts (Delivery Boost)
5. Pre-delivery Check: Confirmation messages sent before delivery to ensure buyer availability

---

## How does RTO Suite help reduce RTO on Engage?

The RTO Suite helps reduce RTO through:
1. Eliminating fake COD orders via order confirmation calls/messages
2. Correcting wrong addresses before shipment via address verification
3. Converting COD to prepaid reduces RTO as prepaid orders have much lower RTO rates
4. NDR management reduces failed deliveries by proactively communicating with buyers
5. Pre-delivery checks ensure buyer is available to receive the order

## How to enable RTO Suite on Engage?

To enable the RTO Suite on Engage 360:
1. Go to the Engage section from the Shiprocket panel
2. Navigate to RTO Suite settings
3. Enable the features you want: Order Confirmation, Address Verification, COD to Prepaid, NDR Management
4. Configure the message flow and timing for each feature
5. Ensure your wallet has sufficient balance
6. Save and activate

## Does RTO Suite work for prepaid orders on Engage?

The RTO Suite primarily targets COD orders since they have significantly higher RTO rates. For prepaid orders, the Address Verification and Pre-delivery Check features can still be applied to ensure successful delivery.

## Can I customize the RTO Suite message flows on Engage?

Yes. With Self-WABA setup in Engage 360, you can customize the message templates used in RTO Suite flows. Default flows use Shiprocket's pre-approved templates.

## How does Address Verification work in RTO Suite on Engage?

After an order is placed:
1. Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the buyer asking them to verify/confirm their delivery address
2. The buyer can confirm the address or provide corrections
3. If corrections are needed, the updated address is sent back to be applied to the order
4. This reduces failed deliveries due to incorrect addresses

---

## How does COD to Prepaid conversion work in RTO Suite on Engage?

1. Engage 360 sends a WhatsApp message to the COD buyer after order placement
2. The message includes a payment link and offers an incentive (discount/cashback) to pay online
3. If the buyer pays via the link, the order converts to prepaid automatically
4. This significantly reduces RTO as prepaid orders have much lower return rates

---

## What is NDR management in RTO Suite on Engage?

When a delivery fails (NDR - Non-Delivery Report):
1. Engage 360 automatically sends WhatsApp messages to the buyer
2. Options provided: Confirm re-attempt, update delivery address, or select preferred time slot
3. Buyer responses are captured and used to schedule re-attempts
4. This proactive approach reduces RTO by resolving delivery issues quickly

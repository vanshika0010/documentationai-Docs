## What is the criteria for High and Low RTO risk on Engage?

Depending on the various factors like, buyer's past behavior, pincode delivery performance, time of the order placement, order value vs buyer's average order value, etc. Engage 360 assigns a risk score to each order. Orders with a high risk score are marked as High RTO risk and orders with a low risk score are marked as Low RTO risk.

Yes. The message got triggered to the buyer with the help of which the buyer could raise the cancellation request. Thus, the seller was able to save on logistics cost of that order. Hence, the charge for RTO suite is applicable.

## What is the RTO Suite in Engage, and what is it used for?

The RTO Suite is designed to help customers manage Return to Origin (RTO) scenarios. It enables:
1. Avoiding cancellations by engaging customers proactively.
2. Reducing RTO by verifying orders before shipping.
3. Increasing COD to prepaid conversions.
4. Streamlining NDR (Non-Delivery Report) management.

---

## What is the RTO Suite on Engage?

A set of tools that reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) by verifying orders and preventing fraud.

## How does Engage help in reducing Return to Origin (RTO) orders?

Engage 360 helps reduce RTO through:

1. Order Confirmation Messages – Automatically confirm orders via WhatsApp or SMS before shipping.
2. Address Verification – Verify delivery addresses to avoid failed deliveries.
3. COD to Prepaid Conversion – Encourage buyers to switch from COD to prepaid to reduce RTOs.
4. NDR Management – Proactively manage Non-Delivery Reports (NDRs) to ensure successful re-delivery.
5. Fraud Detection – Identify and flag suspicious orders before they are processed.

## How do i increase prepaid to COD conversion on Engage?

To increase Prepaid conversions from COD orders, Engage360 recommends implementing the following strategies:

1. Offer Discount Coupons: Provide instant discount coupons to buyers who switch from COD to prepaid payment.
2. Communicate Value: Clearly communicate the benefits of prepaid payments, such as faster delivery or exclusive discounts.
3. Simplify Payment Process: Make the prepaid payment process as simple and quick as possible.
4. Use Automated Messages: Leverage Engage360’s WhatsApp automation to send timely reminders and incentives for prepaid payment.
5. Monitor and Optimize: Regularly review conversion rates and adjust strategies based on performance data.

## My RTO messages are not getting delivered on Engage?

"If your RTO (Return to Origin) messages are not getting delivered, there could be several underlying reasons. Here are the most common ones:

1. Negative Wallet Balance: Your Shiprocket wallet may have a negative balance, which can halt all automated messaging. Please check your wallet and recharge if needed.
2. WABA Issue: There might be a problem with your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). This could be due to a policy violation or quality rating drop.
3. Orders Not Syncing: Your orders may not be properly syncing with Engage360. Ensure that your order management system is correctly integrated."

## Why is my product image not showing in message on Engage?

"Here's a clear step-by-step process to ensure product images are sent properly via the RTO Suite:

1. Go to RTO Suite in your Engage360 dashboard.

2. Navigate to the section where product image settings are configured (usually under WhatsApp Templates or Message Settings).

3. Confirm that the Product Image URL is properly filled in and accessible publicly (not a private/login-required URL).

4. Test the URL in a browser to confirm it loads correctly.

5. If the image URL is correct and accessible, check the image format (should be JPEG/PNG) and size (should be under 5MB)."

## My orders are not syncing on Engage?

"If your orders are not syncing in Engage360, here's what could be happening:

1. You're likely not using the Shiprocket panel to process orders, which means Engage360 won't receive order data.
2. If you use other tools or APIs to manage orders outside of Shiprocket, those won't automatically appear in Engage360.
3. To resolve this, ensure all orders are being managed through the Shiprocket panel."

## How to add my COD coupon in RTO suite on Engage?

"The process to add your COD coupon in the RTO Suite:

1. Go to RTO Suite from your Engage360 dashboard.

2. Click on the “COD Coupons” tab in the sidebar or top navigation.

3. Click on Create Coupon or Add Coupon (depending on the current UI).

4. Fill in the required details:
   - Coupon Code
   - Discount Type (flat / percentage)
   - Discount Value
   - Minimum Order Value (if applicable)
   - Expiry Date (if applicable)

5. Save the coupon.

6. Once saved, the coupon will be automatically used in your COD-to-Prepaid WhatsApp flow."

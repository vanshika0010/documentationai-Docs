## How does Engage help reduce RTO

Engage reduces RTO through:

* Proactive WhatsApp/SMS messages to confirm delivery.
* NDR recovery workflows that re-engage buyers after failed delivery attempts.
* COD to prepaid conversion messages.
* Address verification flows.

---

## What is the NDR recovery workflow in Engage

When an NDR (Non-Delivery Report) is triggered, Engage automatically sends a message to the buyer asking them to confirm their availability or update their address. Based on the buyer's response, the system triggers a re-attempt or RTO.

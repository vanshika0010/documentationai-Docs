## What are Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

Pre-Filled Popups are website widgets on Engage 360 that capture customer opt-ins for WhatsApp communication. Features:
1. Appear on your website as popup forms
2. Customer's phone number is pre-filled using Shiprocket's database (if available)
3. Customer clicks to opt-in to WhatsApp messages
4. Collected opt-ins are added to Engage 360 contact lists
5. Free feature - no additional charge for the popup itself

---

## How to set up Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

To set up Pre-Filled Popups on Engage 360:
1. Go to Engage 360 >> Pre-Filled Popups section
2. Click Create Popup
3. Design the popup (title, message, CTA button text)
4. Choose display rules (when to show, on which pages)
5. Get the JavaScript snippet
6. Add the snippet to your website's HTML
7. Test on your website before publishing

---

## What is the benefit of Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

Benefits of Pre-Filled Popups:
1. Higher opt-in rates (pre-filled number reduces friction)
2. Build a WhatsApp subscriber list for future campaigns
3. Capture leads from website visitors
4. Free feature - no extra cost
5. Works for both new and existing customers
6. Leverages Shiprocket's 25+ crore customer database for address/number pre-fill

---

## Can I customize the Pre-Filled Popup design on Engage?

Yes. You can customize:
- Popup title and message text
- CTA button text
- Popup position (corner, center, etc.)
- Display timing and trigger rules
- Color scheme (in some configurations)

Contact the Engage team for advanced customization options.

---

## On which pages can I show Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

You can configure Pre-Filled Popups to show on:
- All website pages
- Specific pages (product pages, cart page, checkout page)
- Exit-intent (when user is about to leave)
- After specific time on page
- After specific scroll depth

---

## How do Pre-Filled Popups help with abandoned cart recovery on Engage?

Pre-Filled Popups on the cart/checkout page:
1. Capture customer's WhatsApp number before they abandon
2. Customer opts into WhatsApp communication
3. If they abandon, Engage 360 can send WhatsApp recovery messages
4. Even anonymous visitors can be re-engaged if Shiprocket's database has their number

---

## Are Pre-Filled Popups free on Engage?

Yes. The Pre-Filled Popup feature itself is free on Engage 360. You only pay for the WhatsApp messages sent to the collected contacts (standard WhatsApp message rates apply).

---

## Does Pre-Filled Popup work on mobile websites on Engage?

Yes. Pre-Filled Popups are designed to be mobile-responsive. They work on both desktop and mobile browsers.

---

## How to track popup performance on Engage?

Engage 360 provides popup analytics including:
- Impressions (how many times popup was shown)
- Opt-in count (how many customers subscribed)
- Opt-in rate (percentage of visitors who opted in)
Access these metrics from the Pre-Filled Popups section in Engage 360.

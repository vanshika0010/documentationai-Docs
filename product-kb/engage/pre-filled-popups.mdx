## What are Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

Lead capture popups that auto-fill customer details, making sign-ups faster and boosting conversions.

---

## How do I enable Pre-Filled Popups on Engage?

You can choose from Engage360s Pre-Curated Pre-filled Popups templates and customise them according to your brand's logo and image via the Engage 360 dashboard.

---

## Where do pre-filled popups appear on Engage?

They appear on your website pages based on defined triggers (e.g., exit intent, scroll depth, or time spent), prompting users before they drop off.

---

## Does Engage pre-filled popups work on mobile and desktop?

Yes. Pre-Filled Popups are fully responsive and designed to function smoothly across all devices and screen sizes.

## What is e360 identified audience on Engage?

Engage 360 Identified Audience is a powerful feature that helps you recognize and reach potential buyers who visit your website. Using Engage's proprietary technology, it identifies visitors based on their real-time activity and matches them against a vast database of known users.

---

## How is e360 identified audience tracked on Engage? is cookie used?

Engage 360 Identified Audience is driven by Engage's proprietary technology, which detects and identifies potential buyers based on their real-time activity on your website. It uses a combination of advanced techniques that go beyond traditional cookies, allowing for effective tracking and identification of visitors.

## how does the popup automatically prefill data on Engage?

The popup automatically prefills data using Engage 360's backend technology, which identifies visitors based on their interactions and previous sessions on your website, then retrieves relevant details from Shiprocket's database to pre-fill the popup form.

## Does Engage 360 support Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA)?

Yes, it helps drive traffic from ads directly to WhatsApp for real-time engagement.

---

## What is CTWA in Engage?

CTWA stands for Click to WhatsApp Ad. It is a type of advertisement that directs users to start a WhatsApp conversation with a business when they click on the ad. This ad format is available on Facebook and Instagram, and it opens a WhatsApp chat window directly when clicked.

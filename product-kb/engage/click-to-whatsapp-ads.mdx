  <Accordion title="What are Click-to-WhatsApp Ads in Engage?">

Click-to-WhatsApp Ads are Facebook/Instagram ads that, when clicked, open a WhatsApp conversation with your business. Engage helps you set up and track these ads, connecting ad performance to actual WhatsApp conversations and conversions.

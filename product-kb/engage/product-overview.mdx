## What are the benefits of selecting Auto assign AWB on Engage?

Currently, the orders have to be processed manually from the Shiprocket Panel. By enabling "Auto Assign AWB", the sellers can cut down on that manual effort and ensure that the orders on Engage are processed automatically, leading to faster communication and better buyer experience.

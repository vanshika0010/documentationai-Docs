## What are the benefits of selecting Auto assign AWB on Engage?

When Auto Assign AWB is enabled on Engage 360:
1. AWB (Air Waybill / tracking number) is automatically assigned to orders without manual intervention
2. This ensures the RTO Suite flows (order confirmation, address verification) are triggered immediately after order creation
3. Reduces delay between order placement and communication to buyer
4. Helps in faster confirmation and lower RTO rates
5. Removes the need for manual AWB assignment before communication flows begin

## What if the buyer is not available on WhatsApp in Engage?

If the buyer is not available on WhatsApp, the message will fail silently (not delivered). Engage can attempt SMS or IVR fallback if configured. For NDR flows, alternative contact methods can be set up.

---

## What are all the features available in Engage 360?

Engage 360 offers these features:
1. RTO Suite - Order confirmation, Address verification, COD to prepaid conversion, NDR management
2. Abandoned Cart Recovery - Automated WhatsApp/SMS reminders for cart abandoners
3. Campaigns - Broadcast marketing messages on WhatsApp, SMS, Email
4. Chatbot & Chatflows - Automated customer interaction flows
5. Self WABA - Use your own WhatsApp Business number
6. Click-to-WhatsApp Ads - Facebook/Instagram ad integration
7. Pre-filled Popups - Website widgets to capture customer opt-ins
8. Segments & Targeting - Customer segmentation for targeted messaging
9. Analytics & Reports - Performance tracking for all communication

## How to activate Engage 360 on my Shiprocket account?

To activate Engage 360:
1. Go to the Engage section from the left menu in your Shiprocket panel
2. Select the Engage 360 plan
3. Complete WABA setup (WhatsApp Business Account)
4. Configure SMS and Email channels
5. Connect your sales channels
6. Start setting up automation workflows

Contact the Engage team or your Shiprocket account manager for a demo and onboarding assistance.

---

## What is the minimum plan required for Engage 360?

Engage 360 is available as a separate add-on to your Shiprocket account. Contact the Engage sales team or your account manager for current pricing and plan details.

---

## Can I use Engage 360 without a Shiprocket shipping account?

Engage 360 is primarily designed for Shiprocket sellers. It deeply integrates with Shiprocket's order management and logistics. Contact the Engage team for details on standalone access.

---

## How do I get a demo of Engage 360?

To get a demo of Engage 360:
* Go to the Engage section in your Shiprocket panel and click 'Get Demo' or 'Schedule Demo'
* Contact your Shiprocket account manager
* Reach out to the Engage team through the Shiprocket support portal

---

## Does Engage 360 support Shopify integration?

Yes. Engage 360 integrates with Shopify for order event triggers (cart abandonment, order delivery, etc.) and customer data sync. Connect your Shopify store through the Engage integrations section.

---

## Does Engage 360 support WooCommerce integration?

Yes. Engage 360 supports WooCommerce integration via webhook. Configure the webhook URL in your WooCommerce settings to enable order event triggers in Engage 360.

## What if the buyer is not available on WhatsApp in Engage?

If a customer is not available on WhatsApp, an IVR will be initiated. Post IVR, you can opt for manual calling by our dedicated team. If all three methods fail, the order will be fulfilled anyway.

---

## How can I get started on Engage?

Please reach out to your Account Manager or KAM or write to srengage@shiprocket.com and our team will get in touch with you within 24 hours.

---

## What should I do if my onboarding is not complete on Engage?

If your onboarding is not complete, please reach out to your Account Manager or KAM or write to srengage@shiprocket.com and our team will get in touch with you within 24 hours.

---

## How long does it take to onboard on Engage?

The onboarding process is usually completed within 24-48 hours post the required details are shared by the seller.

---

## What are the prerequisites for onboarding on Engage?

The prerequisites for onboarding on Engage360 are:
1. A Shiprocket account
2. A Facebook Business Manager account
3. A WhatsApp Business number (not already active on WhatsApp)
4. Business documents for verification

---

## What is the onboarding process for Engage 360?

The onboarding process for Engage 360 involves:
1. Seller shares required details with the Engage team
2. Engage team creates the account and configures the flows
3. Seller reviews and approves the configuration
4. Go live!

---

## Can I onboard on Engage without a Shiprocket account?

No, you need an active Shiprocket account to use Engage 360.

---

## What happens after I sign up for Engage?

After signing up, our team will reach out to you within 24 hours to complete the onboarding process. You will receive an email with the next steps.

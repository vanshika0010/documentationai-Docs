## What if the buyer is not available on WhatsApp in Engage?

If the buyer is not available on WhatsApp, the message will fail to deliver. Engage can fall back to SMS communication if configured in your Notify settings. For NDR recovery, if the buyer doesn't respond on WhatsApp, the system can also attempt IVR calls.

## How to get started with Engage 360?

To get started with Engage 360:
1. Go to the Engage section from the Shiprocket panel left menu
2. Click on 'Get Started' or 'Upgrade to Engage 360'
3. Complete onboarding by setting up WABA, SMS sender ID, and email configuration
4. Once set up, start creating automation workflows and campaigns
5. Reach out to the Engage team for a demo and onboarding assistance

---

## What are the onboarding steps for Engage 360?

Onboarding steps for Engage 360:
1. Account creation and plan selection
2. WABA (WhatsApp Business Account) setup and Meta verification
3. SMS DLT registration (for India)
4. Email domain verification
5. Integration with your store (Shopify/WooCommerce)
6. Template creation and approval
7. Automation workflow setup
8. Testing and go-live

---

## How long does Engage 360 onboarding take?

Engage 360 onboarding typically takes 3-7 business days, depending on:
* WABA approval timeline from Meta (usually 2-3 days)
* SMS DLT registration (2-5 days)
* Email domain verification (1-2 days)
* Your store integration and testing

## What documents are needed for Engage 360 onboarding?

For Engage 360 onboarding:
* Facebook Business Manager account (verified)
* Business phone number for WhatsApp
* GST certificate and company documents (for SMS DLT)
* Email domain details
* Store access for integration (Shopify/WooCommerce)

* Shiprocket support ticket with category 'Engage'

## Can I use Engage without setting up WABA?

Yes. The basic WhatsApp Notify feature works without a Self-WABA using Shiprocket's shared WhatsApp sender. For Self-WABA (messages from your own number), you need to complete the WABA setup.

---

## How to integrate my Shopify store with Engage 360?

To integrate Shopify with Engage 360:
1. Connect your Shopify store to Shiprocket (Setup & Manage >> Channels)
2. In Engage 360, go to Integrations and connect your Shopify store
3. Configure data sync for orders, customers, and events
4. Test the integration with a test order
5. Order data will flow automatically into Engage for automation triggers

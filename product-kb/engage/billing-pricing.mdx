## What is the billing process on Engage?

Monthly bills raised between 7th & 10th of the next month. It goes hand in hand with the Shiprocket billing process - Prepaid / Secure Postpaid / Unsecure Postpaid.

---

## How do I cancel my Engage 360 subscription?

You can cancel it from the billing section in your dashboard, but your data will be deleted if you do not renew.

---

## Orders are getting synced on the shipping panel but not on Engage panel?

"If orders are getting synced on the shipping panel but not showing on the Engage360 panel, it's often due to a negative wallet balance or unverified account settings. Here are steps to resolve the issue:

1. Check Wallet Balance: Ensure your Shiprocket wallet has a sufficient positive balance. Engage360 functionalities may be paused if the wallet balance is negative.
2. Verify Account Settings: Make sure your Engage360 account settings are correctly configured and verified.
3. Contact Support: If the issue persists, contact Engage360 support at srengage@shiprocket.com."

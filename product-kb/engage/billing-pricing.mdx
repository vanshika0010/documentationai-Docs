## What is the billing process on Engage?

For Engage 360, billing works as follows:
- RTO Suite: Charged per order processed
- WhatsApp messages: Charged per conversation (24-hour window)
  - Utility messages (NDR, COD to Prepaid, Address Verification): Rs. 0.22 per message
  - Marketing messages (Order Confirmation, Abandoned Cart): Rs. 0.99 per message
- All charges are deducted from your Shiprocket wallet
- Monthly invoices are generated and available in the Engage 360 billing section

---

## What are the charges for Shiprocket Engage 360?

Here's a detailed breakdown of the pricing for Engage 360 services:

RTO service - charged per order. You'll be billed for each order processed using the RTO service.

Whatsapp Services Rate:
a. Utility message for NDR, COD to Prepaid conversion & Address Verification - Rs 0.22 per message
b. Marketing message for Order confirmation & Abandoned cart - Rs 0.99 per message

Note: Each campaign on Engage is treated as a unique conversation on Meta, so messages per unique buyer per campaign will be charged.

## How to check my Engage 360 billing and usage?

To check your Engage 360 billing and usage:
1. Go to the Engage 360 dashboard
2. Navigate to the Billing section
3. You can view current balance, usage breakdown, and download invoices
4. Charges are reflected in your Shiprocket wallet transactions

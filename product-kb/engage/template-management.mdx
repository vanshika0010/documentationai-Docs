## My WhatsApp templates got rejected on Engage. What should I do?

WhatsApp may reject a template for the following reasons:
1. The template contains promotional content (WhatsApp has strict rules on promotions).
2. Sensitive or misleading content.
3. The template doesn't meet WhatsApp's guidelines.
4. Variables not properly formatted.

To resolve, review Meta's template guidelines, edit the template to address the rejection reason, and resubmit for approval.

## What happens if my templates get rejected or paused by Meta on Engage?

If a template is rejected, you can revise and resubmit it via Engage360. Meta typically provides a reason for rejection (e.g., policy violation, formatting error). Review the reason, make necessary changes, and resubmit.

## What is the reason for template rejection on Engage?

Meta may reject your WhatsApp message template for several reasons. Common causes include:
1. Policy violations: Templates that include misleading claims, prohibited content (adult, gambling, weapons), or spam-like language.
2. Incorrect category: Using a Marketing template for Utility purposes or vice versa.
3. Formatting issues: Improper use of variables ({{1}}, {{2}}), missing required fields, or too many variables.
4. Content quality: Vague or unclear message content that doesn't provide value.
5. Language mismatch: Template language doesn't match the declared language.

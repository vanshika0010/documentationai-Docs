## How to create a WhatsApp message template in Engage

Go to Engage >> Templates >> Create Template. Select the category (Marketing, Utility, or Authentication), write your message, add variables for personalization (e.g., {{customer_name}}, {{order_id}}), and submit for Meta approval. Approval typically takes 24–48 hours.

---

## Why was my WhatsApp template rejected

Common reasons for rejection: content violates Meta's policies, missing or incorrect variable format, template resembles spam, or incorrect category selected. Review Meta's template guidelines and resubmit.

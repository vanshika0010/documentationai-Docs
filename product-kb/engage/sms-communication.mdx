## How to set up SMS communication in Engage

Go to Engage >> Settings >> SMS. Configure your SMS sender ID (requires DLT registration in India). Once approved, you can create SMS templates and use SMS in automation workflows and campaigns.

---

## What is DLT registration for SMS

DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) registration is mandatory in India for sending commercial SMS. You must register your entity, brand, and message templates on a DLT portal before sending transactional or promotional SMS.

  <Accordion title="What are WhatsApp messaging limits on Engage?">

WhatsApp messaging limits depend on your WABA tier. You start at Tier 1 (1,000 conversations/day) and can upgrade to Tier 2 (10,000/day) and Tier 3 (100,000/day) based on message volume and quality rating.

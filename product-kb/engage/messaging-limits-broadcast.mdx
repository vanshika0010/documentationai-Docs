## How can I increase my messaging limit on WhatsApp in Engage?

To increase your WhatsApp messaging limit on Engage:
1. Verify your Facebook Business Manager account (required to go beyond 250/day)
2. Maintain a high quality rating (Green status) for your WABA
3. Send messages consistently - tiers upgrade automatically when you reach 50% of current limit with good quality
4. Avoid spam reports and high opt-out rates
5. Use only Meta-approved templates

Messaging tiers:
- Unverified: 250 conversations/day
- Tier 1: 1,000 conversations/day
- Tier 2: 10,000 conversations/day
- Tier 3: 100,000 conversations/day
- Unlimited: No cap

## How can I increase my messaging limit on WhatsApp in Engage?

To improve your messaging limit:
1. Get Opt-In: Ensure users agree to receive WhatsApp notifications.
2. Wait Before Resending: Retry failed messages after 24 hours to maintain quality.
3. Verify Your Facebook Business Manager: A verified FBM is required to increase the limit beyond 250 messages/day.
4. Maintain Quality Rating: Keep your WABA quality rating at Green level.
5. Messaging tiers upgrade automatically as you consistently send messages with good quality.

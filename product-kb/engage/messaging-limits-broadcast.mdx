## What are WhatsApp messaging limits on Engage

WhatsApp messaging limits depend on your WABA tier:

* Tier 1: 1,000 unique conversations per day
* Tier 2: 10,000 unique conversations per day
* Tier 3: 100,000 unique conversations per day

Tiers are upgraded automatically based on message volume and quality rating.

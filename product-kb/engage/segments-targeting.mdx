## How do I stop automated messages for a customer on Engage?

You can exclude specific contacts or segments from journeys.

---

## Can I send location-based messages on Engage?

Yes, if customer location data is available, you can create geo-targeted segments and send out messages to that segment.

---

## What are segments in Engage?

You can create custom segments based on purchase activity, demographics, and more. Engage360 also offers pre-defined segments such as Paying Customers, High Spenders, Repeat Buyers, etc.

---

## Can I auto-tag customers on Engage based on their interactions?

Yes, you can create tags based on customer actions (e.g., "Frequent Buyer," "High-Spender") for better segmentation.

---

## Can I set different WhatsApp templates for different customer segments on Engage?

Yes, Engage 360 allows custom templates for each segment based on purchase behavior, location, or other filters.

---

## Can I register multiple phone numbers with Self WABA on Engage?

Yes, Meta allows you to register up to two phone numbers per WABA account using the embedded sign-up flow. You can assign different numbers for different use cases.

---

## Can I register more than one phone number on my Self WABA with Engage?

Yes, Engage360 allows you to register up to two phone numbers under your Self WABA. This is especially helpful if you want to segment communication between different departments, brands, or use cases.

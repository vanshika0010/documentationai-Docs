## What is Self WABA in Engage

Self WABA allows you to connect your own WhatsApp Business Account to Engage instead of using Shiprocket's shared number. Messages are sent from your own brand number, improving trust and recognition with your customers.

---

## How to connect my WhatsApp Business number to Engage

Go to Engage >> Settings >> WhatsApp >> Connect WABA. Follow the Facebook Business Manager verification process to link your WhatsApp Business Account. You'll need an active Facebook Business Manager account and a phone number not already registered on WhatsApp.

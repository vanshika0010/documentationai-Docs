## What is the difference between virtual number and SELF WABA (White Labeled number) on Engage?

A virtual number is a Shiprocket pre-owned & WhatsApp approved number which is assigned as a default on all onboardings. With it, the buyer sees "Shiprocket" as the brand name in their WhatsApp.

Self WABA (White Labeled) is a number that the seller owns. Messages are sent from seller's own verified business number. Benefits include full branding control, access to advanced features like Carousel and Click-to-WhatsApp Ads.

---

## What should I do if as a seller, my seller's Facebook Business Manager is ineligible for verification on Engage?

Ensure that the linked WABA account is a WhatsApp API account. Verification is not required for WhatsApp SMB Business App accounts.

---

## What should I do if I don't see the option to create Carousel Templates on Engage?

If the Carousel Template option is unavailable:
1. Navigate to the Self WABA section in the Engage360 dashboard.
2. Click on Apply Now to migrate to the Self WABA setup.
3. Once your Self WABA is active, the Carousel Templates option will appear in the Templates section.

## What prerequisites are needed before starting Self WABA setup on Engage?

Before beginning, you must have:

## What is the messaging limit for newly created Self WABA accounts on Engage?

By default, Meta allows only 250 messages per day for newly created WABA accounts that are associated with unverified Facebook Business Managers. To scale beyond this, you must verify your Facebook Business Manager, after which the limit increases to 1,000 unique conversations per day (Tier 1), with further tier upgrades based on messaging volume and quality.

## What happens if I delete my Self WABA after onboarding on Engage?

If you delete your Self WABA, all associated templates, phone numbers, and conversation history will be removed. Your WhatsApp number will also be de-registered from the API.

## Will I need to re-onboard if I switch my display name or phone number on Engage?

Changing your display name or phone number is possible with Self WABA but may require Meta's approval. Display name changes usually go through a short review process. Phone number changes require re-registration.

## Can I access message quality scores for my WABA templates on Engage?

Yes. Engage360 allows you to view message quality scores for each template once your Self WABA is active. These scores are calculated by Meta and reflect how recipients are engaging with (or reporting) your messages.

## Is there a difference between Self WABA and BSP (Business Solution Provider) WABA setups on Engage?

Yes, there are key differences. In a BSP-managed WABA, the BSP owns the account and handles everything from template uploads to reporting. You, as the business, have limited visibility. With Self WABA, you own the account directly—giving you full control over templates, messaging limits, quality scores, and billing.

## How do I verify my Facebook Business Manager for higher message limits on Engage?

To verify your FBM, log into Meta Business Manager, go to Business Settings > Security Center, and click Start Verification. You'll need to submit business documentation (business registration, tax ID, etc.) and complete the verification process.

## Will I get a verified blue tick with a Self WABA setup on Engage?

Having a Self WABA doesn't automatically guarantee the blue tick (Verified Business Badge), but it does simplify and speed up the verification process. You can apply for the blue tick via Meta's official business verification through your Facebook Business Manager.

## Do I need to download a certificate when setting up a Self WABA on Engage?

No. When using Engage360's Embedded Sign-Up process, only Meta Solution Partners are allowed to download WABA certificates. Since Engage360 handles the process on your behalf, you don't need to manage certificates manually.

## What happens if I register a phone number already in use on WhatsApp in Engage?

If the number is already registered on WhatsApp (either as a personal account or through another business provider), you'll be prompted to delete it from the existing account first before re-registering it for WhatsApp Business API.

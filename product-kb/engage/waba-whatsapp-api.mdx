## What is the difference between virtual number and SELF WABA in Engage?

Virtual Number (Default): Messages are sent from Shiprocket's shared WhatsApp Business number (6366058044). Shiprocket manages the WABA on behalf of sellers. No additional setup required.

Self WABA: The seller creates and manages their own WhatsApp Business Account. Messages are sent from the seller's own registered business number. Provides full control over templates, branding, and messaging.

---

## What is Self WABA on Engage?

Self WABA (Self-managed WhatsApp Business Account) is a setup where the business directly creates and owns their WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's Embedded Sign-Up process. Benefits include:
- Messages sent from your own verified business number
- Full control over message templates
- Access to advanced features (Carousel, Click-to-WhatsApp Ads)
- Better brand trust with customers
- Direct billing from Meta

---

## How to set up Self WABA on Engage?

To set up Self WABA on Engage 360:
1. Have a verified Facebook Business Manager account
2. Have a phone number not registered on WhatsApp
3. Go to Engage 360 >> Settings >> WhatsApp >> Self WABA
4. Click on "Connect with Meta" and complete the Embedded Sign-Up flow
5. Verify your phone number via OTP
6. The process typically takes 5-30 minutes

Prerequisites for Self WABA:

## What is the messaging limit for WhatsApp on Engage?

WhatsApp messaging limits by tier:
- Tier 1: 1,000 unique conversations per day
- Tier 2: 10,000 unique conversations per day
- Tier 3: 100,000 unique conversations per day
- Unlimited: No daily limit

Tiers upgrade automatically based on messaging volume and quality rating. Start at Tier 1 and upgrade with good performance.

---

## How to increase messaging limits on WhatsApp on Engage?

To increase WhatsApp messaging limits:
1. Verify your Facebook Business Manager account
2. Maintain a high message quality rating (green status)
3. Send messages consistently (volume-based upgrade)
4. Avoid high opt-out rates and spam reports
5. Tiers upgrade automatically every 7 days when you reach 50% of your current limit with good quality

---

## What is the WhatsApp quality rating and how does it affect Engage?

Meta assigns a quality rating (Green/Yellow/Red) to your WABA based on:
- User feedback (spam reports, blocks)
- Opt-out rates
- Message quality and relevance

Green: Good standing, normal operations
Yellow: Caution, messaging limits may be reduced
Red: Poor standing, messaging may be restricted

Maintain Green rating by sending relevant, consented messages.

---

## How to check WhatsApp quality rating on Engage?

You can check your WhatsApp quality rating in:
1. Meta Business Manager >> WhatsApp Accounts >> Phone Numbers
2. The quality indicator shows Green/Yellow/Red status
3. Engage 360 dashboard also shows WABA quality metrics for Self WABA users

## What happens when WABA quality drops to Red on Engage?

When WABA quality drops to Red:
1. Meta restricts the messaging limit
2. You may be unable to send template messages
3. Recovery requires improving message quality and reducing spam reports
4. Contact srengage@shiprocket.com for assistance if this happens

## How to migrate WABA from another provider to Engage?

To migrate your WABA to Engage360:
1. Ensure your Meta Business Account is verified
2. Disable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on existing WABA
3. Contact your current BSP to release the phone number
4. Engage360 team will assist with migration
5. Complete the Embedded Sign-Up flow on Engage360
Migration typically takes 1-3 business days. Chat history won't transfer.

## What is the difference between a BSP and Self WABA on Engage?

BSP (Business Solution Provider): A third-party company that hosts your WABA. Shiprocket acts as BSP for the default Engage setup.

Self WABA: You own and manage your WABA directly through Meta. Full control, direct billing, access to latest Meta features.

Self WABA is recommended for businesses wanting full control over their WhatsApp communication.

---

## What is the cost difference between BSP and Self WABA on Engage?

With BSP (default): Charges include platform fees + Meta's WhatsApp conversation rates.

With Self WABA: Direct Meta billing at official rates, potentially lower cost. No BSP markup. Full transparency on conversation costs.

## Can I connect my existing WhatsApp Business App to Engage?

No. WhatsApp Business API (used by Engage 360) is separate from the WhatsApp Business App. The same phone number cannot be used in both simultaneously. To use a number with Engage, it must be migrated from the WhatsApp Business App.

## What is a WhatsApp conversation window and how does it affect billing on Engage?

A WhatsApp conversation is a 24-hour window that opens when:
- You send a message to a customer (business-initiated)
- A customer sends a message to you (user-initiated)

All messages within one 24-hour window are counted as one conversation. Billing is per conversation, not per individual message.

---

## What are the types of WhatsApp conversations on Engage?

WhatsApp conversation types:
1. Marketing: Promotions, offers, campaigns (Rs. 0.99/conversation)
2. Utility: Order updates, delivery notifications (Rs. 0.22/conversation)
3. Authentication: OTP messages (separate pricing)
4. Service: Customer-initiated support conversations (different pricing)

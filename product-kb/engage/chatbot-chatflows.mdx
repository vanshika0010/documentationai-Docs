## What are the onboarding steps on Engage?

Onboarding steps for Engage 360:
1. Account activation: Enable Engage 360 from your Shiprocket panel
2. WABA setup: Connect WhatsApp Business Account (default virtual number or Self WABA)
3. Channel integration: Connect your store (Shopify/WooCommerce/etc.)
4. RTO Suite configuration: Enable Order Confirmation, Address Verification, COD to Prepaid as needed
5. Journey/Chatflow setup: Configure automated flows for abandoned cart, campaigns, etc.
6. Template creation: Create and get approval for custom WhatsApp templates (if using Self WABA)
7. Testing: Test flows with your own number before going live

## What is the Chatbot Builder on Engage?

The Chatbot Builder in Engage 360 is a no-code, visual interface for creating automated WhatsApp chat flows. Features:
- Drag-and-drop flow builder
- Multiple node types: Message, Condition, Delay, Action
- Dynamic variables ({{customer_name}}, {{order_id}}, etc.)
- Multi-step conversation flows
- Integration with order and customer data
- Available for Self WABA users

## How to create a chatbot on Engage?

To create a chatbot on Engage 360:
1. Go to Chatbot section in Engage 360
2. Click Create New Chatflow
3. Set the trigger (keyword, campaign click, event)
4. Add message nodes with your content
5. Add condition branches for different customer responses
6. Add delay nodes for timed follow-ups
7. Test the flow with your own number
8. Activate the chatflow

---

## What triggers can I use for chatbots on Engage?

Chatbot trigger options in Engage 360:
1. Keyword trigger: Customer sends a specific word (e.g., "TRACK", "ORDER")
2. Campaign click: Customer clicks a button in a campaign message
3. Segment entry: Customer matches a segment criteria
4. Event-based: Order status change (delivered, NDR, etc.)
5. Manual trigger: Activated by agent from inbox

## What is a Chatflow vs a Journey on Engage?

Chatflow: An interactive, conversational flow where the customer's responses determine the next step. Two-way conversation. Best for customer support, order tracking queries, feedback collection.

Journey: A one-directional automated message sequence triggered by events. Messages are sent based on a predefined timeline, not customer responses. Best for abandoned cart recovery, order updates, campaigns.

## How to add human handoff in chatbot on Engage?

To enable human handoff in Engage 360 chatbot:
1. Add a "Handoff to Agent" node in your chatflow
2. Set conditions for when to handoff (e.g., customer says "help" or "agent")
3. When triggered, the conversation moves to the Engage 360 inbox for agent response
4. Agent responds within the 24-hour WhatsApp conversation window

## How to test chatbot flows on Engage?

To test chatbot flows on Engage 360:
1. Use your own phone number as a test contact
2. Send the trigger keyword to your business WhatsApp number
3. Walk through the full conversation flow
4. Check all branches and conditions work correctly
5. Fix any issues in the chatflow builder before activating

## What is the difference between a pre-built and custom chatflow on Engage?

Pre-built chatflows: Ready-to-use templates provided by Engage 360 for common use cases (order confirmation, NDR recovery, abandoned cart). Can be activated in one click.

Custom chatflows: Built from scratch using the visual chatflow builder. Fully customizable for your specific business requirements.

## How to pause or stop a chatflow on Engage?

To pause a chatflow in Engage 360:
1. Go to the Chatbot section
2. Find the active chatflow
3. Toggle it OFF to pause
4. The flow will stop triggering for new conversations
5. Ongoing conversations will complete their current step

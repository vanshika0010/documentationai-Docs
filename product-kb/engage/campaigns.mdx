## How to create a segment on Engage?

Log in to Shiprocket Engage:
* Navigate to your Shiprocket Engage dashboard using your credentials.

Access the Segments Section:
* From the main dashboard, go to the Audience and then Segments tab. This is where you manage and create all your customer segments.

Create a New Segment:
* Click on the “Create Segment” button.

Define Segment Criteria:
* You'll be asked to provide a name for your segment.
* Then, set the criteria for your segment. This could be based on specific order statuses, product preferences, customer behaviors, or other relevant factors.
* Add conditions or filters as needed to define your segment. For example, you might target customers who have made more than 3 purchases in the last 30 days.

Save the Segment:
* Once you've defined the criteria, save the segment by clicking the “Create Segment” or “Save” button.

## What is a WhatsApp Carousel, and how does it work in Engage?

WhatsApp Carousel Messages allow businesses to showcase multiple products or services within a single message. In Engage360, sellers can create Carousel templates with up to 10 cards, each featuring a unique image, header, body text, and customizable buttons. This allows for visually engaging, swipeable product showcases within WhatsApp.

## How do I create a Carousel template for WhatsApp campaigns on Engage?

You can create a WhatsApp Carousel template by following these steps:
1. Login to Engage 360
2. Navigate to Channels > WhatsApp > Carousel Templates
3. Click "Create Template"
4. Set up 2-10 cards with images, body text, and buttons
5. Submit for Meta approval
Approval typically takes 24-48 hours.

## What happens if my WhatsApp campaign message is rejected by Meta on Engage?

If Meta rejects your template:
1. Review the rejection reason in the Templates section
2. Meta typically rejects templates that violate policies (promotional content in utility template, misleading claims, etc.)
3. Edit the template to address the rejection reason
4. Resubmit for approval
Contact srengage@shiprocket.com for assistance.

## How does Engage handle DND (Do Not Disturb) customers for SMS campaigns?

Engage 360 filters out DND numbers when sending promotional SMS campaigns through registered sender IDs. Transactional SMS may still be delivered to DND numbers if you have a registered transactional sender ID.

## What is the difference between a campaign and a broadcast on Engage?

In Engage 360, Campaign and Broadcast refer to the same feature - sending a message to a segment of customers at a scheduled time. The terms are used interchangeably.

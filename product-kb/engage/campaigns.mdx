## How to create a marketing campaign in Engage

Go to Engage >> Campaigns >> Create Campaign. Select your channel (WhatsApp, SMS, or Email), choose your target segment, select or create a message template, schedule the send time, and launch. Monitor performance from the Campaign Analytics dashboard.

---

## What types of campaigns can I run through Engage

You can run:

* **Promotional campaigns** — offers, discounts, new launches
* **Transactional campaigns** — order updates, shipping notifications
* **Re-engagement campaigns** — win-back lapsed customers
* **Event-based campaigns** — festivals, birthdays

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to create a marketing campaign in Engage?">

Go to Engage >> Campaigns >> Create Campaign. Select your channel (WhatsApp, SMS, or Email), choose your target segment, select or create a message template, schedule the send time, and launch. Monitor performance from the Campaign Analytics dashboard.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What types of campaigns can I run through Engage?">

You can run: Promotional campaigns (offers, discounts, new launches), transactional campaigns (order updates, shipping notifications), re-engagement campaigns (win-back lapsed customers), and event-based campaigns.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to create a segment on Engage?

Log in to Shiprocket Engage:
* Navigate to your Shiprocket Engage dashboard using your credentials.

Access the Segments Section:
* From the main dashboard, go to the Audience and then Segments tab. This is where you manage and create your customer segments.

Create a New Segment:
* Click on the Create Segment button.

Define Segment Rules:
Set the criteria for your segment, such as:
* Demographics: Age, location, gender, etc.
* Behavioral Data: Purchase history, website activity, engagement with marketing emails.
* Order Information: Products purchased, total order value, order frequency.
You can combine multiple rules using AND / OR logic to refine the segment.

Preview the Segment:
* Once you define the rules, you'll see a preview of how many users match the criteria.

Save the Segment:
* Name your segment and click Save. The segment is now created and can be used for targeting specific groups in your marketing campaigns.

## Why are my SMS campaigns failing on Engage?

SMS campaigns can fail due to:
1. Invalid or incorrect phone numbers.
2. The recipient's number is registered on DND (Do Not Disturb).
3. The SMS provider has blocked certain promotional content.
4. You have exceeded your SMS sending limit.
5. Check your SMS provider's rules and ensure numbers are correctly formatted.

## Why are my Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) not generating chats on Engage?

Possible causes:
1. The ad link is incorrect or not directing to WhatsApp.
2. The customer's browser settings may block WhatsApp redirection.
3. The WhatsApp Business API may not be set up correctly.
4. The audience targeting in the ad campaign might not be relevant.

Solution: Verify the ad setup, test the link, and refine your targeting.

## Why are my email campaigns via Engage going to spam?

Emails may land in spam due to:
1. Lack of proper email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC settings).
2. Use of spammy words like "Free", "Buy Now", "Limited Offer".
3. Sending emails to a purchased or outdated email list.
4. Poor sender reputation due to high bounce rates.

Solution: Use a verified email domain, clean up your email list, and avoid spammy language.

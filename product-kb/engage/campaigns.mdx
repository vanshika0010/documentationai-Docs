Yes. With the help of the Campaigns in the Marketing module of Engage360, the seller can opt, schedule and send broadcast messages considering promotional campaigns, offers, or updates.

## How to create a campaign on Engage?

To create a campaign on Engage 360:
1. Navigate to the Marketing section and click on the 'Campaigns' option.
2. Click on the 'Create Campaign' button.
3. Choose your communication channel (WhatsApp, SMS, or Email).
4. Select or create a target audience segment for your campaign.
5. Choose a pre-approved message template or create a new one.
6. Preview your campaign to ensure everything looks right.
7. Schedule or immediately send your campaign.

---

## What kind of campaigns can I create on Engage 360?

On Engage 360, you can create the following types of campaigns:
1. Promotional Campaigns: Target specific customer segments with promotional offers or discounts.
2. Informational Campaigns: Send product updates, news, or other relevant information to your customers.
3. Re-engagement Campaigns: Reach out to customers who haven't made a purchase in a while to encourage them to return.
4. Transactional Campaigns: Send order confirmations, shipping updates, or other transactional messages to your customers.
5. Event-triggered Campaigns: Set up automated messages triggered by specific events like cart abandonment or order completion.

## Can I send promotional messages to my existing customers on Engage?

Yes, Engage 360 allows you to send promotional messages to your existing customers. You can create targeted campaigns for different customer segments, schedule campaigns for specific dates and times, and track the performance of your campaigns.

## Can I personalise WhatsApp campaigns on Engage?

Yes. You can use variables like {{customer_name}}, {{order_id}}, etc., in your WhatsApp templates to personalize messages for each recipient.

---

## What is the character limit for WhatsApp messages on Engage?

WhatsApp message templates have a limit of 1024 characters for the body. Headers and footers have smaller limits. Keep messages concise and impactful.

---

## Can I run multiple campaigns simultaneously on Engage?

Yes. You can run multiple campaigns simultaneously on Engage 360. Each campaign is managed independently with its own audience, schedule, and analytics.

---

## What is the best time to send WhatsApp campaigns on Engage?

Best practices suggest sending WhatsApp campaigns during business hours (10 AM - 7 PM local time). Avoid early mornings and late nights. Test different times with your audience to find what works best for your business.

---

## Can I target specific customer segments for campaigns on Engage?

Yes. Engage 360 allows you to create segments based on various criteria (order history, location, purchase value, engagement level) and target specific segments with tailored campaign messages.

---

## How to measure the success of WhatsApp campaigns on Engage?

Engage 360 provides campaign analytics including:
- Sent count
- Delivered count
- Read/opened count
- Click-through rate (for messages with links)
- Conversion rate (orders placed)
- Revenue attributed to the campaign
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

Yes. WhatsApp templates can include a header with:
- Image (up to 5 MB)
- Video (up to 16 MB)
- Document (up to 100 MB)

For Carousel templates (available with Self WABA), you can include multiple product images.

## Can I run campaigns for specific dates or festivals on Engage?

Yes. Use the campaign scheduler in Engage 360 to plan festival campaigns (Diwali, Holi, etc.) in advance. Set the exact date and time for the campaign to launch automatically.

---

## What happens if a recipient opts out during a campaign on Engage?

If a recipient opts out (STOP reply), Engage 360 automatically removes them from future campaigns and marks them as opted-out. No further messages are sent to opted-out contacts.

---

## Can I pause a running campaign on Engage?

Yes. You can pause a scheduled campaign before it goes out from the Campaigns section. For campaigns that have already started sending, you can stop remaining sends.

---

## Does Engage support drip campaigns?

Yes. Using Journeys in Engage 360, you can create drip campaigns that send a series of messages over time based on triggers or schedules.

---

## Can I import a contact list for campaigns on Engage?

Yes. You can import contact lists via CSV in the Contacts section of Engage 360. The CSV requires at minimum a phone number column for WhatsApp campaigns.

---

## What file format is supported for contact import on Engage?

Engage 360 supports CSV format for bulk contact imports. Required column: Phone number. Optional: Name, Email, Extra Attributes.

---

## How to check campaign delivery reports on Engage?

Go to Campaigns in Engage 360, find the specific campaign, and click on Analytics or View Report. You can see per-campaign metrics including sent, delivered, read, clicks, and conversions.

---

## Can I clone a campaign on Engage?

Yes. Use the Duplicate/Clone option on an existing campaign to create a copy with the same settings. You can then modify as needed before launching.

---

## What is a broadcast campaign vs a journey on Engage?

Broadcast Campaign: One-time message sent to a segment at a specific scheduled time. Best for announcements and promotions.

Journey: Ongoing automated flow triggered by customer events. Messages are personalized and sent based on individual customer behavior.

---

## Can I send campaigns to customers who haven't purchased recently on Engage?

Yes. Create a segment of lapsed customers (e.g., no purchase in 30/60/90 days) and run a re-engagement campaign with a special offer to bring them back.

---

## Does Engage support recurring campaigns?

You can set up recurring campaigns by creating a Journey with a time-based trigger (e.g., weekly) or by manually scheduling the same campaign template at regular intervals.

A/B testing is available on Engage 360. Create two campaign variants with different messages/templates and test which performs better before running the full campaign.

There is no hard limit on the number of campaigns per month. Costs are based on actual messages/conversations used.

## What is the difference between WhatsApp Utility and Marketing messages for campaigns on Engage?

Utility: Transactional messages related to specific user actions (order updates, payment reminders). Lower cost. Can be sent any time.

Marketing: Promotional content (offers, sale announcements, re-engagement). Higher cost. Cannot be sent to customers who have explicitly opted out of marketing.

## What is campaign fatigue and how to avoid it on Engage?

Campaign fatigue occurs when customers receive too many messages and start ignoring or opting out. To avoid it:
1. Limit campaigns to 2-4 per month per customer
2. Segment precisely (send relevant messages)
3. Monitor opt-out rates
4. Use personalization to make messages feel valuable
5. Set minimum days between campaigns in Journey conditions

  <Accordion title="How does Engage help recover NDR shipments?">

When a Non-Delivery Report (NDR) is triggered, Engage automatically sends a WhatsApp/SMS message to the buyer to confirm availability, update address, or reschedule delivery. Based on the buyer's response, the system triggers a re-attempt, helping recover failed deliveries and reduce RTO.

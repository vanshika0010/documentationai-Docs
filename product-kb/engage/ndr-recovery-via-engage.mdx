## How to disable RTO suite on Engage?

"To disable the RTO Suite, you have two options:

1. Turn Off Channel Sync:

   a. Go to your Engage360 Dashboard.

   b. Navigate to Settings -> RTO Suite -> Channel Sync.

   c. Toggle off the channel sync for the relevant channels.

   d. This will stop Engage360 from receiving order updates and triggering RTO Suite flows.

2. Disable Individual Flows:

   a. In the RTO Suite settings, you can individually disable specific flows such as Order Confirmation, Address Verification, COD to Prepaid, or NDR Management.

   b. Toggle off the flows you want to disable."

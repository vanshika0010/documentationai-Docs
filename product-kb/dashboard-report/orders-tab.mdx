  <Accordion title="What are the different order tabs in Shiprocket?">

Shiprocket organizes orders into: New Orders (pending processing), Ready to Ship, In Transit, Delivered, RTO Initiated, RTO Delivered, Cancelled, and All Orders. Each tab filters orders by their current status.

## What are the different order tabs in Shiprocket

Shiprocket organizes orders into tabs:

* **New Orders** — pending processing
* **Ready to Ship** — AWB assigned
* **In Transit** — picked up and moving
* **Delivered** — successfully delivered
* **RTO Initiated / RTO Delivered** — return to origin
* **Cancelled** — cancelled orders
* **All Orders** — every order in one view

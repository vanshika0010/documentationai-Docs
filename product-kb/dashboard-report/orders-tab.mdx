## What are the different order tabs in Shiprocket?

Shiprocket organizes orders into the following tabs:
* New Orders — pending processing
* Ready to Ship — AWB assigned, waiting for pickup
* Pickup Scheduled — pickup request generated
* In Transit — picked up and moving to destination
* Out for Delivery — with courier for last-mile delivery
* Delivered — successfully delivered
* RTO Initiated — return to origin started
* RTO In Transit — shipment returning to seller
* RTO Delivered — returned to seller
* Cancelled — cancelled orders
* All Orders — every order in one view

---

## How to move an order from New Orders to Ready to Ship?

In the New Orders tab, select the order(s) and click 'Ship Now.' Assign a courier and confirm. The order will move to Ready to Ship once an AWB is generated.

## How to view the tracking dashboard?

You can view shipment tracking information by:
1. Going to Orders from the left menu.
2. Clicking on any order to see its tracking details.
3. Alternatively, use the Search feature to find a specific order by AWB number.
4. The tracking page shows the complete journey of the shipment from pickup to delivery with timestamps.

---

## Can I Request Real-Time Data on the Dashboard?

The Shiprocket dashboard shows near real-time data. Tracking updates come from courier APIs and may have a slight delay. Click the refresh button on the dashboard to get the latest data. For critical shipment statuses, you can also check directly on the courier's tracking website using the AWB number.

---

## Can we send seller notifications to multiple emails or mobile numbers?

Yes. Go to Settings >> Notifications in your Shiprocket account. You can configure multiple email addresses to receive different types of notifications (order updates, wallet alerts, etc.).

---

## How to download billing reports?

Go to Finances from the left menu. You can download:
* Wallet Transaction Report (from Passbook)
* COD Remittance Report
* Invoice/Freight Invoice
* Weight Discrepancy Report

Select the report type, choose the date range, and click Download.

---

## How to view the Passbook on Shiprocket?

Go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu. The Passbook shows all financial transactions including wallet recharges, shipping deductions, COD credits, refunds, and adjustments with timestamps.

---

## How to check the order-level charges?

Go to the specific order in Orders section and click on it to open order details. The order details page shows the shipping charges applied, including base freight, fuel surcharge, COD charges (if applicable), and any other fees.

---

## How to view the channel-wise order distribution?

Go to Reports >> Analytics from the left menu. The analytics section shows channel-wise breakdown of your orders (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, etc.) to help you understand which channels generate the most orders.

---

## What are the reports available on Shiprocket?

Shiprocket offers the following reports:
* Shipment/Tracking Report
* NDR Report
* COD Remittance Report
* Weight Discrepancy/Reconciliation Report
* Wallet Transaction Report
* Returns Report
* Order Report
* Analytics (Delivery Performance, Courier Performance, Zone Analysis)

---

## How to check the RTO analysis on Shiprocket?

Go to Reports >> Analytics >> RTO Analysis. This shows RTO rates by courier, zone, and time period to help identify patterns and reduce return-to-origin rates.

---

## How to search for a specific order in the Orders tab?

In the Orders section, use the search bar at the top to search by:
* Order ID
* AWB (tracking) number
* Customer name
* Customer phone number
* Customer email

You can also use the filter options to narrow results by date range, status, courier, or channel.

---

## How to export orders data from Shiprocket?

Go to Orders from the left menu, apply the required filters (date range, status, etc.), and click 'Export' or 'Download' to get your orders data in CSV format.

---

## What is the Freight Invoice?

The Freight Invoice is the monthly billing document from Shiprocket that shows all shipping charges for the billing period. It includes base freight charges, fuel surcharges, COD charges, and any other applicable fees. Download it from Finances >> Invoices.

---

## How to check payment status for COD orders?

Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu. This section shows the payment status for all COD orders - which amounts have been remitted to your bank account and which are pending.

---

## How to find delayed or non-moving shipments?

Go to Reports or use the NDR Management section to find delayed shipments. You can also check the 'Delayed Shipments' report if available in your Reports section, which lists shipments with no tracking updates beyond the expected transit time.

---

## What does the Quick Insights section on Dashboard show?

Quick Insights on the Dashboard provides a summary of key performance indicators including delivery percentage, RTO percentage, NDR percentage, and other metrics to give you an at-a-glance view of your shipping performance.

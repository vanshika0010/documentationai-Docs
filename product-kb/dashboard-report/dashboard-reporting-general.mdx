## How to view the tracking dashboard?

Go to the Orders section from the left menu. Use the status tabs (In Transit, Out for Delivery, etc.) to view shipments in different stages. Click on any shipment to see its real-time tracking details.

---

## Can I Request Real-Time Data on the Dashboard?

The Shiprocket dashboard updates in near real-time for most data points. For order statuses and tracking, updates come from courier tracking APIs and may have a slight delay (typically 30-60 minutes). You can click the refresh button to get the latest data.

---

## Can we send seller notifications to multiple emails or mobile numbers?

Yes, you can configure multiple email addresses for notifications in Shiprocket. Go to Settings >> Notifications and add multiple email recipients for different notification types.

---

## How to customize which widgets appear on the Shiprocket Dashboard?

Go to the Dashboard home page and click on the customize/settings option to select and arrange widgets according to your preference. You can add, remove, or rearrange widgets to focus on the metrics most important to your business.

---

## How to check my daily shipping limit on Postpaid?

If you are on the Postpaid plan, your daily shipping limit is based on your COD delivered shipments. Go to Finances >> Wallet/Postpaid to see your current shipping limit and usage.

---

## How to view the pickup scheduled orders?

Go to Orders >> Pickup Scheduled tab from the left menu. This shows all orders for which pickup has been scheduled along with the pickup date and courier details.

---

## What is the purpose of the Activity Log?

The Activity Log in Shiprocket tracks all changes and actions on your account such as order updates, courier assignments, settings changes, and user activity. It helps with auditing and troubleshooting.

---

## How to filter reports by specific criteria?

In the Reports section, use the filter options available:
* Date range filter
* Status filter
* Courier filter
* Channel filter
* Zone filter

Apply filters and click Generate/Download to get filtered report data.

---

## Can I schedule automated daily reports in Shiprocket?

Yes, you can schedule automated reports to be emailed daily/weekly. Check the Reports section for scheduling options. Some report types support automatic scheduling to a designated email address.

---

## How to check the passbook/wallet transaction history?

Go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu. The Passbook shows all wallet transactions including recharges, deductions for shipping, COD credits, refunds, and adjustments with timestamps.

---

## What is the Orders tab on Dashboard?

The Orders tab on the Shiprocket dashboard provides an overview of your orders organized by their current status. It shows counts for New Orders, Ready to Ship, In Transit, Delivered, NDR, and RTO orders. Click any status to drill into that specific order list.

---

## Can I view courier-wise performance data?

Yes. Go to Reports >> Analytics >> Courier Performance. This shows delivery rates, RTO rates, and NDR rates for each courier partner so you can make informed decisions about courier selection.

---

## How to download the Returns report?

Go to Reports from the left menu, select 'Returns Report', choose the date range, and click Download. The report includes all reverse pickup orders, their status, and related details.

---

## How to see my Top Performing Products or Orders by Value?

Go to Reports >> Analytics section where you can view order analytics including top products by order count and order value insights for your business.

---

## What data is included in the RTO Analysis report?

The RTO Analysis report provides insights on Return to Origin shipments including:
* RTO count by courier
* RTO reasons distribution
* Zone-wise RTO rates
* Trend over time

This helps identify patterns and take corrective actions to reduce RTO.

## How can I understand Shiprocket Dashboard?

The Shiprocket Dashboard is your central hub for managing all your shipping activities. Here's a breakdown of the key components:

1. Overview: Provides a summary of your account's performance including key metrics such as Wallet Balance, Pending Orders, In-Transit Shipments, Delivered Shipments, and NDR count.
2. Orders Tab: Displays your current orders and their statuses, allowing you to manage and process orders quickly.
3. Quick Insights: Offers quick insights into your shipment performance, including delivery percentages and returns.
4. Activity Log: Tracks recent activities and changes made to your account.
5. Rate Calculator: Enables you to calculate shipping rates for different couriers and destinations.
6. Serviceability Checker: Lets you check whether a specific pincode is serviceable by available couriers.

---

## What details are available inside the Overview tab of the Dashboard?

The Overview tab provides the following:

* Wallet Balance with quick recharge option
* Pending Orders count
* In-Transit shipments count
* NDR (Non-Delivery Report) count
* Delivered shipments count
* RTO (Return to Origin) count
* Quick access to Rate Calculator and Serviceability Checker
* Recent order activity

---

## What information is there in the orders tab of the Dashboard?

The Orders tab provides:

* List of all orders with their current status
* Filter options by date range, status, courier, and channel
* Quick actions like Ship Now, Cancel, Download Label
* Search by order ID, AWB, customer name, or phone number
* Order details including customer info, product details, weight, and payment type

---

## How can I use the Rate Calculator on the Shiprocket dashboard?

Go to Dashboard >> Rate Calculator. Enter:
* Origin pincode
* Destination pincode
* Package weight (in kg)
* Package dimensions (L x W x H in cm)

The calculator will show rates for all available couriers for that route, along with estimated delivery time.

---

## How can I check serviceability for a specific pincode?

Go to Dashboard >> Serviceability Checker. Enter origin and destination pincodes and click Check. The tool will show which couriers are available for that route and their serviceability status.

---

## What is the Activity Log on the Dashboard?

The Activity Log tracks all recent actions performed on your Shiprocket account, including:
* Orders created, shipped, or cancelled
* Courier assignments and reassignments
* Wallet recharges and deductions
* Settings changes
* User logins and actions

---

## How do I download the orders summary report?

Go to Reports from the left menu, select the report type (e.g., Shipment Report), choose the date range, and click Download. Reports are available in CSV format.

---

## Can I set up automated reports on Shiprocket?

Yes, certain reports can be scheduled for automatic email delivery. Check the Reports section for scheduling options and configure your preferred frequency and recipients.

---

## What are Quick Insights on the Dashboard?

Quick Insights provide a visual summary of your shipping performance including:
* Delivery success rate percentage
* RTO percentage
* NDR percentage
* Channel-wise order distribution
* Courier-wise performance metrics

---

## How to track wallet balance and transactions from the Dashboard?

Your current wallet balance is displayed on the Dashboard's Overview tab. For detailed transaction history, go to Finances >> Passbook from the left menu. You can view all recharges, deductions, COD credits, and adjustments.

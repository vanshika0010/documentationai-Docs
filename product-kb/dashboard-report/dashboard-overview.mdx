## How can I understand Shiprocket Dashboard?

1. You can quickly assess and manage your daily operations and financial metrics. 
2. Also, you will find the banner which is the promotional element, likely advertising the marketing tool associated with Shiprocket.
3. Near the bottom, you will see a Recent Activity section that records all the relevant activities for your account.
4. On the right panel of the dashboard, you will see a Wallet balance to manage and maintain your finances.
5. You can also manage quick access to your Wallet, Rate Calculator, and Pincode Serviceability Check Tool.

---

## What details are available inside the Overview tab of the Dashboard?

The overview tab provides a quick overview of various aspects of your shipping operations., it provides a quick overview of various aspects of your shipping operations. Here’s the information you can find:

1. New Orders: A number representing the new orders you've received.
2. Ready to Ship: The orders that have been processed and are ready to be shipped.
3. Pickup Scheduled: The number of orders where pick-up has been scheduled.
4. In-Transit: The orders currently in transit.
5. Out of Delivery: The orders that are out for delivery.
6. Delivered: The orders that have been delivered.
7. NDR Count: The number of Non-Delivery Reports received.
8. RTO Initiated: The number of Return to Origin (RTO) orders initiated.
9. RTO Delivered: The number of RTO orders that have been delivered back to the seller.

---

## What information is there in the orders tab of the Dashboard?

1. The "Orders" tab on the dashboard is vital for tracking your e-commerce operations. It provides a detailed view of your orders' journey, from pickup to delivery.
2. You'll find key shipping milestones, ranging from "Pickup scheduled" to "RTO Delivered" with each status representing a stage of your order's journey.
3. In the All tab, you will see the complete breakdown of all orders including today's orders across all statuses, giving you a real-time snapshot of your entire inventory.

## How can I use the Rate Calculator on the Shiprocket dashboard?

You can find the Rate Calculator link on the right side of your Shiprocket Dashboard. Click on the link or navigate to the Rate Calculator page, then:
1. Enter the pickup and delivery pincodes.
2. Enter the weight of your shipment in kg.
3. Select if the shipment is COD or Prepaid.
4. Click on 'Search' to view available rates.

---

## What is the Pincode Serviceability Check Tool?

The Pincode Serviceability Check Tool lets you check if a specific pincode is serviceable by couriers on Shiprocket. You can access it from the Dashboard quick links. Enter origin and destination pincodes to check available couriers.

---

## What does the Activity Log show on Dashboard?

The Activity Log or Recent Activity section on the Dashboard records all relevant activities for your account, including order updates, courier assignments, wallet transactions, settings changes, and user actions.

---

## How to view wallet balance on Dashboard?

Your current wallet balance is displayed on the right panel of the Shiprocket Dashboard. You can also click on it to go to the wallet management section for recharging or viewing transaction history.

---

## How do I check the delivery performance on Shiprocket dashboard?

You can check delivery performance through the Analytics/Reports section from the left menu. Key metrics available include delivery percentage, RTO percentage, NDR rate, and courier-wise performance data.

---

## What are the Quick Access features available on Shiprocket Dashboard?

The Shiprocket Dashboard provides quick access to:
* Wallet Balance and Recharge
* Rate Calculator
* Pincode Serviceability Check
* Recent Activity log
* Order status overview
* NDR management

---

## How do I see my most recent orders on the Dashboard?

The Dashboard shows recent orders in the Overview and Orders tab. For a detailed view, go to Orders from the left menu where you can see all orders with their current status, and filter by date range or status.

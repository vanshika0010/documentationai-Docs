## How to read Shipment delays using the Delays Dashboard?

The Delays Dashboard shows shipments that have not received tracking updates within the expected transit time. To use it:
1. Go to Reports >> Delays Dashboard from the left menu.
2. The dashboard shows shipments categorized by delay duration (e.g., 2-5 days, 5-10 days, 10+ days).
3. Click on any category to see the specific shipments.
4. You can raise escalations directly from this dashboard for delayed shipments.

---

## How to view the NDR dashboard?

Go to NDR Management from the left menu. The NDR dashboard shows:
* All shipments with non-delivery reports
* NDR reasons (buyer not available, wrong address, refused delivery, etc.)
* Days remaining to take action
* Options to request reattempt or initiate RTO

You can filter by date range, courier, or NDR reason to manage your NDR shipments effectively.

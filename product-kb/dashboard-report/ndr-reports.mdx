## How to read Shipment delays using the Delays Dashboard?

The Delays Dashboard on Shiprocket shows shipments that have experienced unexpected delays. To access and use it:
1. Navigate to the Reports or Analytics section from the left menu.
2. Look for the 'Delays' or 'Delayed Shipments' report.
3. The dashboard categorizes shipments by delay duration.
4. You can filter by courier, date range, or zone to identify patterns.
5. Use this data to raise escalations with couriers for resolution.

---

## How to view the NDR dashboard?

To view the NDR (Non-Delivery Report) dashboard:
1. Go to NDR Management from the left menu.
2. The dashboard shows all shipments with pending NDRs.
3. You can see NDR reasons, days pending, and available actions.
4. Filter by courier, date range, or NDR reason.
5. Take action (Request Reattempt or Initiate RTO) directly from the dashboard.

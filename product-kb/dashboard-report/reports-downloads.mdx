## What reports are available for download in Shiprocket

Shiprocket offers multiple reports: Shipment/Tracking Report, NDR Report, COD Remittance Report, Weight Discrepancy Report, Wallet Transaction Report, Order Report, and Returns Report. Go to Reports from the left menu to access all options.

---

## How to download the COD remittance report

Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu. Select the date range and click Download to get the COD remittance report in CSV format.

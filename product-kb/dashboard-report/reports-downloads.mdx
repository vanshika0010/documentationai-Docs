## What reports are available for download in Shiprocket?

Shiprocket offers multiple reports:
* Shipment Tracking Report
* NDR Report
* COD Remittance Report
* Weight Discrepancy/Reconciliation Report
* Wallet Transaction Report (Passbook)
* Returns/Reverse Pickup Report
* Order Report
* Freight/Billing Invoice
* Analytics Reports (Delivery Performance, Courier Performance, Zone Analysis, RTO Analysis)

Go to Reports from the left menu to access all available reports.

---

## How to generate and download any report?

To download a report in Shiprocket:
1. Go to Reports from the left menu.
2. Select the report type you need.
3. Set the date range and any filters.
4. Click 'Generate' or 'Download'.
5. The report will be downloaded in CSV format.

---

## How to download the COD remittance report?

Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu. Select the date range and click Download to get the COD remittance report in CSV format showing all COD orders and their payment status.

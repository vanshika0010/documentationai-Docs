<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What reports are available for download in Shiprocket?">

Shiprocket offers: Shipment/Tracking Report, NDR Report, COD Remittance Report, Weight Discrepancy Report, Wallet Transaction Report, Order Report, and Returns Report. Go to Reports from the left menu to access all options.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to download the COD remittance report?">

Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu. Select the date range and click Download to get the COD remittance report in CSV format.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

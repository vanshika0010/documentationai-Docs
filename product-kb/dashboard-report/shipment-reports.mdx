## Does shiprocket have a separate Dashboard for International and Domestic shipments?

Shiprocket handles International shipments through ShiprocketX (SRX) which has its own dedicated section. Go to the SRX section from your Shiprocket panel to view and manage international shipments separately from domestic orders.

---

## What are the recommended actions to set up an account widget on the home page?

To customize widgets on the Shiprocket home page dashboard:
1. Go to the Dashboard home page.
2. Look for the 'Customize' option on the dashboard.
3. Select which widgets/metrics you want to display.
4. Arrange them as per your preference.
5. Save your customization.

---

## How to check which courier partner is available between pickup pincode and delivery pincode?

Use the Rate Calculator from the Dashboard:
1. Go to Dashboard and click on Rate Calculator.
2. Enter your pickup pincode (origin).
3. Enter your delivery pincode (destination).
4. Enter package weight.
5. Click Search/Calculate to see all available couriers for that route with rates and estimated delivery time.

---

## How to download the Shipment Report?

Go to Reports from the left menu:
1. Select 'Shipment Report' from the available report types.
2. Choose the date range.
3. Apply any additional filters (courier, status, channel).
4. Click Download.
The report is downloaded in CSV format and includes AWB, order ID, status, courier, dates, and weight details.

---

## What data is included in the Shiprocket shipment tracking report?

The shipment tracking report includes:
* Order ID
* AWB (tracking) number
* Courier name
* Order date
* Pickup date
* Delivery date
* Current status
* Origin pincode
* Destination pincode
* Weight
* Payment type (COD/Prepaid)
* Channel name

---

## How to download the Weight Discrepancy Report?

Go to Reports >> Weight Reconciliation from the left menu. Select the date range and click Download. The report shows all shipments with weight discrepancies between declared weight and courier-measured weight.

---

## What is the Returns Report and how to download it?

The Returns Report shows all reverse pickup (return) orders and their status. Go to Reports >> Returns Report, select the date range, and click Download.

---

## How to view zone-wise shipment distribution?

Go to Reports >> Analytics from the left menu. The analytics section shows zone-wise breakdown of your shipments (Local, Zonal A, Zonal B, National) to help you understand your shipping patterns.

---

## How to get courier-wise performance report?

Go to Reports >> Analytics >> Courier Performance. This shows delivery rates, RTO rates, and NDR rates for each courier partner to help you make informed courier selection decisions.

---

## How far back can I access historical reports?

You can access reports for up to the last 3 months from the Reports section. For data beyond 3 months, contact Shiprocket support with your specific requirements.

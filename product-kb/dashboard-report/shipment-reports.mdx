## Does shiprocket have a separate Dashboard for International and Domestic shipments?

Yes, Shiprocket has separate sections for International (ShiprocketX) and Domestic shipments. International shipments managed through ShiprocketX have their own tracking and reporting interface.

---

## What are the recommended actions to set up an account widget on the home page?

To set up widgets on the Shiprocket home page dashboard:
1. Go to the Dashboard home page.
2. Click on 'Customize Dashboard' or the settings icon on the dashboard.
3. Select the widgets you want to display (Wallet Balance, Orders, NDR, etc.).
4. Drag and arrange widgets as per your preference.
5. Click Save to apply your customization.

---

## How to check which courier partner is available between pickup pincode and delivery pincode?

Use the Serviceability Checker or Rate Calculator from the Dashboard:
1. Go to Dashboard >> Rate Calculator.
2. Enter your pickup pincode and delivery pincode.
3. Enter the package weight.
4. Click Calculate to see all available courier partners for that route with their rates and estimated delivery time.

---

## How to view my shipment performance metrics?

Go to Reports >> Analytics from the left menu. You can view:
* Delivery percentage by courier
* RTO percentage by courier
* NDR rates
* Zone-wise performance
* Channel-wise order distribution
* Monthly shipment trends

---

## How to download the shipment tracking report?

Go to Reports from the left menu, select 'Shipment Tracking Report', choose the date range, and click Download. The report includes: Order ID, AWB number, courier name, order date, pickup date, delivery date, current status, origin and destination pincode, and weight.

---

## What is the Delivery Performance Report?

The Delivery Performance Report shows the delivery success rates for your shipments across different couriers, zones, and time periods. It helps you identify which courier partners are performing best for your business.

---

## How far back can I access reports in Shiprocket?

You can access reports for up to the last 3 months from the Reports section. For older data, contact Shiprocket support.

---

## Can I export all my orders data from Shiprocket?

Yes. Go to Orders from the left menu, apply the required filters (date range, status, etc.), and click 'Export' or 'Download' to get your orders data in CSV format.

---

## What is the Weight Reconciliation Report?

The Weight Reconciliation Report shows all shipments where the courier has applied different weight charges than what was declared. It helps you identify and dispute weight discrepancy charges. Go to Reports >> Weight Reconciliation to access it.

---

## How to get the COD remittance report?

Go to Finances >> COD Remittance from the left menu. Select the date range and click Download to get the COD remittance report in CSV format showing all COD orders and their payment status.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to generate a shipment report in Shiprocket?">

Go to Reports from the left menu, select the report type, choose the date range, and click Generate/Download. Reports are available in CSV format.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What data is included in the shipment tracking report?">

The shipment tracking report includes order ID, AWB number, courier name, order date, pickup date, delivery date, current status, origin and destination pincode, and weight.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

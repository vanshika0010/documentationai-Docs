<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to install Shiprocket Promise on my Shopify store?">

Shiprocket Promise is available as a Shopify app. Install it from the Shopify App Store, connect it to your Shiprocket account, and configure the widget appearance and placement settings. The EDD will start showing on your product pages automatically.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which Shopify plan is required for Shiprocket Promise?">

Shiprocket Promise works with all Shopify plans. However, some advanced customization options may require Shopify Online Store 2.0 themes.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

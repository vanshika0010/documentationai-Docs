## How to install Shiprocket Promise on Shopify?

To install Shiprocket Promise:
1. Go to the Shopify App Store
2. Search for 'Shiprocket Promise'
3. Click Install
4. Authorize the app to access your Shopify store
5. Connect with your Shiprocket account
6. Configure the EDD widget settings
7. Add the widget to your product and cart pages

---

## How to configure the EDD widget in Shiprocket Promise?

To configure the EDD widget:
1. Go to the Shiprocket Promise app settings in Shopify
2. Set your cutoff times for same-day processing
3. Configure pincode detection (auto-detect or buyer input)
4. Customize the widget appearance (colors, text, position)
5. Enable/disable for specific product collections
6. Save and preview

---

## How to add Promise badge to product pages?

To add the Promise badge:
1. In Shiprocket Promise app settings, go to Widget Placement
2. Select Product Page placement
3. Configure the badge style and position
4. The badge will show EDD on all product pages
5. Test by viewing a product page on your store

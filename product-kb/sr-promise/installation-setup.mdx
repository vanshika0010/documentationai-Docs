## How can i install promise app?

Shiprocket Promise can be easily installed on any Shopify store by installing the Promise app from Shopify App Store. After installation, connect it with your Shiprocket account. The widget and trust badges will then be available to configure and embed on your store pages.

---

## How to configure the EDD widget in Shiprocket Promise?

To configure the EDD widget:
1. Open the Shiprocket Promise app in your Shopify admin
2. Set your order processing cutoff time
3. Configure pincode detection (auto-detect or manual buyer entry)
4. Customise the widget appearance (colours, text, position)
5. Enable/disable for specific product collections
6. Save and preview on your store

---

## How to add the Promise badge to my Shopify product pages?

To add the Promise badge:
1. In the Shiprocket Promise app, go to Widget Placement settings
2. Select Product Page as the placement
3. Choose badge style and position
4. Save — the badge will appear on all product pages showing the EDD
5. Test by viewing a product page on your live store

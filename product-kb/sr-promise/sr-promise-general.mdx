## What is the billing for Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise billing is based on your subscription plan. Check the Shiprocket Promise app on Shopify App Store or contact your Shiprocket account manager for current pricing plans.

---

## Who is eligible to use Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise is available for:
1. Shopify store owners
2. Active Shiprocket account holders
3. Sellers who have configured pickup addresses and courier preferences in Shiprocket

---

## Does Shiprocket Promise work with all Shopify themes?

Shiprocket Promise is designed to work with standard Shopify themes. For custom themes, minor configuration may be required. The app settings include guidance for custom theme integration.

---

## How does Shiprocket Promise help in reducing cart abandonment?

Shiprocket Promise reduces cart abandonment by:
1. Showing accurate EDD on product pages (before buyer decides to purchase)
2. Displaying delivery date on cart page (reducing uncertainty)
3. Building trust with pincode-specific delivery timelines
4. Allowing buyers to make informed purchase decisions

---

## Can I show Shiprocket Promise on checkout page?

Shiprocket Promise is primarily configured for product and cart pages. For checkout page integration, check the app's advanced configuration options or contact Shiprocket support.

---

## How to check the performance of Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise provides analytics on:
1. Widget impressions (how many times EDD was shown)
2. Pincode searches
3. Estimated conversion impact

Access from the app dashboard in Shopify.

---

## What happens if the actual delivery doesn't match the Promise EDD?

Shiprocket Promise EDD is based on historical courier performance and configured parameters. If delivery doesn't match the EDD, review:
1. Your processing cutoff time configuration
2. Courier performance for that route
3. Consider adding buffer days to the EDD calculation

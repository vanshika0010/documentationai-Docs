## How is Shiprocket Promise billed

Shiprocket Promise charges are based on the number of EDD API calls made per month. A free tier is available for low-volume stores. Contact Shiprocket for current pricing details.

---

## Does Shiprocket Promise work for international shipping

Currently, Shiprocket Promise is optimized for domestic (India) shipping. International EDD support is limited and depends on the courier's available TAT data.

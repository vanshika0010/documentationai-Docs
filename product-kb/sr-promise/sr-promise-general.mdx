## Can we do custom changes in Promise app ?

Promise widget and the trust badges can be customised by the seller themselves according to their shop's theme. Colour, text, font size, and position can all be adjusted from within the Promise app settings in your Shopify admin.

---

## What is the billing for Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise billing is subscription-based. Check the Shiprocket Promise app on Shopify App Store or contact your Shiprocket account manager for current pricing plans and available tiers.

---

## Who is eligible to use Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise is available for:
1. Shopify store owners
2. Active Shiprocket account holders with configured pickup addresses
3. Sellers who have set courier preferences in their Shiprocket panel

---

## Does Shiprocket Promise work with all Shopify themes?

Shiprocket Promise is designed to work with standard Shopify themes. For custom or headless themes, minor configuration may be needed. The app provides guidance for custom theme integration.

---

## How does Shiprocket Promise help reduce cart abandonment?

Shiprocket Promise reduces cart abandonment by:
1. Showing accurate EDD on product pages before the buyer commits to purchase
2. Displaying the delivery date on the cart page to reduce uncertainty
3. Building trust through pincode-specific delivery promises
4. Showing trust badges that communicate purchase protection

---

## Can I show Shiprocket Promise on the checkout page?

Shiprocket Promise is primarily configured for product and cart pages. For checkout page integration, check the advanced settings in the app or contact Shiprocket support.

---

## How to check the performance of Shiprocket Promise?

Shiprocket Promise provides basic analytics within the app dashboard:
1. Widget impressions (how many times EDD was shown)
2. Pincode queries
3. Estimated conversion impact

Access from the app dashboard in your Shopify admin.

---

## What if the actual delivery doesn't match the Promise EDD?

If delivery doesn't match the EDD, review:
1. Your processing cutoff time configuration in the app
2. Courier performance for that route in Shiprocket panel
3. Consider adding buffer days in EDD calculation
4. Check if the priority courier is consistently meeting the SLA

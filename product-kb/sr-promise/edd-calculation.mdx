## How does Shiprocket Promise calculate EDD?

Shiprocket Promise calculates EDD based on:
1. Seller's configured order processing cutoff time
2. Buyer's pincode (auto-detected or entered)
3. Seller's warehouse pincode
4. Courier partner transit times
5. Working days and holidays

The EDD is shown dynamically based on the buyer's location.

---

## Can I customize the EDD calculation parameters in Promise?

Yes. You can customize:
1. Processing cutoff time (e.g., orders before 2 PM processed same day)
2. Processing days (working days vs all days)
3. Courier preference for EDD calculation
4. Buffer days for safety margin

---

## What happens to EDD if no courier is available for a pincode?

If no courier is serviceable for the buyer's pincode, Shiprocket Promise can show a fallback message or estimated range. Configure fallback behavior in the app settings.

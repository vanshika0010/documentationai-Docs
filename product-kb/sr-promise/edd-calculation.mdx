<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the EDD calculated in Shiprocket Promise?">

EDD is calculated using: seller's dispatch time (configurable), courier transit TAT from origin to destination pincode, courier working days/holidays, and any custom buffer days configured. The widget shows a date range or specific date based on your settings.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I customize the EDD displayed to customers?">

Yes. You can configure dispatch cutoff time, add buffer days, set non-working days, and choose whether to show a date range or a specific delivery date.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

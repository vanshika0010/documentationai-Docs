## How is the EDD calculated in Shiprocket Promise

EDD is calculated using:

* Seller's dispatch time (configurable)
* Courier transit TAT from origin to destination pincode
* Courier working days and holidays
* Any custom buffer days you've configured

The widget shows a date range or specific date based on your settings.

---

## Can I customize the EDD displayed to customers

Yes. You can configure dispatch cutoff time, add buffer days, set non-working days, and choose whether to show a date range or a specific delivery date.

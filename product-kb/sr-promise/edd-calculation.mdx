## How is EDD calculated in Promise app ?

EDD is calculated by using the courier set on priority by the seller on their Shiprocket seller panel and the pincode of the buyer. The EDD displayed is based on the courier's transit time from the seller's warehouse pincode to the buyer's delivery pincode, adjusted for processing cutoff time and non-working days.

---

## What factors affect EDD calculation in Shiprocket Promise?

EDD is calculated considering:
1. Seller's order processing cutoff time
2. Buyer's pincode (auto-detected or entered)
3. Seller's warehouse/pickup pincode
4. Priority courier set in Shiprocket panel
5. Courier's transit time for that pincode pair
6. Working days and holidays

---

## What happens if no courier is serviceable for buyer's pincode in Promise?

If no courier is serviceable for the buyer's entered pincode, Shiprocket Promise can show a fallback message. Configure fallback text in the app settings to handle unserviceable pincodes gracefully.

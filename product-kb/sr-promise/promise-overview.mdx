## What is Shiprocket Promise App on shopify?

Shiprocket Promise is a purchase protection programme created to build customer trust at multiple touchpoints throughout the buying journey on your Shopify store. It displays Estimated Delivery Dates (EDD) on product and cart pages, and shows trust badges to increase buyer confidence and reduce cart abandonment.

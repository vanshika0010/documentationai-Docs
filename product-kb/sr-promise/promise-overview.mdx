## What is Shiprocket Promise

Shiprocket Promise is an EDD (Estimated Delivery Date) widget that displays accurate, real-time delivery date promises on product and cart pages of your Shopify store. It increases buyer confidence and reduces cart abandonment by showing when the order will arrive.

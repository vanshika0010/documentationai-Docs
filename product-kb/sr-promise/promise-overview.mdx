## What is Shiprocket Promise App on shopify?

Shiprocket Promise is a Shopify app that displays accurate Estimated Delivery Dates (EDD) on your product and cart pages. It increases buyer confidence by showing precise delivery timelines, reducing cart abandonment and post-purchase anxiety.

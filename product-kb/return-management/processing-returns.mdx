## How to process a New Return Request?

To process the new returns on the Shiprocket panel, follow the below steps:
* Go to Returns from the left side of the panel.
* Click on the return order you want to process.
* You will see the return order details.
* Click on the 'Process Return' button.
* You will be redirected to the QC page where you can perform quality check.
* After QC, you can process the refund or exchange.

---

## How to mark a return as received on Shiprocket?

To mark a return as received:
1. Go to Returns section
2. Find the return with status 'Out for Pickup' or 'In Transit'
3. Once the item physically arrives at your warehouse
4. Click on 'Mark as Received'
5. Begin the QC process

---

## What are the steps after receiving a return at the warehouse?

After receiving a return:
1. Mark it as received in the Shiprocket panel
2. Perform quality check (inspect condition, take photos)
3. Update QC status (Good/Damaged/Wrong Item/etc.)
4. Process refund or replacement based on QC result and your policy
5. Restock if item is in sellable condition

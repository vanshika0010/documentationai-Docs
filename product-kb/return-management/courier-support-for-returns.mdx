  <Accordion title="Which couriers support reverse pickups on Shiprocket?">

Most major couriers on Shiprocket support reverse pickup including Delhivery, Ecom Express, Xpressbees, DTDC, and Shadowfax. Availability depends on the pickup pincode. Check courier serviceability when creating a return order.

## Which couriers support reverse pickup on Shiprocket?

Most major courier partners on Shiprocket support reverse pickup for returns, including Delhivery, Bluedart, Ekart, Xpressbees, and others. Availability depends on the buyer's pincode. Check serviceability in the Returns section when creating a return order.

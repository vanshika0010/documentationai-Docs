## How to add a return order?

* You can create a return order by following the steps below on your Shiprocket account:
   * Go to the 'Returns' tab in the left panel
   * Now, click on 'Add Return Order'.
   * You will be redirected to a new page where you will have to fill in details like:
   * Buyer Details
   * Warehouse Details
   * Order Details
   * Package Details
   * Return Reason
   * Delivery

## How do I Enable Quality Check (QC) for my Returns?

In order to enable QC for your returns follow the steps below:
* Go to your Settings panel
* Click on "Returns" under Company Setup
* Scroll down to 'Quality Check'
* Toggle on to enable quality check for your returns orders

---

## What is Shiprocket Returns?

Shiprocket returns is a reverse logistics solution which allows sellers to accept returns from their buyers. The orders are picked up by the courier executives and delivered to the seller's warehouse. Sellers can configure return preferences such as quality check, refund mode, etc. Returns can be initiated through the Shiprocket panel or through the Post-Ship page.

---

## How do I initiate a return from the Post Ship page?

* To initiate a return from the Post Ship page, follow these steps:
   * Go to the 'Post Ship' tab in the left panel
   * Click on "Return Order" button on the tracking page.
   * Fill in the return details like return reason and the return address.
   * Click on 'Create Return Order'.

---

## How to use exchange order feature?

* To use the exchange feature, follow these steps:
   * Go to the 'Returns' tab in the left panel
   * Now, click on 'Add Return Order'.
   * You will be redirected to a new page.
   * Fill in all details (Buyer, Warehouse, Order, Package Details and Return Reason)
   * Under the Delivery section, toggle on the 'Exchange Order' option.
   * Please provide the required information for the new exchange order
   * Review and Confirm your exchange Order.

## What is the QC rating system on Shiprocket returns?

The QC rating system allows sellers to grade returned items based on their condition. Sellers can set up different grades (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor) based on their quality standards. This helps in making informed decisions about restocking, refurbishing, or disposing of returned items.

## How to export or download returns data on Shiprocket?

To export or download returns data:
1. Go to the Returns section in your Shiprocket panel
2. Apply filters (date range, status, channel)
3. Click on Download/Export
4. The returns data will be downloaded in CSV format

## What refund modes are available for returns on Shiprocket?

There are 4 modes of refund powered by Shiprocket:
1. Manual Refund - Manually process the refund outside Shiprocket
2. Shiprocket Wallet Credit - Credit the refund to the buyer's Shiprocket wallet
3. Bank Transfer - Transfer the refund to the buyer's bank account
4. Store Credit - Credit the refund to the buyer's store wallet

## How to configure automatic QC pass/fail criteria?

To configure QC criteria:
1. Go to Settings >> Returns
2. Find QC Settings
3. Set your pass criteria (e.g., item must be unused, original packaging)
4. Configure fail conditions (damaged, missing parts)
5. Save settings
Items will be automatically evaluated against these criteria during the QC process.

---

## Can I make image upload mandatory for QC on Shiprocket?

Yes. You can configure the QC settings to require image uploads during quality check. Go to Settings >> Returns >> QC Settings and enable the mandatory image upload option.

---

## What happens to items that fail QC in returns?

Items that fail QC:
1. Are marked with the fail status (Damaged, Wrong Item, Used, etc.)
2. Are quarantined separately from sellable stock
3. Can be subject to courier damage claims if damage occurred in transit
4. Are handled per your configured QC fail policy (dispose, return to vendor, etc.)

---

## How to handle customer-damaged return items?

For items damaged by customer:
1. During QC, mark the item as 'Customer Damaged'
2. Document with photos
3. Based on your return policy, you may:
   * Deduct repair cost from refund
   * Reject the return
   * Accept as damaged stock
4. Communicate the decision to the buyer

---

## Does Shiprocket provide return pickup from buyer?

Yes. Shiprocket coordinates reverse pickup from the buyer's address when a return is created. The courier partner assigned handles collection from buyer and delivery to seller's warehouse.

## What is the return pickup SLA on Shiprocket?

Return pickup SLA varies by courier:
* Metro cities: 2-3 business days
* Tier-2/3 cities: 3-5 business days
* Remote areas: 5-7 business days

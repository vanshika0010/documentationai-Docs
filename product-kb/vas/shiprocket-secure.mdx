## What is Shiprocket Secure

Shiprocket Secure is a shipment protection service that covers your shipments against loss or damage in transit. It provides financial protection for high-value orders and gives both sellers and buyers peace of mind.

---

## How to enable Shiprocket Secure for my shipments

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Secure from the left menu. Enable the toggle to activate Secure for all shipments, or enable it per order at the time of shipping. A small fee is charged per shipment based on the declared value.

---

## What does Shiprocket Secure cover

Shiprocket Secure covers:

* Shipments lost in transit.
* Shipments damaged during delivery.

Claims must be raised within the specified timeframe with supporting documentation (photos, buyer confirmation).

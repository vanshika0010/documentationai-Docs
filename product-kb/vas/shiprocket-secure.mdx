title: "Shiprocket Secure"
description: "Add-on services to protect shipments, reduce RTO, and improve delivery and customer experience"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Secure?">

Shiprocket Secure is a shipment protection service that covers your shipments against loss or damage in transit. It provides financial protection for high-value orders and gives both sellers and buyers peace of mind.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to enable Shiprocket Secure for my shipments?">

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Secure from the left menu. Enable the toggle to activate Secure for all shipments, or enable it per order at the time of shipping. A small fee per shipment is charged based on the declared value.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Shiprocket Secure cover?">

Shiprocket Secure covers shipments lost in transit and shipments damaged during delivery. Claims must be raised within the specified timeframe with supporting documentation (photos, buyer confirmation).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

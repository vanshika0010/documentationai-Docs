## What is claim amount for non secure shipments?

Liability for Speed Post is capped at the lesser of double the freight charges or the value of the consignment. For most standard courier partners without Secure, liability is limited to a small multiple of the freight charges, which may be much less than the actual shipment value.

---

## What is Shiprocket Secure?

Shiprocket Secure is a shipment insurance product that protects sellers against financial losses due to:
1. Lost shipments
2. Damaged shipments
3. Theft during transit

With Secure, sellers can claim the full declared value of the shipment instead of the limited courier liability.

---

## How does Shiprocket Secure work?

Shiprocket Secure works by:
1. Seller enables Secure for their account
2. A small insurance premium is added to each shipment
3. If a shipment is lost or damaged, seller files a claim
4. Shiprocket processes the claim and reimburses the declared value

---

## How to enable Shiprocket Secure?

To enable Shiprocket Secure:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Shiprocket Secure
2. Click Activate/Enable
3. Review the terms and premium rates
4. Confirm activation
All future shipments will have Secure coverage.

---

## What is the premium for Shiprocket Secure?

Shiprocket Secure premium is a percentage of the shipment's declared value. The exact percentage depends on your plan and shipment value. Check the Secure section in Value Added Services for current rates.

---

## What can I claim under Shiprocket Secure?

Coverage under Shiprocket Secure includes:
1. Full declared value of lost shipments
2. Repair cost or declared value for damaged shipments
3. Not covered: Perishable goods, prohibited items, undeclared value

---

## How to file a claim under Shiprocket Secure?

To file a Secure claim:
1. Go to Shiprocket Secure section or raise a support ticket
2. Provide AWB number and incident details
3. Attach supporting documents (photos of damage, courier's confirmation of loss)
4. Submit the claim
Claims are reviewed within 7-10 business days.

---

## What documents are needed to file a Shiprocket Secure claim?

Documents needed for Secure claim:
1. AWB/tracking number
2. Proof of shipment value (invoice)
3. Photos of damaged goods (for damage claims)
4. Courier's confirmation of loss (for lost shipments)
5. Description of incident

---

## What is the claim processing time for Shiprocket Secure?

Shiprocket Secure claims are typically processed within 7-10 business days after all required documents are submitted. Complex cases may take longer.

---

## Is there a deductible for Shiprocket Secure claims?

Check your Shiprocket Secure policy for deductible terms. The claim payout is typically the declared value minus any applicable deductible.

---

## What is the maximum claim value under Shiprocket Secure?

The maximum claim value corresponds to the declared value of the shipment at the time of creation. Ensure you declare the accurate market value of goods when creating shipments.

---

## Does Shiprocket Secure cover RTO orders?

Shiprocket Secure primarily covers loss and damage during transit. If an RTO shipment is lost or damaged during the return journey, it may be covered. Verify specific RTO coverage terms in your Secure policy.

---

## Can I enable Secure for specific orders only?

Shiprocket Secure can be configured as opt-in per shipment or as always-on for all shipments. Check the Secure settings in Value Added Services for available configuration options.

---

## What is the difference between Shiprocket Secure and Shipsure?

Shiprocket Secure: Shipment insurance covering loss and damage for individual shipments.

Shipsure: A comprehensive seller protection plan covering RTO losses, stuck shipments, and delivery failures with payouts based on number of affected shipments.

---

## How to check claim status for Shiprocket Secure?

To check claim status:
1. Go to the Shiprocket Secure section or raise a support ticket
2. Provide your claim reference number
3. Current claim status will be shown

---

## Does Shiprocket Secure cover international shipments?

Shiprocket Secure primarily covers domestic shipments. For international shipment insurance, check the international shipping section or contact your account manager.

---

## What is the coverage area for Shiprocket Secure?

Shiprocket Secure covers all domestic shipments processed through the Shiprocket platform within India.

---

## Can I get a refund of Secure premium if I cancel an order?

If an order is cancelled before pickup, the Secure premium may be refunded to your wallet. After pickup, the premium is typically non-refundable. Contact support for specific cases.

---

## What are excluded items from Shiprocket Secure coverage?

Excluded items from Secure coverage typically include:
1. Perishable/fragile items not properly packaged
2. Prohibited/restricted items
3. Items with undeclared or undervalued declarations
4. Cash, currency, and negotiable instruments
5. Electronics without proper packaging

---

## How to view all Shiprocket Secure-insured shipments?

To view Secure-covered shipments:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Shiprocket Secure
2. View the list of insured shipments
3. Filter by date range or claim status

---

## What is the waiting period for Shiprocket Secure claims?

For lost shipments, there is typically a waiting period after the last tracking event before a claim can be filed. This is to allow time for the carrier to locate the shipment. Check current Secure terms for specific waiting periods.

---

## Does Shiprocket Secure cover partial damage?

Yes, Shiprocket Secure covers partial damage. For damaged shipments, the claim amount is assessed based on the extent of damage. You'll need to provide photos and damage assessment documentation.

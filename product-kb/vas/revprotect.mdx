---
title: "RevProtect"
description: "Add-on services to protect shipments, reduce RTO, and improve delivery and customer experience"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is RevProtect?">

RevProtect is a revenue protection service that helps sellers recover revenue from high-risk COD orders. It verifies buyer intent before dispatch using OTP confirmation or prepayment nudges, reducing the chances of delivery refusal.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to enable RevProtect?">

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> RevProtect. Configure the trigger conditions (e.g., high RTO score, COD orders above a certain value) and the action (OTP verification or prepayment request). Enable the toggle to activate.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

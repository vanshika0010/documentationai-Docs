## What is RevProtect, and how does it work?

RevProtect helps you recover losses from delayed shipments and RTOs by providing payouts when your shipments fail to meet delivery expectations. It works by:
1. Monitoring all your shipments
2. Identifying shipments that breach SLA (delayed or RTO)
3. Automatically processing payouts to your Shiprocket wallet
4. No manual claim required - payouts are automatic

---

## What types of losses does RevProtect cover?

RevProtect covers:
1. RTO (Return to Origin) - payout per RTO order
2. Delayed deliveries - payout when delivery exceeds SLA
3. Stuck shipments - for shipments with no tracking update beyond a threshold

---

## How is the RevProtect payout calculated?

RevProtect payout is calculated based on:
1. Number of RTO/delayed orders in the billing period
2. Pre-defined payout rate per affected order
3. Your account tier and RevProtect plan

Payouts are credited to your Shiprocket wallet automatically.

---

## How to enable RevProtect on Shiprocket?

To enable RevProtect:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> RevProtect
2. Review the plan and payout terms
3. Click Activate
4. RevProtect will start monitoring your shipments automatically

---

## What is the RevProtect plan period?

RevProtect typically works on a monthly billing period. Payouts for the month's RTO/delayed orders are processed at the end of the billing period. Check RevProtect settings for specific billing cycle details.

---

## Is there a minimum volume requirement for RevProtect?

RevProtect may have minimum volume requirements. Check the RevProtect section in Value Added Services or contact your account manager for eligibility criteria.

---

## What is the payout rate for RevProtect?

RevProtect payout rates depend on your plan. Check Value Added Services >> RevProtect or contact your account manager for current payout rates.

---

## Does RevProtect cover all courier partners?

RevProtect covers shipments processed through eligible courier partners on Shiprocket. Check the coverage list in RevProtect settings for included couriers.

---

## How to check RevProtect payouts?

RevProtect payouts are credited to your Shiprocket wallet. Check:
1. Finances >> Passbook for RevProtect credit entries
2. Value Added Services >> RevProtect for detailed payout history

---

## What is the SLA threshold for RevProtect delayed delivery payout?

Delayed delivery payout triggers when a shipment exceeds the expected delivery date by the threshold defined in your RevProtect plan. Check RevProtect terms for specific SLA thresholds.

---

## Can I combine RevProtect with Shiprocket Secure?

Yes. RevProtect (revenue protection for RTO/delays) and Shiprocket Secure (insurance for loss/damage) are complementary products and can be used together for comprehensive protection.

---

## Does RevProtect automatically renew?

RevProtect subscription typically auto-renews monthly. Check your RevProtect subscription settings for renewal terms.

---

## How to cancel RevProtect subscription?

To cancel RevProtect:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> RevProtect
2. Find the cancel/deactivate option
3. Confirm cancellation
Or contact Shiprocket support to cancel.

---

## What is the maximum payout limit for RevProtect?

RevProtect may have maximum payout caps per month based on your plan tier. Check your RevProtect plan details for applicable limits.

---

## Does RevProtect cover B2B shipments?

RevProtect coverage depends on your specific plan. Contact your account manager for RevProtect eligibility for B2B shipments.

---

## How does RevProtect benefit high-RTO sellers?

For sellers with high RTO rates, RevProtect provides:
1. Financial recovery for each RTO order
2. Reduces the net cost of RTO
3. Provides predictability in RTO-related expenses
4. Complements RTO reduction tools (RTO Score, Engage 360)

---

## What reports are available for RevProtect?

RevProtect reports available:
1. Monthly payout summary
2. Order-level RTO/delay breakdown
3. Courier-wise payout breakdown
4. Trend analysis of RevProtect payouts over time

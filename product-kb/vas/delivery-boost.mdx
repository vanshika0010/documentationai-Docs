## What is Delivery Boost?

Delivery Boost is a smart AI backed feature that helps you communicate with your customers at the right time for failed deliveries. It uses automated WhatsApp, IVR, and SMS communication to resolve NDR (Non-Delivery Reports) and prevent RTOs.

---

## How does Delivery Boost reduce NDR and RTO?

Delivery Boost reduces NDR/RTO by:
1. Automatically contacting buyers via WhatsApp/IVR/SMS when delivery fails
2. Asking buyers to confirm re-delivery or update address
3. Capturing preferred delivery time slots
4. Providing options to reschedule or update address
5. Auto-triggering reattempts based on buyer responses

---

## How to enable Delivery Boost on Shiprocket?

To enable Delivery Boost:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Delivery Boost (or NDR/Delivery Boost from left menu)
2. Click Activate/Enable
3. Configure communication preferences
4. Save settings
All NDR orders will automatically get Delivery Boost communication.

---

## What communication channels does Delivery Boost use?

Delivery Boost uses:
1. WhatsApp: Primary channel for buyer engagement
2. IVR (Interactive Voice Response): Automated voice calls
3. SMS: Fallback when WhatsApp is unavailable

Multiple channels ensure maximum buyer reach.

---

## Is Delivery Boost the same as Engage NDR management?

Delivery Boost and Engage 360's NDR Recovery are related features:
* Delivery Boost is the standard NDR communication feature available to all sellers
* Engage 360 provides advanced customization, WhatsApp chatflows, and self-WABA for NDR recovery

Both help reduce RTO through automated buyer communication.

---

## How does Delivery Boost contact the buyer?

Delivery Boost contacts buyers through automated:
1. WhatsApp message with delivery options (confirm, reschedule, update address)
2. IVR call if WhatsApp is unresponsive
3. SMS as final fallback

All within a defined timeframe after NDR is generated.

---

## What are the buyer options in Delivery Boost?

When a buyer receives Delivery Boost communication:
1. Confirm re-delivery (I'm available, please attempt again)
2. Update delivery address
3. Select preferred delivery time slot
4. Request RTO (don't want the order)

Buyer's response is captured and acted upon automatically.

---

## How long after NDR does Delivery Boost trigger?

Delivery Boost triggers automatically when an NDR is generated for a shipment. Timing of communication can be configured in the settings.

---

## What is the Delivery Boost success rate?

Delivery Boost helps sellers see significant reduction in RTO rates, typically 20-45% RTO reduction depending on product category and delivery area. Actual results vary by seller.

---

## Can I customize Delivery Boost messages?

Basic Delivery Boost uses Shiprocket's standard message templates. For custom messages and templates, use Engage 360 with Self-WABA configuration.

---

## What is the pricing for Delivery Boost?

Delivery Boost pricing is per order processed through the feature. Check Value Added Services >> Delivery Boost for current pricing or contact your account manager.

---

## Does Delivery Boost work for all courier partners?

Delivery Boost works for shipments processed through Shiprocket across all courier partners. It triggers on NDR events from any courier.

---

## How to check Delivery Boost performance analytics?

Delivery Boost analytics are available in:
1. NDR/Delivery Boost section in the Shiprocket panel
2. Dashboard >> NDR tab

Metrics include: messages sent, buyer response rate, reattempts, successful deliveries, RTO reduction.

---

## Can Delivery Boost recover stuck/held NDR orders?

Yes. Delivery Boost is specifically designed to recover NDR orders by getting buyer confirmation and updated delivery information to enable successful reattempts.

---

## How does Delivery Boost integrate with the NDR panel?

Delivery Boost is built into the NDR/Delivery Boost section of the Shiprocket panel. When Delivery Boost is enabled:
1. NDR orders automatically receive Delivery Boost communication
2. Buyer responses appear in the NDR panel
3. Actions based on responses (reattempt, RTO) are automatically triggered or shown for confirmation

---

## What happens if buyer doesn't respond to Delivery Boost?

If buyer doesn't respond within the configured timeframe:
1. Additional attempts may be made
2. After maximum attempts, the order may be automatically marked for RTO
3. Seller can also manually process the NDR from the panel

---

## Does Delivery Boost support multi-language communication?

Delivery Boost currently supports English and Hindi communication. For additional language support, use Engage 360 with custom templates.

---

## Can I disable Delivery Boost for specific orders?

Delivery Boost applies to all NDR orders when enabled. For order-specific exclusions, contact Shiprocket support.

---

## What is the difference between Delivery Boost and standard NDR processing?

Standard NDR Processing: Manual - seller reviews each NDR and takes action.

Delivery Boost: Automated - system automatically contacts buyer, captures response, and triggers action without manual intervention.

---

## How to see Delivery Boost messages sent to buyers?

Delivery Boost communication history per order is available in:
1. NDR/Delivery Boost section
2. Specific order details page
3. Communication logs in the NDR panel

---

## Does Delivery Boost work for COD and prepaid orders?

Yes. Delivery Boost works for all shipments regardless of payment mode. NDR can happen with both COD and prepaid orders.

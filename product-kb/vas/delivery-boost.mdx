## What is Delivery Boost

Delivery Boost helps improve delivery success rates for your shipments. It uses proactive buyer communication, address verification, and priority handling to reduce NDRs and RTO.

---

## How does Delivery Boost reduce RTO

Delivery Boost sends pre-delivery notifications to buyers confirming their availability and address, enabling couriers to plan deliveries more effectively and reducing first-attempt failures.

---
title: "Delivery Boost"
description: "Add-on services to protect shipments, reduce RTO, and improve delivery and customer experience"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Delivery Boost?">

Delivery Boost is a service that helps improve delivery success rates for your shipments. It uses proactive buyer communication, address verification, and priority handling to reduce NDRs and RTO.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Delivery Boost reduce RTO?">

Delivery Boost sends pre-delivery notifications to buyers confirming their availability and address, enabling couriers to plan deliveries more effectively and reducing first-attempt failures.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

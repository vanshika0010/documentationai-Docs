## What is RTO Score in Shiprocket

RTO Score is an AI-powered risk assessment for each shipment that predicts the probability of return-to-origin. A high RTO score indicates the order is at risk of not being delivered. Use it to decide whether to send the order prepaid-only, add COD charges, or flag for manual review.

---

## How to use the RTO Score to reduce returns

When an order has a high RTO score, you can:

* Automatically block COD for that order.
* Send an OTP verification to the buyer.
* Add a COD confirmation step.
* Proactively reach out to verify the delivery address.

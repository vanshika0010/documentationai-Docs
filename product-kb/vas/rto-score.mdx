<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is RTO Score in Shiprocket?">

RTO Score is an AI-powered risk assessment for each shipment that predicts the probability of return-to-origin. A high RTO score indicates the order is at risk of not being delivered. Use it to decide whether to send the order prepaid-only, add COD charges, or flag for manual review.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to use the RTO Score to reduce returns?">

When an order has a high RTO score, you can: automatically block COD for that order, send an OTP verification to the buyer, add a COD confirmation step, or proactively reach out to verify the address.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How does Shipsure work?

Once you're onboarded to ShipSure, the program tracks your shipments. If a shipment status remains as "stuck" (no update) for more than 30 days, or if it's returned (RTO) without being delivered, you receive a payout based on the number of affected shipments in your billing cycle.

---

## What is Shipsure on Shiprocket?

Shipsure is a seller protection program that provides financial coverage for:
1. Stuck shipments (no status update for 30+ days)
2. RTO orders that were not delivered

It provides predictable payouts to offset losses from these events.

---

## How to enable Shipsure?

To enable Shipsure:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Shipsure
2. Review the program terms and payout structure
3. Click Activate
4. Shipsure will start protecting your eligible shipments

---

## What is the difference between Shipsure and Shiprocket Secure?

Shipsure: Covers stuck shipments (30+ days no update) and RTO losses with per-shipment payouts.

Shiprocket Secure: Shipment insurance for lost or physically damaged shipments, with claims based on declared value.

Both can be used together for comprehensive protection.

---

## What is the payout structure for Shipsure?

Shipsure payouts are based on the number of affected shipments (stuck or RTO) in the billing cycle. The payout rate per shipment is defined in your Shipsure plan. Contact your account manager or check the Shipsure section for current payout rates.

## What is Shipsure

Shipsure is Shiprocket's shipment insurance product that protects sellers against financial loss from shipments that are lost, damaged, or stolen in transit. It provides coverage up to the declared value of the shipment.

---

## How is Shipsure different from Shiprocket Secure

Both provide shipment protection, but Shipsure may offer different coverage terms, claim processes, or pricing structures. Check the current product pages in your Shiprocket panel for the latest details on each option.

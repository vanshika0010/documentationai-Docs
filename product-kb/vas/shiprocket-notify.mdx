## What is Shiprocket Notify?

Shiprocket Notify is a platform suite which comprises of multiple communication channels (WhatsApp, SMS, Email) through which businesses can send personalised and consistent post-purchase communications to their customers. It helps in reducing post-purchase anxiety and building customer trust through timely order updates.

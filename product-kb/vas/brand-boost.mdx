## What is Brand Boost

Brand Boost enhances your brand visibility during the post-purchase experience. It includes branded tracking pages, custom packaging inserts, and branded buyer communication that reinforces your brand identity after the order is placed.

---

## How to set up Brand Boost

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Brand Boost. Configure your branded tracking page (logo, colors, social links) and enable branded buyer communications. The tracking link shared with buyers will show your branded experience.

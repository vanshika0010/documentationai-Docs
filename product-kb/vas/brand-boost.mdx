<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Brand Boost?">

Brand Boost is a value-added service that enhances your brand visibility during the post-purchase experience. It includes branded tracking pages, custom packaging inserts, and branded buyer communication that reinforces your brand identity after the order is placed.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to set up Brand Boost?">

Go to Settings >> Value Added Services >> Brand Boost. Configure your branded tracking page (logo, colors, social links) and enable branded buyer communications. The tracking link shared with buyers will show your branded experience.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

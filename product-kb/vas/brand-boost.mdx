## What is Brand Boost?

Brand Boost is a feature offered by Shiprocket, a shipping and logistics platform. It allows businesses to enhance their brand presence during the shipping process. With Brand Boost, businesses can customize their shipping experience with branded tracking pages, personalized notifications, and more to create a cohesive brand experience for their customers.

---

## What is the Branded Tracking Page in Brand Boost?

The Branded Tracking Page in Brand Boost allows sellers to customize the post-purchase tracking experience:
1. Add your logo and brand colors
2. Include promotional banners and product recommendations
3. Custom domain for tracking page (yourstore.com/track)
4. Social media links and brand messaging
5. NPS/review collection widgets

---

## How to enable Brand Boost on Shiprocket?

To enable Brand Boost:
1. Go to Value Added Services >> Brand Boost
2. Click Activate
3. Customize your branded tracking page
4. Configure branded notifications
5. Save and go live

## How does Brand Boost help in customer retention?

Brand Boost helps in customer retention by:
1. Creating a branded post-purchase experience (not generic courier tracking)
2. Using the tracking page for upselling and cross-selling
3. Collecting reviews and feedback at the right moment
4. Reducing WISMO (Where Is My Order) queries with proactive updates
5. Building brand recall with consistent communication

---

## What is the custom domain feature in Brand Boost?

Custom domain allows your tracking page to be at your own URL (e.g., track.yourbrand.com) instead of a Shiprocket URL. This keeps buyers on your brand experience throughout the post-purchase journey.

---

## How to set up custom domain for Brand Boost tracking page?

To set up custom domain:
1. In Brand Boost settings, go to Domain Configuration
2. Enter your custom domain (e.g., track.yourbrand.com)
3. Add a CNAME record pointing to Shiprocket's tracking server (instructions provided)
4. Verify the domain
5. Tracking page will be live at your custom domain

---

## Can I show product recommendations on the Brand Boost tracking page?

Yes. Brand Boost allows adding product recommendation widgets on the tracking page. This creates an additional sales touchpoint while buyers are checking delivery status.

## How to collect customer reviews through Brand Boost?

Brand Boost includes review collection widgets on the tracking page:
1. Configure the review widget in Brand Boost settings
2. Set when to show the review prompt (after delivery)
3. Choose review platform (Google, Trustpilot, your own site)
4. Customers can submit reviews directly from the tracking page

## How to add social media links to the Brand Boost tracking page?

In Brand Boost settings, go to the social media section and add your links for:
1. Instagram
2. Facebook
3. Twitter/X

These will appear as icons on your branded tracking page.

## What is the NPS survey feature in Brand Boost?

NPS (Net Promoter Score) survey in Brand Boost asks buyers to rate their experience on a scale of 0-10 after delivery. This helps measure customer satisfaction and identify promoters vs detractors.

---

## Can I integrate Brand Boost tracking page with my Shopify store?

Yes. Brand Boost tracking pages work with Shopify stores. You can embed the tracking widget on your Shopify store or use the custom domain to keep buyers in your branded environment.

## How to view which customers visited my Brand Boost tracking page?

Brand Boost analytics in the dashboard shows aggregate tracking page visit data. For individual visitor data, check the analytics section for available reporting granularity.

---

## What is EDD (Estimated Delivery Date) display in Brand Boost?

Brand Boost tracking pages can display the EDD to buyers, reducing WISMO queries. The EDD is shown based on courier tracking data and configured business rules.

---

## How to add a promotional banner to Brand Boost tracking page?

To add a promotional banner:
1. Go to Brand Boost >> Page Customization
2. Find the Banner section
3. Upload your promotional image
4. Add the destination URL
5. Set display conditions (always show, or show after delivery)
6. Save

---

## Can I show different content on the tracking page based on order status?

Yes. Brand Boost supports dynamic content based on shipment status:
1. In-transit: Show expected delivery date, promotional content
2. Out for delivery: Show excitement message, preparation tips
3. Delivered: Show review request, reorder CTA
4. NDR/RTO: Show support information

---

## Does Brand Boost support custom email notifications?

Yes. Branded email notifications can be configured in Brand Boost/Notify settings with your logo, brand colors, and custom messaging for each order status update.

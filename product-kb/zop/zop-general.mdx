## Is special packaging mandatory for ZOP?

No, special packaging is not mandatory, but secure and attractive packaging is recommended to ensure products reach buyers in good condition and create a positive unboxing experience.

---

## How to contact ZOP support?

For ZOP-related queries:
1. Go to your Shiprocket panel >> Apps >> ZOP
2. Use the support option within the ZOP section
3. Or raise a support ticket from Help & Support (select ZOP category)
4. Contact your Shiprocket account manager for ZOP-related escalations

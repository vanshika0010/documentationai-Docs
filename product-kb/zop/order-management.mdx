## How are ZOP orders managed

ZOP orders appear in your Shiprocket panel under the ZOP Orders section. Process them like regular Shiprocket orders — assign a courier, generate a label, and schedule pickup. Order status syncs back to the ZOP marketplace automatically.

---

## What happens if I fail to process a ZOP order on time

Delayed processing may result in order cancellation by the buyer or a penalty from ZOP. Maintain adequate stock and process orders within the stipulated timeframe to maintain your seller rating.

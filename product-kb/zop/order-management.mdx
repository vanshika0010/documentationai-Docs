## Where can I see my ZOP orders?

Sign in to your Shiprocket seller panel, navigate to ZOP under the 'Apps' section. Here, you can view all your ZOP orders with their status, buyer details, and order specifics.

---

## How are ZOP orders fulfilled?

ZOP orders are fulfilled through Shiprocket:
1. Order placed on ZOP by buyer
2. Order syncs to your Shiprocket panel
3. Process the order like any other Shiprocket order (assign courier, print label)
4. Courier picks up from your warehouse
5. Delivery tracking is available for both you and the buyer

---

## What happens when a ZOP order is cancelled?

For ZOP order cancellations:
1. If cancelled before shipping: No charges, no commission deducted
2. If cancelled after shipping: Standard courier charges may apply
3. Commission is only deducted for delivered orders

---

## How to handle ZOP returns?

For ZOP returns:
1. Buyer initiates return through ZOP platform
2. Return request appears in your Shiprocket Returns section
3. Process the return as normal
4. Commission may be reversed for returned orders as per ZOP policy

---

## What is the SLA for fulfilling ZOP orders?

ZOP expects orders to be processed within 24-48 hours of placement. Delayed processing may impact your seller rating on ZOP.

---

## Can I track ZOP order performance?

ZOP order performance analytics are available in the ZOP seller dashboard showing:
1. Orders placed vs fulfilled
2. Delivery success rate
3. Return rate
4. Revenue and commission summary

---

## How does ZOP handle buyer disputes?

For buyer disputes on ZOP:
1. ZOP's customer service team handles initial buyer queries
2. Sellers are notified if resolution requires seller input
3. Respond to disputes promptly to maintain seller rating

---

## What is my seller rating on ZOP?

ZOP maintains seller ratings based on:
1. Order fulfillment rate
2. Processing time
3. Delivery success rate
4. Return rate
5. Buyer feedback

Maintain high ratings to improve product visibility on ZOP.

---

## How to increase ZOP order volume?

To increase orders on ZOP:
1. Optimize product listings (images, descriptions, pricing)
2. Maintain good seller rating
3. Ensure consistent product availability
4. Participate in ZOP promotional events
5. Offer competitive pricing

---

## What payment methods does ZOP accept from buyers?

ZOP accepts standard payment methods for D2C e-commerce including prepaid options. COD availability on ZOP depends on ZOP's platform settings. Check the ZOP seller portal for current payment options.

## How will I update my catalogue on zop?

Sign in to your Shiprocket seller panel, navigate to ZOP under the 'Apps' section. Here, you can manage your ZOP product catalog. To update:
1. Add new products or edit existing ones
2. Update pricing, descriptions, or images
3. Enable or disable products for ZOP listing

---

## How to add products to ZOP catalog?

To add products to ZOP:
1. Go to your Shiprocket panel >> Apps >> ZOP
2. Navigate to the Catalog section
3. Click Add Product
4. Fill in product details (name, description, price, images, category)
5. Submit for review
Products are reviewed by ZOP team before going live.

4. Home & Lifestyle

Check the ZOP platform for current supported categories.

## How to optimize product listings for ZOP?

To optimize your ZOP listings:
1. Use high-quality product images (multiple angles)
2. Write clear, detailed product descriptions
3. Set competitive pricing
4. Add relevant keywords in title and description
5. Ensure accurate product specifications
6. Maintain good inventory levels

---

## What are the image requirements for ZOP product listings?

ZOP product image requirements:
1. High resolution (minimum 800x800 pixels)
2. White or clean background
3. Product should occupy 80-85% of image frame
4. Multiple angles recommended
5. No watermarks or promotional text on images

---

## How to set pricing on ZOP?

When listing products on ZOP:
1. Set the selling price (what buyer pays)
2. Remember 30% commission is deducted from this price
3. Ensure your selling price covers:
   * Product cost
   * 30% ZOP commission
   * Shiprocket shipping charges
   * Desired profit margin

---

## Can I run promotions or discounts on ZOP?

For promotional pricing and discounts on ZOP, check the ZOP seller panel for available campaign and promotion options. Contact the ZOP team for specific promotional opportunities.

---

## How many products can I list on ZOP?

There is no fixed limit on the number of products you can list on ZOP. However, all products must comply with ZOP's content guidelines and category rules.

---

## How to remove a product from ZOP?

To remove/deactivate a product from ZOP:
1. Go to Shiprocket panel >> Apps >> ZOP >> Catalog
2. Find the product
3. Toggle to inactive or click Remove/Deactivate
The product will no longer be visible to buyers on ZOP.

---

## What is the product review process for ZOP?

After submitting products for ZOP listing:
1. ZOP team reviews for compliance and quality
2. Review typically takes 2-3 business days
3. Approved products go live on ZOP
4. Rejected products are returned with reason for re-submission

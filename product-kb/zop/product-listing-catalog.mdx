## How to list products on ZOP

Go to ZOP >> Catalog from your Shiprocket panel. Click 'Add Product,' enter product title, description, images, price, and available quantity. Submit for review. Once approved, the product goes live on the ZOP marketplace.

---

## Can I sync my existing Shiprocket catalog to ZOP

Yes. You can import products from your Shiprocket catalog to ZOP to avoid re-entering product details. Go to ZOP >> Catalog >> Import from Catalog.

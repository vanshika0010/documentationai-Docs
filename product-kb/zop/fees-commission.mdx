## What is the Fees/Commission Structure for Selling on ZOP?

We charge 30% commission on every order delivered (inclusive of taxes). This fee covers marketplace listing, discovery, payment processing, and customer acquisition on ZOP.

---

## Are there any listing fees on ZOP?

There are no separate listing fees on ZOP. You only pay the 30% commission on delivered orders - no upfront cost to list your products.

---

## How are ZOP commission charges deducted?

ZOP commission is deducted from the order value before remittance to the seller. If your product is priced at Rs. 1000 and it's delivered:
* Commission (30% incl. taxes): Rs. 300
* Amount remitted to you: Rs. 700

---

## What is the remittance cycle for ZOP orders?

ZOP order remittance follows Shiprocket's standard COD/prepaid remittance cycle after deducting the 30% commission. Check your Shiprocket Finances section for ZOP order payments.

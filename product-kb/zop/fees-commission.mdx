## What fees or commission does ZOP charge sellers

ZOP charges a commission on each sale made through the marketplace. Commission rates vary by product category. There may also be payment processing fees. Check the ZOP seller agreement for the current commission structure.

---

## How is remittance processed for ZOP sales

ZOP remittance is processed on a regular cycle (typically weekly or bi-weekly) after deducting commission and applicable charges. The net amount is transferred to your registered bank account.

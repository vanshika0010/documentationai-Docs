<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What fees or commission does ZOP charge sellers?">

ZOP charges a commission on each sale made through the marketplace. Commission rates vary by product category. There may also be payment processing fees. Check the ZOP seller agreement for current commission structure.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is remittance processed for ZOP sales?">

ZOP remittance is processed on a regular cycle (typically weekly or bi-weekly) after deducting commission and any applicable charges. The net amount is transferred to your registered bank account.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

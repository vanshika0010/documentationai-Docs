<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is ZOP?">

ZOP is Shiprocket's seller marketplace that connects verified sellers with buyers. It provides an additional sales channel where sellers can list products and reach ZOP's buyer network.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to onboard as a seller on ZOP?">

Go to the ZOP section in your Shiprocket panel. Complete the seller registration by providing business details, KYC documents, and bank account information. Once verified, you can start listing products.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to get onboarded on ZOP?

If you're already using Shiprocket, connect your Shopify channel (keeping it active on Shiprocket) and apply for ZOP. Once approved, your products will be listed on ZOP for buyers to discover and purchase.

---

## What is ZOP?

ZOP is Shiprocket's D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) marketplace that helps sellers reach new customers. It aggregates products from multiple D2C brands and offers them to buyers at one place.

---

## What are the prerequisites to join ZOP?

Prerequisites to join ZOP:
1. Active Shiprocket account
2. Shopify store connected to Shiprocket
3. Approved product catalog (no prohibited items)
4. Ability to fulfill orders through Shiprocket
5. KYC completed on Shiprocket

## How is Shiprocket Sense priced

Sense uses a credit-based pricing model. Each API call consumes credits. You purchase credit packs based on your expected volume. Contact Shiprocket for current credit pack pricing and volume discounts.

---

## How do I purchase credits for Shiprocket Sense

Log in to your Sense dashboard and go to the Billing section. Select a credit pack and complete the payment. Credits are added instantly to your account.

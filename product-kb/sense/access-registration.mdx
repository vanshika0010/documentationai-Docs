## Where can I get key for API?

You can find your API key by clicking the "View Credentials" button on the particular product page. This will show you your Client ID and Client Secret.

---

## How to register for Shiprocket Sense?

To register for Shiprocket Sense:
1. Go to sense.shiprocket.in
2. Sign up using your Shiprocket account email
3. Or activate from the Sense section in your Shiprocket panel
4. Complete the registration form
5. Your account will be activated with initial trial credits
6. Access API credentials from the dashboard

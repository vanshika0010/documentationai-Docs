## Does Shiprocket Sense require technical knowledge to use?

No, you it's not required to have any technical knowledge

For using the Sense dashboard (analytics, reports): No technical knowledge required. The dashboard is user-friendly and provides visual insights.

For API integration: Some technical knowledge is required to integrate APIs into your system. Your development team or a developer can handle the integration.

---

## What analytics are available in the Sense dashboard?

The Sense dashboard provides:
1. RTO Risk distribution (Low/Medium/High) across your orders
2. Pincode-level performance maps
3. Courier recommendation reports
4. Address quality analytics
5. Trend analysis over time
6. API usage and credit consumption reports

---

## How to view pincode performance in Shiprocket Sense?

To view pincode performance:
1. Log into the Sense dashboard
2. Navigate to Pincode Intelligence
3. Enter or select a pincode
4. View delivery success rate, RTO rate, and best courier recommendations for that pincode

---

## Can I export Sense analytics data?

Yes. Analytics data from the Sense dashboard can be exported in CSV/Excel format for further analysis in your own tools.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What APIs does Shiprocket Sense offer?">

Sense offers: RTO Risk API (probability score for an order not being delivered), Address Quality API (validation and quality score for delivery addresses), and Pincode Intelligence API (delivery performance data for specific pincodes).

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How accurate is the Sense RTO Risk Score?">

The RTO Risk Score is trained on millions of Shiprocket shipments and has high accuracy. It considers factors like buyer's past delivery history, pincode performance, COD/prepaid split, and address quality to predict RTO probability.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

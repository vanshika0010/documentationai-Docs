## What APIs does Shiprocket Sense offer

Sense offers:

* **RTO Risk API** — probability score for an order not being delivered.
* **Address Quality API** — validation and quality score for delivery addresses.
* **Pincode Intelligence API** — delivery performance data for specific pincodes.

---

## How accurate is the Sense RTO Risk Score

The RTO Risk Score is trained on millions of Shiprocket shipments. It considers factors like buyer's past delivery history, pincode performance, COD/prepaid split, and address quality to predict RTO probability.

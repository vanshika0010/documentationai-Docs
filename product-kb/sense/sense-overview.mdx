## What is Shiprocket Sense

Shiprocket Sense is an API product that provides delivery risk intelligence and address quality scoring. It offers RTO risk scoring, address quality scores, and pincode-level delivery intelligence to help businesses reduce failed deliveries and optimize logistics operations.

---

## Who should use Shiprocket Sense

Sense is primarily for developers and businesses that want to integrate delivery intelligence into their checkout, order management, or logistics systems via API. It's ideal for large-volume sellers, logistics companies, and platforms wanting to proactively reduce RTO.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Shiprocket Sense?">

Shiprocket Sense is an API product that provides intelligence about delivery risk and address quality. It offers RTO risk scoring, address quality scores, and pincode-level delivery intelligence to help businesses reduce failed deliveries and optimize their logistics operations.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who should use Shiprocket Sense?">

Sense is primarily for developers and businesses that want to integrate delivery intelligence into their checkout, order management, or logistics systems via API. It's ideal for large-volume sellers, logistics companies, and platforms wanting to reduce RTO proactively.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

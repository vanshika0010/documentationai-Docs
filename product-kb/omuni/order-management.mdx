## How does Omuni handle order routing

Omuni automatically routes orders to the most appropriate warehouse based on inventory availability, proximity to the delivery address, and configured routing rules. This minimizes shipping cost and delivery time.

---

## Can Omuni handle partial order fulfillment

Yes. Omuni supports partial order fulfillment where items from the same order can be shipped from different warehouses or at different times, with tracking updates managed centrally.

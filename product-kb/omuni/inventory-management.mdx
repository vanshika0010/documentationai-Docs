## What all reporting is available on Omuni?

Omuni provides reporting on:
1. Order reports (by channel, status, date)
2. Inventory reports (current stock, low stock, movement)
3. Returns reports
4. Fulfillment performance reports
5. Channel-wise revenue and order volume

---

## How does inventory sync work across channels in Omuni?

Omuni maintains a central inventory pool. When an order is fulfilled from any channel:
1. Available inventory is deducted from the master pool
2. Updated inventory is pushed to all connected channels simultaneously
3. This prevents overselling across channels

---

## How to set safety stock levels in Omuni?

Safety stock (buffer stock) can be configured per SKU per warehouse in Omuni. When actual stock reaches the safety stock level, an alert is triggered for replenishment.

---

## Can I do cycle counts in Omuni?

Yes. Omuni supports cycle counts for inventory accuracy verification. You can schedule regular cycle counts for specific product categories or warehouse zones.

---

## How to handle stock adjustments in Omuni?

For stock adjustments (damage, returns, discrepancy):
1. Go to Inventory >> Adjustments
2. Select the SKU and warehouse
3. Enter the adjustment quantity and reason
4. Confirm the adjustment

All adjustments are logged with timestamps and reasons.

---

## Does Omuni support serialized inventory tracking?

Omuni supports batch/lot tracking and can be configured for serialized inventory tracking. Contact the Omuni team for specific serialized tracking requirements.

---

## How to transfer inventory between warehouses in Omuni?

For inter-warehouse transfers:
1. Go to Inventory >> Transfers
2. Create a new transfer request
3. Select source and destination warehouse
4. Add SKUs and quantities
5. Confirm and initiate transfer

Inventory is deducted from source and added to destination upon confirmation.

---

## What is the inventory accuracy in Omuni?

Omuni maintains high inventory accuracy through:
1. Barcode scanning for all stock movements
2. System-enforced pick verification
3. Regular cycle counts
4. Real-time inventory updates

For specific accuracy guarantees, contact the Omuni team.

## Can I use the Shiprocket wallet for Omuni?

Yes. Omuni is integrated with Shiprocket, and charges are managed through your Shiprocket wallet.

---

## What is Omuni?

Omuni is Shiprocket's Order Management System (OMS) and Warehouse Management System (WMS) that enables sellers to manage orders from multiple sales channels, inventory across multiple warehouses, and fulfillment operations from a single unified platform.

---

## Who should use Omuni?

Omuni is ideal for:
1. Multi-channel sellers (Shopify + Amazon + Flipkart)
2. Sellers with multiple warehouse locations
3. Businesses needing centralized inventory management
4. High-volume sellers needing advanced order routing
5. Brands looking to streamline omnichannel operations

---

## What is the difference between Omuni OMS and WMS?

OMS (Order Management System): Manages the lifecycle of orders from placement to fulfillment across multiple channels.

WMS (Warehouse Management System): Manages warehouse operations including inventory storage, picking, packing, and dispatch.

Omuni provides both OMS and WMS capabilities in a single integrated platform.

---

## How does Omuni integrate with Shiprocket for shipping?

Omuni is natively integrated with Shiprocket's shipping platform. Once an order is fulfilled by Omuni, it automatically creates a shipment in Shiprocket, selects the courier, generates the label, and tracks the delivery.

---

## Does Omuni support returns management?

Yes. Omuni supports end-to-end returns management:
1. Returns are received from connected channels
2. Return orders are processed in Omuni
3. Quality check can be performed at warehouse
4. Inventory is restocked or quarantined
5. Refund/replacement is triggered

---

## Can I use Omuni without a warehouse?

Omuni primarily serves sellers with physical inventory and warehouse operations. For sellers without their own warehouse, Shiprocket Fulfillment (SRF) may be a better option.

---

## What are the key features of Omuni?

Key Omuni features:
1. Multi-channel order management
2. Inventory management across warehouses
3. Order routing and prioritization
4. Returns and exchange management
5. Pick-pack-ship workflow management
6. Real-time inventory sync across channels
7. Analytics and reporting

---

## How to contact Omuni support?

For Omuni support:
1. Raise a ticket from the Help section in Shiprocket panel (select Omuni category)
2. Contact your dedicated Omuni account manager
3. Email the Omuni support team

---

## What is the onboarding process for Omuni?

Omuni onboarding involves:
1. Account setup and configuration
2. Channel integrations (Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
3. Warehouse setup
4. Product catalog import
5. Order routing rule configuration
6. Testing with sample orders
7. Go-live

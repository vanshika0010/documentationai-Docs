## What is Omuni OMS

Omuni OMS (Order Management System) is a multi-channel order management platform that helps brands manage orders from multiple sales channels (Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, etc.) from a single interface along with inventory management across warehouses.

---

## Which channels can be integrated with Omuni

Omuni supports integration with major e-commerce platforms and marketplaces including Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Nykaa, and custom websites via API.

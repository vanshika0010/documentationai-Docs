## Which sales channels can I integrate with Omuni?

Easily connect with Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Nykaa Fashion, TataCliq, and Meesho will be added to the list of marketplaces shortly.

---

## Can I add custom channels to Omuni?

Yes, Omuni supports custom channel integrations via API. Contact the Omuni team for custom channel setup.

---

## How to integrate Shopify with Omuni?

To integrate Shopify with Omuni:
1. Go to Omuni panel >> Settings >> Channels
2. Click on Add Channel
3. Select Shopify
4. Enter your Shopify store URL
5. Authenticate with your Shopify credentials
6. Configure sync settings
7. Save and enable the integration

---

## What data is synced from channels to Omuni?

Omuni syncs the following data from connected channels:
1. Orders (new, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled)
2. Products/SKUs
3. Inventory levels
4. Customer details
5. Returns/Exchange orders

---

## How frequently does Omuni sync orders from channels?

Omuni syncs orders from connected channels in near real-time (typically within a few minutes of order placement). You can also trigger a manual sync from the Omuni panel.

---

## Can I manage orders from multiple channels in one place with Omuni?

Yes. That's the primary benefit of Omuni. All orders from Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, and other connected channels flow into a single unified order management dashboard.

---

## What happens if a channel goes offline or disconnects from Omuni?

If a channel disconnects:
1. Order sync stops for that channel
2. You receive an alert notification
3. Reconnect the channel from Settings >> Channels
4. Omuni will sync missed orders after reconnection

---

## Can I filter orders by channel in Omuni?

Yes. In the Omuni order management dashboard, you can filter orders by:
* Channel (Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart, etc.)
* Order status
* Date range
* Location/warehouse

---

## How to handle channel-specific pricing in Omuni?

Omuni supports channel-specific pricing rules. You can configure different prices for different channels in the Product Catalog settings.

---

## Does Omuni support international channel integrations?

Omuni primarily focuses on India-based channels. For international channel requirements, contact the Omuni team.

---

## What is the maximum number of channels I can integrate with Omuni?

The number of channels depends on your Omuni plan. Check your plan limits in the Omuni settings.

---

## How to troubleshoot channel sync issues in Omuni?

If orders are not syncing from a channel:
1. Check channel connection status in Settings >> Channels
2. Verify API credentials are valid and not expired
3. Check for any error messages in the channel logs
4. Disconnect and reconnect the channel
5. Contact Omuni support if the issue persists

---

## Can I map products from different channels to the same SKU in Omuni?

Yes. Omuni supports SKU mapping across channels. This allows you to manage inventory under a single master SKU even if the product has different IDs on different channels.

---

## Does Omuni update inventory on channels after fulfillment?

Yes. When an order is fulfilled or returned, Omuni automatically updates the inventory levels on all connected channels in real-time.

---

## How to set up automatic order routing in Omuni?

Omuni supports automatic order routing rules based on:
* Channel
* Product type
* Location/pincode
* Inventory availability

Configure routing rules in Settings >> Order Routing in the Omuni panel.

---

## Can I set channel-specific fulfillment rules in Omuni?

Yes. You can configure channel-specific fulfillment rules in Omuni, such as routing Amazon orders to a specific warehouse or assigning specific couriers for Flipkart orders.

---

## What is the process to add a new channel to Omuni?

To add a new channel:
1. Go to Settings >> Channels in Omuni
2. Click Add Channel
3. Select the channel from the available list
4. Authenticate with the channel credentials
5. Configure sync preferences
6. Enable the channel
7. Test with a few orders to verify the integration

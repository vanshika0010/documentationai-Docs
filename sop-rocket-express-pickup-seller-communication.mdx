**A. Please find the SOP on Rocket Express Pickup and its charges:**

# 1. Overview

Rocket Express is Shiprocket’s in-house pickup fleet, primarily activated for low-volume sellers and the pincode identified facing repeated pickup failures by regular courier partner riders.

### 2. Key Benefits

- Supports smooth pickup operations and improves seller experience.
- Provides a guaranteed pickup attempt for eligible shipments.

### 3. Seller Communication

**Scenario 1: Seller enquires about Rocket Express charges**

Inform the seller that Rocket Express charges are included in the freight charges and are based on the applicable Shiprocket plan.

**Scenario 2: Seller insists on knowing the specific charges**

If the seller specifically asks for the charges, communicate that the approximate Rocket Express pickup cost is **₹20–₹25 per shipment**.

### 4. Important Guidelines

- Explain the benefits of Rocket Express pickup to the seller.
- Share the approximate charges only if the seller specifically insists.
- Do not communicate the approximate range as a fixed charge.
- For any charge-related dispute or request for exact pricing, verify the applicable charges through the approved internal process.

B. Call handling by the champ :

## **Call Script – Rocket Express Pickup**

**Scenario 1: Seller ko Rocket Express ke baare mein samjhana hai**

“Rocket Express Shiprocket ki apni in-house pickup service hai. Ye mainly un sellers ke liye activate ki jaati hai jinke shipments comparatively kam hain ya jahan regular courier partners ke riders multiple reasons ki wajah se pickup attempt nahi kar paate. Isse aapke pickup operations smooth rehte hain aur seller experience improve hota hai. Is service mein eligible shipments ke liye guaranteed pickup attempt provide kiya jaata hai.”

**Scenario 2: Seller charges ke baare mein poochta hai**

“Sir/Ma’am, Rocket Express ke charges aapke applicable Shiprocket plan ke according freight charges mein included hote hain.”

**Scenario 3: Seller specifically charges poochta hai ya insist karta hai**

“Rocket Express pickup ka approximate charge ₹20–₹25 per shipment hai".

**Advisor ke liye important points:**

- Pehle service ka benefit samjhaayein, phir charges discuss karein.
- ₹20–₹25 ko approximate charges hi batayein, fixed rate nahi.
- Guaranteed pickup attempt ko guaranteed successful pickup ke roop mein commit na karein.

  Amazon Courier Availability for Sellers

  Overview


  Amazon Courier is available only for verified Business Sellers as per Amazon's
  compliance policy.


  Individual Sellers who are not registered as Business Sellers will not have
  access to Amazon Courier.


  Seller Eligibility

  Amazon Courier is available only for verified Business Sellers.

  Individual Sellers who are not registered as Business Sellers will not be able
  to access Amazon Courier.

  The Seller must complete the required KYC.

  The Seller must have a valid GSTIN to register as a Business Seller.

  If the Seller Asks How to Activate Amazon Courier


  If a Seller proactively asks how to activate Amazon Courier:


  Advise the Seller to register as a Business Seller.

  The Seller must complete the required KYC process.

  The Seller must provide or complete their GSTIN details.

  Once the Seller is verified and registered as a Business Seller, Amazon
  Courier can be activated, subject to eligibility.

  Champ Process - When a Call Lands


  When a Seller contacts us because Amazon Courier is not visible:


  Open the Company tab.

  Check whether the Seller's KYC is completed.

  Verify whether the Seller is registered as a Business Seller.

  If KYC is incomplete:

  Inform the Seller that Amazon Courier is available only to verified Business
  Sellers.

  Guide the Seller to complete KYC.

  If KYC is completed but the Seller is not registered as a Business Seller:

  Inform the Seller that they need to register as a Business Seller.

  Guide the Seller to complete the required Business Seller registration and
  GSTIN requirements.

  If KYC is completed and the Seller is registered as a Business Seller, but
  Amazon Courier is still not available:

  Create a ticket for the Tech Team for further investigation.

  Key Reminder


  Do not raise a Tech Team ticket solely because an Individual Seller cannot see
  Amazon Courier.


  First check the Seller's KYC status and Business Seller registration in the
  Company tab.


  Only create a Tech Team ticket when the Seller has completed KYC, is
  registered as a Business Seller, and Amazon Courier is still unavailable.

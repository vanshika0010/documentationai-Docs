## Overview

Amazon Courier is available only for verified Business Sellers as per Amazon's compliance policy. Individual Sellers who are not registered as Business Sellers will not have access to Amazon Courier.

## Seller Eligibility

- Amazon Courier is available only for verified Business Sellers.
- Individual Sellers who are not registered as Business Sellers will not be able to access Amazon Courier.
- The Seller must complete the required KYC and have a valid GSTIN to register as a Business Seller.

## If the Seller Asks How to Activate Amazon Courier

If a Seller proactively asks how to activate Amazon Courier:

1. Advise the Seller to register as a Business Seller.
2. The Seller must complete the required KYC process.
3. The Seller must provide or complete their GSTIN details.
4. Once the Seller is verified and registered as a Business Seller, Amazon Courier can be activated, subject to eligibility.

## Champ Process — When a Call Lands

When a Seller contacts us because Amazon Courier is not visible:

1. Open the **Company** tab.
2. Check whether the Seller's KYC is completed.
3. Verify whether the Seller is registered as a Business Seller.

**If KYC is incomplete or the Seller is not registered as a Business Seller:**

- Explain that Amazon Courier is available only to verified Business Sellers.
- Guide the Seller to complete KYC and Business Seller registration, including GSTIN requirements where applicable.

**If KYC is completed and the Seller is registered as a Business Seller, but Amazon Courier is still not available:**

- Create a ticket for the Tech Team for further investigation.

## Quick Decision Guide

| Scenario                                                                           | Action                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| KYC not completed                                                                  | Guide the Seller to complete KYC.                                                  |
| KYC completed but not registered as a Business Seller                              | Guide the Seller to register as a Business Seller and complete GSTIN requirements. |
| KYC completed and Business Seller registered, but Amazon Courier still unavailable | Create a ticket for the Tech Team.                                                 |

## Key Reminder

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Do not raise a Tech Team ticket solely because an Individual Seller cannot see Amazon Courier. First verify KYC completion and Business Seller registration in the Company tab.
</Callout>

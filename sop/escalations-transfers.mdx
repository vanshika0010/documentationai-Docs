## SOP — L1 to L2 Transfer Protocol

**Important Note:**

All social threats, management, or consumer forum issues will be **live-transferred**. If L2 is unavailable, TL/SME/Mentor will take a call exceptionally.

If the seller requests the next level at the L2 stage, the TL/SME must call back — no denial will be given to the seller. Direct transfer to the manager level is not possible.

### Case 1: L1 Process (L2 Available)

1. Gather details: AWB, Ticket ID, Last resolution.
2. Create ticket if not available.
3. Inform seller → take hold permission & share hold time.
4. Mark **RED SOS**.
5. Brief L2 (AWB, Ticket No., VOC, hold time).
6. Transfer call to L2.

### Case 2: L2 Not Available

_Except Social/Management/Consumer cases._

1. Apologize for delay & inform seniors are busy.
2. Offer callback within **30 minutes**.
3. Mention the ticket notes and share the ticket ID with the respective TL's.
4. Mark **RED SOS**.
5. Close the call.

### L2 Opening Script (Internal)

> "Good Morning/Afternoon/Evening. Your call has been transferred to the supervisor desk. My name is [Name]. How may I assist you?"

### L1 to L2 Handover Script (Internal)

> "Good Morning/Afternoon/Evening, this is [Name]. I have Mr./Ms. [Seller Name] on the line facing [issue]. AWB: [AWB] | Ticket ID: [ID] | Last update: [Date]. Seller is aware of the resolution but wants to speak with a senior."

### L2 to Seller (After Transfer)

> "Thank you for your patience. Your call has been transferred to the supervisor desk. Apologies for the wait."

> "I understand your concern regarding [issue]. Let me assist you further."

## SOP — KAM Callback

If seller is requesting a callback from KAM or action is required from KAM:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Properties Tag | Action by KAM |
| Ticket Status | Awaiting Customer Response |
| Group / Agent | Respective KAM Group and Name |

- Click **Reply to All** on the ticket.
- Remove seller's email ID from the reply.
- Add KAM's email ID.
- Include callback request details, Company ID, and registered number in notes.

**Callback TAT:** 24 working hours.

## SOP — Refund

### When to Give Refunds

- Seller is very upset, frustrated, or distressed in voice or communication.
- Seller asks for a refund immediately.
- Seller has contacted customer support **3 or more times** for the same issue.
- Cases involving ORM / Legal / Management threats.

### Refund Matrix

| Type | L1 Authority | L2 Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Freight Refund | Up to Rs. 500 | Up to Rs. 1,000 |

> L2 freight refund does not apply to heavy or large shipments over 10 kg.

### Cases Where Freight Refund Can Be Initiated on Seller Call

- EDD + 5 days and shipment has not been attempted.
- Without attempt RTO, whether LOPS reason is available or not.
- Shipment has BPR, less than 3 attempts, or zero attempts.

### Soft Loss — In Case of Dispute

- Full amount refunded at L2 if SLA is breached.
- Forward SLA: 7 days.
- RTO SLA: 15 days.

**Script if seller asks why a refund is being processed now:**

> We understand how frustrating this experience must have been. After reviewing the details shared by the courier and carefully assessing the situation, we sincerely regret the inconvenience. As a gesture of goodwill, we are processing a refund for you.

## SOP — OSLA Cases

If a seller approaches **outside of SLA (Out of SLA)**, tag the case as **OSLA** on FD (excluding international and buyer desk cases).

**Do NOT deny at any instance.**

**Sample OSLA scenarios:**
- TAT of 72 hours is breached for ACR response and seller approaches CC.
- Manifest was requested but seller approached after 72 hours of manifest request.
- TAT to raise weight or delivery dispute is breached.

## SOP — Common Escalation Scenarios

### Case 1: Seller Reaches for Same Issue 2nd Time in a Day

**Context:** Committed SLA has been breached, or ticket was closed without resolution and SOP requires the seller to raise a new ticket for the same issue.

1. L1 transfers live call to L2 — **do not stretch the call**.
2. L1 apologizes to the seller before transfer.
3. L2 assigns the case as **RED SOS** to LOPS with **4 working hours TAT**.
4. **RED SOS** to be tracked by SR team.
5. In case of threat, tag case as **BAU ED**.

### Case 2: Seller Reaches Post 6PM with Callback Committed in 4–5 Hours

1. L1 tries live transfer to L2.
2. If L2 not connected, inform the seller that callback will happen before **12 noon next day** by L2/Experience Center team.
3. Ensure callback is completed before 12 noon by the Experience Center team.

## Brand Boost

**1. Objective**

To standardize the process of introducing, explaining, activating, deactivating, and handling queries/escalations related to the Brand Boost feature for Shiprocket sellers.

**2. Brand Boost Introduction**

Inform the seller:

- Shiprocket has launched Brand Boost, a feature that provides a branded tracking experience

- Helps increase brand awareness among buyers via the Shiprocket platform

- Charges: ₹2.99 + 18% GST per shipment

**3. Feature Explanation**

- Brand Logo – Add your logo to the tracking page

- Menu Links – Display brand and contact details

- Social Media Links – Add social platform links for promotion

- Instagram Feed – Showcase brand images and posts

- YouTube Integration – Display product/brand videos

- Promotional Banners – Highlight offers and discounts

- Custom URL – Add product links for easy navigation

- Product Recommendations – Promote top-selling/similar products (Limit: 3 sections × 8 products each)

- Multiple Themes – Customize tracking page appearance

- Allow returns from tracking page

**4. Benefits Communication**

- 65% reduction in "Where is my order" queries

- 45% reduction in support cost

- 15% increase in repeat purchases

- 2X improvement in NPS (customer satisfaction)

**5. Billing & Charges**

Applicable on: Delivered + RTO Delivered shipments
Charges deducted: When shipment is marked as Pickup Scheduled
Billing: Monthly VAS (Value Added Service) invoice shared with seller

**6. Important Note**

Billing logic updated since June 2023. Charges apply to all shipments reaching terminal status.

In case of disputes (seller claims non-activation):

- Assign ticket to Tech Team

- Request validation of activation logs

- Attach invoice/sample if required

**7. Activation/Deactivation Process**

Login to Seller Panel >> Go to "Value Added Services" >> Brand Boost >> Activate/Deactivate

**8. Invoice Communication**

- Invoice will be shared like regular invoices

- It will be a monthly VAS invoice

**9. Validation via ICRM Plugin**

Search with company id >> Go to Financial >> Tech Product, here we can see who activated/deactivated the VAS and along with date.

**10. FAQs Handling**

Q1: Is this available for offline sellers?

- Primarily beneficial for online sellers

Q2: Can seller activate via mobile app?

- Not available on Shiprocket app

Q3: Instagram image selection option?

- No manual selection; auto-sync applies

**11. Additional Guidelines**

- Ensure correct and complete information is provided

- Avoid committing features that are under development

### ShipSure — RTO Refund Program

Automated RTO refund program — no claims or paperwork required.

**How it works:**

- On onboarding to ShipSure, a small premium fee is charged per shipment.

- For every RTO, a flat pre-decided refund is automatically credited to the wallet.

- All RTOs are eligible (except fraudulent and very high risk orders).

- Covers both COD and Prepaid orders.

- No cap on the refund amount.

**Refund cycle:** Monthly — all eligible RTOs within a month are settled together on the **7th of the following month**.

## Notify

Shiprocket Notify allows sellers to send WhatsApp, sms and email communication to buyers for real-time order updates.

**Seller Panel Path**: Value Added Services (left menu) > Notify

### WhatsApp Communication

A seamless tool designed to send real-time order tracking updates directly to buyers via WhatsApp. Shiprocket sends WhatsApp order updates to buyers at the following statuses: Packed, Picked, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Arriving Early, Delivery Delayed, and Delivered.

**Benefits:**

- Keeps buyers updated in a non-intrusive manner.

- Reduces post-purchase anxiety.

- Improves read rate to 94%.

- Reduces customer queries by 30%.

**Themes**: You can choose from 3 themes:&#x20;

- **Visual**: Best suited for sending images with tracking updates

- **Professional**: Best suited for buyers ordering items for their business.

- **Casual**: Best suited for buyers ordering items for their personal use.

**Charges:** Deducted from wallet - a small amount per shipment.

**Demo:** Value Added Services > Notify > Try Demo > Enter phone number with active WhatsApp > Get Demo Message.

**Activate/Deactivate:** Value Added Services > Notify > Activate/Deactivate.

### Email & SMS Notification

This allows sellers to send order updates via email and sms.&#x20;

- SMS header for buyer notifications **cannot be changed**. Current notifications are sent for: Picked, Shipped, Out for Delivery, etc.

- If a buyer has DND enabled, they cannot be reached via the in-built customer connect module. Contact them through another medium.

### RTO Score

Enable RTO Score to improve your Delivery Success Rate and reduce RTO risk.

**Trial period:** Free for 30 days from activation.

- Charges will be automatically deducted from Wallet.

- Charges deducted for High and Low prediction for COD orders only.

| Score | Action                                             |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| High  | Take buyer/purchase confirmation before shipping   |
| Low   | Ship any order value; no pre-confirmation required |

#### Benefits:

- **All new Risk AI**: Enhanced RTO Prediction, trained on 1.8 billion data points.

- **Next best actions**: Get preventive actions \&amp; accurate insights to reduce RTO risk.

- **All new Address quality score**: Reduce shipping costs for bad quality addresses with an AI, trained on 2.9 billion data points.

- **Boost customer engagement**: A new customer support tool with RTO risks, insights, customer calling scripts and free calls.

- **Revised hassle-free billing**: Charges for only COD orders will be deducted from the wallet automatically.

> Not visible to SR Engage sellers.

**Demo:** Seller Panel > Value Added Services (left menu) > RTO Score > See Video

### Delivery Boost

AI feature that auto-initiates delivery failure messages to buyers and requests reattempt confirmation.

- Free for sellers who joined after 22nd February 2023.

- Trial activates after **20 BPR** (Buyer Positive Responses). Before 20 BPR, no communication is sent.

- After free trial ends, seller cannot avail free service again.

**Check activation status:** ICRM > NDR tab

### Shipment SR Secure

Sellers can secure shipments valued **Above ₹2,500 to ₹4,76,190**.

Provides protection/claim eligibility for high-value shipments.

**Selective Secure (Manual Selection):**

How to Opt:

1. While creating order → Select "Secure Shipment = Yes"

2. Freight cost increases accordingly

3. Shipment gets secured

**Bulk Orders:**
Select multiple shipments → Click Ship → Choose Secured / Unsecured option → Applies to all selected shipments

**Blanket Secure (Auto Secure):**

How it Works:

- All eligible shipments (above ₹2,500–₹4,76,190) are auto-secured

- No toggle visible during order creation

**Path:** Seller Panel → Settings → Value Added Services → Auto-Secure Shipments

**How to Identify Secured Shipment:**
Check ICRM Plugin — Field: "SR Secured" = "YES" — Located under Freight / Shipping Charges tab

**Important Notes:**

1. Not applicable for Return Shipments

2. Applicable only within defined value range

**Claim TAT Breach — Assignment Rule:**

- Above ₹2,500 – ₹5,000 → Assign to Tech Support Channel

- Above ₹5,000 → Assign to: Vipin Kumar, Channel: SR Secure, Claim Not Received

**Advisor Tips:**

- Always check if shipment is secured before raising claim

- Verify shipment value before assigning case

- Guide seller on Selective vs Blanket based on usage

**Quick Summary:**

- Selective = Manual choice

- Blanket = Auto-secure

- Check "SR Secured = YES"

- Follow value-based escalation matrix

## How to Do on Seller Panel

### Activate / Deactivate Brand Boost

- **Activate/Deactivate:** Login to Seller Panel >> Go to "Value Added Services" >> Brand Boost >> Activate/Deactivate

### Enable Order Verification

Follow this path on seller panel: Settings > Additional Settings > Shipment Features > Order Verification > Toggle ON

- **Verify single order:** Orders > New tab > More (...) > Mark as Verified

- **Verify bulk orders:** Orders > Select all > Bulk Actions > Verify Orders

- **Call buyer for verification:** Orders > Select order > Call Buyer > Enter contact > Initiate Call to Buyer

### Brand Boost (Tracking Page)

**Use this path to activate Brand Boost:&#x20;**&#x42;uyer Experience > Tracking Page > Activate Now

#### Customize Tracking Page

**Buyer Experience > Tracking Page** — upload brand logo, add social media links, set themes, add promotional banners.

#### Allow Returns from Tracking Page

Buyer Experience > Brand Boost > scroll to bottom > Toggle ON "Allow Buyers to initiate return of products directly from the tracking page" > Save

#### Tracking Page Analytics (Dashboard)

Dashboard > Tracking Page tab:

- **Unique Visitors** — distinct individuals who visited the tracking page.

- **Views** — total number of times tracking page was loaded.

- Filter by date range (date picker top-left below header).

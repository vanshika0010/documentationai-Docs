  Buyer Protect — Terms and Conditions  


  1. What is Buyer Protect 


  Buyer Protect is an optional, paid add-on that assists a customer if an order
  is lost in transit, or 

  is delivered in damaged condition. If a covered event occurs and a claim is
  approved, the 

  customer will receive a refund. 


  2. Coverage (What is covered) 


  2.1 Lost in transit 

  When covered: The shipment does not arrive within twenty days from the order
  date. 

  Benefit: Partial or Full Refund (dependent on claim assessment). 

  2.2 Damaged on arrival 

  When covered: The item arrives in physically damaged condition – product that
  has suffered 

  physical harm during transit or delivery, resulting in breakage, deformation,
  malfunction, 

  leakage, or the absence of essential components, beyond usage. 

  Benefit: Partial or Full Refund (dependent on claim assessment). 


  3. Exclusions (What is not covered) 

  ● Product shown as Delivered but Not Received. 

  ● Product delivered is different to what was ordered, or delivered in a
  quantity short of what 

  was ordered. 

  ● Damage or defect occurring after delivery, or damage due to improper
  handling or 

  installation not arranged through an authorised partner. 

  ● Digital goods or services, gift cards, or items sold “as-is” or “open-box”. 

  ● Orders never sent (cancelled) or never shipped. 

  ● Address issues resulting in mis-delivery, including entering the wrong
  address or missing 

  specific address information. 

  ● Intentional acts, frauds, criminal or evil acts of the delivery staff or
  service users that lead 

  to package delay or loss. 

  ● Fake logistics information used for claims. 

  ● Delay or loss caused by wars, hostilities, military actions, armed
  conflicts, strikes, riots, 

  civil commotion, terrorism. 

  ● Delay or loss caused by natural disasters, such as earthquakes, floods,
  tsunamis, 

  mudslides, typhoons, and snowstorms. 

  ● Claims arising from repeated purchases of the same product from the same
  merchant by 

  the same customer. 

  ● An incorrect or incomplete delivery address entered by the customer; or
  deliveries that 

  were refused or left unsigned where a one-time password or signature was
  captured, if 

  applicable. 

  ● Events of force majeure (including, as examples, extreme weather and natural
  disasters). 

  ● Prohibited or fragile items shipped without adequate packing. 

  ● Minor cosmetic imperfections (such as small scratches, dents, packaging
  damage, or 

  minor colour shade differences) that do not affect functionality or usage are
  not 

  considered damaged. 

  ● Buyer Protect does not apply to disputes related to authenticity,
  originality, or perceived 

  brand value of products purchased from third-party sellers. If a seller lists
  a branded or 

  branded-style product, Buyer Protect applies only if the delivered product
  does not match 

  the images, specifications or description mentioned on the product page — not
  to 

  expectations regarding global brand authenticity. 

  Buyer Protect is intended to safeguard against transit-related issues. It is
  not a replacement for 

  product verification or due diligence on branded goods offered at high
  promotional discounts. 

  We encourage buyers to verify product/brand claims directly from the seller
  before purchase.

  Lost / Not delivered 

  Earliest time to raise 

  Twenty days from the order 

  date 

  Latest time to raise 

  Thirty days from the order 

  date 

  Damaged 

  On delivery 

  Claims submitted outside these time limits will be declined. 

  Five days from delivery 


  5. Conditions to raise a claim (What must be 

  submitted) 


  ● Order identification number with the air waybill number, and contact
  details. 

  ● For Damaged claims: one continuous unboxing video, plus two photographs – a
  clear 

  close-up of the damage, and a photo of the packaging/parcel as received with
  the AWB 

  visible. A claim missing any of these three items will not be processed. 

  ● Any additional information reasonably requested to verify the claim. 


  Your unboxing video must show, in one continuous take: 


  1. The sealed parcel and the shipping label (full label visible) 

  2. Opening the parcel (cutting the seal) 

  3. The inner packaging and all contents laid out together 

  4. Close-ups of the item, model/SKU/variant, and any damage or discrepancy 

  6. Outcomes and refunds 

  If a claim is approved, the refund will be processed to the bank account
  details provided by the 

  customer.The bank account provided for refund processing must belong to the
  user who has 

  purchased buyer protect. Refunds eligible will only be processed for such
  cases. Processing 

  times depend on the customer’s bank. 

  Shiprocket shall not be liable for any refund, reimbursement, or payment that
  is processed to an 

  incorrect bank account as a result of the customer providing inaccurate,
  incomplete, or incorrect 

  account details. The customer is solely responsible for self-declaring and
  verifying the 

  correctness of their bank account information, and any loss arising from such
  incorrect 

  declarations shall be borne entirely by the customer. 


  7. Maximum refund amount (Caps and limits) 


  ● Per-customer cap: The total refund amount is capped at ₹2,500 per customer
  in any 

  rolling thirty-day period across all claims. 

  ● Per-order cap: The refund will not exceed the order value and may be subject
  to a partial 

  or full refund depending upon assessment. 

  8. How to file a claim (Process steps) 

  1. Message us on WhatsApp from the number linked to your order, or reply to
  the order 

  confirmation message. 

  2. Select the order you need help with, then select the reason (Haven't
  received my order; 

  Received in Damaged Condition; Wrong Item Received; Short Item Received 

  3. Confirm the issue type and follow the steps shown – this will differ
  depending on what you 

  select. Reports of a wrong or short item, or anything outside of the above, do
  not lead to a 

  refund under Buyer Protect; instead, we'll help you draft an email directly to
  the seller. 

  4. For Lost or Damaged claims that are covered, upload the required evidence
  as prompted. 

  5. If approved, you'll be asked to confirm the refund amount before it's
  processed. 

  Confirmation and updates will be sent to you on the same WhatsApp thread. 

  9. Buyer Protect Coupon (Rewards) 

  From time to time, Shiprocket may issue a complimentary Buyer Protect coupon
  to a customer, 

  entitling them to purchase Buyer Protect free of charge on a future order.
  Such a coupon: 

  ● is issued at Shiprocket's discretion and is not guaranteed on any order; 

  ● is valid for a limited period of 30 days or, as communicated at the time it
  is issued; 

  ● may be applied automatically at the point of purchasing Buyer Protect on an
  eligible future 

  order, without the need for a separate code, where technically supported;
  alternatively, a 

  code may be provided for manual entry; 

  ● is limited to one use, is non-transferable, and has no cash value; 

  ● does not affect the coverage, exclusions, evidence requirements, or refund
  caps 

  described elsewhere in these Terms, which apply identically to an order
  protected using a 

  coupon. 

  Special Terms & Conditions 

  1) Free / Promotional Products 

  Exclusion: Items supplied free of charge (including gifts, samples, or
  promotional add-ons 

  marked at ₹0) are not covered under Buyer Protect. 

  No claims permitted: Claims for non-delivery or damage relating to such
  free/promotional 

  items will not be accepted. 

  2) Multiple-Item Orders — Pro Rata Refund for Partial Discrepancy 

  Scope: If an order contains multiple items and a verified issue (non-delivery
  or damage) affects 

  only part of the order, any approved refund will be issued on a pro rata
  basis, at the individual 

  unit level where an item was ordered in multiple quantity. 

  Method: The pro rata amount will be calculated using the per-item price (or,
  where applicable, 

  the price allocated to the affected item/variant within the order invoice).
  Only the affected units 

  qualify for refund. 

  3) Coverage Limited to In-Transit Issues 

  Transit only: Buyer Protect applies only to issues occurring during transit
  (for example, 

  non-delivery or damage during shipment). 

  Not covered: Orders that are cancelled, not dispatched, or otherwise not
  handed over to the 

  carrier fall outside the scope of Buyer Protect and are not eligible for
  claims under this program. 

  4) Fast-Track Claims and No Double Recovery 

  Fast-track process: Buyer Protect facilitates expedited claim resolution,
  under which 

  Shiprocket may refund the customer on the merchant's behalf for covered issues
  in accordance 

  with the program policy. 

  No double refund: If the customer receives a refund via Buyer Protect, the
  customer is not 

  eligible to also receive a refund directly from the merchant for the same
  issue (“no double 

  recovery”). 

  Return on refund: Where a refund is approved, the customer must return the
  product to the 

  merchant in accordance with the return instructions provided. Return freight
  costs shall be borne 

  by the customer. 

  These Special Terms form part of the Buyer Protect Terms & Conditions and
  should be read 

  together with the main terms (coverage, evidence requirements, timelines, and
  exclusions). 

  Buyer Protect — Frequently Asked 

  Questions 

  What is Buyer Protect? 

  Buyer Protect is an optional add-on you can purchase on your order's tracking
  page. If 

  something goes wrong with your delivery – it gets lost in transit, or arrives
  damaged – Buyer 

  Protect gives you a fast way to get a refund, without a long back-and-forth. 

  What does Buyer Protect cover? 

  ● Lost in transit – your order doesn't arrive within twenty days of your order
  date. 

  ● Damaged on arrival – your order arrives physically damaged, in a way that
  affects its use 

  (breakage, deformation, malfunction, leakage, or missing essential parts). 

  If either of these happens and your claim is approved, you'll receive a
  partial or full refund, 

  depending on assessment. 

  What isn't covered? 

  A few common examples: 

  ● Your order shows as delivered, but you say you didn't receive it (we can
  still help – see 

  below). 

  ● You received the wrong item, or fewer items than you ordered (we can still
  help – see 

  below). 

  ● Minor cosmetic marks (small scratches, dents, or packaging damage) that
  don't affect 

  how the product works. 

  ● Damage that happens after delivery, or from handling/installation not done
  through an 

  authorised partner. 

  ● Orders that were cancelled or never shipped. 

  ● Delivery issues caused by an incorrect address you entered. 

  ● Events outside anyone's control – natural disasters, wars, riots, and
  similar. 

  The full list is in the Terms and Conditions. 

  How much does Buyer Protect cost? 

  The fee depends on your order value and is shown upfront on the tracking page
  before you 

  purchase – there's never a hidden or last-step charge. 

  How do I raise a claim? 

  Just message us on WhatsApp – from the number your order is linked to, or by
  replying to your 

  order confirmation message. Select your order, then tell us what happened.
  We'll guide you 

  through the rest right there on WhatsApp. 

  What evidence do I need for a damaged item claim? 

  Three things, every time: 

  1. One continuous unboxing video – starting from the sealed parcel with the
  shipping label 

  visible, through opening it, to a close-up of the damage. 

  2. A clear close-up photo of the damage. 

  3. A photo of the packaging/parcel as it arrived with AWB visible. 

  A claim submitted without all three won't be processed, so it's worth filming
  before you unpack 

  rather than after. 

  How much will I get refunded? 

  It depends on assessment, and is capped at whichever is lower: your order
  value, or ₹2,500. 

  There's also a combined limit of ₹2,500 across all your Buyer Protect claims
  in any rolling 

  30-day period – so if you've claimed recently, a new claim might be partially
  or fully limited by 

  that. We'll always tell you the exact amount before asking you to confirm. 

  How and when will I get my refund? 

  Refunds are processed only to the bank account of the user who purchased Buyer
  Protect. 

  The account holder name must match the customer’s name exactly. 

  Refund requests with mismatched names will not be processed. 

  What if I received the wrong item, or fewer items than I ordered? 

  These aren't covered by a Buyer Protect refund, but we don't leave you without
  help – we'll draft 

  an email to the seller for you, right there on WhatsApp, that you can review
  and send yourself. 

  My claim was rejected – can I ask for a second look? 

  Yes. Whenever a claim is declined, you'll be given the option to have it
  manually reviewed by 

  our team instead of just accepting the outcome. Manual review can take a
  little longer, but it's a 

  genuine second look, not a formality. 

  I have a free Buyer Protect coupon – how do I use it? 

  If you've been issued one, it can be applied when you go to purchase Buyer
  Protect on an 

  eligible order. It's valid for a limited time from when it's issued (30 days),
  so it's worth using it on 

  your next order rather than saving it indefinitely. 

  Can I get a refund from both Buyer Protect and the seller for the same 

  issue? 

  No – if you receive a refund through Buyer Protect, you won't also be eligible
  for a separate 

  refund from the seller for the same issue. If your claim is approved, you may
  be asked to return 

  the item to the seller as instructed; return shipping in that case is your
  responsibility. 

  Who do I contact if I still need help? 

  Just message us on the same WhatsApp thread – if something doesn't fit any of
  the above, we'll 

  point you in the right direction from there.

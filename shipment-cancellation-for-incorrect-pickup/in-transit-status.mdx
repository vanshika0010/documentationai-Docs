---
title: Shipment Cancellation for Incorrect Pickup/In-Transit Status
---
If a shipment is marked as **Picked Up/In Transit** but has not been physically picked up, and the seller requests cancellation, Champs must follow the below procedure:

1. **Seller Communication:** Inform the seller that we will verify the actual shipment status with the courier partner before proceeding with the cancellation.
2. **Ticket Creation:** The Champ must create an **NFCR ticket and route it to the LOPS Pickup channel** for further investigation and resolution.
3. **Cancellation and Refund:** If the courier partner confirms that the shipment has not been physically picked up, our team will cancel the shipment and refund the freight charges.
4. **Resolution TAT:** Communicate a resolution timeline of **5–7 working days** to the seller.

**Important Instructions:**

- Champs must not raise force cancellation requests directly to the leads.
- Champs must not cancel the shipment directly through their ICRM or Plugin ICRM.
- All such cases must be routed through the **NFCR ticket to the LOPS Pickup channel** for verification and further action.

Please ensure strict adherence to this updated process to avoid unauthorized cancellations and ensure a consistent seller experience.

Issue category: Shipment Pickup

Sub category: Shipment status Picked Up but not picked from Seller.

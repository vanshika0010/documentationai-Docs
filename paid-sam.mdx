title: Paid SAM | How to avail Paid SAM service
---
We have launched **Paid SAM subscription**, enabling sellers to subscribe to a Dedicated Account Manager directly from the Shiprocket panel at **₹499/month**.

This initiative makes dedicated account management accessible to a wider set of sellers, allowing them to avail themselves of personalised account support through a self-serve subscription journey.

### **1. Discovery & Entry Points**

Sellers can discover and subscribe to SAM through two entry points on the Shiprocket panel:

**Entry Point 1: Home Dashboard → Need Help**

A new option, **“Talk to a Dedicated Manager,”** has been added to the Need Help dropdown in the top navigation bar.

### **2. Subscription & Purchase Journey**

The subscription journey comprises three steps:

**Step 1: Explore SAM**

- Both entry points redirect sellers to the SAM information pop-up.
- The pop-up highlights the three key benefits of having a Dedicated Account Manager, along with the subscription fee of ₹499/month.
- Sellers can proceed by clicking **Pay Now**.\

**Step 2: Approve UPI Autopay Mandate**

- Sellers are prompted to approve a monthly UPI Autopay mandate of ₹499.
- Payment can be completed by scanning the QR code using any UPI app or through the UPI payment link.\
  \

**Step 3: Subscription Activation & Account Manager Assignment**

- Once the UPI mandate is successfully approved, the SAM subscription is activated automatically.
- A Dedicated Account Manager is assigned to the seller immediately.
- The seller receives an email confirmation.
- The subscription status and assigned Account Manager details are updated in the seller’s profile section.

With this launch, sellers can independently discover, purchase and activate a Dedicated Account Manager without requiring manual intervention from internal teams.

**The entire journey—from discovery and payment to subscription activation and Account Manager assignment—is self-serve.**

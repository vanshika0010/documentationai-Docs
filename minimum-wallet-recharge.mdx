---
title: 'Minimum Wallet Recharge '
---
- **Direct Recharge:** ₹500 is now the minimum amount for both first-time and subsequent recharges.
- **Ship Now:** A minimum of ₹500 is now enforced even for this flow. If the required amount is below ₹500, the seller will be charged ₹500 and the excess amount will be credited to their wallet. If the required amount is ₹500 or more, the exact amount will be charged. The Pay Per Ship feature has also been removed.
- **Shiprocket Quick:** Existing recharge and wallet conditions remain unchanged.

The change applies to both **new and existing sellers** using the standard recharge flows.
